Cat Breeding for Profit

A complete 12-module course on running a small cat breeding cattery - breeds, housing, feeding, queening, health, and business.

12 modules · practical lessons · quizzes · free

What you will learn

  • How to choose the right cat breed for your market and climate
  • How to build or adapt a cattery that keeps cats healthy and productive
  • How to feed breeding queens for condition, fertility and milk
  • How to manage breeding, pregnancy, queening and kitten care
  • How to prevent and treat the six diseases that kill kittens
  • How to socialise kittens and sell them at premium prices
  • How to keep records, pedigrees and contracts that protect you
  • How to build a reputation and scale a breeding business

Modules

Who this is for

  • Beginners starting a first breeding pair of cats
  • Existing breeders wanting a more systematic operation
  • Catteries adding a new breed or improving production
  • Anyone considering a low-space, high-value animal enterprise near a city
