2. Local Ingredients and Their Value

Fish meal is the most expensive ingredient in fish feed. Learning to replace it with cheaper local proteins is the single biggest cost saving available.

Lesson 2.4 - Replacing fish meal

Fish meal costs $1.00-1.80/kg. Soya meal costs $0.50-0.80/kg. Replacing half the fish meal with soya cuts cost 20-25% with almost no growth loss - if you add the missing amino acids (methionine and lysine).

Fish meal %Soya %Growth vs full fish mealCost saving
25%0%100%Baseline
15%15%95%15%
10%25%90%22%
5%35%80%28%
Never use stale or rancid fish meal. Poor quality fish meal carries bacteria that kill fish. If it smells of ammonia or is damp, do not use it.
