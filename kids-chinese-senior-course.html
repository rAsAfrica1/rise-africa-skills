Chinese for Teens

Learn the system behind the characters and you stop memorising and start reading.

Ages 12–168 modules 🪨 Starts with no device

Nobody memorises fifty thousand Chinese characters. They learn a few hundred building blocks and then see those blocks inside everything else. This course gives you the blocks, the tones, the market vocabulary and the scholarship vocabulary — and finishes with you introducing yourself properly to a Chinese speaker.

The 8 modules

1
🪨 No device needed

Blocks, Not Characters

Complicated characters are simple characters stacked together, and once you see the stacking you never look at a character the same way again.

木 tree → 林 woods → 森 forest
2
🪨 No device needed

Buy or Sell — One Stroke Apart

Tones are not an accent, they are part of the word, and two words can differ only by their tone and mean opposite things.

买 mǎi = buy 卖 mài = sell
3
🪨 No device needed

How Much Is That, Really

Price vocabulary is the most immediately useful Chinese you can own, because the first Chinese speaker you meet may well be selling something.

多少钱？ = how + few + money = 'how much money?'
4
📱 Shared phone

zh ch sh Against z c s

Six consonants split into two families, and getting them apart is the difference between being understood and being asked to repeat yourself.

zh ch sh 👅⬆️ | z c s 👅➡️
5
📱 Shared phone

The Scholarship Words

There are real Chinese government scholarships open to students across the continent, and the vocabulary that surrounds them is small enough to learn in one sitting.

学 xué = to study → 学生 student · 学校 school · 大学 university
6
📱 Shared phone

Typing Chinese With a Phone Keyboard

You type Chinese by typing Pinyin and choosing the character — which means your Pinyin and your tones are what unlock the writing.

type 'shouji' → 手机 · 收集 · 首级 → pick 手机
7
🤖 Salvage kit

Read the Signs and the Shipping Marks

Chinese appears on containers, packaging and equipment near you, and the handful of characters used on signs is a short, closed list.

出口 = out + mouth = exit / export
8
🤖 Salvage kit

Capstone: Introduce Yourself Like You Mean It

Today you deliver a real spoken self-introduction in Chinese — the exact thing asked for at a scholarship interview or a first meeting with a Chinese trader.

你好，我叫… 我来自… 我在学中文。很高兴认识你。
For a parent or teacher

Your teenager will finish able to read and write around sixty characters, distinguish the tones and the difficult zh/ch/sh consonants, handle basic price negotiation vocabulary, and deliver a competent spoken self-introduction. Chinese-language ability is a real differentiator for trade apprenticeships, contracting work and the Chinese government scholarship programmes that recruit across the continent every year.
