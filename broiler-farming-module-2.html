2. Housing and Equipment

Broilers have no tolerance for bad housing. Get the basics right and the batch runs itself.

Module 2 of 6 · Broiler Farming for Profit · quiz at the end

For 200 birds you need 20 m2 at finish - a 4m x 5m house. Overstocking above 12/m2 reduces final weight by 10-20% and increases mortality.

Lesson 2.2 - House design

  1. Open sides. Wire mesh on 2-3 sides. Airflow is critical.
  2. Roof overhang 60 cm minimum. Rain and sun protection.
  3. Mesh 2 cm x 2 cm. Keeps out wild birds and rats.
  4. Concrete or raised floor. Damp floor = disease.
  5. Roll-down curtains. For cold nights and the first 2 weeks of brooding.
  6. External feed access. Do not walk through the flock to feed.

Lesson 2.3 - Litter

Depth 8-10 cm at start. Top up as needed. Best materials: wood shavings, rice hulls, chopped straw. Do not use wet, mouldy, or chemically treated material. Remove caked litter around drinkers daily.

Lesson 2.4 - Feeders and drinkers

For 200 birds at finish: 8 tube feeders + 5 bell drinkers. Raise feeders and drinkers as birds grow - lip level with bird's back.

The ventilation rule: Ammonia smell at bird height = more airflow needed. Open more of the house. Poor ventilation costs more weight than any feed issue.
