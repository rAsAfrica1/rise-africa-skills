Kids Drones — Junior

Module 4 of 8: Safety First — Nobody Gets Hurt

No device needed

🧠 Brain Opener — 60 seconds

Before anything else

A drone propeller spins at 10,000 rotations per minute. That is 167 times per second. A plastic blade spinning that fast can cut through skin like a knife through paper. What is the first rule of drones?

📚 Lessons

1. The five rules that never change

(1) Never fly over people. (2) Never fly near an airport. (3) Never fly out of sight. (4) Never fly with damaged blades or a swollen battery. (5) Never fly without an adult who is watching. These five rules apply to every drone, everywhere, forever. No exceptions.

2. The 3-part drill

Round 1: before every flight, touch each propeller gently and spin it. Listen for strange sounds. Look for cracks. Round 2: check the battery — is it puffy? Warm? Smells sweet? If yes to any, do not use it. Round 3: point to every person within 30 metres and count them out loud. If anyone is too close, wait or move.

3. Where the idea breaks

The biggest danger is not the drone — it is the pilot who thinks it is fine. Every serious drone accident involves someone who skipped a check "just this once." The check takes 30 seconds. The accident takes a lifetime.

🤖 Real-Time AI-Era Skill

Ask Claude: "List 10 things that can go wrong with a toy drone and how to prevent each one." Print or write the list. Tape it to your drone box. Every flight, check the list.

🎮 Game

Do it now

Hazard hunt. Walk around the place you would fly. Find five hazards: a dog that might chase, a tree branch, a power line, a person walking, a puddle. Draw the space on paper and mark each hazard with a red X. Only fly when you can name every X.
