Daily Workflow: Phase 3 (3-4 hours/day)

  1. Morning (30 min): Receive & organize orders from 15-20 farmers
  2. Raw Material Prep (40 min): Weigh ingredients for 200 kg batches
  3. Motorized Grinding (40 min): Process coarse materials (100+ kg/hr)
  4. Horizontal Mixing (50 min): Mix 200 kg batches to consistency
  5. Bagging (40 min): Package into 20 kg bags using bagging machine
  6. Storage & Delivery (30 min): Prepare for delivery to farms

Daily Production: 200-300 kg × $0.60 profit = $120-180/day

Business Requirements: Phase 3

  • Dedicated workspace (20 sq meters minimum)
  • 3-phase electrical connection or generator ($500+)
  • Raw material storage facility
  • Finished product storage (temperature, humidity controlled)
  • Vehicle for delivery (own or rent)
