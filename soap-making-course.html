What this course covers

MODULE 1  Foundation: The Chemistry & The Business5 videos

What you will be able to do:

  • Explain saponification: the reaction between sodium hydroxide and oil that produces soap
  • Understand why every oil needs a different amount of lye (its SAP value) and why guessing burns money and skin
  • Calculate startup costs: caustic soda, oils, moulds, scale, safety gear
  • Understand the real profit chain: raw ingredients often cost a fraction of the selling price of a finished bar, but curing time ties up your capital for weeks
  • Choose a starting scale that matches your capital, not your ambition
Included in this module: 5 video lessons, a saponification (SAP) reference table for common oils, a 6-step implementation guide, a scored knowledge check, and an 8-phase capstone project.

MODULE 2  Safety First: Handling Caustic Soda5 videos

What you will be able to do:

  • Handle sodium hydroxide without injury: gloves, goggles, ventilation, and the rule of always adding soda to water, never water to soda
  • Recognise and respond to a caustic burn immediately — the difference between a minor incident and a serious injury is often the first thirty seconds
  • Store caustic soda safely, away from children, food, and metal containers that corrode
  • Set up a dedicated soap-making workspace, separate from anywhere food is prepared
  • Know when raw, uncured soap is still dangerous to handle bare-handed
Included in this module: 5 video lessons, a printable safety checklist for your workspace, a 6-step implementation guide, a scored knowledge check, and an 8-phase capstone project.
📖 Open Module 2
MODULE 3  Your First Batch: Cold Process Method5 videos

What you will be able to do:

  • Measure oils and lye accurately by weight, not volume — the single most common beginner mistake
  • Mix a lye solution safely and let it cool to working temperature
  • Combine oils and lye solution to reach "trace" — the point where the mixture will hold its shape
  • Pour into moulds and understand the 24-48 hour cold-process set time
  • Use a superfat of 3-8% so your soap is gentle rather than harsh — too little lye discount leaves excess caustic, too much leaves the bar greasy
Included in this module: 5 video lessons, a working recipe with exact gram measurements, a 6-step implementation guide, a scored knowledge check, and an 8-phase capstone project.

MODULE 4  Curing, Scenting & Quality Control5 videos

What you will be able to do:

  • Test pH before selling: safe soap sits between roughly 7 and 10, never higher
  • Add natural colourants and scents without ruining the batch chemistry
  • Spot and diagnose common faults: soda ash, cracking, soft bars, oil separation
  • Build a simple quality log so every batch is consistent enough to sell
Included in this module: 5 video lessons, a fault-diagnosis chart, a 6-step implementation guide, a scored knowledge check, and an 8-phase capstone project.

Quick reference: SAP values for common oils

SAP value times grams of oil = grams of sodium hydroxide needed for that oil. Always verify with a lye calculator before mixing.
