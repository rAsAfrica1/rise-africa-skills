The Business Seed — Sheep Farming

This is not a farming course. It is a small business blueprint. Read this before you plant, buy or build anything.

Read time: 15 minutes Then: Module 1

What you will find here

1. The Business Idea 2. Three Capital Tiers 3. Value-Addition Ladder 4. Buyers You Will Approach 5. Your First-Year One-Page Plan

1. The Business Idea

You are not training to become a hobbyist. You are training to run one of these real businesses around sheep meat and wool:

  • Meat lamb production
  • Wool production (where climate suits)
  • Breeding stock sales
  • Manure and compost

Every module teaches the method. This page teaches the business around it. Read them together.

2. Three Capital Tiers — Pick Your Entry Point

Tier 1 — Starter

$800–3,000

Small flock — 10–20 sheep

Tier 2 — Commercial

$6,000–25,000

Commercial flock — 50–100 sheep

Tier 3 — Scale

$40,000+

Integrated sheep enterprise with processing

Rule: choose the tier that matches your available capital, land and market access. Do not start at Tier 3 because you want to be big. Start at Tier 1 and prove the model.

3. Value-Addition Ladder

The same crop or product sells at very different prices depending on how far up the ladder you take it. Read from top (raw) to bottom (highest value):

Product
Live sheep
Carcass meat
Wool
Breeding stock
Manure

Do not jump. Master one rung, then climb to the next. Each rung pays for the equipment of the next.

4. Buyers You Will Approach Before You Start

Name three real buyers. Call them this week. If you cannot find three, do not start.

  • Butcheries
  • Live animal markets
  • Wool buyers
  • Other farmers for breeding
Rule: a business without a named buyer is a hobby. Before you spend money, know the name and phone number of the person who will buy your output.

5. Your First-Year One-Page Plan

  1. Business type: which of the four above are you running?
  2. Capital tier: Tier 1, 2 or 3? How much are you investing?
  3. Land/location: where will this operate?
  4. Products: what exactly will you sell?
  5. Target buyers: three real names you will approach.
  6. Expected revenue: realistic annual gross.
  7. Total cost: every line from setup to delivery.
  8. Net margin: revenue minus cost. Is it worth your time?
  9. Value step: what single next-step product will you add in 12 months?
  10. Scaling trigger: what condition must be met before you expand?
