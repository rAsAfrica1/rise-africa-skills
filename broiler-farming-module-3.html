3. Brooding Day-Old Chicks

90% of broiler batch failures happen in the first 14 days. Almost all of them are preventable.

Module 3 of 6 · Broiler Farming for Profit · quiz at the end

Lesson 3.1 - Before chicks arrive

  1. Pre-heat the house 24 hours before. Floor at 2 cm = 33-35 C.
  2. Spread 8 cm of fresh litter. Clean, dry, not dusty.
  3. Fill drinkers with clean water plus vitamins.
  4. Fill feeders with chick starter crumble. Also scatter feed on paper for the first 3 days.
  5. Ring the brooding area. Cardboard or mesh to keep chicks within 2 m of heat for the first 5 days.

Lesson 3.2 - Temperature schedule

AgeFloor temp (2 cm)Signs of correct
Day 1-333-35 CEvenly spread, active
Week 132-34 CSame

Week 4+20-25 CFully feathered

Read the chicks, not the thermometer:

  • Huddled under the lamp, loud cheeping: too cold. Add heat.
  • Spread to walls, panting: too hot. Reduce heat, increase airflow.
  • Evenly spread, quiet, active: correct.

Lesson 3.3 - Feeding the brooding chick

Broilers must have feed available at all times. A bird that goes 4 hours without feed will not recover its growth curve.

Lesson 3.5 - Mortality

SymptomCauseFix
Died within 24 hoursChilled / weak chicksPre-heat, source from reputable hatchery
Piling in cornersCold or draughtsMore heat, block draughts
Panting, spread to edgesToo hotReduce heat, more airflow
Pasted ventOverheating, low humidityIncrease humidity, gently clean
Sudden deaths week 2-3Coccidiosis or GumboroVaccinate, medicate if diagnosed
The 3-day rule: If 95%+ of chicks are alive at day 3, brooding was done right.
