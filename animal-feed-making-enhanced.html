SIMPLEST STARTER FORMULA - Chicken Feed (By Hand)

No weighing needed - use cups/containers (1 cup = ~200g)

IngredientAmount (Cups)What to Mix WithWhere to Get
Crushed Maize/Corn5 cupsPound with rock if neededLocal mill or grind by hand
Bran (wheat/maize)3 cupsMix in same bucketLocal mill waste (free!)
Protein (fish/groundnut cake)1 cupBreak into pieces firstLocal market, affordable
Limestone (eggshells crushed)0.5 cupPound until powderCollect, dry, crush
Salt1 pinchAdd lastKitchen salt - any type
✅ MIXING METHOD - NO EQUIPMENT:
  • Get a clean bucket (plastic 20L ideal)
  • Pour corn in bucket
  • Pour bran on top
  • Pour protein on top
  • Use shovel/stick: lift from bottom, throw back in (10-15 times)
  • Add limestone, mix again (5 times)
  • Add salt, final mix (3 times)
  • Done! Total time: 10-15 minutes

⚠️ CRITICAL PRECAUTIONS - PHASE 1

Safety First (Hands-On Method):
  • Never use moldy grains (can kill animals)
  • Wash hands after handling ingredients
  • Don't mix with pesticides or chemicals nearby
  • Keep feed dry (cover bucket with cloth)
  • Use only food-grade containers (no poison containers)
  • Test feed: Let chickens eat small amount first, watch for 2 hours

⚠️ DAIRY CATTLE PRECAUTIONS:
  • Fish meal makes milk taste fishy - use ONLY 0.5 cup max per batch

  • Never use moldy grain - reduces milk production immediately

🐛 ALTERNATIVE PROTEINS (When Fish/Soybean Unavailable)

INSECT PROTEIN - LOCAL PRODUCTION:
  • Crickets: Catch locally, dry, grind = 55-70% protein
  • Grasshoppers: Free seasonal harvest = 60% protein
  • Black soldier fly larvae: Can breed at home = 42-50% protein
  • Cost to produce: $0.10-0.20/kg (vs $0.40/kg for soybean)
  • Use: 10-20% of total feed
PLANT-BASED ALTERNATIVES:
  • Moringa leaves (dried): 25-30% protein = 10-15% of feed
  • Groundnut cake: 45-50% protein = 12-18% of feed
  • Pea meal: 22-25% protein = 10-15% of feed
  • Cassava leaves (dried): 20-25% protein = 5-10% of feed
  • Brewers' dried grains: 20-25% protein = 15-20% of feed

📋 SAFETY & QUALITY CHECKLIST

Before EVERY batch (hands-on or commercial):
  • ☑ Inspect corn - NO mold, no smell
  • ☑ Check bran - dry, not humid
  • ☑ Verify protein - fresh, right color
  • ☑ Crush eggshells well - no sharp pieces
  • ☑ Mix thoroughly (10-15 minutes by hand, 5 min with mixer)
  • ☑ Label with: DATE MADE, ingredients, animal type
  • ☑ Store dry (cover bucket, not in moisture)
  • ☑ Separate from chemicals/pesticides
  • ☑ Wash hands after handling
  • ☑ Test small amount with animals first (watch 2 hours)
