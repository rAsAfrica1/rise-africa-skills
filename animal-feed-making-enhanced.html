⚠️ CRITICAL PRECAUTIONS:
  • Never use moldy grains - causes aflatoxins (deadly toxin)
  • Calcium-phosphorus imbalance causes lameness and paralysis
  • Excess salt (>1%) causes kidney damage
  • Store feed in dry, cool place away from moisture and pests
  • Always wear mask when grinding (silica dust hazard)
  • Keep ingredients away from chemicals and pesticides
  • Wash hands after handling feed ingredients

IF SOYBEAN MEAL NOT AVAILABLE:
  • Increase fish meal to 12% (watch smell in milk)
  • Add pea meal 10% + sunflower cake 10%
  • Use brewers' dried grains 15% (lower quality)
  • Mix: cottonseed meal 10% + groundnut cake 10%
⚠️ DAIRY CATTLE SPECIFIC PRECAUTIONS:
  • Avoid fish meal >5% (taints milk flavor)
  • Never use urea >1% (causes nitrate poisoning)
  • Calcium:Phosphorus MUST be 1.5:1 to 2:1 (poor ratio = milk fever)
  • Check vitamin A levels (deficiency = blind calves)
  • Mold = aflatoxins = illness & reduced milk production

Safety & Quality Checklist

Before Every Feed Production Batch:
  • ☑ Inspect all grains - NO mold, no discoloration, no strange smell
  • ☑ Check moisture - dry to touch, <12% moisture ideal
  • ☑ Verify protein source - not rancid, proper color
  • ☑ Weigh all ingredients precisely (errors = malnutrition)
  • ☑ Mix thoroughly (minimum 10 minutes for uniform mix)
  • ☑ Label bags with: date made, expiry date (28 days max), ingredients
  • ☑ Store in cool, dry, rodent-proof container
  • ☑ Never re-use contaminated bags/containers
  • ☑ Wash hands after handling ingredients
  • ☑ Keep separate from chemicals, pesticides, medications

Capstone Project: Build Your Animal Feed Business

Phase 1: Research (Week 1-2)

Identify: Local animal farmers, current feed prices, available raw materials, competition. Calculate demand for 5 most common animals in your area.

Phase 2: Formulation (Week 2-3)

Choose 3 feed types to start (e.g., broiler starter, layer, pig finisher). Source ingredients locally. Calculate costs. Set retail prices (2-3x markup).

Phase 3: Testing (Week 3-4)

Make small batches. Test palatability (do animals eat enthusiastically?). Monitor for 2 weeks. Track costs vs. actual expenses.

Phase 4: Production Setup (Week 4-8)

Get basic equipment (grinder, mixer, scale). Set up storage. Produce first commercial batch (100 kg minimum). Document process.

Phase 5: Marketing (Week 6-12)

Visit 10 local farmers. Offer free samples. Get testimonials. Set up delivery schedule. Calculate profit per kg.

Phase 6: Scaling (Month 4-6)

Based on demand, increase production. Add 2-3 more feed types. Track quality metrics. Plan equipment upgrades.

Phase 7: Quality Control (Ongoing)

Test each batch for: consistency, smell (no mold), moisture, palatability. Get customer feedback. Adjust formulas as needed.

Phase 8: Financial Review (Month 6)

Calculate: Total revenue, Total costs, Net profit per kg, Profit margin %, Monthly profit. Plan reinvestment or expansion.
