🛠️ Implementation Steps

  1. Assess Health Needs: Survey community for common diseases
  2. Train Health Workers: Conduct basic medical training
  3. Establish Clinic: Set up primary care facility
  4. Stock Medicines: Obtain essential medications
  5. Create Health Records: Document patient information
  6. Launch Education Program: Teach preventive health
