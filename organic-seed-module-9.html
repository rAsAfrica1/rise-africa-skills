🧠 Brain Opener

Weeds take 30 to 40 percent of your crop if you do not control them. That is a third of your revenue. In organic farming, there are no herbicides - only your hands, your planning, and your timing.

  • Why are weeds worse in organic systems?
  • When is the critical weeding period?
  • What tools do you need for weeding?
🎁 Reveal a strong answer

Weeds are worse in organic because you cannot spray them away. The critical weeding period is the first 4 to 6 weeks after planting - this is when the crop is small and loses the most from competition. You need a hoe, hands, and possibly a machete for stubborn weeds.

📖 Kudzai Learns the Cost of Delay

Kudzai was twenty-two and had planted 500 square metres of organic kale seed. He was proud of his plot. He had watered, composted, and planted carefully.

Then his cousin came to visit for two weeks. Kudzai forgot his plot. When he returned, the weeds were taller than his young kale plants.

He pulled weeds for three days. He recovered the plot. But the damage was done.

The kale plants were stunted. They had been in full shade for two weeks. Their stems were thin and weak.

At harvest, Kudzai collected 15 kilograms of kale seed from 500 square metres. His neighbour, who planted the same variety the same week, collected 38 kilograms from the same size plot.

The only difference was the weeding.

"I did not weed for two weeks," Kudzai said.

"Two weeks in the first month is everything," his neighbour said. "The first month decides the season."

Kudzai wrote down the number 15 and the number 38 in his notebook. He underlined both.

The next season, he weeded every single week for the first month. He hired help when he could not do it himself. He collected 42 kilograms.

The cost of two weeks of weeds was 23 kilograms of seed. At 25 dollars per kilogram, that was 575 dollars lost.

Kudzai never skipped a weeding again.

💡 The Big Idea

Weeds cost more than they look

Weeds do not just take space. They take water, light, and nutrients. In the first month of a crop, this competition is brutal. A crop that loses the first month never fully recovers. Weed on time and you win. Weed late and you can lose a third of your revenue.

📚 Three Deep Lessons

1

The critical weeding period

For most vegetable seed crops, the first 4 weeks after planting are the critical period. During this time, the crop is small and cannot compete. Weeds that establish now will stay.

The rule is simple: weed at weeks one, two, three, and four. After that, the crop is tall enough to shade out most weeds.

After week four, weed once every two weeks until flowering. If weeds go to seed, they add to the problem next season. Never let weeds flower.

2

Weeding tools and technique

Hand hoe. The main tool. A well-sharpened hoe removes weeds in seconds. Costs 5 to 10 dollars. Buy two.

Hands. For weeds growing close to your crop plants. Never use a hoe near the crop stem - you will cut the crop.

Machete or slasher. For tall weeds between rows or on paths.

Mulch. Dry grass or leaves spread on the soil surface. Suppresses weeds and keeps moisture. Reduces weeding time by 40 percent.

Cover crops. Plant a ground-covering species between rows. It occupies the space weeds would take.

3

The economics of weeding

Weeding takes 30 to 40 percent of all field labour. On a half-hectare plot, you may need 20 to 30 person-days per season just for weeding.

If you hire labour at 5 dollars per day, that is 100 to 150 dollars per half hectare. Add this to your costs.

Mulching reduces weeding by 40 percent. That saves 40 to 60 dollars per half hectare.

Delay weeding past week two adds another 20 to 30 percent to your labour cost - because larger weeds are harder to remove.

🛠 Hands-On - Design a Weeding Calendar

You need

  • A calendar or notebook
  • A pen
  • Your planting date
  1. Write down your planting date.
  2. Mark every Sunday for the next 12 weeks.
  3. Plan to weed on weeks 1, 2, 3, 4.
  4. Then mark weeks 6, 8, 10, 12.
  5. For each weeding day, write down how many hours you think it will take.
  6. Add up the total hours for the season.
  7. Decide how many people you will need on the busiest days.
  8. Write it in your notebook and pin it on the wall.
Why it works: Weeding planned on paper happens on time. Weeding not planned slips.

🎮 Game - Weed Race

4 to 12 players - 20 min

The teacher spreads 200 small pieces of paper (weeds) among a bed of 20 larger pieces (crops). Players have 2 minutes to remove weeds without removing crops. Score: weeds removed minus crops accidentally removed. Highest score wins. Discuss: which crops were hardest to protect?

🌍 Real Stories from Africa

Zambia agroecology mulch

Zambian agroecology farmers use 5 to 8 tonnes of mulch per hectare. Their weeding labour drops by 40 percent. Their yield increases by 15 percent because the mulch also conserves water and feeds the soil. Mulching pays for itself in one season.

Kenya kale seed weeding

Kenyan organic kale seed producers weed at weeks 1, 2, 3, and 4. Their weed control costs are 40 percent of total labour. Their yields are 30 percent higher than producers who weed irregularly. The discipline is the difference.
