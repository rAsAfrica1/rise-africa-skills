Beehive - Wood and Brick

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the craft of building durable beehives by combining woodworking and brick masonry techniques. This hands-on course takes you from material selection and tool safety to constructing complete hives with brood chambers, supers, frames, and weatherproof covers. Designed for aspiring beekeepers, carpenters, and agripreneurs across Africa.
