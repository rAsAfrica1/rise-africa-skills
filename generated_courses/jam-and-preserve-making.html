Jam and Preserve Making

FOOD & PRESERVATION · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the craft of turning fresh fruit into shelf-stable jams, jellies, marmalades, curds, and chutneys. This hands-on course takes you from fruit selection and pectin science to water bath canning, gifting, and selling at local markets. Built for home cooks and aspiring food entrepreneurs across Africa who want to create value-added products from abundant seasonal harvests.
