Fruit Juice and Concentrate Production

AGRO-PROCESSING · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the science and business of turning African fruit harvests into safe, shelf-stable juices and concentrates for local and export markets. From fruit reception through extraction, clarification, concentration, preservation, packaging, and plant economics, this programme builds the practical skills employers and founders need.
