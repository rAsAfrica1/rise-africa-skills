Fortified Corn-Soy Porridge

Nutrition & Food Processing · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete production of fortified corn-soy porridge, from raw material sourcing and fortification technology to quality assurance, packaging, and community distribution. This hands-on programme equips you to produce safe, nutritious, and market-ready porridge at home, community, or industrial scale.
