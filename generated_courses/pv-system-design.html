PV System Design

RENEWABLE ENERGY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the design of grid-tied, off-grid, and hybrid solar photovoltaic systems from site assessment to commissioning. This hands-on programme covers solar resource analysis, module and inverter selection, battery storage, electrical and mechanical design, codes and standards, and project economics. Built for technicians, engineers, and entrepreneurs ready to deliver reliable solar power across Africa.
