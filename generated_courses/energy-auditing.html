Energy Auditing

ENERGY & SUSTAINABILITY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to assess how buildings, industry, and facilities use energy, and where savings can be found. This practical course takes you from utility bills and meters to full audit reports, covering HVAC, lighting, motors, steam, and compressed air, with economic analysis and measurement & verification. Designed for technicians, facility managers, and engineers across Africa who want to cut energy costs and build a career in energy auditing.
