Basic Carpentry for Furniture

CONSTRUCTION · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to build durable, beautiful furniture from raw timber using hand tools and basic power tools. This hands-on course takes you from workshop safety and wood selection through joinery, assembly, and finishing, culminating in a complete furniture piece you design and build yourself.
