Transport and Logistics Business

BUSINESS · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the movement of goods across road, rail, air, and sea while building the commercial, operational, and financial skills to run a profitable logistics enterprise. This course takes you from foundational transport systems through fleet, warehouse, and supply chain management to the technologies and strategies shaping Africa's fast-growing logistics sector.
