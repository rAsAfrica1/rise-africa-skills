Food Preservation Smoking

FOOD PRESERVATION · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the ancient craft of smoking food as a practical preservation method, from wood selection and smoke chemistry to hot and cold smoking techniques. This comprehensive course equips you with the skills to preserve meats, fish, vegetables, and more while developing rich, complex flavors. Whether for home use or a small business, you will learn safe, effective smoking practices rooted in both tradition and science.
