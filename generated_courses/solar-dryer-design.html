Solar Dryer Design

Renewable Energy · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the design, construction, and deployment of solar dryers for agricultural and food processing applications. This hands-on course takes you from solar radiation fundamentals through psychrometrics, heat transfer, and collector design to complete dryer fabrication, testing, and business implementation. Built for engineers, technicians, and entrepreneurs across Africa looking to reduce post-harvest losses and build climate-smart food systems.
