Convert a Car Engine to an Irrigation Water Pump

AGRICULTURAL ENGINEERING · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to convert a salvaged car engine into a reliable, fuel-powered irrigation water pump for smallholder and commercial farming. This hands-on course covers engine selection, pump matching, mechanical coupling, cooling, fuel systems, and full testing so you can build, run, and maintain your own irrigation power unit.
