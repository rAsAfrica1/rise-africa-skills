Briquette Making

RENEWABLE ENERGY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to turn agricultural and organic waste into clean-burning, affordable briquettes for cooking and heating. This hands-on course covers everything from feedstock selection and carbonization to pressing, drying, quality control, and building a profitable briquette enterprise.
