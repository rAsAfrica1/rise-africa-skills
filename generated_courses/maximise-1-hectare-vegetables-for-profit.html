Maximise 1 Hectare - Vegetables for Profit

Agriculture · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Turn a single hectare into a high-yield, profitable vegetable enterprise. This practical course covers everything from soil preparation and intensive planting to irrigation, pest control, post-harvest handling, marketing, and farm finance. Designed for African smallholder farmers and agripreneurs ready to grow more, waste less, and sell smarter.
