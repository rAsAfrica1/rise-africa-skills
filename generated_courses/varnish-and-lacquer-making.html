Varnish and Lacquer Making

Construction & Finishing · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the craft of formulating varnishes and lacquers from natural and synthetic resins, oils, and solvents. This hands-on course takes you from raw material selection to cooking, testing, and application, with a focus on building a viable finishing products business in African markets.
