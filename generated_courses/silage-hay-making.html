Silage & Hay Making

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the science and practice of preserving forage as silage and hay for profitable livestock production. Learn crop selection, harvesting, fermentation, storage, quality testing, and feeding management tailored to African farm conditions. Build the skills to cut feed costs, bridge dry-season gaps, and run a reliable on-farm forage enterprise.
