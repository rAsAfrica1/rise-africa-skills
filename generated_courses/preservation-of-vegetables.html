Preservation of Vegetables

FOOD & AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to preserve vegetables safely and profitably using drying, canning, freezing, fermenting, pickling, and cold storage. This hands-on course covers food safety, quality control, and small-scale business skills for turning garden harvests into year-round value-added products.
