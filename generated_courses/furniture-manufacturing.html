Furniture Manufacturing

MANUFACTURING · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete furniture manufacturing process from raw materials to finished products. This hands-on program covers woodworking, machining, joinery, finishing, upholstery, and modern production techniques. You will also learn business and entrepreneurship skills to launch or grow a furniture manufacturing enterprise.
