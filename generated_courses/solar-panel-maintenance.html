Solar Panel Maintenance

RENEWABLE ENERGY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the inspection, cleaning, testing, and repair of solar photovoltaic systems in real-world African conditions. This hands-on program takes you from basic electrical concepts to advanced diagnostics, drone inspections, and building your own maintenance business. Graduates are equipped to keep residential, commercial, and off-grid solar installations performing at peak efficiency.
