Food Preservation Canning

FOOD PRESERVATION · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete art and science of home and cottage-scale canning, from water bath basics to pressure canning meats, vegetables, and full meals. Built for African kitchens and small food businesses, this course turns seasonal harvests into safe, shelf-stable products that reduce waste and create income. Learn food safety, equipment care, and recipe development step by step.
