Pizza Oven Construction - Wood Fired 12 Pizza

CONSTRUCTION · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete build of a wood-fired pizza oven optimized for 12-inch pizzas, from foundation and dome construction to insulation, chimney systems, and high-heat baking techniques. This hands-on course equips you with the practical skills to construct, fire, and maintain a professional-grade oven for personal or commercial use.
