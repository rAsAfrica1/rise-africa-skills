Floor Layer

CONSTRUCTION · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete trade of floor laying, from subfloor preparation and tile setting to hardwood, carpet, and resilient flooring installation. This hands-on program combines safety, tool mastery, and business skills to prepare you for employment or self-employment in Africa's growing construction industry.
