Packaging Materials

Manufacturing · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the materials, processes, and design principles behind modern packaging. This course takes you from paper, glass, metal, and plastic fundamentals through converting, labeling, closures, machinery, testing, and sustainable innovation. Built for African manufacturers, entrepreneurs, and packaging professionals ready to compete in local and export markets.
