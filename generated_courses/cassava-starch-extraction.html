Cassava Starch Extraction

AGRO-PROCESSING · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete process of extracting high-quality starch from cassava roots, from harvesting and grating to drying, packaging, and quality control. This practical course equips you with the skills to start or scale a profitable cassava starch enterprise, with a focus on small-scale, low-cost African production systems.
