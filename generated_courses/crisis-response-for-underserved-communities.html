Crisis Response for Underserved Communities

Community Health & Social Impact · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Equip yourself to deliver culturally responsive, trauma-informed crisis response in marginalized and underserved African communities. This practical program blends rapid needs assessment, de-escalation, mental health first aid, resource mobilization, and community-led planning into a complete field-ready toolkit.
