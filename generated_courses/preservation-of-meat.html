Preservation of Meat

FOOD PROCESSING & PRESERVATION · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the science and craft of meat preservation, from traditional drying, curing, and smoking to modern freezing, canning, and vacuum packaging. This hands-on course equips you with practical skills to extend shelf life, ensure food safety, and build a profitable meat preservation business in any African market.
