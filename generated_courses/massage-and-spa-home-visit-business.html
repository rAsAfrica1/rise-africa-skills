Health and Safety Protocols for Home Visits

Health and safety are non-negotiable in mobile massage. Start with your own hygiene: shower daily, trim your nails short, tie back long hair, and wear clean clothes. Wash your hands thoroughly before and after every client, using soap and water or hand sanitizer with at least 60% alcohol. If you have a cold, cough, or fever, cancel appointmentsâyou don't want to spread illness. For each client, ask about health conditions: pregnancy, high blood pressure, skin infections, recent surgeries, or allergies. Keep a client intake form (paper or digital) and have them sign it. Avoid massaging over open wounds, rashes, or varicose veins. For pregnant clients, only do prenatal massage if you are trained; otherwise, refer them to a specialist. Use fresh sheets and towels for every client. Wash them in hot water with detergent and dry them in sunlight if possible. Disinfect your table and any tools (stones, cups) with a hospital-grade disinfectant after each use. For oils, pour from a bottle into your handânever dip your fingers into the bottle to avoid contamination. If you use hot stones, check the temperature on your own wrist before placing on the client. Keep a first-aid kit handy with bandages, antiseptic cream, and disposable gloves. In case of an emergency, know the nearest hospital and have the client's emergency contact. Also, protect yourself: never go to an isolated location alone, especially at night. Share your schedule with a trusted friend. If a client makes you uncomfortable, end the session politely and leave. Finally, stay updated on local health regulations. In some African countries, you may need a yellow fever vaccination certificate if you travel between regions. Always prioritize safety over a sale..Trim()

Module 2 · Business Planning and Strategy

Defining Your Niche and Target Market

To succeed in mobile massage, you must know exactly who you serve. A niche is a specific group of clients with similar needs. Instead of offering every massage type to everyone, focus on one or two niches. For example, in Lagos, you could target busy female professionals aged 28-45 who work in banks on Victoria Island. They have disposable income, high stress, and little time to visit a spa. Another niche: expatriate families in Nairobi's Kilimani area who want monthly relaxation massages at home. Or postpartum mothers in Accra who need gentle recovery massage. Your target market is the people within that niche. To find them, use simple tools: Facebook groups (e.g., 'Lagos Mums'), WhatsApp community groups, and local business associations. Ask potential clients: What service do you need? When? How much would you pay? For instance, a survey of 20 women in your church might reveal that 15 would pay 8,000 Naira for a 60-minute home massage on weekends. That's your market. Write down your niche in one sentence: 'I provide prenatal massage to pregnant women in Pretoria East who want relief from back pain.' This clarity helps you choose prices, marketing messages, and service packages. Without a niche, you compete with every spa and mobile therapist. With a niche, you become the go-to expert for that group, allowing you to charge more and get referrals. Spend one week talking to at least 10 potential clients. Note their age, income level, location, and pain points. That data becomes the foundation of your business plan..Trim()

Pricing Strategies for Home Visits

Pricing your mobile massage correctly is critical. You must cover transport, time, and profit. Start by calculating your costs per session. Example: In Dar es Salaam, a 60-minute massage at a client's home. Transport: 5,000 TZS (round trip by bajaji). Oils and linens: 1,000 TZS. Phone data and booking: 500 TZS. Your time: 1.5 hours (including travel). If you want to earn 15,000 TZS per hour, that's 22,500 TZS for time. Total cost = 29,000 TZS. Add 30% profit = 8,700 TZS. So minimum price = 37,700 TZS, round to 40,000 TZS. Now check competitors: a spa charges 50,000 TZS for the same massage, but you offer convenience. You can charge 45,000 TZS and still be cheaper. For home visits, many therapists add a call-out fee of 5,000-10,000 TZS. Instead, build it into the price to avoid surprising clients. Offer packages: 4 massages for 160,000 TZS (save 20,000 TZS). This encourages loyalty. For peak times (evenings, weekends), charge 10-20% more. For off-peak (weekday mornings), offer a 10% discount. In Nairobi, a mobile massage might cost KES 3,500, while a spa charges KES 4,500. Test your price with 5 clients: if no one objects, you're too cheap. If everyone objects, lower slightly. Use mobile money to accept payments: M-Pesa, Airtel Money, etc. Always take a 50% deposit for new clients to reduce no-shows. Review your prices every 3 months. Setting Business Goals and Objectives

Goals give your mobile massage business direction. A goal is a broad target, like 'become the top home massage service in my area.' An objective is specific and measurable. Use the SMART method: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound. For example: 'Get 10 regular clients within 3 months.' That's specific (10 clients), measurable (count them), achievable (if you work hard), relevant (grows income), and time-bound (3 months). Write 3-5 goals for your first year. Example: Goal 1: Achieve 20 sessions per month by month 6. Objective: Book 5 sessions per week by week 24. Goal 2: Increase average price from 30,000 Naira to 40,000 Naira by month 9. Objective: Add a premium package (90-minute massage with hot stones) and sell it to 5 clients per month. Goal 3: Build a client database of 100 contacts by month 12. Objective: Collect phone numbers and birthdays, and send monthly promotions via WhatsApp. Break each goal into weekly actions. For instance, to get 5 sessions per week, you must contact 10 potential clients per day (via WhatsApp, calls, or referrals). Track your progress in a notebook or a free app like Trello. Every Sunday, review: Did I meet my weekly target? If not, why? Adjust. Celebrate small wins: first paying client, first 5-star review. Share goals with a mentor or accountability partner. In many African cities, business networks like local chambers of commerce or church groups can help. Remember, goals should stretch you but not break you. If you have no car, don't set a goal to cover 50km in a day. Start with your immediate neighborhood. As you grow, set new goals. This habit turns a hustle into a sustainable business..Trim()

Financial Projections and Break-Even Analysis

Financial projections estimate your future income and expenses. Break-even analysis tells you how many massages you must sell to cover costs. Start with your fixed monthly costs: phone data (e.g., 10,000 Naira), transport (if you hire a bike daily, 20,000 Naira), marketing (flyers, 5,000 Naira), and equipment replacement (5,000 Naira). Total fixed = 40,000 Naira. Variable costs per massage: oils, linens washing, and mobile money fees = 2,000 Naira. If you charge 15,000 Naira per massage, your contribution margin per massage = 15,000 - 2,000 = 13,000 Naira. Break-even point = fixed costs / contribution margin = 40,000 / 13,000 = 3.08, so 4 massages per month. That means after 4 massages, you start making profit. But that's just covering costs; you also want a salary. If you want to earn 50,000 Naira per month, you need additional profit: 50,000 / 13,000 = 3.85, so 4 more massages. Total 8 massages per month to earn that salary. Now project 12 months. Month 1: 5 massages = income 75,000 Naira, costs 40,000 + (5x2,000)=50,000, profit 25,000 Naira. Month 2: 8 massages = income 120,000, costs 56,000, profit 64,000. Month 3: 12 massages = income 180,000, costs 64,000, profit 116,000. Reinvest some profit: buy a better table, pay for a small Instagram ad. Use a simple spreadsheet (Google Sheets or Microsoft Excel) to track actual vs projected. In Kenya, many therapists use M-Pesa statements to track income. At the end of each month, compare. If you're below break-even, find ways to reduce costs (e.g., combine trips) or increase prices. If you're above, consider hiring a helper. This analysis removes guesswork and helps you make decisions like whether to buy a motorbike for transport. Always keep a cash reserve for slow months (e.g., during heavy rains)..Trim()
