Guinea Fowl & Quail Farming

Livestock & Poultry · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the rearing of guinea fowl and quail from day-old keets and chicks to profitable processing and sales. This practical course covers housing, feeding, breeding, incubation, health, and business planning for small-scale and commercial African poultry enterprises.
