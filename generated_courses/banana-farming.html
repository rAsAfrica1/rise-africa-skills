Banana Farming

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the full banana value chain, from site selection and planting material through pest management, harvesting, processing, and marketing. This practical course equips African farmers and agripreneurs with the skills to grow, add value to, and profit from banana production.
