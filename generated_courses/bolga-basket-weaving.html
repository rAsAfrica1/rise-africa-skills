Bolga Basket Weaving

CRAFTS · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the traditional art of Bolga basket weaving, from sourcing and preparing Veta Vera grass to advanced patterns, finishing techniques, and building a sustainable craft business. This comprehensive course takes you from complete beginner to skilled artisan, with hands-on projects and real-world business guidance.
