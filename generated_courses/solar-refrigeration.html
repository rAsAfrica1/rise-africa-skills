Solar Refrigeration

Renewable Energy · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the design, installation, and maintenance of solar-powered refrigeration systems for off-grid and hybrid applications. Learn thermal, absorption, adsorption, desiccant, and PV-driven cooling technologies suited to African climates. Build job-ready skills for cold chain, vaccine storage, agri-processing, and commercial cooling markets.
