Silver Smithing Basics

JEWELLERY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to saw, solder, form, and finish silver into wearable jewellery, starting from a bare workbench and ending with a completed custom piece. This hands-on course covers silver alloys, heat management, wire and chain work, stone setting, and polishing, with practical projects in every module.
