Entrepreneurship · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn how to launch and grow a successful venture in Africa's diverse and fast-changing markets. From validating your idea and registering your company to financing, hiring, marketing, and scaling, this course gives you practical, locally grounded tools for every stage of your entrepreneurial journey.
