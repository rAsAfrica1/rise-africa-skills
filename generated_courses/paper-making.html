Paper Making

CATEGORY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the art and business of handmade paper making, from raw fiber preparation to finished specialty papers. This comprehensive course takes you through every stage of production, including plant fibers, recycled paper, coloring, and finishing techniques. You will also learn how to turn your craft into a sustainable income stream.
