Agricultural Cooperative Formation

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn how to establish and grow a thriving agricultural cooperative from the ground up. This comprehensive course guides you through community needs assessment, legal registration, governance, capitalization, and sustainable growth strategies tailored to African agricultural contexts.
