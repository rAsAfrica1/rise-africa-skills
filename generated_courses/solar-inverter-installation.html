Solar Inverter Installation

RENEWABLE ENERGY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete installation of solar inverters, from site assessment and DC/AC wiring to grid interconnection, commissioning, and troubleshooting. This hands-on programme equips technicians and entrepreneurs across Africa with the practical skills to install, configure, and maintain string, hybrid, and battery-based inverter systems to industry safety standards.
