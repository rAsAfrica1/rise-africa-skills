Well Digger

Water & Sanitation · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to site, dig, construct, and maintain water wells that serve rural and peri-urban communities across Africa. This hands-on program covers manual excavation, drilling methods, casing, pumps, water quality, safety, and the business skills needed to run a well-digging enterprise.
