Duck Farming

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the full cycle of duck farming, from breed selection and housing to health, breeding, egg and meat production, and profitable marketing. This practical course equips African smallholders and agripreneurs with the skills to build sustainable, income-generating duck enterprises.
