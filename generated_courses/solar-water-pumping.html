Solar Water Pumping

RENEWABLE ENERGY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the design, installation, and maintenance of solar-powered water pumping systems for agriculture and rural development. This hands-on course covers solar resource assessment, hydraulic calculations, pump sizing, system installation, and the economics of replacing diesel and grid-powered pumping across Africa.
