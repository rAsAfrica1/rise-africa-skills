Food Preservation (10 Methods)

FOOD & NUTRITION · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master ten proven food preservation methods to reduce waste, extend shelf life, and build food security for your household or business. From canning and freezing to fermenting and root cellaring, this hands-on course equips you with practical, safe techniques for preserving seasonal harvests year-round.
