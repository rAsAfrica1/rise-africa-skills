Calculating profit margins on parts and scrap metal

Profit margin is the difference between what you sell an item for and what it cost you to get it ready for sale. For a part, your cost includes the price you paid for the scrap car, plus labor to remove the part, plus any cleaning or testing. For example, you buy a scrap car for 20,000 KES. If the car has 10 main parts, each part's share of the car price is 2,000 KES. Add labor 200 KES (if worker spent 1 hour on it at 200 KES/hour). Total cost = 2,200 KES. Profit = 8,000 - 2,200 = 5,800 KES. Profit margin = (5,800 / 8,000) x 100 = 72.5%. For scrap metal, say you buy 100 kg of mixed metal for 5,000 KES.
