Honey Processing & Packaging

AGRO-PROCESSING · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete honey value chain from hive to shelf, covering harvesting, extraction, dehydration, filtration, packaging, and quality assurance. This practical course equips African entrepreneurs and agri-processors with the technical skills and regulatory knowledge to produce safe, high-quality honey products for local and export markets.
