Welding for Beginners

Vocational & Trades · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn welding from the ground up with hands-on training in SMAW, GMAW/MIG, and GTAW/TIG processes. This beginner-friendly course covers safety, tools, techniques, blueprint reading, and quality control, culminating in a practical metal structure project.
