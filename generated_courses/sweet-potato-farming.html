Sweet Potato Farming

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete sweet potato value chain from land preparation and vine selection to curing, storage, and profitable marketing. This practical course equips African farmers, agripreneurs, and extension officers with the skills to grow high-yielding, disease-free sweet potatoes for local and export markets.
