Horse Breeding

Master the science and business of equine reproduction, from anatomy and genetics to foaling, neonatal care, and advanced breeding technologies. This comprehensive program equips breeders, farm managers, and veterinary technicians with practical skills to build healthy, profitable horse breeding operations across Africa.
