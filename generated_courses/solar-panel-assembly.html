Solar Panel Assembly

Renewable Energy · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete process of assembling photovoltaic solar panels, from cell sorting and soldering to lamination, framing, and quality control. This hands-on course prepares you for employment or entrepreneurship in Africa's rapidly growing solar energy industry.
