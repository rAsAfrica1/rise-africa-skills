Clay Preparation and Wedging

CERAMICS · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the foundational craft of preparing clay for pottery and ceramics. This comprehensive course covers essential wedging techniques, from spiral and ram's head to ox-head methods, ensuring your clay is perfectly conditioned for any forming technique. Learn to troubleshoot common issues, improve efficiency, and develop a professional routine for consistent, high-quality results.
