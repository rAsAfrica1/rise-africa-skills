Cattle and Steer Feed Formulation

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the science and economics of formulating balanced, least-cost cattle and steer diets for African production systems. Learn nutrient requirements, feed ingredient evaluation, formulation mathematics, and feedlot feeding strategies through practical case studies and software exercises.

Module 1 · Introduction to Cattle Nutrition and Feedstuffs

  • Overview of beef cattle industry and feeding systems
  • Digestive anatomy and physiology of ruminants
  • Nutrient requirements: energy, protein, minerals, vitamins
  • Common feed ingredients: grains, forages, protein meals
  • Feed quality and safety considerations
  • Regulatory and labeling requirements for feed

Module 2 · Nutrient Requirements and Feeding Standards

  • Mineral and vitamin supplementation guidelines
  • Adjusting requirements for environmental factors

Module 3 · Feed Ingredient Evaluation and Analysis

  • Proximate analysis: moisture, ash, protein, fat, fiber

  • Anti-nutritional factors and mycotoxin screening
  • Interpreting feed analysis reports

  • Setting nutrient constraints and ingredient limits
  • Evaluating and adjusting formulated diets

Module 8 · Formulating Diets for Different Production Stages
