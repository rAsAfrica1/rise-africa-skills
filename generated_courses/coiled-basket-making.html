Coiled Basket Making

CRAFTS · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the ancient art of coiled basketry, from selecting natural fibers to creating sculptural forms with fitted lids. This comprehensive course blends traditional African techniques with modern design, giving you the skills to craft beautiful, marketable baskets while preserving cultural heritage.
