Cold Storage

CATEGORY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the design, operation, and maintenance of cold storage facilities essential to Africa's food, pharmaceutical, and agricultural supply chains. This comprehensive course covers refrigeration principles, equipment, control systems, energy efficiency, and regulatory compliance. Gain practical skills to reduce post-harvest losses, ensure product quality, and build sustainable cold chain infrastructure.
