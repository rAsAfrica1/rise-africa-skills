Leather Making and Tanning

AGRICULTURE & MANUFACTURING · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete leather value chain from raw hide selection to finished products, combining traditional African tanning knowledge with modern industrial techniques. This hands-on programme equips you to produce quality leather, meet export standards, and build a sustainable tanning business.
