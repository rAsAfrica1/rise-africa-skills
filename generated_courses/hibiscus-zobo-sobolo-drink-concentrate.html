Quality testing and sensory evaluation

Test sugar level with a refractometer (Brix). Aim for 40-50 Brix for concentrate. If you don't have a refractometer, use a hydrometer. For microbial safety, do a simple test: leave a sample at room temperature for 24 hours. If it smells or looks off, your preservation is inadequate. Increase preservative or sugar.
