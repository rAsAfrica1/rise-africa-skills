Cooking Oil Production

AGRO-PROCESSING · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete cooking oil production chain, from oilseed preparation and mechanical or solvent extraction through refining, bleaching, deodorization, modification, and packaging. This practical course equips entrepreneurs and technicians with the skills to set up, operate, and quality-assure a profitable cooking oil processing business.
