Brick & Paver Making

CONSTRUCTION · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete brick and paver production process, from clay selection and hand molding to machine extrusion, kiln firing, and concrete paver manufacturing. This hands-on course also covers quality control, business planning, and sustainable practices so you can launch or scale a profitable brickmaking enterprise.
