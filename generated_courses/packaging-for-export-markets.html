Packaging for Export Markets

Export & Trade · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the technical, regulatory, and commercial requirements for packaging goods destined for international markets. This course equips African exporters, packaging professionals, and logistics teams with practical skills to design compliant, cost-effective, and protective packaging that survives the global supply chain.
