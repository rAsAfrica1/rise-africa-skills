Coconut Oil Production

AGRICULTURE & PROCESSING · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete coconut oil value chain, from selecting and harvesting coconuts to producing premium virgin and refined oils for local and export markets. This hands-on course equips you with the technical, quality control, and business skills to launch or scale a profitable coconut oil enterprise in Africa.
