Beaded Accessories

CRAFTS · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the art of handmade beaded accessories, from simple stringing and wirework to advanced mixed-media design. Build a complete product line of bracelets, necklaces, earrings, rings, bags and brooches, then learn to package, price and sell your work. Perfect for artisans, side hustlers and small workshop owners ready to turn beadwork into income.
