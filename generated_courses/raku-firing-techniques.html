Raku Firing Techniques

CERAMICS · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the dramatic art of Raku firing, from clay selection and glaze chemistry to reduction chambers, special surface effects, and studio safety. This hands-on course takes you from your first bisque firing to exhibition-ready Raku pieces, blending Japanese tradition with contemporary African studio practice.
