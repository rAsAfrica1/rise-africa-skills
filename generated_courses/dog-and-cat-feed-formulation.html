Dog and Cat Feed Formulation

AGRICULTURE & ANIMAL SCIENCE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the science and business of formulating complete, safe, and palatable diets for dogs and cats. This practical course takes you from essential nutrition and ingredient selection through least-cost formulation, kibble and canned processing, therapeutic diets, and a full capstone formulation project built for the African pet food market.
