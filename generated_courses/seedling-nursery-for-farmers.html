Seedling Nursery for Farmers

Agriculture · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to establish and run a productive seedling nursery, from seed selection and soil mixes to pest control and marketing. This practical course equips farmers and agripreneurs to grow healthy, high-quality seedlings and build a profitable nursery business.
