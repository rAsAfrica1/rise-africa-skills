EV Motorbike Assembly

RISE AFRICA SKILLS · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to assemble electric motorbikes from frame preparation to final handover, covering battery packs, motors, controllers, drivetrains, brakes, wiring, and quality testing. This hands-on course prepares you for work in Africa's fast-growing e-mobility assembly and service sector.
