Biogas Plant Construction

Renewable Energy · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to design, build, and operate biogas plants that turn organic waste into clean cooking fuel, electricity, and fertilizer. This hands-on course covers fixed-dome, floating-drum, and bag digesters, from site selection and masonry to start-up, maintenance, and scaling to community level.
