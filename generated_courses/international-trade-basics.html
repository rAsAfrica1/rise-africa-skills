International Trade Basics

TRADE & EXPORT · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Build a practical, working command of how goods, money, and contracts move across borders. From classical trade theory and WTO rules to Incoterms 2020, letters of credit, customs clearance, and market entry strategy, this course equips African entrepreneurs, exporters, and trade officers with the knowledge to trade confidently and profitably.
