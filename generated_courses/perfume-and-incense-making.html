Perfume and Incense Making

AROMATICS & CRAFT · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the art and science of natural perfume and incense making, from extracting essential oils and working with resins to formulating signature scents and building a sustainable aromatic brand. This hands-on program blends traditional African and global aromatic knowledge with modern formulation techniques for makers ready to create, sell, and scale.
