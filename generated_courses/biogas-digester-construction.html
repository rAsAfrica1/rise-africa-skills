Biogas Digester Construction

RENEWABLE ENERGY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to design, build, and operate biogas digesters that turn animal manure and organic waste into clean cooking gas and nutrient-rich fertilizer. This hands-on course covers everything from site selection and masonry to gas handling, feeding schedules, and community project planning. Ideal for rural technicians, farmers, and entrepreneurs building off-grid energy solutions across Africa.
