Beehive Construction

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete craft of building durable, productive beehives from raw lumber to finished apiary setup. This hands-on course covers bee biology, woodworking safety, hive components, and sustainable construction practices tailored for African beekeepers and entrepreneurs.
