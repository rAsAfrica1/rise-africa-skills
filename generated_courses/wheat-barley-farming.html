Wheat & Barley Farming

CROP PRODUCTION · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete production cycle of wheat and barley, from soil preparation and variety selection to harvesting, storage, and marketing. This practical course equips African farmers and agripreneurs with the agronomic and business skills needed to grow high-yielding, profitable cereal crops.
