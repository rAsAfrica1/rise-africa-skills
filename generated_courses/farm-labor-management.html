Farm Labor Management

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to recruit, train, schedule, pay, and retain farm workers while staying compliant with labor laws and safety standards. This practical course equips farm owners and managers across Africa with tools to build productive, motivated, and legally sound workforces.
