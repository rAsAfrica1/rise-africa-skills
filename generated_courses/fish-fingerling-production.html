Fish Fingerling Production

AQUACULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the science and business of producing healthy, fast-growing fish fingerlings for Africa's expanding aquaculture sector. This comprehensive program covers hatchery design, broodstock management, larval rearing, nutrition, health, and the business skills needed to run a profitable fingerling operation.
