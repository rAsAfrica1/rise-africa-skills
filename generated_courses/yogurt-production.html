Yogurt Production

FOOD PROCESSING · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the science and business of yogurt production, from milk quality and starter cultures to fermentation control, packaging, and plant-scale manufacturing. Built for African dairy entrepreneurs, technicians, and food scientists, this course combines practical processing skills with food safety and commercial know-how.
