Building a Simple Pot Still from a Pressure Cooker and Copper Pipe

To build a pot still, you need a pressure cooker (6-8 litres), copper pipe (1/2 inch diameter, about 1.5 metres), a compression fitting to connect the pipe to the cooker lid, a bucket for cooling water, and a heat source (gas stove or charcoal). Environmental Impact: Managing Wastewater and Spent Grain
