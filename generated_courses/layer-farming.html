Layer Farming

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete layer poultry value chain, from brooding day-old chicks to marketing eggs profitably. This practical, Africa-focused course covers housing, nutrition, health, welfare, and business management for small- to medium-scale egg producers. Learn the skills to run a productive, compliant, and profitable layer farm.
