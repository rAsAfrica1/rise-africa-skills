Terracotta Sculpture

ARTS & CRAFTS · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the ancient art of terracotta sculpture from clay preparation to fired, finished pieces. Learn handbuilding, sculpting, surface design, and kiln work while building a professional portfolio. Designed for African artisans, hobbyists, and entrepreneurs ready to turn clay into a sustainable craft business.
