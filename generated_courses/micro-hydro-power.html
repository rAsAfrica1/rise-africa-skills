Micro-Hydro Power

RENEWABLE ENERGY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the design, construction, and management of micro-hydro power systems for rural electrification. This comprehensive course covers hydrology, civil works, turbines, generators, and community project development. Gain practical skills to deliver sustainable off-grid energy solutions across Africa.
