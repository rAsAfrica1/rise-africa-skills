Battery Maintenance

TECHNICAL & INDUSTRIAL SKILLS · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the inspection, testing, charging, and repair of lead-acid, lithium-ion, and advanced battery systems used in vehicles, solar installations, forklifts, and backup power. This hands-on programme takes you from battery chemistry fundamentals to diagnostic testing, restoration, and certification-ready maintenance practice.
