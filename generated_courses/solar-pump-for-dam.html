Solar Pump for Dam

RISE AFRICA SKILLS · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the design, installation, and maintenance of solar-powered pumping systems for dam water management. This comprehensive course covers everything from solar resource assessment and PV array design to hydraulic system engineering, pump selection, and economic analysis. Gain practical skills to deliver reliable, sustainable water pumping solutions for agricultural, municipal, and industrial dam applications across Africa.
