Woodturning Fundamentals

CRAFTS & WOODWORKING · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the lathe from first cut to finished art piece. This hands-on programme takes you from lathe safety and tool sharpening through spindle, bowl, and hollow-form turning, ending with advanced and sculptural projects. Built for aspiring artisans, workshop owners, and makers across Africa who want to turn raw timber into sellable craft.
