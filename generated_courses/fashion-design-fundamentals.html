Fashion Design Fundamentals

CATEGORY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Build a complete foundation in fashion design, from the history of the craft and design principles to pattern making, draping, sewing, and portfolio presentation. This hands-on program takes you from first sketch to finished garment, ending with the branding and marketing skills to launch your own label.
