Charcoal Production

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the science and business of charcoal production, from sustainable feedstock selection and carbonization principles to improved kilns, briquetting, and marketing. This hands-on course equips you with practical skills to produce high-quality charcoal efficiently and profitably while managing environmental and safety concerns.
