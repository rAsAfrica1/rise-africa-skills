Avocado Farming

Agriculture · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete avocado value chain from site selection and propagation to harvest, post-harvest handling, and profitable marketing. This practical course is designed for African smallholders, agripreneurs, and extension officers ready to tap into the fast-growing global avocado market.
