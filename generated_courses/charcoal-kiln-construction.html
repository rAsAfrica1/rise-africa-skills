Charcoal Kiln Construction

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to design, build, and operate efficient charcoal kilns using earth, metal, brick, and retort designs suited to African conditions. This hands-on course covers site selection, materials, construction, carbonization, emissions control, safety, and the business of producing quality charcoal at scale.
