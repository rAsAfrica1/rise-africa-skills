Stone Masonry

CONSTRUCTION · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the craft of building with natural stone, from selecting and dressing raw material to constructing foundations, walls, arches, and decorative features. This hands-on program blends traditional masonry techniques with modern tools, project planning, and business skills for a career in Africa's growing construction industry.
