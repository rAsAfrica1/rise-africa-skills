Briquette Production

Clean Energy · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn how to turn agricultural waste, sawdust, and charcoal fines into high-quality briquettes for cooking and heating. This hands-on course covers raw materials, binders, machines, carbonization, quality control, and the business skills to launch a profitable briquette enterprise.
