Maasai Beadwork

CRAFTS · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn the vibrant art of Maasai beadwork, from its cultural roots and color symbolism to advanced three-dimensional designs. Build practical skills across bracelets, necklaces, earrings, leatherwork, and home decor, then turn your craft into income with pricing, marketing, and ethical sourcing guidance.
