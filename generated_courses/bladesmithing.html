Bladesmithing

Skilled Trades · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the ancient craft of forging blades from raw steel to finished knife. This comprehensive program takes you from building your first forge fire through advanced pattern-welding, heat treating, handle construction, and into the business of professional bladesmithing.
