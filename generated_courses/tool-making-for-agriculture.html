Tool Making for Agriculture

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to design, forge, fabricate and maintain the hand tools, animal-drawn implements and tractor-mounted equipment that African farms depend on. This hands-on programme takes you from basic metalworking and heat treatment through to welding, irrigation hardware, post-harvest machinery and running your own tool-making workshop as a business.
