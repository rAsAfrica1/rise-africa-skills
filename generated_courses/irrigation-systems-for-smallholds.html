Irrigation Systems for Smallholds

Agriculture · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to plan, install, and maintain practical irrigation systems suited to smallholder farms across Africa. From water sourcing and pumps to drip, sprinkler, and gravity-fed systems, this course builds the skills to grow more with every drop.
