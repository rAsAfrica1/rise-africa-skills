Preservation of Fish

FOOD PROCESSING · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the science and practice of fish preservation, from traditional drying, salting, and smoking to modern freezing, canning, and advanced technologies. This hands-on course equips fish handlers, processors, and entrepreneurs with the skills to reduce post-harvest losses, meet safety standards, and build profitable fish preservation businesses.
