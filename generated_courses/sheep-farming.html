Sheep Farming

Agriculture · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete cycle of profitable sheep farming, from breed selection and nutrition to health, breeding, wool, meat marketing, and farm business management. This practical program is designed for African smallholders and emerging commercial farmers ready to build a sustainable, income-generating sheep enterprise.
