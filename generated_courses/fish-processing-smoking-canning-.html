Fish Processing (Smoking & Canning)

AGRO-PROCESSING · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete fish processing value chain, from selecting fresh fish and safe handling through hot and cold smoking to commercial canning and thermal sterilization. This hands-on program equips you with the technical skills, food safety knowledge, and business acumen to launch or grow a profitable smoked and canned fish enterprise.
