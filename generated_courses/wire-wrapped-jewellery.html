Wire-Wrapped Jewellery

CATEGORY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the art of wire-wrapped jewellery, from simple loops and findings to advanced weaving and gemstone settings. This hands-on course takes you from complete beginner to confident maker, with practical projects in every module. You'll also learn the business essentials to turn your craft into income.
