Basic First Aid for Business and Community

HEALTH & SAFETY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to respond confidently to medical emergencies in the workplace and community. This hands-on course covers CPR, AED use, choking relief, bleeding control, and more, preparing you for certification and real-life emergencies.
