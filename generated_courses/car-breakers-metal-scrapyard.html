Car Breakers & Metal Scrapyard

TRADES & RECYCLING · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete operation of a modern car breakers and metal scrapyard, from safe vehicle depollution and dismantling to metal grading, cutting, and commodity trading. This hands-on program prepares you to run a compliant, profitable recycling business that turns end-of-life vehicles into valuable salvage parts and scrap metal.
