Grasscutter Farming

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the full cycle of grasscutter (cane rat) farming, from housing and feeding to breeding, health management, processing, and profitable marketing. This practical course equips you with the skills to start, run, and scale a sustainable grasscutter enterprise in Africa.
