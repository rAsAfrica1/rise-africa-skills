Smelting Fundamentals

METALLURGY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Build a solid working foundation in the science and practice of smelting, from ore preparation and furnace design to thermodynamics, slag chemistry, and safety. Designed for operators, technicians, and entrepreneurs in Africa's growing metals and minerals processing sector, this course blends theory with real plant applications across iron, copper, lead, zinc, aluminum, and ferroalloys.
