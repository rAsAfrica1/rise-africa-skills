Solar Installation in the Community

RISE AFRICA SKILLS · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to assess, design, install, and maintain solar photovoltaic systems that meet real community energy needs. This hands-on programme blends technical skills with community engagement and project management, preparing you to deliver reliable solar power in off-grid and underserved areas.
