Dried Fruit & Vegetable Processing

AGRO-PROCESSING · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the science and business of transforming fresh produce into high-value dried fruits and vegetables. This hands-on program covers raw material selection, drying technologies, food safety, value addition, and industrial scale-up. Designed for entrepreneurs, processors, and agribusiness professionals across Africa.
