Basic Blacksmithing

TRADES · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn the ancient craft of blacksmithing from forge setup to finished ironwork. This hands-on course covers fire management, tool making, heat treating, forge welding, and decorative techniques. Build practical skills to create hooks, tools, blades, and ornamental pieces for sale or personal use.
