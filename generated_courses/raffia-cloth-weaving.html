Raffia Cloth Weaving

Textile Crafts · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the traditional African craft of raffia cloth weaving, from harvesting and processing raffia fibers to advanced structural weaves and professional product design. This comprehensive course blends cultural heritage with modern techniques, equipping you to create marketable bags, home decor, and textile art while building a sustainable craft business.
