Breakfast Cereal Production

FOOD PROCESSING · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete breakfast cereal manufacturing process, from grain science and milling through flaking, extrusion, puffing, coating, and packaging. This hands-on program equips you with the technical and quality assurance skills needed to launch or work in a modern cereal production facility, with practical focus on African grain value chains and market opportunities.
