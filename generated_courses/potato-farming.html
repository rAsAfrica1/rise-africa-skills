Potato Farming

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete potato value chain from site selection and seed handling to storage, processing, and marketing. This practical course equips African smallholder and commercial farmers with climate-smart techniques to boost yields, reduce post-harvest losses, and build profitable potato enterprises.
