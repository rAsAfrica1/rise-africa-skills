Crocodile Farming

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to establish and run a profitable crocodile farming operation, from species selection and facility design to skin processing, meat production, and export marketing. This comprehensive course covers husbandry, health, safety, and business management tailored to African conditions and CITES compliance.
