Wheel Throwing Fundamentals

CATEGORY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the potter's wheel from first centering to finished glazed ware. This hands-on course takes you through clay preparation, throwing core forms, trimming, glazing, and firing while building the muscle memory and studio discipline professional ceramicists rely on.
