Distillery & Spirits

AGRICULTURE & PROCESSING · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the craft and commerce of distillation, from fermentation science and still operation to barrel aging, blending, and bottling. Built for African entrepreneurs, this programme covers whiskey, brandy, rum, gin, vodka, and liqueurs, and equips you to launch and run a compliant, profitable distillery.
