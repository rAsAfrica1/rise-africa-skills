Beaded Jewellery for Beginners

CRAFTS & JEWELLERY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to design and make beautiful beaded jewellery from scratch, from simple stringing and elastic bracelets to bead weaving, wirework, and gemstone pieces. This hands-on course takes you from your first bracelet to a sellable collection, with practical skills for building a small jewellery business.
