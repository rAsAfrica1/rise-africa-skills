Mud Cloth Production

Textiles & Craft · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the ancient West African art of Bogolanfini, from harvesting riverbed mud and preparing natural plant dyes to painting traditional motifs and finishing cloth for market. This hands-on course takes you from raw cotton to a sellable, culturally authentic mud cloth product.
