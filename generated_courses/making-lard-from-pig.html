Making Lard from Pig

FOOD PROCESSING · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete craft of rendering premium lard from pig fat, from sourcing and preparing raw fat to dry and wet rendering, flavoring, filtering, packaging, and cooking. This hands-on course equips African small-scale food producers, butchers, and home cooks with practical skills to create high-quality lard for personal use or profit.
