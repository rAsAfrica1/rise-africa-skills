Solar Dryer Construction

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to design, build, and operate solar dryers that preserve crops, reduce post-harvest losses, and create new income streams. This hands-on course takes you from site selection and materials through construction, airflow, controls, and commercialization. Ideal for artisans, farmers, agripreneurs, and technicians across Africa.
