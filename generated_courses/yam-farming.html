Yam Farming

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete cycle of yam production, from site selection and seed preparation to harvesting, storage, and value addition. This practical course equips African farmers and agripreneurs with the skills to grow yams profitably and sustainably for local and export markets.
