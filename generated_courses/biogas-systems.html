Biogas Systems

RENEWABLE ENERGY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the science, design, and business of biogas systems, from anaerobic digestion microbiology to plant construction, operation, and upgrading. This hands-on program equips you to develop biogas projects that turn organic waste into clean energy, biofertilizer, and income across African farms, municipalities, and industries.
