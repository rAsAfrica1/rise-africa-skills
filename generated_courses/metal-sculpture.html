Metal Sculpture

ARTS & CRAFT · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the art and craft of metal sculpture, from basic hand tools and welding to forging, casting, and large-scale public art. This hands-on programme equips you with practical fabrication skills, design thinking, and professional practice for a career in contemporary metal sculpture.
