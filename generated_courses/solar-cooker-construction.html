Solar Cooker Construction

RENEWABLE ENERGY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to design, build, test, and maintain box, panel, and parabolic solar cookers using locally available materials. This hands-on course covers solar principles, construction techniques, cooking methods, and the business skills needed to bring clean cooking solutions to African communities.
