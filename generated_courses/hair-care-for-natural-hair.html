Hair Care for Natural Hair

CATEGORY · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the science and art of caring for natural, textured hair from root to tip. This comprehensive course takes you from understanding your unique hair type through to advanced styling, protective techniques, and long-term length retention. Whether you are transitioning, doing the big chop, or caring for a child's hair, you will build the confidence and skills to grow healthy, thriving natural hair.
