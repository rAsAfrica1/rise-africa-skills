Kiln Construction and Firing

CERAMICS · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the design, construction, and operation of ceramic kilns from the ground up. This hands-on course covers everything from selecting refractory materials and building arches to advanced firing schedules like raku, salt, and wood firing. Learn to build and run kilns safely and efficiently, whether for a small studio or a growing production pottery business.
