Petrol and Diesel Engine Fundamentals: 0 to 12 Litres

AUTOMOTIVE & POWER SYSTEMS · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the theory, construction, and servicing of petrol and diesel engines from tiny 0-litre motorcycle units to 12-litre heavy-duty truck powerplants. This hands-on programme takes you from internal combustion basics through fuel, air, lubrication, and electronic management systems to real-world diagnostics and performance testing. Built for technicians, apprentices, and workshop owners across Africa who want practical, job-ready engine skills.
