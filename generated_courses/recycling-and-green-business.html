Recycling and Green Business

Green Economy · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Turn Africa's growing waste challenge into a thriving enterprise. This practical course takes you from understanding waste streams and recycling technologies to launching, marketing, and scaling a profitable green business. Learn circular economy principles, financial management, and real African market strategies to build a venture that serves people, planet, and profit.
