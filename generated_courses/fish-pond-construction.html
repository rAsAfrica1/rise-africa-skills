Fish Pond Construction

Aquaculture · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn to plan, design, and build productive fish ponds from site assessment through commissioning and sustainable maintenance. This hands-on course covers earthen, lined, and concrete systems, water supply and aeration, and the practical engineering skills needed to launch a profitable aquaculture enterprise in Africa.
