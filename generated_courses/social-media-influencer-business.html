Module 10 · Analytics and Performance Optimization

Key Metrics: Engagement Rate, Reach, and Conversions

As an influencer, you need to know if your content is working. Three numbers matter most: engagement rate, reach, and conversions. Engagement rate is the percentage of people who interact with your post (likes, comments, shares, saves) out of everyone who saw it. For example, if 1,000 people see your post and 50 like or comment, your engagement rate is 5%. On Instagram, a good engagement rate is 1-3% for accounts with over 10,000 followers, but for smaller accounts (under 5,000), 4-8% is common. In Africa, where audiences often engage more actively, you might see even higher rates. Reach is the total number of unique people who see your content. On a public account, reach can be higher than follower count if your post is shared. Conversions are the actions that make you money: clicks to a website, promo code uses, or sales. For example, if you sell a hair product and 100 people use your code, that's 100 conversions. Track these metrics weekly using Instagram Insights, TikTok Analytics, or Twitter Analytics. Write them in a simple notebook or Google Sheet. Look for patterns: Does a video get more reach than a photo? Do posts with questions get more comments? Use this data to decide what to create next. Remember, high engagement doesn't always mean high salesâbut it's a good sign. Focus on building a loyal audience that trusts your recommendations, and conversions will follow..Trim()
