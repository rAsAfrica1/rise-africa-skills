Road Making

CONSTRUCTION · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the full road construction lifecycle, from site surveying and earthworks to asphalt and concrete paving, drainage, and sustainable practices. Built for Africa's growing infrastructure sector, this course equips you with hands-on skills for road building, maintenance, and project management careers.
