Organic Fertilizer & Compost

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the science and practice of turning kitchen, garden, and farm waste into nutrient-rich organic fertilizer and compost. From hot piles and vermicomposting to compost tea, green manures, and soil health management, this course equips you to build fertile soil, cut input costs, and grow healthier crops sustainably.
