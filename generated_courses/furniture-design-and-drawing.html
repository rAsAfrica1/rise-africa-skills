Furniture Design and Drawing

CREATIVE & TECHNICAL SKILLS · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the art and science of furniture design, from hand sketching and technical drawing to CAD modelling and full-scale prototyping. This hands-on programme equips you with the practical skills to design, document, and build functional, beautiful furniture for the African market and beyond.
