Buildings for Preservation (10 Methods)

Heritage & Built Environment · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the ten core methods used to protect, repair and renew historic buildings across Africa. From preventive conservation to adaptive reuse and documentation, this practical course equips surveyors, builders, architects and craftspeople to care for heritage structures with confidence.
