Pesticides & Agrochemicals

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the science, safe handling, and sustainable use of pesticides and agrochemicals in African crop production. From chemistry and toxicology to regulation, residue analysis, and integrated pest management, this program equips agronomists, input dealers, and farm managers with practical, field-ready expertise.
