Soybean Farming

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the complete soybean value chain from seed selection and soil preparation to harvest, post-harvest handling, and profitable marketing. This comprehensive course equips African farmers, agripreneurs, and extension officers with practical, climate-smart skills to grow high-yielding soybeans sustainably. Learn proven techniques for nutrient management, pest and disease control, irrigation, and farm business management to build a thriving soybean enterprise.
