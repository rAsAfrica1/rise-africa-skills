Preservation of Grains

AGRICULTURE · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Master the science and practice of protecting harvested grains from moisture, insects, molds, and rodents. This comprehensive course equips farmers, agripreneurs, and storage managers with practical skills to reduce post-harvest losses and safeguard food security across Africa.
