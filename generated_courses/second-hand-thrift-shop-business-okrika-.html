Second-Hand / Thrift Shop Business (Okrika)

BUSINESS · 12 modules · 72 lessons

Learn how to build a profitable Okrika (second-hand clothing) business from the ground up. This course covers sourcing, grading, cleaning, pricing, marketing, online sales, and scaling so you can turn affordable fashion into a sustainable income.
