FOOD PROCESSING

Fortified Corn-Soy Porridge for Malnourished Children

A practical, low-cost recipe for protein-energy malnutrition — using local maize, soy and milk powder.

This course teaches you how to make a genuinely nutritious fortified porridge for children aged 6 months to 5 years, using ingredients already available in most African markets. It is designed for mothers, community health workers, small food processors and anyone feeding children in crisis. You will learn the science of malnutrition, the exact formulation, safe production methods, quality control, and how to package and distribute — including for NGOs, clinics and informal markets.
