Customer Acquisition Strategy: Finding Your First Farmers

  1. Research Phase (Week 1-2): Identify 20+ dairy farmers within 30km radius
  2. Cold Outreach (Week 3-4): Visit farms with samples, explain 60% cost savings
  3. Trial Offer: Offer first 50 kg at $0.90/kg (10% discount), money-back guarantee
  4. First Bulk Sale: Land 2-3 farmers on recurring monthly orders (500 kg each)
  5. Referral System: Ask satisfied customers for referrals (5-10% discount for referrals)
  6. Scaling: Build to 10-20 farmers = $3,000-8,000 monthly recurring revenue

Bulk Contract Template (Example: 500 kg/month)

  • Customer: XYZ Dairy Farm (30 cows)
  • Monthly Volume: 500 kg (16% dairy formula)
  • Delivery: Every 2 weeks (250 kg x 2 deliveries)
  • Price: $1.00/kg = $500/month ($6,000/year)
  • Farmer Savings: $300-500/month vs commercial feed
  • Your Profit: $200/month per farm ($2,400/year)
  • Contract Term: 12 months with 30-day cancellation
  • Quality Guarantee: Money-back if cattle health issues arise

  3. Which customer segment is most profitable? (Answer: Direct dairy farmers)
  4. What's the typical cost savings for farmers vs commercial feed? (Answer: 25-50%)
