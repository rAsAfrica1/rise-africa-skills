Tree and Agroforestry Seed - The Long Game

🧠 Brain Opener

Tree seed is the slowest seed business. It is also the most valuable per kilogram. Tephrosia seed sells for 1.50 dollars per kilogram - more than cotton or tobacco. And a single tree produces seed for 20 years.

  • What makes tree seed different from crop seed?
  • Which tree species have the best seed markets in Africa?
  • How long until you can harvest tree seed?
🎁 Reveal a strong answer

Tree seed differs because trees take 2 to 5 years to first harvest, then produce for decades. The best African tree seed markets are Tephrosia (green manure), Brachiaria (fodder grass), Desmodium (push-pull system), Faidherbia albida (agroforestry), and indigenous species. First harvest is usually year 3 to year 5.

📖 Rudo Plants the Long Game

Rudo had grown vegetables for five years. She wanted a crop that would earn money while she slept.

Her uncle, who ran a tree nursery, asked her one question: "Are you patient?"

"How patient?"

"Three years before the first harvest. Thirty years of harvest after that."

Rudo thought about it. She had one hectare of her own land and two hectares rented. The rented land could not hold trees - the owner might want it back. But her own hectare could.

She planted 400 trees on half of her hectare. Half were Tephrosia candida - a legume used as green manure in coffee and banana plantations. Half were Faidherbia albida - an indigenous species that improves soil and provides fodder.

The first year, the trees grew to 1 metre. No harvest. She kept her vegetables on the other half of the hectare.

The second year, the trees grew to 2 metres. Tephrosia began to flower. She collected a small trial harvest - 2 kilograms of seed. She sold it to a coffee cooperative for 3 dollars.

The third year, the trees were 3 metres tall. Tephrosia produced 20 kilograms of seed. Faidherbia produced 5 kilograms. She sold the Tephrosia at 1.50 per kg and the Faidherbia at 4 per kg.

Total tree seed revenue in year 3: 50 dollars from half a hectare. Not much. But the trees were only getting started.

The fourth year, the harvest was 60 kilograms of Tephrosia and 15 of Faidherbia. Revenue: 150 dollars.

By year 6, her tree seed was producing 300 dollars per year from half a hectare - with almost no work. The trees kept producing, and the soil under them was improving.

Rudo learned the lesson. Tree seed is not fast money. It is money that comes while you are doing other things.

💡 The Big Idea

Tree seed is patient capital

Tree seed does not pay in year one. It pays in year three and keeps paying for decades. For a farmer with secure land, tree seed is the best long-term investment. Two hours of work per year for 20 years of harvest. Nothing else in farming works this way.

📚 Three Deep Lessons

1

The best African tree seed species

Tephrosia candida. Green manure legume used in coffee, banana, and tea plantations. Price: 1.50 dollars per kilogram. First harvest: year 2 to 3. Peak production: year 4 to 10. Demand: high from coffee cooperatives and NGO reforestation programmes.

Faidherbia albida. Indigenous African tree. Sheds leaves in the rainy season and keeps them in the dry season, making it a perfect agroforestry species. Price: 3 to 5 dollars per kilogram. First harvest: year 5 to 7. Long-lived - 50 to 100 years of production.

Brachiaria and Desmodium. Fodder species used in push-pull systems and dairy farming. Price: 2 to 4 dollars per kilogram. First harvest: year 1 to 2. Continuous production.

2

Planting and managing trees for seed

Plant at the start of the rainy season. Spacing depends on species - Tephrosia 2 by 2 metres, Faidherbia 5 by 5 metres. Prepare planting holes 30 by 30 by 30 centimetres. Mix compost into the soil. Mulch around the base.

Trees need less care than crops but they need patience. Weed the first year to give young trees a head start. Water young trees in dry periods for the first two years. Once established, they survive on rainfall alone.

Seed harvest: watch for pods or fruit to mature. Harvest before they shatter. Dry in the shade. Extract seed by hand or by beating. Store in sealed containers.

3

The tree seed market

Buyers of tree seed are different from crop seed buyers. They are: coffee and tea cooperatives (Tephrosia), dairy cooperatives (Brachiaria, Desmodium), NGOs running reforestation programmes (indigenous species), government forestry departments (all species), and research institutes.

The prices are stable and the demand is growing. Every African country has a national tree planting programme. Reforestation targets have been rising for 10 years. Tree seed production cannot keep up with demand in most countries.

A farmer with 400 mature Tephrosia trees produces 100 to 150 kilograms of seed per year. At 1.50 dollars per kilogram, that is 150 to 225 dollars per year. From half a hectare. With almost no work after year 3.

🛠 Hands-On - Plant 20 Trees for Seed

You need

  • 20 tree seedlings or seeds
  • A shovel
  • Compost
  • Mulch
  • A notebook
  1. Choose a species - Tephrosia is the best beginner tree.
  2. Prepare 20 planting holes 30 by 30 by 30 centimetres.
  3. Mix compost into the soil.
  4. Plant the seedlings at the start of the rains.
  5. Mulch around the base.
  6. Water for the first two dry seasons.
  7. Weed the first year.
  8. Record planting date, species, and location.
  9. Record first flowering date and first harvest date.
Why it works: Twenty trees is a small experiment. In five years it will be producing 20 to 30 dollars of seed per year with no ongoing work.

🌍 Real Stories from Africa

Kenya Tephrosia for coffee

Kenyan coffee cooperatives pay 1.50 to 2 dollars per kilogram for Tephrosia seed. Demand exceeds supply every year. Farmers with 500 mature trees earn 500 to 800 dollars per year from tree seed alone, with no ongoing labour after year 3.

Rwanda agroforestry seed

Rwandan farmers plant Faidherbia albida on their land for agroforestry. The seed sells for 4 dollars per kilogram to government reforestation programmes. A mature tree produces 5 to 15 kilograms per year.
