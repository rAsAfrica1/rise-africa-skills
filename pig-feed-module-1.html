1. Pig Nutrition Basics

Pigs are monogastric animals - they eat what we eat, and their feed must be balanced daily. Understanding the six nutrient groups is the foundation of every profitable piggery.

Lesson 1.3 - Why feed is 70% of cost

A 90 kg market pig eats 250-280 kg of feed from weaning to slaughter. At $0.40/kg, that is $100-112 per pig. Add the piglet cost ($30-50), housing, health, and labour, and the total cost per pig is $150-180. Sell at $2.50/kg live = $225. Margin: $45-75 per pig.

If you cut feed cost from $0.40/kg to $0.28/kg through home-mixing, you save $30-34 per pig. On 50 pigs/year that is $1,500-1,700 - the difference between a hobby and a business.

The 70% rule: Every $0.01 you save per kg of feed is $2.50-2.80 saved per pig. Focus every improvement on feed cost and feed efficiency.

Lesson 1.4 - FCR - the number that defines profit

FCR = kg of feed consumed ÷ kg of live weight gained. Good pig FCR is 2.8-3.2 from weaning to market.

Practical exercise 1.1: Weigh your current feed sack. Record the price. Calculate cost per kg. Multiply by 270 kg. That is your feed cost per finished pig.
