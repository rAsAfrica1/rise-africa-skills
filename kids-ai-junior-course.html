How Machines Think

Machines are not clever. They are just very, very good at following your rules.

Ages 7–128 modules 🪨 Starts with no device

You are about to find out the biggest secret in the world of computers: machines cannot think at all. They only sort, count, and follow steps — and you already know how to do all three. By the end of this course you will have taught a machine to see something with your own two hands.
