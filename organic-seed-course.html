What you will build

By the end of this course you will have a working organic seed business with real customers, real certifications, and real income. Not a plan on paper - a business in motion.

Real numbers from Africa: amaranth seed sells at 72 dollars per kilogram. Tomato seed at 80 to 90. Traditional African vegetable seed at 80 to 120. Contracted organic seed producers earn above 50 percent margins. The market is 0.23 billion dollars today, growing to 0.83 billion by 2034.

Who this is for

Smallholder farmers with 0.25 to 2 hectares who want to move from low-margin crops to premium seed production.

Young entrepreneurs with land or access to land who want to build a real agricultural business.

Farmer cooperatives who want to multiply seed together for their members and for external buyers.

NGOs and training organisations who want a structured curriculum to train producer groups.
