3. Ration Formulation

Formulation is not guesswork. With a notebook, a scale, and the Pearson square, you can build any pig ration in 10 minutes.

Module 3 of 6 · Pig Feed Production · quiz at the end

Lesson 3.1 - The Pearson square method

The Pearson square balances two ingredients to hit a target protein level.

Example: Make an 18% protein grower ration using maize (9% protein) and soya meal (45% protein).

  1. Write the target (18%) in the middle of the square.
  2. Write maize (9%) and soya (45%) in the two left corners.
  3. Subtract diagonally: 45 - 18 = 27, and 18 - 9 = 9.
  4. The answer is 27 parts maize to 9 parts soya, i.e. 75% maize + 25% soya.
IngredientParts% of mix
Maize (9% CP)2775%
Soya meal (45% CP)925%
Total36100%

For three or more ingredients, use the same method in two steps: first balance the two energy sources, then balance the protein.

The formulation rule: Weigh every ingredient. A bucket is not a measurement. Accurate weighing is the difference between a 14% ration and a 20% ration - and the pig pays for the difference.
