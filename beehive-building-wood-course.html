Beehive Building — Wood

Build Langstroth, top-bar and Kenyan top-bar hives in wood. Cutting lists, joinery, hive stands, roofs, and every design decision that decides how the bees live in it.

Who this is for

Smallholder beekeepers, apiary operators and anyone wanting to start a hive-making business. The course assumes basic woodworking tools and no prior beekeeping training.

This course teaches general principles of wooden beehive construction for African conditions. Hive dimensions vary between regions and bee races. Confirm dimensions with your national beekeeping authority or a local beekeeper with working hives before committing timber.
