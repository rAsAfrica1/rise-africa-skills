5. Breeding and Kidding

Good breeding turns 10 does into 30 goats a year. Bad breeding wastes feed on empty animals.

Module 5 of 6 · Goat Farming for Profit

When does a doe breed?

EventAge/Interval
Puberty6-8 months (breed at 10-12 months)
Oestrus cycleEvery 18-21 days
Heat duration24-48 hours
Gestation145-155 days (5 months)
Kidding interval8-10 months for 3 kiddings in 2 years

Signs of heat

  • Restless, bleating more than usual
  • Tail wagging
  • Vulva swollen and red
  • Mucus discharge
  • Seeks the buck, stands to be mounted

Breeding method

  1. Keep the buck separate from does except during breeding.
  2. Watch for heat - check twice daily.
  3. Introduce the doe to the buck when in heat.
  4. Allow 2 services in 12 hours.
  5. Mark the breeding date on your calendar.
  6. Expect kidding 150 days later.

Late pregnancy care

  • Increase concentrate feed from week 12.
  • Give a dewormer 3-4 weeks before kidding.
  • Vaccinate against enterotoxaemia (pulpy kidney) 4 weeks before kidding.
  • Prepare a clean kidding pen.
  • Watch for signs of kidding: restlessness, pawing, isolating.

Kidding

  1. Most does kid without help. Give them privacy.
  2. Normal kidding takes 1-2 hours for 1-2 kids.
  3. If a doe strains for more than 1 hour with no progress, call a vet.
  4. Clear the kid's nose of mucus. Let the doe lick it dry.
  5. Ensure the kid nurses within 1 hour. Colostrum is critical.
  6. Dip the umbilical cord in iodine.
  7. Weigh and record the kid.

The 3-in-2 rule: The best goat farms get 3 kiddings per doe in 2 years. Breed back within 60-90 days of kidding.
Practical exercise 5: Record breeding dates of all your does. Track pregnancies. Compare kidding intervals to the target.
