Talking to Claude — Junior

Module 8 of 8: Opening Day

Real customers

🧠 Brain Opener — 60 seconds

Before anything else

Today you stop pretending and start selling. One real customer, real money, real receipt. Everything before this was rehearsal.

📚 Lessons

1. The first sale is the hardest

It feels like jumping. It is not. It is one small transaction. Make it as small as possible. Sell one thing to one person for one small amount. The second sale is easier. The third is easy.

2. The 3-step drill

Round 1: prepare (materials ready, price ready, change ready). Round 2: pitch (one sentence, said kindly). Round 3: deliver (on time, exactly what was promised).

3. Where it breaks

Giving up after one "no". A "no" is data — it tells you what to change. A "no" today is a "yes" for the next customer, if you adjust.

🎯 Cross-Skill Links

♟️
Chess

Even a bad opening move teaches you something. Play the game, then review it. A first sale is the same: play, then look back and adjust.

🎹
Music & Piano

The first concert is terrifying. The second is easier. The tenth is fun. Nobody remembers your first — they remember your tenth.

🀄
Chinese-Style Drill

开始 means begin. 完成 means finish. A business is 开始 + 完成 + 开始 again — the loop never stops.

🤖 Real-Time AI-Era Skill

Write down three objections a customer might say ("too expensive", "I do not need it", "maybe later"). Ask Claude how to answer each one politely. Rehearse them aloud.

🎮 Game

Do it now

Mock sale. Class plays customer, you play seller. Do it three times. The third time should be twice as good as the first.
