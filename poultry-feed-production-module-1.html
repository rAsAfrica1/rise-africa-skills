Poultry Nutrition Requirements

Poultry require different protein levels based on age and production purpose:

Feed Type Age/Stage Protein % Energy (kcal/kg) Key Nutrients
Broiler Starter 0-3 weeks 23% 3,050 Amino acids, P, Ca, Vitamins
Broiler Finisher 3-6 weeks 19% 3,100 Balanced minerals
Layer Maintenance 16+ weeks 16% 2,800 Ca for shell, balanced P
Breeder Adult 17% 2,900 Fertility nutrients

Poultry Market Economics

Poultry feed commands premium pricing due to specialized requirements:

Market Segment Production Cost Wholesale Price Retail Price Margin
Broiler Starter (23%) $0.092/kg $0.95/kg $1.40-1.60/kg $1.31-1.51/kg
Broiler Finisher (19%) $0.085/kg $0.90/kg $1.30-1.50/kg $1.20-1.40/kg
Layer Feed (16%) $0.080/kg $0.85/kg $1.20-1.40/kg $1.10-1.30/kg
Market Opportunity: 1,000-bird broiler operation = 60kg feed per cycle. At $1.45/kg average: $87 revenue. Annual (6 cycles): $522 from that one customer. With 15-20 customers: $7,800-$10,440 annual revenue.
