Eyes in the Sky

A drone is not a toy that flies. It is a camera that can stand on air.

Ages 7–128 modules 🪨 Starts with no device

Look up. Everything a drone does — push air down, hold itself steady, look at the ground from above — you can understand with paper, string and a bit of running about. You will also learn the rules of the sky, because the sky belongs to everybody and it has real laws.
