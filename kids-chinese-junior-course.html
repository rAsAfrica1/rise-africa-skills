Nǐ hǎo! Chinese

Every Chinese character started as a little picture — and you can draw them all.

Ages 7–128 modules 🪨 Starts with no device

Chinese looks like a wall of tiny drawings, and that is exactly what it is. A person walking. Three mountain peaks. The sun. Once you see the picture inside the character, you cannot un-see it. You will draw them in sand, shout them at your friends, and count goats in Chinese before this course is done.

The 8 modules

1
🪨 No device needed

The Sun, the Mountain and the Walking Person

Chinese characters are not random squiggles — they are pictures that got tidier over three thousand years.

🚶 → 𠆢 → 人 ☀️ → ⊙ → 日 ⛰️ → 山
2
🪨 No device needed

Nǐ hǎo — Say It Loud Enough to Startle a Chicken

Chinese is a language you sing, and the tune changes the meaning, so you must say it out loud and big.

mā ˉ má ˊ mǎ ˅ mà ˋ
3
🪨 No device needed

Counting Goats in Chinese

The first three Chinese numbers are just one, two and three lines — the easiest writing in any language on earth.

一 = 1 line 二 = 2 lines 三 = 3 lines

📱 Shared phone

Mother, Horse, Hemp, Scold

Say 'ma' with the wrong tune and you have just called your mother a horse — so today you check your tones against a real voice.

妈 mā 👩 麻 má 🌾 马 mǎ 🐎 骂 mà 😠

📱 Shared phone

Everybody in the House

Family words are the words you will say most often, and in Chinese most of them are one syllable said twice.

爸爸 bàba 妈妈 māma 哥哥 gēge 姐姐 jiějie
6
📱 Shared phone

Watch the Character Build Itself

Every character is drawn in a fixed order of strokes, and watching that order animate makes the character finally make sense.

口 → 日 → 田 → 中 → 国

🤖 Salvage kit

Hunt for Chinese in Your Own Market

There is real Chinese writing within walking distance of you, and today you go and read some of it.

🤖 Salvage kit

Capstone: Your First Real Conversation

Today you stop practising Chinese and simply have a conversation in it, start to finish, with another human being.

你好！→ 你叫什么名字？→ 我叫… → 很高兴认识你！
For a parent or teacher

By the end your child will recognise about forty Chinese characters by sight, produce all four tones clearly, and hold a short spoken introduction with a Chinese speaker. Chinese traders, contractors and scholarship programmes are present across the continent, and a child who can greet someone properly in Mandarin is remembered.
