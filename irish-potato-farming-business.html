The Business Seed — Irish Potato Farming

This is a business blueprint. Read this before you buy a single seed potato.

1. The Business Idea

You are training to run one of these real businesses around Irish potato:

  • Fresh table potato production — grow, grade, bag, sell
  • Processing potato supply — contract with chip/crisp manufacturers
  • Certified seed potato producer — highest margin, strict certification
  • Potato storage operator — buy at harvest, store, sell off-season

2. Three Capital Tiers

Tier 1 — Smallholder Producer

– ,500

0.5–1 hectare. Certified seed, base fertiliser, basic blight programme. Sell grade 1 to local market and grade 2 to vendors. Save your own seed from the cleanest field.

Tier 2 — Commercial Producer + Storage

,000 – ,000

1–3 hectares plus a diffuse-light storage shed. Full blight programme. Grade for supermarkets and processors. Store 3–5 months to sell into the off-season price.

Tier 3 — Seed Potato Enterprise

,000+

3+ hectares under seed multiplication with isolation, roguing, certification. Cold storage. Sell certified seed at 2–3x table price. Contract farming with neighbouring smallholders.

Rule: start at the tier your water and market can support. Potato is capital-intensive — do not over-extend in year one.

3. Value-Addition Ladder

ProductMultiplier
Unwashed field-run potato1x baseline
Washed, graded table potato1.3–1.6x
Bagged, branded retail packs1.5–2x
Off-season stored potato1.8–2.5x
Certified seed potato2–3x
Potato flour / starch3–5x

For most smallholders, storage is the first value step — not processing. It requires less capital and captures 50–100% more revenue per kg.

4. Buyers You Will Approach

  • Local market vendors — daily cash purchases, grade 2
  • Urban retailers and supermarkets — weekly contract, grade 1, 30-day terms
  • Chip and crisp processors — 5–50 t lots, contract, specific varieties
  • Institutional buyers — schools, hospitals, army, 30–60 day terms
  • Seed distributors — bulk certified seed, premium price
Rule: name three buyers before you plant. If you cannot name three, do not buy seed.
