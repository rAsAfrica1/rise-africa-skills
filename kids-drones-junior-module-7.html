Kids Drones — Junior

Module 7 of 8: Photos from Above — The Camera Is a Tool, Not a Toy

Shared phone

🧠 Brain Opener — 60 seconds

Before anything else

Look at any photo you have taken. Now imagine the same scene from 20 metres up. What would you notice that you missed?

📚 Lessons

1. Composition is the whole art

Three rules: (1) Rule of thirds — imagine the frame is cut into 9 squares. Put the subject where lines cross. (2) Leading lines — roads, fences, rivers pull the eye toward something. (3) Light — morning and late afternoon are gold; midday is flat and harsh. These three rules apply to phone photography as much as drone photography. Learn them before you buy a camera.

2. The 3-part drill

Round 1: take 5 photos of the same object from ground level. Round 2: take 5 from a chair. Round 3: take 5 through a window. Which set tells the best story? Write one sentence about each set. Story wins.

3. Where the idea breaks

A drone photo of a beautiful landscape can still be boring. The camera does not save you. Beauty in the world does not automatically become beauty in a photo. You have to compose it. Choose what to leave out.

🎯 Cross-Skill Links

♟️
Chess

A good move tells a story: "I threaten your queen so you must respond, and meanwhile I develop my bishop." A good photo tells a story too. Both reward planning over reacting.

🎹
Music & Piano

A phrase in music has a beginning, a middle, and an end. A photo has the same shape — where the eye enters, where it rests, where it leaves. Practice seeing photos as phrases.

🀄
Chinese-Style Drill

拍照 (pāizhào) = to take a photo. 拍 (to hit / to shoot) + 照 (to shine / to reflect) — a photo is a hit of light.

🤖 Real-Time AI-Era Skill

Show Claude three photos you took. Ask: "Which is the strongest composition and why? What is the weakest and why?" Then retake the weakest with the suggestion in mind. Compare.

🎮 Game

Do it now

Photo scavenger hunt. List: a curve, a line, a texture, a person working, a shadow. Find and photograph each from the highest point you can reach safely. No drones needed. Winner has the most striking photo of each.
