2. Local Ingredients and Their Value

The cheapest feed is the feed you can grow, buy in bulk, or process on your own farm. This module teaches you how to value every ingredient.

The premix is small in volume but critical. Without it, pigs grow slowly and get sick even on a "balanced" ration.

Lesson 2.4 - Cassava - the cheap energy king

Dried cassava chips cost 40-60% less than maize. But cassava is only 2-3% protein. If you use cassava, you must buy more soya or fish meal to balance it. Do the math before switching.

RationCost/kg feedCost per pig (270 kg)
100% maize-based$0.42$113
50% maize + 50% cassava$0.34$92
30% maize + 70% cassava (needs more soya)$0.33$89
Never use raw fresh cassava without drying. Fresh cassava contains cyanide compounds. Sun-dry chips 3-4 days until they snap before grinding.
