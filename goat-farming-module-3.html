3. Housing and Fencing

Goats hate rain. A dry, ventilated shelter is the difference between profit and dead stock.

Module 3 of 6 · Goat Farming for Profit

A 20-doe flock needs 40-50 m2 of shelter.

House design

  1. Raised floor. Slatted wood or concrete 30-50 cm above ground.
  2. Open sides. Airflow on 2-3 sides with wire mesh.
  3. Roof overhang 60 cm.
  4. North-south orientation.
  5. Feeding rack. Hay off the ground.
  6. Water trough. Clean, in shade.
  7. Mineral block holder.
  8. Kidding pen. Separate, clean, quiet.
  9. Sick pen. Isolate sick animals.

Never use barbed wire for goats - it damages coats and eyes.

Rotational grazing

Divide land into 4-6 paddocks. Move goats every 3-7 days. Benefits:

  • Worm burden drops 60-80%
  • Pasture recovers better
  • Less overgrazing
  • More animals per hectare
The shelter rule: Goats need 2 m2 each indoors. Half that and disease and stress hurt your margin.
Practical exercise 3: Draw your planned goat house. Include raised floor, feed rack, water, mineral holder, kidding pen.
