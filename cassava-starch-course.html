â  SAFETY WARNING â Aflatoxin and Chemical Handling

Groundnut, maize, and other staples can develop aflatoxin if dried poorly or stored above 13% moisture. Aflatoxin is a carcinogen and cannot be removed by cooking. Never sell or consume mouldy grain. Sort out discoloured kernels before storage.

When using storage pesticides (e.g. phosphine, Actellic): wear gloves, a mask, and long sleeves; never eat, drink, or smoke during application; store chemicals away from food and children; observe the full withholding period before sale or consumption. Follow national pesticide registration rules.

Quick Reference Checklist

  • Harvest at the right moisture. Target 13â14% for grains; test with a moisture meter before storage.
  • Dry on raised platforms. Never dry directly on bare ground â it causes contamination and loss.
  • Clean and sort. Remove broken, discoloured, and mouldy kernels before storage.
  • Use hermetic storage. PICS bags or metal silos cut insect losses dramatically and pay back in one season.
  • Treat storage pests correctly. Follow label rates, use protective gear, and respect withholding periods.
  • Record everything. Track weight in, weight out, and losses per batch to build your business case.
  • Aggregate to sell better. Pool with neighbours or a cooperative to meet buyer volume and quality specs.
  • Check for aflatoxin risk. If grain smells musty or shows mould, do not sell or eat it â seek testing.
