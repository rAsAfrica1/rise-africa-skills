â  SAFETY WARNING â Agrochemicals, Food Handling and Storage

  • Pesticides are poisons. Always wear gloves, boots, and a face mask when handling or spraying. Never eat, drink, or smoke during application. Wash thoroughly afterwards.
  • Respect pre-harvest intervals. Selling produce before the label's waiting period is illegal in most African markets and can cause buyer rejection or poisoning.
  • Store chemicals separately. Never store agrochemicals in the same room as food, feed, or seed. Use locked, labelled containers out of reach of children.
  • Mycotoxins (aflatoxin) are a food safety and trade risk. Dry grains to safe moisture (maize â¤13.5%) and store off the floor. Aflatoxin-contaminated grain cannot be sold into regulated markets and is a health hazard.
  • Record every input. Batch numbers and application dates protect you legally and commercially if a buyer raises a residue or quality claim.

Quick Reference Checklist

  • Know your cost per unit. Calculate cost per kg or per litre including labour, transport, and finance before you negotiate a price.
  • Measure loss, don't guess it. Weigh in and weigh out at each stage for one full season to find your real loss hotspots.
  • Grade before you sell. Grading and cleaning consistently lift price and reduce buyer rejections.
  • Test moisture before storage. Use a moisture meter; maize above 13.5% will mould and lose value.
  • Model cash flow, not just profit. Value addition ties up cash â plan working capital for at least one full cycle.
  • Secure at least two buyers. Single-buyer dependence is the most common cause of margin collapse.
  • Keep records for finance. Lenders and grant programmes require production, sales, and cost records; start them now.
  • Check compliance before export. Verify pesticide residue, aflatoxin, and phytosanitary requirements for your target market.
