How to use this site

  1. Pick the course that matches what you are building right now.
  2. Work through the modules in order — each one builds on the previous.
  3. Do the assignment at the end of every module. Write your own numbers, not the examples.
  4. Build, measure or test at least one thing from each module before moving on.
  5. Keep your assignments in one folder. By the end of a course you will have a complete design document for your own operation.

Not a course — a build manual

These are not academic lessons. Every module is written to be read at the bench, with the materials in front of you and a tape measure in hand. The worked calculations use real numbers you can substitute your own into.
