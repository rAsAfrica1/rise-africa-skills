🛠️ Implementation Steps

  1. Site Selection: Choose humid, tropical location
  2. Soil Preparation: Test soil and add amendments
  3. Plant Seedlings: Space 3m apart, 500-600 trees/hectare
  4. Establish Shade: Plant banana or coconut trees
  5. Maintenance: Prune annually, manage weeds
  6. Harvest Management: Pick ripe pods, ferment and dry
