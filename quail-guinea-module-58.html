Online and Social Media Selling

58 Online and social media selling

You can reach buyers 500km away with a phone. Reach is free. Conversion is skill.

Platform priorities:

  1. WhatsApp Business - catalogue, quick replies. Best for direct orders.
  2. Facebook Marketplace and Groups - biggest reach in Africa.
  3. Instagram - visual. Perfect for quail egg photos, processed products.
  4. TikTok - short videos, young buyers.
  5. Jiji.ng, Jumia - buyers actively looking to buy.
  6. Your own website
  7. YouTube - farm tours, how-tos.

Content that sells:

  • Behind the scenes - feeding, hatching, brooding
  • Customer reviews - screenshots of happy buyers
  • Before and after - eggs to packaged product
  • Educational posts - builds trust
  • Price list posts - clear, updated weekly
  • Live videos - Q&A, farm walk

Converting browsers into buyers:

  1. Post a clear price and minimum order
  2. Give a fast way to order (WhatsApp link)
  3. Show proof of delivery
  4. Offer payment on delivery for local buyers
  5. Bundle offers
  6. Referral discount

Handling delivery: local bike/motorbike, regional bus parcel service, cold chain for processed meat, padded trays for eggs with FRAGILE labels.

30 minutes a day on social media can generate 5-20 orders a week. Consistency beats perfection - post daily.
