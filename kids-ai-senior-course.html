Anyone can train a model. You are going to learn where it fails — and that is the valuable part.

You will build classifiers, then attack them until they fall over. Not because breaking things is fun — though it is — but because the person who knows exactly where a system fails is the person worth hiring. We start on the ground with beans and chalk, because the maths is easier to see than to read.
