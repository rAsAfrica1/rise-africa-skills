Guinea Fowl as Guard Birds and Pest Control

61 Guinea fowl as guard birds and pest control

Guinea fowl have two unique values most farmers never monetize: they alarm at intruders, and they eat ticks.

1. Guard birds

Guinea fowl screech loudly when strangers, snakes, or predators approach. Farmers use them as:

  • Early warning for theft in the compound
  • Snake alarm (they will attack snakes)
  • Predator alert - hawks, owls, cats
  • Night watch reinforcement

You can sell trained pairs to other farmers at 30-50% above normal live bird price. Market them as "natural security".

2. Tick and pest control

Guinea fowl eat ticks, grasshoppers, beetles, termites. Used in cattle farms, grain storage areas, vegetable gardens, orchards.

This is a service you can sell: rent a small flock for a week to a neighbouring farm.

3. Renting out guinea fowl

  • Farmers pay 10,000-30,000 FCFA per week per 5 birds
  • You keep ownership, they get pest control
  • Birds return to your farm at night

4. Selling to livestock keepers

Cattle, goat, and sheep farmers have a serious tick problem. Sell them guinea fowl directly: "Tick Control Flock - 5 birds" at 25,000-40,000 FCFA. Include care guide and 30 days support.

Practical tips: guinea fowl bond to a location and always come home. Provide grain at night to keep them returning. Never fully trust them near seedlings - they scratch and eat young plants.

A flock of 10 guinea fowl can generate multiple income streams: eggs, meat, pest-control rentals, breeding stock, and guard bird sales. All from the same birds.
