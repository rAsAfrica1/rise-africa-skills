Capstone: Meet Me, in Russian

Today you introduce yourself to another person in Russian, from hello to goodbye, with nothing written in front of you.

🎒 What you need

  • a friend, sibling or parent
  • ten minutes
  • the phone once, to check the audio

✋ Do this

  1. Learn the four lines. Meня зовут literally means 'they call me' — Russian does not say 'my name is'.
  2. Check the pronunciation once on the phone, especially зовут — za-VOOT, punch on the second half. Then put the phone away.
  3. Practise both halves with a sibling, swapping roles so you have asked as well as answered.
  4. Now do it for real with somebody who has not watched you practise. Greet them, ask their name, give yours, say очень приятно.
  5. Write the four lines out in Cyrillic on cardboard, by hand, and hang it up. Every letter on that card is one you can now read.
  6. Finish with до свидания. You have had a conversation in Russian.

💡 Why it works

An introduction is the one conversation you will repeat for the rest of your life in this language, so it is the right thing to own completely. Doing it once, live, without a script, is what turns four memorised lines into something you can actually use.

🔥 Challenge

Introduce yourself in Russian to somebody who has no idea you were learning it, and see how long it takes them to ask a question.
