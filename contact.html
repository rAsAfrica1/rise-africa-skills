Common questions

How do I enrol in a course?

Go to the course directory, choose a course, and follow the enrolment steps. For any difficulty, WhatsApp us and we will walk you through it.

Can I pay with mobile money?

Yes. We accept EcoCash, OneMoney, M-Pesa, Airtel Money, and card payments. WhatsApp us for the current payment options.

Do I get a certificate?

No. rAs is not a school, college, or university. We do not issue diplomas, degrees, or accredited qualifications. You get a Business Seed Record - a shareable document that is not an academic qualification.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes, within 14 days if you have completed less than 20 percent of the course. WhatsApp or email us to start the refund.

Can I use your content offline?

Yes. Print and download options start at 3.50 dollars. The Print/Download/Share package gives you a high-res PDF and a physical print option.

Are the kids courses and language courses free?

Yes. All rAs Kids courses (48 modules) and all language courses (Arabic, Chinese, Russian, Swahili, Shona, Spanish) are completely free.
