Talking to Claude — Junior

Module 7 of 8: What Will You Charge?

Real customers

🧠 Brain Opener — 60 seconds

Before anything else

Free is not a price. Free is a gift. Gifts are lovely and rare. Prices keep businesses alive. Learn to charge.

📚 Lessons

1. Cost + time + fair profit = price

Add what you spent. Add what your hour is worth. Add a small fair profit. That number is your starting price. You can adjust down — but you cannot adjust up from zero.

2. The 3-step drill

Round 1: list every cost (flour, sugar, packaging, transport). Round 2: estimate your time in hours and multiply by a fair hourly rate ($2–$5 for a beginner). Round 3: add 20% profit. That is the price.

3. Where it breaks

Pricing so high nobody buys, or so low you quit by month two. The middle wins. Test the price on 5 customers. Adjust after 10 sales.

🎯 Cross-Skill Links

♟️
Chess

Every move has a cost — a tempo, a pawn, a square. A trade is fair if both sides gain something they wanted. Price is the same: both sides must feel the trade is fair.

🎹
Music & Piano

A concert ticket has a price. Free concerts are practice. Learn the difference — and price your work like a concert, not a rehearsal.

🀄
Chinese-Style Drill

本 means cost. 利 means profit. 价 means price. Three characters, one formula: 本 + 利 = 价.

🤖 Real-Time AI-Era Skill

Ask Claude: "If I bought materials for $2 and spent 1 hour making bracelets, what should I charge? Show 3 pricing options with reasoning."

🎮 Game

Do it now

Price auction. Show 5 objects. Each person writes what they would charge. Reveal market prices. Closest wins.
