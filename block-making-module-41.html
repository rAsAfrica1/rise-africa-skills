Selling Online and by Phone

8 lessons · 30 quiz questions · Business

1 WhatsApp Business setup

WhatsApp is the #1 business tool in Africa. Set it up properly for maximum impact.

Setup steps:

  1. Download WhatsApp Business (free, separate from personal WhatsApp)
  2. Register with your business phone number
  3. Complete your business profile:
    • Business name (e.g. "Harare Block Makers")
    • Category (Construction / Building Materials)
    • Description — what you make, delivery area
    • Address, opening hours, email, website
  4. Add product catalog — up to 500 items with photo, name, price
  5. Set greeting message (for new customers)
  6. Set away message (out of hours)
  7. Set quick replies (common questions)

Your WhatsApp number becomes a marketing channel. Every customer, every enquiry, every delivery update — through one app.

💡 WhatsApp Business is free. There's no excuse not to have it. Do this on day one.

2 Product photos that sell

Blocks are boring to look at. Your photos must show them clearly and in context.

10 photos every block yard should have:

  1. Single dense block, clean background
  2. Single hollow block, top view (showing cores)
  3. Stack of blocks on pallet
  4. Delivery truck loaded with blocks
  5. Wall under construction (work in progress)
  6. Finished wall (multiple angles)
  7. Blocks in different colours side by side
  8. Close-up of block texture and edges
  9. Human scale — a person next to a block stack
  10. Your yard layout — production area, curing area

Photo tips:

  • Natural light — never flash
  • Clean background (not muddy yard, not clutter)
  • Take 20 shots, use best 5
  • Same angle for each block type (consistency builds trust)

3 Simple price list

A one-page price list is the most useful sales tool you can have.

What to include:

  • Business name, logo, phone, WhatsApp
  • Photo of each block type
  • Dimensions of each block
  • Price per block, per 1,000, per pallet
  • Delivery charges by zone or per km
  • Payment methods accepted
  • Any current promotion
  • Date price list was issued (prices change!)

Print 200 copies. Hand out at every sales call, trade show, and site visit.

Also make a digital version: A single image (PNG or PDF) that you can send on WhatsApp in one tap. Customers love receiving price lists as images.

4 Taking orders by phone

Phone orders are convenient but require discipline — always get the details right.

Order intake checklist:

  • Customer name and phone number
  • Delivery address (or collection)
  • Block type and quantity
  • Preferred delivery date and time
  • Total price agreed
  • Payment method (cash on delivery, bank transfer, mobile money)
  • Any special instructions (gate code, site access, etc.)

Confirm the order back to the customer in writing via WhatsApp. This prevents disputes.

Use a simple order book or spreadsheet to track all orders. Never rely on memory.

5 Payment options (EcoCash, card)

Offer as many payment options as possible. Each one removes a barrier to purchase.

Payment methods to support:

  • Cash on delivery: Standard. 2% discount to encourage.
  • Mobile money: EcoCash (Zimbabwe), M-Pesa (Kenya, Tanzania), Airtel Money. Instant, low fee, no chargebacks.
  • Bank transfer: For large orders. Use business account, not personal.
  • Card (Stripe, PayPal): For international or card-paying customers. Higher fees (2.9%+).
  • Layby: Pay weekly, receive when fully paid.
  • POS machine: For card payments on-site at delivery.

Display accepted methods clearly on your price list and yard signage.

⚠ Never accept payment to a personal phone number or account. Use business accounts only — it protects you legally and tax-wise.

6 Delivery scheduling

Efficient delivery saves fuel and time.

Scheduling principles:

  • Batch deliveries by area: 3 deliveries in one direction beats 3 scattered deliveries
  • Fixed delivery days: "We deliver to [area] on Tuesdays and Fridays."
  • Time slots: Morning (7–12), afternoon (13–17). Avoid lunchtime and after 17:00.
  • Confirm 24 hours before: WhatsApp customer the day before
  • Confirm on the day: A 30-minute "on my way" message reduces failed deliveries

Delivery tools:

  • Simple WhatsApp group for your delivery driver and yard
  • Paper delivery schedule on the wall
  • Google Maps for route planning

Failed deliveries (customer not home) cost fuel and time. Confirm twice.

7 Handling returns

Blocks are rarely returned, but sometimes customers complain about quality or quantity.

Return policy (publish it):

  • Returns accepted within 7 days of delivery
  • Blocks must be unused and undamaged
  • Customer pays return transport (except if our fault)
  • If our fault — full refund or replacement
  • If customer error — refund minus 10% restocking fee

What to do with returned blocks:

  • Inspect — if first-grade, restock
  • If damaged — use as fill or crush for aggregate
  • Never resell second-grade as first

Returns are rare if quality is good. Don't fear them — handle them well and customers trust you more.

8 Building reviews

Reviews build trust faster than any advertising.

Where to collect reviews:

  • Google Business Profile (free — big SEO value)
  • Facebook page reviews
  • WhatsApp testimonials (screenshot and share)
  • Instagram mentions

How to ask: After every delivery, message: "Thanks for your order! If you're happy with the blocks, could you leave us a review on Google? Here's the link."

Incentive: "Leave a review, get 2% off your next order." Costs little, drives reviews.

Respond to all reviews:

  • Positive: thank them, mention the project
  • Negative: apologise, offer to fix, take it offline
  • Never argue publicly

10 good reviews is worth more than any brochure. Aim for 50 within a year.

💰 Online selling is high-margin, low-cost, and reaches customers you'd never meet otherwise. WhatsApp Business + Google Business Profile + a good price list = a complete digital sales operation for under $100.
