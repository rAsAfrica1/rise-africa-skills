Talking to Claude — Junior

Module 1 of 8: Fat Questions and Thin Questions

No device needed

🧠 Brain Opener — 60 seconds

Before anything else

Two children meet a genie. The first says, "Make me rich." The genie laughs. The second says, "Show me how to sell 20 mangoes before Sunday at the market." The genie listens. Same wish — different size. Which question would you rather ask?

📚 Lessons

1. A thin question gets a thin answer

"Tell me about farming" is thin. There is nothing to hold. "Give me three crops I can plant in November in Zimbabwe with $5" is fat. It names a place, a month, a budget, a number. Fat questions force a real answer.

2. The 3-part drill

Round 1: take a fat question and cut it down until it becomes thin. Round 2: take a thin question and fatten it by adding WHO, WHAT, WHEN. Round 3: invent a fat question about something you actually want.

3. Where fat questions break

A fat question about the wrong thing is still wasted. Before you ask, ask yourself: "If I got the perfect answer, would I use it today?" If no — the question is not fat enough yet.

🎯 Cross-Skill Links

♟️
Chess

A thin move is "develop a piece." A fat move is "develop the knight to f3 to control d4 and prepare castling." Chess players think in fat moves. Askers win the same way.

🎹
Music & Piano

A thin note is just a pitch. A fat note has rhythm, articulation, and volume. Same key, different meaning. Fatten your questions the same way you would fatten a phrase in music.

🀄
Chinese-Style Drill

三字诀 — three characters that carry a whole sentence. Fat questions follow the same pattern: 谁-什么-何时 (who-what-when). Say it like a Chinese phrase: subject-verb-object, tight and complete.

🤖 Real-Time AI-Era Skill

Ask Claude a thin question. Screenshot the answer. Now ask the same thing as a fat question. Compare. The fat answer is the one you can use today.

🎮 Game

Do it now

Question auction. Five questions on the board. Everyone bids (in paper clips) on which will give the most useful answer. Reveal the actual answers. Whoever bid highest on the winner keeps the clips.
