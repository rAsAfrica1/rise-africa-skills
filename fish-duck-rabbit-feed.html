What You'll Learn

Master high-protein fish feeds (32-35% protein for catfish/tilapia), duck feeds (16%), and rabbit feeds (16%). These specialty segments command premium pricing and serve growing aquaculture operations. Fish fingerlings: 1,000-fingerling ponds generate $5,000-8,000 profit per cycle.

Learn to formulate feeds that optimize feed conversion ratios (FCR), reduce waste, and maximize fish/animal growth rates. Premium specialty feeds retail at $1.50-2.50/kg vs standard livestock feeds at $0.80-1.50/kg.
