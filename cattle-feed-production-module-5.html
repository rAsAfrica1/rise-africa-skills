Commercial Facility Requirements

  • Space: 100-200 sq meters (processing + storage + office)
  • Power: 3-phase, 20+ amp electrical connection
  • Water: Clean water supply for testing & cleaning
  • Licenses: Feed production license, food safety certification
  • Staff: 2-4 full-time employees minimum
  • Transportation: Vehicle for bulk deliveries (truck/van)
