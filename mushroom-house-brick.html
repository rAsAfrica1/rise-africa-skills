How to work through this course

  1. Read the module end to end. Do not skip the worked calculations — they are the point.
  2. Complete the assignment at the end of each module in writing. The numbers must be your numbers, not the examples.
  3. Build, measure or test at least one thing from each module before moving on.
  4. Keep all your assignment answers in one folder. By Module 12 you will have a complete design document for your own operation.
