Fish Feed Production

Fish feed is 60-70% of the cost of fish farming. Commercial pellets are expensive - often more than the market value of the fish they produce. This course teaches you how to make your own floating and sinking feed using local ingredients, at half the cost, with the same growth results.

6 modules · practical exercises · quiz at the end of every module · free to read
How to use this course: Read one module, then do its practical exercises before moving on. Real costs, real yields, real market prices.

What you will learn

  • What tilapia and catfish need at every stage of growth
  • Which local ingredients replace expensive fish meal without losing growth
  • How to formulate rations with the Pearson square
  • How to make both sinking and floating pellets on a small farm
  • How to feed for the best FCR and lowest cost per kg of fish
  • How to dry, store, and preserve your own fish feed

Who this course is for

  • Fish farmers currently buying commercial pellets who want to cut costs
  • Smallholders starting a pond who need to plan feed correctly
  • Anyone with access to cheap local by-products - rice bran, brewery waste, soya, cassava
  • Extension officers teaching integrated fish farming
  • Agripreneurs making feed for their own ponds and for sale

What you need

  • Access to at least 3 of the main ingredients - fish meal, soya, maize, rice bran, cassava
  • A scale for weighing (5-50 kg)
  • A grinder or hammer mill (or access to one)
  • A mixing area and drying trays or racks
  • A notebook for recording formulas, prices, and results
