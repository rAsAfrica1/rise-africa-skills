1. Counting to 100 and Beyond for Bulk Sales

In this lesson, you will learn to count in Spanish from 1 to 100 and beyond, which is essential for quoting prices, discussing quantities, and negotiating bulk sales. Start with numbers 1-20: uno, dos, tres, cuatro, cinco, seis, siete, ocho, nueve, diez, once, doce, trece, catorce, quince, diecisis, diecisiete, dieciocho, diecinueve, veinte. Then, learn tens: treinta, cuarenta, cincuenta, sesenta, setenta, ochenta, noventa, cien. For numbers between 21 and 29, combine veinte with y and the unit: veintiuno, veintids, etc. For 31 and above, use tens + y + unit: treinta y uno, cuarenta y dos, etc. For hundreds: doscientos, trescientos, cuatrocientos, quinientos, seiscientos, setecientos, ochocientos, novecientos. Mil is 1,000. For example, 250 is doscientos cincuenta. Practice with real prices: A sack of maize might cost 50,000 shillings in Uganda. In Spanish: cincuenta mil chelines. If you sell 100 bags at 2,000 shillings each, total is 200,000 shillings: doscientos mil chelines. When buying in bulk, you might ask: Cunto por 50 unidades? (How much for 50 units?). If each unit is 3,500 shillings, total is 175,000: ciento setenta y cinco mil. Remember that in many African countries, prices are often in thousands or millions due to currency values. For instance, in Nigeria, 1,000 naira is mil nairas. Practice counting money: 500 is quinientos, 1,500 is mil quinientos. Also learn to say 'half' (medio) and 'quarter' (cuarto) for fractions. For bulk sales, you might say: 'Vendo diez cajas por cien mil chelines' (I sell ten boxes for one hundred thousand shillings). Use these numbers daily when at the market or negotiating with suppliers. Write down prices in Spanish to reinforce. With practice, you'll handle large numbers confidently.

2. Talking About Costs, Profits, and Losses

To run a business in Spanish-speaking contexts, you must discuss costs, profits, and losses. Key vocabulary: costo (cost), precio (price), ganancia (profit), prdida (loss), ingreso (income), gasto (expense). Start with basic sentences: 'El costo de produccin es alto' (The production cost is high). 'Vendo a un precio de diez mil chelines' (I sell at a price of ten thousand shillings). To calculate profit: ganancia = ingreso total - costo total. For example, if you buy a product for 5,000 shillings and sell for 8,000, your ganancia is 3,000: 'Mi ganancia es tres mil chelines'. If you sell for less than cost, you have a prdida: 'Tengo una prdida de dos mil chelines'. Discuss percentages: 'El margen de ganancia es del 20 por ciento' (The profit margin is 20 percent). In many African markets, bargaining affects costs. You might say: 'Necesito reducir costos para aumentar ganancias' (I need to reduce costs to increase profits). Track expenses: 'Gasto mucho en transporte' (I spend a lot on transport). Use numbers: 'Mis ingresos mensuales son 500,000 chelines' (My monthly income is 500,000 shillings). Compare: 'Este mes tuve ms ganancias que el mes pasado' (This month I had more profits than last month). For losses: 'Perd 100,000 chelines en la ltima venta' (I lost 100,000 shillings in the last sale). Also learn to ask: 'Cul es el costo total?' (What is the total cost?). Practice with real scenarios: If you sell 200 bags of rice at 15,000 each, total ingreso is 3,000,000. If cost per bag is 12,000, total costo is 2,400,000, so ganancia is 600,000. In Spanish: 'La ganancia es seiscientos mil chelines'. Use these phrases in your daily business to communicate clearly with Spanish-speaking partners or customers.

3. Negotiating Prices with Suppliers

Negotiating prices in Spanish requires polite but firm language. Start with greetings: 'Buenos das, cunto cuesta esto?' (Good morning, how much does this cost?). To ask for a discount: 'Puede darme un descuento?' (Can you give me a discount?). If the price is too high: 'Es muy caro, puede bajar el precio?' (It's too expensive, can you lower the price?). Common responses: 'Le puedo ofrecer un 10% de descuento' (I can offer you a 10% discount). Or 'El precio es fijo' (The price is fixed). In African markets, negotiation is expected. Use phrases: 'Si compro al por mayor, me hace un precio especial?' (If I buy wholesale, will you give me a special price?). For bulk: 'Compro 100 unidades, cul es su mejor precio?' (I'm buying 100 units, what's your best price?). Counter-offers: 'Le ofrezco 8,000 chelines por unidad' (I offer you 8,000 shillings per unit). If they refuse: 'Qu tal 8,500?' (How about 8,500?). Use numbers fluently. For example, supplier says 10,000, you say 'Demasiado. Puede hacer 7,500?' (Too much. Can you do 7,500?). If they agree: 'Trato hecho' (Deal done). If not, walk away politely: 'Gracias, lo pensar' (Thanks, I'll think about it). Learn to discuss payment terms: 'Puedo pagar en dos plazos?' (Can I pay in two installments?). Or 'Pago al contado si me da descuento' (I'll pay cash if you give me a discount). In many African countries, mobile money is common: 'Acepta pago por mvil?' (Do you accept mobile money?). Practice role-plays with a friend. Remember to be respectful: use 'por favor' and 'gracias'. Negotiation is about building relationships, so start with small talk. With these phrases, you can secure better prices and improve your profit margins.

4. Discussing Payment Methods: Cash, Mobile Money, Cards

In Spanish, discussing payment methods is crucial for transactions. Key terms: efectivo (cash), dinero mvil (mobile money), tarjeta de crdito/dbito (credit/debit card), transferencia bancaria (bank transfer). Start with questions: 'Cmo prefiere pagar?' (How do you prefer to pay?). If cash: 'Pago en efectivo' (I pay in cash). For mobile money: 'Acepta M-Pesa?' (Do you accept M-Pesa?) or 'Puedo pagar con dinero mvil' (I can pay with mobile money). In many African countries, mobile money like M-Pesa, Airtel Money, or Orange Money is dominant. Learn to say: 'Enve el dinero a este nmero' (Send the money to this number). For cards: 'Aceptan tarjetas?' (Do you accept cards?). If yes: 'Pago con tarjeta' (I pay by card). For bank transfers: 'Hago una transferencia bancaria' (I make a bank transfer). Discuss fees: 'Hay algn cargo por el pago?' (Is there a fee for the payment?). For cash on delivery: 'Pago contra entrega' (Cash on delivery). In negotiations, you might say: 'Si pago en efectivo, me da un descuento?' (If I pay cash, will you give me a discount?). Or 'El dinero mvil es ms rpido' (Mobile money is faster). Learn to confirm: 'Recibi el pago?' (Did you receive the payment?). For receipts: 'Puede darme un recibo?' (Can you give me a receipt?). Practice with real scenarios: You buy goods worth 50,000 shillings. You say: 'Voy a pagar con M-Pesa. Cul es su nmero?' (I'll pay with M-Pesa. What's your number?). Then you send and show confirmation: 'Aqu est la confirmacin' (Here is the confirmation). If using card: 'Tienen terminal POS?' (Do you have a POS terminal?). Also learn to discuss payment terms: 'Pago la mitad ahora y la mitad despus' (I pay half now and half later). With these phrases, you can handle any payment situation confidently.

5. Invoicing and Receipts Vocabulary

Invoicing and receipts are essential for record-keeping and legal compliance. In Spanish, key terms: factura (invoice), recibo (receipt), comprobante (proof of payment), cliente (client), vendedor (seller), fecha (date), cantidad (quantity), descripcin (description), precio unitario (unit price), total (total), subtotal (subtotal), impuesto (tax), IVA (VAT), descuento (discount), nmero de factura (invoice number). A simple invoice includes: header with your business name and contact, client details, invoice number, date, list of items with quantities and prices, subtotal, tax, total, and payment terms. For example: 'Factura N 001. Fecha: 15 de mayo de 2025. Cliente: Juan Prez. Descripcin: 10 sacos de arroz. Precio unitario: 20,000 chelines. Subtotal: 200,000. IVA (18%): 36,000. Total: 236,000 chelines. Condiciones de pago: Pago a 30 das.' For a receipt: 'Recibo de pago. Recib de Mara Lpez la cantidad de 50,000 chelines por concepto de transporte. Fecha: 10 de mayo de 2025. Firma: ______.' Learn to ask for: 'Me puede dar una factura?' (Can you give me an invoice?). Or 'Necesito un recibo por este pago' (I need a receipt for this payment). When issuing: 'Aqu tiene su factura' (Here is your invoice). If there's an error: 'Hay un error en la factura, por favor corrjalo' (There's an error in the invoice, please correct it). Discuss payment terms: 'El pago vence el 30 de junio' (Payment is due on June 30). For taxes: 'El IVA est incluido' (VAT is included). Practice creating a simple invoice in Spanish. Use these templates in your business to maintain professionalism and avoid disputes. Keep copies for your records. In many African countries, formal invoices are required for tax purposes, so mastering this vocabulary is vital.

6. Practice: Create a Simple Invoice in Spanish

Now, let's practice creating a simple invoice in Spanish. Follow this step-by-step guide. First, gather your business information: name, address, phone, email, and tax ID if applicable. Then, client information: name, address, and contact. Next, assign an invoice number and date. List items sold: description, quantity, unit price, and total per item. Calculate subtotal, apply tax (e.g., IVA at 18% in many countries), subtract any discount, and get the final total. Add payment terms: due date, accepted payment methods (efectivo, dinero mvil, tarjeta). Finally, include a thank-you note. Here's a template in Spanish:

FACTURA
[Nombre de tu negocio]
[Direccin]
[Telfono]
[Correo electrnico]
[Nmero de identificacin fiscal]

Factura N: 001
Fecha: 20 de mayo de 2025
Cliente: [Nombre del cliente]
Direccin: [Direccin del cliente]

Descripcin | Cantidad | Precio Unitario | Total
Sacos de maz | 20 | 15,000 chelines | 300,000 chelines
Aceite de cocina | 10 | 8,000 chelines | 80,000 chelines

Subtotal: 380,000 chelines
IVA (18%): 68,400 chelines
Descuento: 10,000 chelines
Total a pagar: 438,400 chelines

Condiciones de pago: Pago a 15 das. Aceptamos efectivo, M-Pesa, y transferencia bancaria.
Gracias por su compra.

Now, practice with your own products. Use real numbers from your business. For example, if you sell 5 bags of rice at 25,000 each, that's 125,000. Add IVA if applicable. Write the invoice in Spanish. Then, create a receipt for a cash payment: 'Recibo. Recib de [Cliente] la cantidad de [monto] por [concepto]. Fecha: [fecha]. Firma: [tu firma].' Practice filling in the blanks. Use these templates in your daily transactions. If you need to issue an invoice to a Spanish-speaking client, you'll be prepared. Also, learn to check invoices you receive: verify quantities, prices, and totals. If there's a mistake, politely ask for correction: 'Hay un error en el total, puede revisarlo?' (There's an error in the total, can you check it?). With this practice, you can handle invoicing confidently.

As intra-African trade grows under the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA), Spanish-language financial literacy becomes a competitive advantage for agribusinesses dealing with Spanish-speaking partners, buyers, and financial institutions.</p>
