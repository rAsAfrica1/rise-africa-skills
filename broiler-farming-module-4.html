4. Feeding for Weight and FCR

Feed is 70-75% of your broiler cost. Every point of FCR you improve is direct profit.

Lesson 4.2 - FCR and cost per kg

FCR = kg feed consumed / kg live weight gained.

FCRVerdictFeed cost/kg live at 0.55/kg
1.6-1.8Excellent0.88-0.99
1.8-2.0Good0.99-1.10
2.0-2.2Acceptable1.10-1.21
2.2-2.5Poor1.21-1.38
Above 2.5Not profitableAbove 1.38
The FCR rule: Cutting FCR from 2.2 to 1.9 saves 0.17 per kg live weight. On 200 birds x 2 kg that is 68 per batch.

Lesson 4.3 - Reducing feed waste

  • Fill feeders to 2/3, not full. Birds scatter feed when overfilled.
  • Raise feeder lip as birds grow. Level with the back.
  • Use nipple drinkers. Wet feed is thrown away.
  • Store feed sealed, off the ground, dry.
  • Feed at the same time daily.
  • Do not overstock. Crowded birds scratch feed onto litter.
