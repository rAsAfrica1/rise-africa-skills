6. Health and Selling

A healthy goat flock is a profitable flock. Learn the five diseases that matter and the buyers who pay best.

Module 6 of 6 · Goat Farming for Profit

The five diseases that kill goats

DiseaseSignsPrevention
Peste des Petits Ruminants (PPR)Fever, discharge, mouth sores, diarrhoea, sudden deathAnnual vaccine
Enterotoxaemia (pulpy kidney)Sudden death, especially after feed changeVaccinate does 4 weeks before kidding, kids at 6 weeks
Haemonchus (barber's pole worm)Pale gums, bottle jaw, weight lossRotational grazing, deworm every 3 months
CCPP (contagious caprine pleuropneumonia)Coughing, laboured breathing, feverVaccinate, quarantine new stock
Orf (sore mouth)Scabs around mouth, noseVaccinate, isolate affected

Routine health schedule

FrequencyTask
DailyCheck for limping, cough, off-feed animals
WeeklyHoof check, weigh kids, check for worms
MonthlyBody condition score all adults
Every 3 monthsDeworm (rotate active ingredients)
AnnuallyVaccinate against PPR, CCPP
Pre-kiddingVaccinate against enterotoxaemia

Selling

BuyerVolumePricePayment
Local marketMediumMarket rateCash
ButchersWeekly+10-20%Weekly or cash
Restaurants/hotelsWeekly+20-40%Weekly/monthly
Festive season (Christmas, Eid)High+40-100%Cash
Breeders (registered stock)Low+100-300%Cash

Time to sell

  • Meat goats: 8-10 months, 30-40 kg live weight.
  • Festive season: hold bucks/kids for December or Eid.
  • Breeding stock: 12-18 months for registered does and bucks.
  • Cull does: after 5-6 years, or when teeth go.

Records

RecordWhy
Breeding datesPredict kidding, plan feed
Birth weightsTrack growth and survival
Weights at 3, 6, 9 monthsIdentify best-performing lines
Deaths and causeSpot health patterns
Feed and costCalculate margin per goat
SalesTrack price by month
The dual-purpose rule: Keep your best does as mothers. Their daughters become your next generation of profit.
Practical exercise 6: Start a goat record book this week. Weigh 5 kids every month until sale.
