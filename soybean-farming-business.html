1. The Business Idea

You are training to run one of these real businesses around soybean:

  • Raw soybean production — grow, dry, sell to feed mills and aggregators
  • Seed multiplication — certified soybean seed at 2–3x food price
  • Soya flour production — mill, package, retail (2–4x raw)
  • Soya chunks (TVP) — extruder-based, 3–6x raw
  • Soybean oil + cake — press, sell oil and feed cake

2. Three Capital Tiers

Tier 1 — Smallholder Producer

– ,500

0.5–1 hectare. Certified seed, inoculant, DAP, basic pest programme. Sell raw to a feed mill or aggregator. Learn the crop.

Tier 2 — Commercial Grain Producer

,000 – ,000

2–4 hectares. Full rust/pod borer programme. Contract with a feed mill. Optional seed multiplication block. Hermetic storage for off-season sales.

Tier 3 — Value-Added Soy Enterprise

,000+

5+ hectares or contract farming. Small processing unit for soya flour, chunks, milk or oil. Direct consumer sales or supply to food processors. 3–8 staff.

Rule: start at the tier your market can support. Soybean volume sells; but food-grade and seed sell at 2–3x.

Master one rung, then the next. Soya flour is the easiest first step — a small mill and packaging is all it takes.

4. Buyers You Will Approach

  • Feed mills — 5–50 t lots, low moisture, protein spec
  • Local market vendors — daily small lots, cash
  • Soya food processors — food-grade, 5–20 t lots
  • Oil millers — bulk, oil content spec
  • Seed distributors — certified seed at 2–3x
  • Export aggregators — 20 t containers
Rule: name three buyers before you plant. Soybean is easy to sell but the price gap between channels is 2–3x — pick the right channel.
