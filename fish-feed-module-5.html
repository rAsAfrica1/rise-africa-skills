5. Feeding Systems and FCR

Correct feeding is not just 'give them enough'. It is about rate, frequency, and adjusting to water temperature and fish behaviour.

Module 5 of 6 · Fish Feed Production · quiz at the end

Lesson 5.1 - Feeding rate by fish size

Fish sizeFeeding rate (% body weight/day)Meals/day
Fry (0.01-1 g)15-20%4-6
Fingerling (1-20 g)8-10%3-4
Juvenile (20-100 g)5-6%3
Grow-out (100-500 g)3-4%2-3
Catfish (500 g+)2-3%2

To calculate the daily ration: sample 10-20 fish every 2 weeks, weigh them, average, multiply by number of fish in pond, then multiply by the feeding rate.

Lesson 5.2 - Sample calculation

Pond has 500 tilapia at average 80 g.

  1. Total biomass: 500 × 80 g = 40,000 g = 40 kg
  2. Feeding rate at 80 g: 5%
  3. Daily feed: 40 kg × 5% = 2 kg
  4. Split into 3 meals: 670 g per meal

Adjust every 2 weeks as the fish grow.

Lesson 5.3 - Feeding frequency

StageMeals/dayBest times
Fry4-6Every 3 hours during daylight
Fingerling3-48am, 11am, 2pm, 5pm
Juvenile38am, 12pm, 5pm
Grow-out2-38am, 12pm, 5pm

Fish feed better in the cooler parts of the day. Avoid feeding during the hottest hours of midday in very hot climates.

Lesson 5.4 - Reading fish behaviour

  • Fish come to surface fast, eat within 5 minutes: correct feeding rate. Continue.
  • Fish ignore feed, sink to bottom: overfeeding, or water quality problem, or disease. Reduce feed 50% for 2 days and test water.
  • Fish gasping at surface, especially morning: low oxygen. Reduce feed, aerate, or add fresh water.
  • Feed left over after 15 minutes: overfeeding. Reduce amount next time.
  • Fish swimming erratically, rubbing: parasite or disease. Check water and treat.

Lesson 5.5 - FCR monitoring

Keep a simple pond log:

DateFeed given (kg)Sample weight (g)Number of fishBiomass (kg)Daily ration (kg)

Every 2 weeks:

  1. Sample and weigh 20 fish.
  2. Multiply average weight by number in pond = biomass.
  3. Feed given since last sample ÷ biomass gain = FCR.
  4. Adjust the next 2 weeks based on the FCR result.

Lesson 5.6 - Water quality and feeding

Feed and water interact. Bad water = fish won't eat, and uneaten feed fouls the water.

ParameterIdeal for tilapiaIdeal for catfish
Water temperature25-30°C25-30°C
Dissolved oxygenAbove 4 mg/LAbove 4 mg/L
pH6.5-8.56.5-8.5
Ammonia (NH3)Below 0.05 mg/LBelow 0.05 mg/L
Water clarityGreen (30-40 cm visibility)Green (30-40 cm visibility)

Test water weekly. Ammonia is the biggest killer of fish in ponds - it comes from too much uneaten feed and fish waste.

The feeding rule: Feed twice a day, at fixed times, at the correct rate. Fish that are overfed grow slower than fish fed correctly - the excess feed rots and poisons the water.
Practical exercise 5.1: Sample 20 fish today. Calculate your biomass and daily ration. Feed for 1 week at that rate. Sample again and calculate FCR.
Practical exercise 5.2: Test your pond water for pH and ammonia. Fix any issue before adjusting feed.
