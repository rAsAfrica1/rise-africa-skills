🐕🐈 Dog and Cat Feed Formulation

Formulating complete, balanced diets for dogs and cats across every life stage, including therapeutic and alternative-protein diets.

What this course covers

12 modules · 72 written lessons · about 53,690 words · 360 quiz questions · 12 capstone projects.

Every lesson has learning objectives, worked numbers, a "do this today" task and a knowledge check. Videos are free material made by other people; the written lessons are the course.
