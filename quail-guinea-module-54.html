Feathers and By-Products

54 Feathers and by-products

Feathers, eggshells, and bones are usually thrown away. Each can become a small income stream.

Feathers:

  • Craft supplies - washed, dried, sorted by color. Sell to crafters, hat makers, fly-tying fishermen. $5-20 per bag.
  • Fishing flies - guinea fowl feathers are prized for trout flies. Sell direct to fly shops and online.
  • Decorative items - earrings, hair clips, wall art. Partner with local artisans.
  • Feather meal - grind and sell as protein supplement for animal feed (10% of bird weight = feathers).

Eggshells:

  • Calcium supplement - dry, grind to powder, sell to other poultry farmers or as garden lime.
  • Soil amendment - tomatoes and peppers love crushed eggshells. Sell in 1kg bags.
  • Artisan crafts - mosaic art, jewelry, decorative planters.

Bones and offal:

  • Bone meal - dry, grind, sell as fertilizer or feed supplement.
  • Pet food - cooked bones and organs sell to dog and cat owners.
  • Soup stock - bones make excellent stock. Bottle and sell to local cooks.
By-products turn waste into cash. Even 500 FCFA/day adds up to 180,000 FCFA/year - enough to buy more breeding stock.
