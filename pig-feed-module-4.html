4. Mixing, Storage and Preservation

A correctly formulated ration is useless if it spoils, separates, or grows mould. This module teaches you to mix and store feed safely.

Module 4 of 6 · Pig Feed Production · quiz at the end

Lesson 4.1 - Grinding

Pigs cannot digest whole grains. Every ingredient must be ground to the right particle size.

StageParticle sizeScreen size
StarterFine (0.5-0.8 mm)3 mm
GrowerMedium (0.8-1.2 mm)4 mm
FinisherMedium-coarse (1.0-1.5 mm)5 mm
SowCoarse (1.2-2.0 mm)6 mm

Too fine = dust and ulcers. Too coarse = poor digestion and wasted feed.

Lesson 4.2 - Mixing

  1. Weigh every ingredient into separate buckets or basins.
  2. Layer the ingredients in a wide, flat pile - biggest quantity at the bottom.
  3. Turn the pile 3 times with a shovel, mixing thoroughly each time.
  4. Or use a cement mixer for batches over 100 kg - 5 minutes per batch.
  5. Never mix with a bucket for a full ration - the small ingredients (premix, salt) settle at the bottom.

Lesson 4.3 - Storage

RiskCausePrevention
Mould / aflatoxinDamp feed, humid storeStore dry, on pallets, off walls. 12% moisture maximum.
Rat and insect damagePoor store hygieneSealed sacks, bait stations, cats
Vitamin lossHeat, sunlight, ageUse within 4-6 weeks. Store in the dark.
SeparationVibration, transportRe-mix before feeding. Do not transport long distances
Rancid fatsOld oilseed cakesBuy fresh. Use within 8 weeks.

Store rules:

  • Use within 4 weeks for best quality.
  • Never store feed directly on the ground - use pallets.
  • Keep the store dry, dark, and ventilated.
  • Record date mixed on each sack.
  • Oldest feed is used first.

Lesson 4.4 - Wet feeding vs dry feeding

MethodProsConsBest for
Dry feedingSimple, less spoilage, easier automationMore dust, slower intakeCommercial operations, dry feed
Wet feeding (1:2.5 water)Better FCR (5-10%), faster growth, less wasteMust be eaten within 2 hours or spoilsSmallholder farms with time to mix daily
Fermented wet feedBetter gut health, cheaper (uses waste), probiotic effectRequires daily routine, less consistentFarms using kitchen waste, brewery grains

Wet feed rules:

  • Mix only what will be eaten in one meal.
  • Feed at the same time daily.
  • Clean troughs between meals - leftover wet feed breeds bacteria.
  • Never feed wet feed that has been standing over 4 hours in heat.

Lesson 4.5 - Kitchen and brewery waste

SourceProtein %Max in rationNotes
Brewery grains (wet)20-25%15-25%Use fresh (3 days max) or dry
Restaurant / kitchen wasteVariableUp to 30%Must be boiled 30 min. Never raw - disease risk
Vegetable market waste2-4%Up to 20%Adds vitamins. Wash, chop.
Fruit waste1-3%Up to 15%High sugar. Balance with protein.
Dairy waste (whey, skim)3-13%Up to 30%Good protein. Must be fresh.
Never feed raw restaurant or kitchen waste. It carries African Swine Fever and other deadly pig diseases. Boil for 30 minutes, cool, then feed.
Practical exercise 4.1: Mix a 50 kg ration on a flat surface. Turn it 3 times. Check for uniform colour and texture before feeding.
Practical exercise 4.2: Set up your feed store with pallets, off the wall. Record the date on every sack.
