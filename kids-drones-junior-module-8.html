Kids Drones — Junior

Module 8 of 8: A Drone Business — What Would Someone Pay For?

Real customers

🧠 Brain Opener — 60 seconds

Before anything else

Three people in your town want something you could do with a drone. Who are they? Write three names or types of person. Do not name drone pilots — name people who need a picture.

📚 Lessons

1. Three services that pay today

(1) Farm mapping — farmers pay to see which parts of their field are dry or sick. $20–$100 per job. (2) Event photography — weddings, church events, football matches. $30–$150 per hour. (3) Real-estate photos — sellers pay to show their land or house from above. $20–$80 per photo set. All three work in Africa today. All three need an adult pilot with a licence, plus a child who can frame the shot and pitch the service.

2. The 3-part drill

Round 1: pick one customer type — a farmer, a wedding party, a house seller. Round 2: name exactly what they want ("photos of my whole farm, so I can see which rows need water"). Round 3: name your price and what you deliver ("20 photos of the farm from 30 metres up, delivered in 2 days, $40"). That three-part sentence is your entire business.

3. Where the idea breaks

Drone flying is regulated in every country. In most of Africa you need a permit to fly commercially, and rules vary between countries. Before you sell one job, check with the civil aviation authority in your country. Ask a real adult to help. The rules are not there to stop you — they are there so you fly safely and legally.

🎯 Cross-Skill Links

♟️
Chess

In the endgame, you stop planning and start converting. You already have the material advantage; now you play simply and carefully and win. A first paid job is the same — you have trained, now deliver exactly what you promised.

🎹
Music & Piano

The performance is what you sell. Practice is private; the concert is public. A drone job is the same — the flight is the performance, the customer is the audience, the photo is the program.

🀄
Chinese-Style Drill

生意 (shēngyì) = business. 生 (life) + 意 (meaning) — business is the business of living.

🤖 Real-Time AI-Era Skill

Ask Claude: "Write a 30-second pitch for a 12-year-old offering drone photography to a wedding party in Zimbabwe. Include price, what is delivered, and one reassurance about safety." Rehearse the pitch aloud. Then change it to sound like you.

🎮 Game

Do it now

Pitch practice. In 30 seconds, pitch a drone service to the class. They vote on: (1) Would they pay for this? (2) Is the price fair? (3) Did the pitch name exactly what they get? The highest-scoring pitch gets a paper trophy and one homework pass — the start of every business is a good pitch.
