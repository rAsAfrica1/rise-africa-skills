Module 7 of 8: Drawing with AI

Real customers

🧠 Brain Opener — 60 seconds

Before anything else

Ask yourself before reading: what would change if you already understood "Drawing with AI"? Write one sentence. Check at the end.

📚 Lessons

1. The idea in one picture

Draw "Drawing with AI" — even badly. Images stick 6× longer than sentences.

2. Three-round drill

Round 1: copy an example exactly. Round 2: change one thing. Round 3: invent your own. Repetition → variation → creation.

3. Where it breaks

Every skill has a failure mode. Find it on purpose. If it cannot break, you do not understand it yet.
