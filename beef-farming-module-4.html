4. Feeding Cattle for Growth

Cattle eat a lot. Feed is 60-70% of beef production cost. Here is how to feed for growth at the lowest cost.

Module 4 of 12 · Beef Farming for Profit

The four feed types

Pasture / roughageGrass, hay, silage, crop residues60-90% of diet - fibre and rumen health
Energy concentrateMaize, barley, sorghum, molasses10-40% - weight gain
Protein concentrateSoya, cottonseed, sunflower cake, urea5-20% - muscle growth

Creep feeding calves

Creep feed = supplement for calves only. Speeds growth and reduces weaning stress.

  • Start at 3-4 weeks old.
  • 18-20% protein, high energy, very palatable.
  • Feed free-choice in a creep feeder calves can access but cows cannot.
  • Target: 1-2 kg per calf per day by weaning.
  • Weaners that were creep-fed gain 20-30 kg more by 12 months.

Finishing cattle (feedlot)

Finishing = feeding high-energy ration to reach market weight and fat cover. Duration: 90-150 days.

DayConcentrate %Roughage %
Days 1-7 (adaptation)30%70%
Days 8-1450%50%
Days 15-2170%30%
Days 22+80-85%15-20%
Never jump straight to a high-concentrate ration. Cattle need 2-3 weeks to adapt. Abrupt changes cause acidosis and death.

Silage crops: maize, sorghum, Napier grass, forage legumes. Chop at 2-3 cm, pack tightly, seal air out.

Always provide a free-choice mineral lick. Cheap prevention beats expensive treatment.

Water quality

  • Clean water available 24 hours/day.
  • Adult cow drinks 40-60 L per day, lactating cow 60-80 L.
  • In hot weather, intake doubles.
  • Clean troughs weekly - algae discourages drinking.
  • Water temperature below 25 C is ideal; above 30 C reduces intake.

Body condition scoring (BCS)

ScoreDescriptionTarget for
1Very thin, bones visibleEmergency only
2Thin, ribs and hip bones visibleGrowing heifers
3Ideal, ribs felt but not seenDry cows, bulls
4Fat, ribs barely feltPre-calving cows
5Obese, ribs not palpableOnly finishing steers

Score monthly. Target BCS 3-4 for cows (higher before calving, lower after).

The feed rule: Feed is 60-70% of beef cost. Every dollar saved on feed is a dollar of profit. Home-mix, use local ingredients, and store feed in bulk.
Practical exercise 4: Weigh 5 cows. Body condition score each one. Adjust the ration so every dry cow is at BCS 3, and every pre-calving cow at BCS 4.
