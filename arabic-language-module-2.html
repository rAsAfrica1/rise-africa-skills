🚀 Real-World Projects

Language Exchange

Find Arab language partner. Weekly conversation practice. Record and review for improvement.

Media Immersion

Watch Arabic shows/movies. Listen to Arabic music and podcasts. Reading Arabic news.

Cultural Participation

Attend Arab cultural events. Join Arab communities. Deepen understanding of Arab world.

Speaking Goals

Set specific targets. Record yourself. Self-evaluate. Improve progressively.

Reading Practice

Start simple texts. Progress to news and literature. Maintain vocabulary notebook.

Grammar Application

Apply grammar in real conversations. Learn patterns, not rules. Practice regularly.

90-Day Commitment

Intensive practice plan. Daily immersion (1 hour+). 3+ conversation sessions weekly. Track progress.
