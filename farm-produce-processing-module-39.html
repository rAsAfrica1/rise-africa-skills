Citrus Processing

8 lessons · 30 quiz questions · Fruits

1 Citrus varieties

Citrus covers oranges, lemons, limes, grapefruit, tangerines and more. Africa (South Africa, Egypt, Morocco, Zimbabwe, Nigeria) is a major citrus producer.

Sweet orange: Navel, Valencia. Best for juice.

Lemon: Eureka, Lisbon. Best for juice, zest, essential oil.

Lime: Persian, Key lime. Best for juice and food service.

Grapefruit: Ruby Red, Star Ruby. Best for juice and fresh.

Mandarin: Tangerine, Clementine. Best for fresh and canned segments.

For juice, Valencia orange and Eureka lemon are the standards.

2 Washing and juicing

Citrus juice is the world's biggest juice category — over $30 billion market.

  1. Wash fruit in cold water
  2. Sanitise in 100 ppm chlorine solution
  3. Rinse again
  4. Cut in half (or use a citrus press / juice extractor for volume)
  5. Extract juice, keeping peel intact (peel oil is valuable)
  6. Strain juice to remove seeds and pulp pieces

Yield: 100 kg fresh citrus → 45–55 L juice.

💡 Save the peels. Citrus peel is valuable for essential oil, candied peel, and animal feed.

3 Pasteurised citrus juice

Fresh-squeezed juice spoils in 3–5 days. Pasteurised juice lasts 12 months.

  1. Extract juice as above
  2. Add 0.1% ascorbic acid (preserves colour)
  3. Heat to 90°C for 30 seconds (or 95°C for 15 seconds for longer shelf life)
  4. Immediately cool or hot-fill into bottles
  5. Cap and cool

Two options:

  • Hot fill: Fill bottles at 88°C, cap immediately. Bottle sterilises itself.
  • Cold fill + refrigerate: Cool juice rapidly, fill at 4°C, keep refrigerated. Shorter shelf life (30 days) but better flavour.

Retail: $2–$4 per litre. Premium fresh-pressed: $5–$8.

4 Lemon and lime juice concentrate

Lemon and lime concentrate is used in beverages, cocktails, cooking, and food service.

  1. Extract juice from lemons or limes
  2. Strain through fine mesh
  3. Add 0.2% sodium benzoate or potassium sorbate (preservative)
  4. Bottle in glass or PET
  5. Refrigerate or freeze for longer shelf life

Yield: 100 kg lemons → 40–45 L juice.

Retail: $6–$12 per litre (concentrate is more expensive than juice).

5 Citrus peel and zest drying

Citrus peel has multiple uses: dried zest for baking, candied peel, animal feed, and essential oil extraction.

Dried zest:

  1. Zest peel with a fine grater (avoid the white pith — it's bitter)
  2. Spread on drying trays
  3. Dry at 50°C for 4–6 hours
  4. Store in airtight jars

Yield: 100 kg citrus → 1.5–2 kg dried zest.

Retail: $25–$50 per kg. Premium for organic.

6 Candied peel

Candied peel is used in baking (fruitcakes, panettone, hot cross buns) and as a confection.

  1. Cut peel into strips (5 mm × 30 mm)
  2. Boil in water 3 times (5 min each) to remove bitterness
  3. Simmer in sugar syrup (1:1 sugar:water) for 45 minutes
  4. Drain and roll in sugar
  5. Dry 24 hours

Yield: 100 kg fresh peel → 120 kg candied (sugar adds weight).

Retail: $8–$15 per kg.

7 Essential oil from peel

Citrus essential oils (orange, lemon, lime, bergamot) are used in cosmetics, aromatherapy, cleaning products and flavourings.

Cold press (traditional): Press peel to release oil + water. Separate oil from water by centrifugation or settling.

Steam distillation: Pass steam through peel. Oil condenses with water. Separate.

Yield: 100 kg fresh peel → 300–600 g essential oil.

Price: $15–$50 per kg for lemon/lime oil; $20–$80 per kg for premium.

⚠ Essential oil extraction requires equipment ($2,000–$15,000). Start with juice and dried zest first.

8 Bottling and shelf life

Packaging by product:

  • Juice — glass or PET bottles (250 ml, 500 ml, 1 L)
  • Concentrate — small bottles (100 ml, 250 ml)
  • Dried zest — glass jars or foil pouches (10 g, 50 g)
  • Candied peel — glass jars (200 g, 500 g)
  • Essential oil — amber glass with dropper (10 ml, 50 ml)

Shelf life:

  • Fresh juice: 3–5 days refrigerated
  • Pasteurised juice (hot fill): 12 months unopened
  • Concentrate: 6–12 months refrigerated
  • Dried zest: 24 months sealed
  • Candied peel: 12 months sealed
  • Essential oil: 3–5 years sealed
💰 Citrus juice is the world's biggest juice market. But highest margin per kg: essential oils, candied peel, dried zest. Do both — juice for volume, extracts for margin.
