1. Why Goats Make Money

Goats are the fastest-growing livestock sector in Africa. Here is why.

Module 1 of 6 · Goat Farming for Profit

Why goats

AdvantageWhy it matters
Low capitalA breeding doe costs $40-100 vs $400-800 for a cow
Fast reproduction2-3 kids per year per doe
Thrive on poor landBrowse shrubs cattle cannot eat
Small sizeWomen and children can handle them
Dual purposeMeat and milk from the same animal
Cultural demandFestive season prices spike 40-100%

Meat vs dairy goats

PurposeBreedsYield
MeatBoer, Kalahari Red, Savanna35-50 kg mature

Dual purposeGalla, indigenous East African30-40 kg + 1 L milk

What you need to start

  • Land - 0.2 ha per 10 goats for grazing
  • Simple shelter - 2 m2 per goat
  • Reliable water source
  • Initial stock - start with 10-20 does
  • A breeding buck
  • Basic veterinary supplies
The goat rule: Start with 10-20 does, not 100. Learn the system, breed your own replacement stock, expand from within.
Practical exercise 1: Visit 3 goat markets. Ask prices for does, bucks and kids. Find the seasonal peak.
