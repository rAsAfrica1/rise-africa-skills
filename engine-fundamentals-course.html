Petrol and Diesel Engine Fundamentals: 0 to 12 Litres

The complete mechanical engineering course — from a lawnmower engine to a 12-litre industrial diesel.

This is a full 46-module mechanical engineering course covering every petrol and diesel engine from 0 to 12 litres displacement. From the four-stroke cycle to engine rebuilds, from carburettors to modern ECUs, from motorcycle engines to 12-litre industrial diesels — you will learn how engines actually work, how to diagnose them, how to repair them, and how to rebuild them. Whether you are a self-taught mechanic upgrading your skills, a farmer maintaining your own equipment, or someone planning a career in engine repair, this is the deepest practical engine course available.
