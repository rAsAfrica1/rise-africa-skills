2. Housing and Equipment for Layers

Good layer housing keeps hens calm, cool, and laying.

For 100 layers: 20-25 m2 indoor - a 4m x 5m house.

Lesson 2.2 - House design

  1. Open sides with mesh. Two or three sides open.
  2. Roll-down curtains for rain and cold nights.
  3. Roof overhang 60 cm.
  4. North-south orientation where possible.
  5. Concrete or raised floor.
  6. External feed and water access.

Lesson 2.3 - Litter

Depth 5-8 cm at start. Top up as needed. Replace completely every 6-12 months. Use dry wood shavings, rice hulls, or chopped straw. Remove caked litter around drinkers daily. Never allow wet litter - it causes coccidiosis and ammonia.

Lesson 2.4 - Nest boxes

One nest box per 5 hens. A 100-hen flock needs 20 nest boxes.

TypeCostNotes
Wooden box 30x30x303-6Durable, easy to clean
Plastic commercial8-15Easy to clean, long lasting
Bucket or basin1-3Cheapest, works fine

Nest rules:

  • Line with clean dry straw or shavings.
  • Place in the darkest, quietest part of the house.
  • Raise 60-90 cm off the ground.
  • Replace nest material weekly.
  • Add one fake egg per nest to teach hens where to lay.

Lesson 2.5 - Perches

Give each hen 15-20 cm of perch length. A 100-hen flock needs 15-20 m of perch. Wooden poles 5-6 cm diameter, or 4x5 cm timber. Raised 60-90 cm. Removable for cleaning.
