60 Training, consulting, and knowledge products

Once you know quail and guinea fowl farming, others will pay to learn it. This is often the highest-margin income on the farm.

What you can sell:

  • One-day farm workshop - 10 people x 15,000 FCFA = 150,000 FCFA
  • Weekend intensive - 2 days, 5 people x 40,000 = 200,000 FCFA
  • Online course - record once, sell forever. 5,000-20,000 FCFA per student.
  • One-on-one consulting - 25,000-100,000 FCFA per session
  • Farm setup package - 200,000-1,000,000 FCFA per project
  • Ebook / PDF guide - 2,000-5,000 FCFA
  • YouTube channel - ads and affiliate links
  • Paid WhatsApp group - 5,000 FCFA/month
  • Business plan templates - 5,000-15,000 FCFA

What makes your training valuable: real photos and video from your farm, actual numbers (costs, prices, profits), common mistakes, worksheets, follow-up support.

How to start:

  1. Document your own journey
  2. Record one video a week on a single topic
  3. Write a short free guide, use it to collect emails
  4. Run one free workshop for your local community
  5. Ask attendees for testimonials
  6. Turn it into a paid workshop
  7. Record the paid workshop as an online course
A single weekend workshop can pay more than a month of egg sales. Knowledge scales. Your time is your most valuable asset.
