3. Housing, Handling and Infrastructure

Cattle need less housing than other livestock - but handling facilities are essential.

Module 3 of 12 · Beef Farming for Profit

Why infrastructure matters

Cattle live outdoors in most of Africa. But you cannot manage a beef herd without proper handling facilities. Vaccinating, dipping, deworming, pregnancy testing, weighing, and sorting all require safe restraint. Without a crush and race, you cannot do these tasks safely or efficiently.

Housing requirements

FacilityPurposeSpace needed
Night kraal (boma)Protection from predators and theft5-10 m2 per adult
Calf penSeparate calves at weaning2-3 m2 per calf
Sick penIsolate sick animals10-15 m2 per animal
Bull penContain the bull separately20-30 m2 + strong fence
Calving penAssist difficult births15-20 m2
Feedlot pensFor finishing cattle15-20 m2 per animal

For an extensive cow-calf operation, a night kraal and a working race are the minimum. For feedlot finishing, pens and feed troughs are essential.

The night kraal (boma)

  • Strong fence - wooden posts (10-15 cm diameter) or steel, 1.5 m high.
  • Wire or rails - 4-5 strands of wire or timber rails.
  • Gate - wide enough for a tractor or vehicle in emergencies (3-4 m).
  • Drainage - slight slope, no standing water.
  • Space - 5-10 m2 per adult cow.
  • Location - close to the house for security, but away from water sources.

A basic crush and race pays back in one season by making vaccination and treatment 10x faster and safer.

Race and crush design

  1. Race: 70-80 cm wide, 2 m high, curving gently towards the crush.
  2. Length: 4-6 m of race leading to the crush.
  3. Crush: headlock at the end, with a gate behind the animal.
  4. Solid sides - cattle move better when they cannot see out.
  5. Non-slip floor - grooved concrete or packed earth.
  6. Escape route - a side gate for handlers.
  7. Anti-backing bar - prevents animals backing out.

Rotational grazing infrastructure

Divide land into 4-8 paddocks. Move cattle every 3-7 days. Benefits:

  • Worm burden drops 60-80%
  • Pasture recovers better
  • More animals per hectare
  • Less overgrazing and erosion
  • Better feed quality year-round

Infrastructure needed:

  • Central water point accessible from all paddocks
  • Races or gates between paddocks
  • Shade in each paddock
  • Mineral licks in each paddock

Water infrastructure

AnimalWater per day
Adult cow40-60 L
Lactating cow60-80 L
Growing steer30-50 L
Weaner20-30 L

Water infrastructure options:

  • Troughs: Concrete or heavy plastic. 50-100 L per 10 cattle.
  • Nose pumps: Cattle pump their own water from a borehole or river.
  • Solar pumps: Pump from a dam or borehole to a tank, gravity feed to troughs.
  • Windmill: Traditional, reliable if wind is consistent.
  • Direct river or dam access: Cheapest but causes erosion and disease.

For herds under 50 animals, a spray race or pour-on is usually more practical than a dip tank.

Fencing for calves

Calves are escape artists. Use:

  • Woven wire mesh 1.2 m high
  • No gaps under the bottom wire
  • Electric wire offset if calves push through
  • Strong gate latches - calves are clever at opening gates

Predator protection

PredatorProtection
Lions, leopardsLocked night kraal with strong fence
Hyenas, wild dogsNight kraal, guard dogs
Jackals (calves)Keep calves close to house at night
Crocodiles (near water)Fence off water access, use troughs
TheftBranding, ear tags, community watch, guards

Investment priorities

  1. Crush and race - essential for all herd health work
  2. Water system - reliable year-round water
  3. Night kraal - protects from predators and theft
  4. Fencing and paddocks - for rotational grazing
  5. Weighing crate - for growth monitoring and marketing
  6. Dip or spray facility - for parasite control
  7. Feed storage - for dry season reserve
The infrastructure rule: A crush and race is the best investment on any cattle farm. It pays back in one season by making every handling task faster and safer. Do not manage cattle without one.
Practical exercise 3: Draw your planned cattle infrastructure. Include the night kraal, crush, race, water point, and paddock layout. Estimate the cost of each. Prioritise what to build first.
