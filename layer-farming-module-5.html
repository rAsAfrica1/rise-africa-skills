5. Health, Vaccination and Egg Quality

A healthy flock lays 88%. A sick flock lays 40%. The difference is prevention.

Module 5 of 6 · Layer Farming for Profit · quiz at the end

Lesson 5.1 - Biosecurity

  1. One gate, one path, one pair of boots.
  2. Footbath at entrance. Fresh disinfectant daily.
  3. No visitors inside. Sell at the gate.
  4. All-in, all-out. Never mix ages.
  5. Clean and disinfect between flocks. Rest 10-14 days.
  6. Quarantine new birds 3 weeks. Separate pen.
  7. Isolate sick birds immediately.
  8. Control wild birds and rodents.
  9. Never bring live birds from a market into your flock.
  10. Record everything.

Lesson 5.2 - Maintenance vaccination

Do not vaccinate while the flock is under stress. Wait for a calm period.

Lesson 5.3 - The five key layer diseases

DiseaseSignsPrevention
NewcastleGreenish diarrhoea, twisted neck, gasping, sudden drop in layVaccinate day 7, 21, week 16, quarterly
Infectious bronchitisGasping, sneezing, thin-shelled or misshapen eggsVaccinate week 18

External parasites (mites, lice)Restlessness, drop in lay, damaged feathersClean litter, dust bath, diatomaceous earth

Lesson 5.4 - Egg quality problems

ProblemCauseFix
Thin or soft shellsCalcium deficiency, heat stress, ageAdd oyster shell, cool water, cull old hens
Misshapen eggsInfectious bronchitis, stress, overcrowdingVaccinate, reduce stress, more space
Dirty shellsWet litter, dirty nests, diarrhoeaClean litter, fresh nest material, treat diarrhoea
Blood spotsVitamin A or K deficiencyLayer premix with vitamins
Cracked eggsThin shells, poor handling, high nest densityMore calcium, careful collection, more nests

Lesson 5.5 - Daily observation

5 minutes twice a day:

  • Feed and water intake - normal? A drop of 10% is an early warning.
  • Droppings - normal, firm, brown/green.
  • Comb and wattles - bright red = healthy. Pale or purple = sick.
  • Behaviour - active, eating. Huddling or droopy = problem.
  • Egg production - a 15% drop in 3 days is an emergency.
  • Eggshell quality - a change signals something.
The 15% rule: If egg production drops more than 15% in 3 days with no obvious cause, call a vet.
Practical exercise 5.1: Set up a footbath. Use it for a month. Track any disease events.
