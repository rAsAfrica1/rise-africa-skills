Buy or Sell — One Stroke Apart

Tones are not an accent, they are part of the word, and two words can differ only by their tone and mean opposite things.

🎒 What you need

  • a large piece of cardboard
  • a marker or charcoal
  • a partner

✋ Do this

  1. Write 买 mǎi and 卖 mài on cardboard, very large, one on each side. Note that 卖 has an extra stroke on top — the seller has something on their head to carry to market.
  2. Above each character draw the tone shape as a line: a dipping valley for mǎi, a steep drop for mài.
  3. Say each ten times with a matching hand movement. Dip for third tone, chop down for fourth.
  4. Do the same with 水 shuǐ (water) and 睡 shuì (sleep). Write both, mark both tone lines, say both twenty times.
  5. Hold up a card at random and have a partner say the word with the correct tone. Swap. Ten rounds each.
  6. Leave both cardboard sheets on the wall where you sit. You will glance at them a hundred times this week.

💡 Why it works

Drawing the tone as a line above the character binds the pitch to the word visually, so you cannot store the word without the tone. Learners who file tones separately from vocabulary end up with a large vocabulary nobody can understand.

🔥 Challenge

Say mǎi or mài to a partner with your back turned and see whether they can tell buying from selling.
