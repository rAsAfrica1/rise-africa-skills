Talking to Claude

Ask a better question, get a better answer — and people will pay you for the answer.

Ages 7–128 modules 🪨 Starts with no device

Claude is a helper made of words. It cannot see you, it cannot smell the rain, and it does not know your street — so everything it gives you depends on how well you ask. In this course you will learn to ask like a champion, and by the last module you will have a tiny real shop that makes real money for you.

For a parent or teacher

A grown-up holds the Claude account and opens it — Claude's terms are for adults, so the phone stays in your hand while the child does the thinking, the asking and the selling. There is a free tier with usage limits that change over time, so check the current limits before a session and plan short turns; the first two modules need no device at all.
