Cocoa Farming

A complete 6-month program that follows one real production cycle. Theory, hands-on practicals, business planning and quizzes every month.

⏱️ 6 Months (8–12 hrs/week) 📊 Beginner → Business-ready 🌍 Hybrid: Online + Offline 📄 Business Seed Record on completion

Course Overview

Why 6 months? Because that is one real production cycle for cocoa farming. You study each month's material while your own operation (or your planning) goes through exactly that stage. By graduation you have either harvested/sold your first production or hold a complete, costed plan ready for funding.

Structure: 6 monthly modules → lessons → practical assignments → a 10-question quiz each month → final business project.

How to study (hybrid): Online — work through each month on your phone, log sessions in the Study Diary, ask the rAs AI assistant anything. Offline — save this page and do the practicals in the real world.

📅 Month 1: Cocoa Botany & Climate

  • Theobroma cacao: the cocoa tree (to die for chocolate)
  • Optimal climate: 15–30°C, shade requirement (not full sun)
  • Soil: pH 6–7, deep, well-drained, rich in organic matter
  • Altitude: sea level to 1000m (higher = finer flavour)
  • Lifecycle: tree produces pods after 3–5 years
  • Calculate: 1 hectare holds 400–600 cocoa trees

📅 Month 2: Propagation & Land Prep

  • Cuttings or seeds: cuttings clone; seeds grow true
  • Nursery stage: 12–18 months before planting
  • Land: clear forest or intercrop with shade trees (banana, coconut)
  • Soil prep: compost, manure, pH adjustment
  • Shade trees: essential (30–50% shade); nitrogen-fixers preferred
  • Calculate: 500 seedlings × 2 years × labour = $3,000 setup cost

📅 Month 3–4: Planting & Early Care

  • Spacing: 3m × 3m (trees need air flow)
  • Planting: rainy season; mulch heavily
  • Weeding: first 2 years critical (remove competition)
  • Watering: 2,000mm rainfall annually; supplement if dry
  • Shade management: thin canopy as cocoa grows
  • Calculate: 1 hectare = 1,000–1,200 trees at standard spacing

📅 Month 5: Pest & Disease Management

  • Frosty pod rot (Moniliophthora): fungal (major threat)
  • Black pod disease (wet rot, dry rot); Witches' broom (fungus)
  • Capsids (tiny bugs); scale insects; mites
  • Prevention: sanitation, pruning, fungicide when necessary
  • Organic: sulfur, neem oil, copper fungicide (certification)
  • Calculate: disease loss: 10–30% of crop if unmanaged

📅 Month 6: Fruiting & Harvesting (Year 4+)

  • Flowers emerge on trunk/branches (cauliflory)
  • Pollination: small midges (not bees); hand-pollinate if needed
  • Pod development: 5–6 months from flower to ripe pod
  • Harvest signs: yellow/red pod (species dependent), hollow feel
  • Timing: 2–3 harvests per year (main crop + secondary)
  • Calculate: mature tree yields 20–40 pods/year = 1–2 kg dry beans

📅 Month 7: Fermentation & Drying

  • Pod splitting: machete or knife; extract wet beans + pulp
  • Fermentation: pile beans in wooden boxes, 5–7 days
  • Temperature rises to 50°C; kill bean embryo (stop sprouting)
  • Turnover: day 3 and day 5; proper fermentation = flavour
  • Drying: sun-dry 1–2 weeks until 7–8% moisture (brittle)
  • Calculate: 100 wet beans (fermented + dried) = 40 dry beans

🎯 Practical Assignments

  • Grading: flat, plump beans command premium
  • Storage: dry, cool place; vacuum bag preserves flavour
  • Sales: local traders, exporters, craft chocolate makers
  • Organic certification: +30% premium; needs 3-year transition
  • Direct-to-consumer: chocolate bar branding (micro-roastery)
  • Calculate: 1 hectare mature = 400kg dry beans/year × price

💼 Final Business Plan Project

  • Prepare a 100m² plot: shade tree planting + soil enrichment
  • Observe or visit a mature cocoa farm (timing of harvests)
  • Ferment and dry 100 pods worth of beans (test batch)
  • Cost sheet: 5-year maturation timeline + ROI
