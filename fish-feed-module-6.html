Lesson 6.3 - Cost per kg gain

The single most important number:

Cost per kg gain = total feed cost ÷ total kg fish produced

ScenarioCost per kg gainCost per tilapia
Commercial feed at $1.20/kg, FCR 1.8$2.16$0.94
Home mix at $0.80/kg, FCR 1.7$1.36$0.58
Home mix at $0.60/kg, FCR 1.6$0.96$0.38

The difference between the best and worst scenario is $0.56 per fish. On 1,000 fish that is $560 per cycle - more than the entire profit of a small farm.

Lesson 6.6 - When to scale

Scale up your fish farm only when:

  • Your FCR has been under 1.8 (tilapia) or 1.4 (catfish) for 2 cycles.
  • Cost per kg gain is under $1.20.
  • Mortality is under 10% per cycle.
  • You have a confirmed buyer for the extra fish.
  • You have the feed storage and pond capacity for the larger numbers.
  • You can finance the feed for a whole cycle without borrowing at high interest.

Lesson 6.7 - Vertical integration

The most profitable fish farms integrate:

  1. Feed making - cuts cost 40-50%.
  2. Fingerling production - own hatchery saves $0.20-0.40 per fish.
  3. Grow-out - full margin per fish.
  4. Processing - smoked or frozen fish earn 1.5-2x more than fresh.

Start with feed making. It is the cheapest, fastest win.

The integration rule: Home feed mixing pays back inside one cycle. Fingerling production pays back inside one season. Both together transform a hobby pond into a business.
