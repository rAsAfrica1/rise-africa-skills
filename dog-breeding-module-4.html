4. Feeding Dogs for Health and Reproduction

Feed is 40-60% of the cost of a breeding kennel. Here is how to feed for condition, fertility and milk.

Module 4 of 12 · Dog Breeding for Profit

Why feeding matters

A breeding dog needs more than survival rations. Poor nutrition causes:

  • Failed conceptions
  • Small litters
  • Weak or stillborn puppies
  • Poor milk production
  • Stunted puppies
  • Skin, coat and joint problems

Feed cost is 40-60% of running a kennel. Every improvement in feeding shows directly in puppy health and weight - which is what sells.

Target protein: adult 22-25%, puppies 28-30%, lactating 28-30%.

Never feed dogs these foods. They are toxic: chocolate, grapes, raisins, onions, garlic, macadamia nuts, xylitol (artificial sweetener), avocado, raw yeast dough, alcohol, coffee. Even small amounts can kill.

Special feeding for reproduction

Breeding female - before mating

  • Feed to body condition score 3 (ribs felt but not seen).
  • Reduce carbohydrates slightly 2 weeks before mating - lean females conceive better.
  • Ensure adequate vitamin E and selenium - important for fertility.

Pregnancy

Week of pregnancyFeed
Week 1-4Normal maintenance diet
Week 5-6Gradually increase 10-20%
Week 7-9Increase 25-50%, split into smaller meals (puppies take up space)
Do not overfeed a pregnant dog. Fat females have more difficult whelpings. Target body condition score 3 at whelping.

Lactation

  • Feed free-choice or 3-4 meals per day.
  • Increase protein to 28-30%.
  • Increase fat and calories - milk production is exhausting.
  • Provide constant access to fresh water - lactating bitches drink 3-4x normal.
  • Continue high-quality feeding until puppies are weaned.

Stud male

  • Maintain body condition score 3.
  • Extra protein and vitamin E during breeding season.
  • Do not overfeed - fat males have lower fertility.

Puppies

  • Introduce solid food at 3-4 weeks (starter gruel).
  • Feed 4 meals per day from 6-12 weeks.
  • Puppy food until 12-18 months (depending on breed size).
  • Large-breed puppies need controlled growth - don't overfeed.

Water

  • Clean, fresh water always available.
  • Adult dog drinks 50-100 ml per kg body weight per day.
  • Lactating bitch drinks 2-3x normal.
  • In hot weather, water intake doubles.
  • Wash bowls daily.
