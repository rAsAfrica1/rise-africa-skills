Drone Tech & Flight

Four forces, three axes, one set of laws you must not break.

Ages 12–168 modules 🪨 Starts with no device

Drones are a serious tool in Africa right now — carrying medical supplies, surveying farmland, mapping after floods. This course treats you like a future operator: you will learn the physics properly, learn the law properly, plan real missions, measure real thrust, and produce a survey report you could hand to somebody.

For a parent or teacher

Your teenager will learn the aerodynamics, control axes, mission planning and — crucially — the genuine regulatory and safety obligations of drone flight, without needing a drone. The capstone is a planned, executed and written-up aerial survey of a real site using only a phone and a raised camera position.
