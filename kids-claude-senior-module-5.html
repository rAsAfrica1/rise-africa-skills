📱 Shared phone

Draft, Check, Improve — And Say Who Helped

Claude drafts fast, you judge slowly, and the customer is told the truth about both.

🎒 What you need

  • one shared phone
  • account holder
  • notebook
  • pen

✋ Do this

  1. Produce one full sample of your actual service with Claude's help — a tidy CV, a set of birthday card messages, a market price board layout, a WhatsApp broadcast of maize and cassava prices, a study summary sheet.
  2. Edit it hard by hand. Cut anything that sounds foreign to your town, fix names and places, and remove any sentence you could not defend if challenged.
  3. Give it to one honest human to read before any customer sees it. Write down what they stumbled over and fix that.
  4. Ethics moment, write these in your notebook and keep them: I never type another person's private details — ID numbers, medical matters, someone else's phone number — into any AI. I tell customers plainly that AI helped with the drafting. I never submit AI writing as my own schoolwork.
  5. Write the exact sentence you will say to customers, for example: 'I draft this with an AI assistant and then check and finish it myself.' Practise it five times aloud.
  6. File your finished sample in a folder or plastic sleeve. It is your portfolio now.

💡 Why it works

Disclosure is not a weakness in a small community — it is why you get sent the next customer. And keeping other people's private details out of any AI tool protects both them and you, especially when the service you sell involves handling someone's documents. A sample checked by a real reader is worth more than ten Claude drafts nobody has seen.

🔥 Challenge

Find one thing in Claude's draft that is confidently wrong about your area and correct it before anyone else spots it.
