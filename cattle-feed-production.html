What You'll Learn

Master cattle feed production from foundational formulations to commercial-scale operations. This comprehensive course covers dairy cattle (16% protein), beef cattle (12% protein), and profitable feed production systems for operations of any size. Start with zero equipment and scale to 300+ kg/day production with motorized systems.

Learn proven formulations, cost control strategies, and profit maximization techniques used by successful feed producers across Africa and beyond. Real-world ROI data shows 50-60 dairy cattle operations generating $15,000-$20,000 annual profit.
