Lessons

1. Creating a Simple Flyer or Poster

A flyer or poster is one of the cheapest ways to advertise your business. In Spanish, you need a few key phrases. Start with a headline: 'Oferta especial!' (Special offer!) or 'Nuevo!' (New!). Then describe your product: 'Vendo camisetas de algodn' (I sell cotton t-shirts). Add your location: 'Estamos en el mercado central' (We are at the central market). Include a phone number: 'Llama al 123-456-789' (Call 123-456-789). Use big, bold letters for the headline and your phone number. In many African countries, Spanish is spoken in Equatorial Guinea, but you might also use Spanish to attract customers from Spanish-speaking countries or for tourism. If you're in a tourist area, bilingual flyers work well. For example, in Malabo, Equatorial Guinea, you might write: 'Prueba nuestro pescado fresco! Fresh fish!' Use bright colors like yellow, red, and green. Print at a local print shop for about 100-200 CFA francs per copy (or equivalent in your currency). Distribute at markets, churches, and community centers. You can also make a digital flyer using Canva (free app) and share on WhatsApp. Keep text short: 5-10 words per line. Use action words: 'Compra' (Buy), 'Visita' (Visit), 'Ahorra' (Save). Add a special offer: '2 por 1' (2 for 1). Always include a call to action: 'Ven hoy!' (Come today!). Practice writing a flyer for your own business. Remember: correct spelling matters. Double-check accents: 'ms' (more) vs 'mas' (but). Use a dictionary or Google Translate to verify. A good flyer can bring 10-20 new customers per week if placed in high-traffic areas.

2. Describing Your Business in One Sentence

You need a one-sentence pitch in Spanish to tell people what you do. This is called an 'elevator pitch'. Start with 'Somos' (We are) or 'Vendo' (I sell). For example: 'Vendo ropa usada de buena calidad a precios bajos' (I sell good quality second-hand clothes at low prices). Or 'Ofrecemos servicios de peluquera a domicilio' (We offer hairdressing services at home). Keep it under 15 words. Include what you sell, who you serve, and why you're different. For a food business: 'Preparamos comida casera africana para eventos' (We prepare homemade African food for events). For a farm: 'Cultivamos tomates orgnicos sin qumicos' (We grow organic tomatoes without chemicals). Practice saying it clearly and confidently. In Spanish, adjectives usually come after the noun: 'ropa barata' (cheap clothes), not 'barata ropa'. Use 'para' (for) to show your target: 'para estudiantes' (for students). Use 'con' (with) to show a feature: 'con entrega gratis' (with free delivery). Write your sentence on a card and memorize it. Test it on friends: do they understand? If not, simplify. You can also use it on social media bio, business cards, and flyers. For example, in Accra, Ghana, a tailor might say: 'Hago vestidos a medida para mujeres profesionales' (I make custom dresses for professional women). In Nairobi, Kenya, a tech repair shop: 'Reparo telfonos y computadoras rpidamente' (I repair phones and computers quickly). Always end with a smile and eye contact. This sentence opens conversations and closes sales.

3. Social Media Posts for Your Products

Social media is powerful for small businesses. In Spanish, a good post has a photo, a short caption, and a call to action. Use platforms like WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok. For WhatsApp status, post a photo of your product with text: 'Disponible hoy! Zapatos de cuero, talla 38-42. 15.000 FCFA. Envo gratis en Malabo.' (Available today! Leather shoes, size 38-42. 15,000 CFA. Free delivery in Malabo.) Use hashtags: #Oferta #Moda #NegocioLocal. On Facebook, write longer: 'Buscas un regalo? Tenemos cestas tejidas a mano. Hechas en Ghana. Precio: 5.000 FCFA. Pide por WhatsApp: 123-456-789.' (Looking for a gift? We have hand-woven baskets. Made in Ghana. Price: 5,000 CFA. Order via WhatsApp: 123-456-789.) Post 3-5 times a week. Best times: 7-9 AM and 7-9 PM. Use emojis: . Respond to comments in Spanish: 'Gracias' (Thank you), 'S, tenemos' (Yes, we have). For Instagram, use Stories with polls: 'Prefieres rojo o azul?' (Do you prefer red or blue?). For TikTok, make a 15-second video showing your product in use. Speak simple Spanish: 'Mira esto' (Look at this), 'Es fcil' (It's easy). Always include price and how to buy. Example: 'Solo 10 unidades. Enva un mensaje para pedir.' (Only 10 units. Send a message to order.) Track what works: if a post gets many likes, do more like that. Use free tools like Canva for graphics. Remember: consistency beats perfection. A daily post with a clear photo and price will bring sales.

4. Word-of-Mouth Marketing Phrases

Word-of-mouth is the oldest and cheapest marketing. In Spanish, you need phrases to ask for referrals and to thank customers. After a sale, say: 'Si te gusta, cuntale a un amigo' (If you like it, tell a friend). Or 'Recomindanos y recibe un descuento' (Recommend us and get a discount). Give a small reward: 'Trae un amigo y ambos reciben 10% de descuento' (Bring a friend and both get 10% off). Use these phrases in daily interactions. When someone compliments your product, respond: 'Gracias! Conoces a alguien que tambin lo necesite?' (Thanks! Do you know someone who also needs it?). For services, ask: 'Puedes dejar una resea en Google?' (Can you leave a review on Google?). In African markets, trust is key. Speak with respect: use 'usted' for older customers, 't' for peers. Example in Equatorial Guinea: 'Doa Mara, si le gusta el pescado, dgale a sus vecinas. Tenemos entrega a domicilio.' (Mrs. Maria, if you like the fish, tell your neighbors. We have home delivery.) Also, create a simple referral card: 'Recomienda a un amigo y obtn 500 FCFA de descuento en tu prxima compra.' (Recommend a friend and get 500 CFA off your next purchase.) Train your employees to say: 'Gracias por su compra. Vuelva pronto!' (Thanks for your purchase. Come back soon!). Word-of-mouth works best when you exceed expectations. Give a little extra: 'Un regalo pequeo por tu compra' (A small gift for your purchase). Ask for feedback: 'Qu podemos mejorar?' (What can we improve?). Happy customers become your best advertisers. In a study by Nielsen, 92% of people trust recommendations from friends over ads. So, focus on making each customer a promoter.

5. Promotional Offers and Special Deals

Promotions drive quick sales. In Spanish, use clear, urgent phrases. Common offers: '2x1' (two for one), '3 por 2' (three for two), 'Descuento del 20%' (20% discount), 'Envo gratis' (free shipping), 'Precio especial' (special price). For a limited time: 'Solo hoy' (only today), 'Vlido hasta el viernes' (valid until Friday), 'ltimas unidades' (last units). Create a sense of urgency: 'Date prisa!' (Hurry!). For loyalty: 'Tarjeta de fidelidad: compra 5, lleva 1 gratis' (Loyalty card: buy 5, get 1 free). For holidays: 'Oferta de Navidad' (Christmas offer), 'Especial Ramadn' (Ramadan special). Use numbers: 'Ahorra 2.000 FCFA' (Save 2,000 CFA). In African markets, bundle deals work well: 'Paquete familiar: 5 kg de arroz + 2 litros de aceite por 10.000 FCFA' (Family pack: 5 kg rice + 2 liters oil for 10,000 CFA). Advertise on WhatsApp, flyers, and in-person. When a customer hesitates, say: 'Si compras ahora, te doy un 10% de descuento' (If you buy now, I'll give you 10% off). But don't devalue your product. Use promotions sparinglyonce a month. Track results: did sales increase? If not, change the offer. Example: a salon in Kigali, Rwanda, offered 'Martes de descuento: 30% en trenzas' (Discount Tuesday: 30% off braids) and saw a 40% increase in Tuesday customers. Always state conditions: 'Vlido de lunes a mircoles' (Valid Monday to Wednesday). Be honest: don't say 'gratis' if it's not. Use 'sin costo adicional' (no extra cost) for add-ons. Practice writing three offers for your business in Spanish.

6. Practice: Write a Social Media Ad for Your Business

Now it's time to write your own social media ad in Spanish. Follow this template. First, choose a platform: WhatsApp, Facebook, or Instagram. Write a headline: 'Oferta imperdible!' (Unmissable offer!) or 'Nuevo producto!' (New product!). Then describe your product or service in one sentence: 'Vendo miel pura de abejas de las montaas de Kenia.' (I sell pure honey from the mountains of Kenya.) Add a benefit: 'Ideal para la salud y la energa.' (Ideal for health and energy.) State the price: '500 ml por solo 3.000 FCFA.' (500 ml for only 3,000 CFA.) Include a call to action: 'Pide ahora por WhatsApp: 123-456-789.' (Order now via WhatsApp: 123-456-789.) Add a hashtag: #MielNatural #Kenia #Salud. Use emojis: . Post a clear photo of your product. For a video ad, show yourself using it: 'Mira qu rica' (Look how delicious). Keep it under 30 seconds. Write in simple Spanish. Avoid long sentences. Use 't' for informal, 'usted' for formal. If you're targeting a wider audience, use 'ustedes'. Check your spelling. Use Google Translate to verify, but ask a Spanish speaker if possible. In Equatorial Guinea, you can find Spanish speakers. In other African countries, look for language exchange groups on Facebook. Practice by writing three versions: one for WhatsApp status, one for Facebook post, one for Instagram caption. Share with a friend and ask: 'Entiendes qu vendo y cmo comprar?' (Do you understand what I sell and how to buy?). If not, revise. Finally, post it and track responses. Even one sale means success. Repeat weekly. Over time, you'll build a Spanish-speaking customer base.

<h3>1. Creating a Simple Flyer or Poster in Spanish</h3>
<p>A flyer (folleto or volante) is one of the cheapest ways to reach Spanish-speaking customers in markets, trade fairs, and community centres. Keep it simple: one product, one benefit, one contact. Use large text and a clear image of your product. In Africa, where mobile phone use is high, always include a WhatsApp number and a QR code.</p>

<p><strong>Key vocabulary:</strong> Oferta (offer), Precio (price), Descuento (discount), Envo gratis (free delivery), Producto natural (natural product), Hecho en frica (made in Africa), Pedidos por WhatsApp (orders via WhatsApp), Llame ahora (call now), Vistenos (visit us).</p>

<p><strong>Example flyer text for a Ghanaian shea butter business:</strong></p>
<p><em>Manteca de karit 100% natural Hecha en Ghana. Hidrata la piel y el cabello. Precio: 5 el frasco de 250 ml. Envo gratis en pedidos superiores a 20 . Pedidos por WhatsApp: +233 XX XXX XXXX. Vistenos en el Mercado de Makola, Accra.</em></p>

<p><strong>Translation:</strong> 100% natural shea butter Made in Ghana. Moisturises skin and hair. Price: 5 per 250 ml jar. Free delivery on orders over 20. Orders via WhatsApp: +233 XX XXX XXXX. Visit us at Makola Market, Accra.</p>

<h3>2. Describing Your Business in One Sentence</h3>
<p>Your one-sentence description (descripcin en una frase) is your elevator pitch. It must say what you do, for whom, and why it matters. Use this formula: <strong>[Business name] + [verb] + [product] + para + [audience] + en + [place].</strong></p>

<p><strong>Examples:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><em>Kofi Naturals vende manteca de karit pura para mujeres que cuidan su piel en Accra.</em> (Kofi Naturals sells pure shea butter for women who care for their skin in Accra.)</li>
<li><em>Nairobi Fresh exporta aguacates Hass de calidad premium a compradores en Espaa.</em> (Nairobi Fresh exports premium quality Hass avocados to buyers in Spain.)</li>
<li><em>Zanzibar Spice Co. ofrece especias orgnicas para restaurantes en Ciudad del Cabo.</em> (Zanzibar Spice Co. offers organic spices for restaurants in Cape Town.)</li>
</ul>

<h3>3. Social Media Posts for Your Products</h3>
<p>Social media (redes sociales) is the fastest way to reach Spanish speakers. Use Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp Business. Post 35 times per week. Use hashtags in Spanish and English. Include a call to action (llamada a la accin).</p>

<p><strong>Post template:</strong> [Hook] + [Product benefit] + [Offer] + [Call to action] + [Hashtags]</p>

<p><strong>Example for Ethiopian coffee:</strong></p>
<p><em>Sabas que el caf etope es el mejor del mundo? Nuestro caf Yirgacheffe es 100% arbica, cultivado a 2.000 metros. Esta semana: 10% de descuento en tu primer pedido. Escrbenos por WhatsApp para pedir. #CafEtope #CafDeEspecialidad #HechoEnfrica #EthiopianCoffee</em></p>

<p><strong>Translation:</strong> Did you know Ethiopian coffee is the best in the world? Our Yirgacheffe coffee is 100% arabica, grown at 2,000 metres. This week: 10% discount on your first order. Write to us on WhatsApp to order. #EthiopianCoffee #SpecialtyCoffee #MadeInAfrica</p>

<h3>4. Word-of-Mouth Marketing Phrases</h3>
<p>Word-of-mouth (boca a boca) is still the most trusted form of marketing in African communities. Give your customers simple phrases they can repeat. Offer a small reward for referrals (recomendaciones).</p>

<p><strong>Useful phrases:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><em>Recomienda nuestro producto a un amigo y recibe un 5% de descuento.</em> (Recommend our product to a friend and get a 5% discount.)</li>
<li><em>Si te gusta, cuntaselo a tus vecinos.</em> (If you like it, tell your neighbours.)</li>
<li><em>Gracias por su confianza.</em> (Thank you for your trust.)</li>
<li><em>Su opinin es importante para nosotros.</em> (Your opinion is important to us.)</li>
<li><em>Psalo bien y comparte.</em> (Enjoy and share.)</li>
</ul>

<h3>5. Promotional Offers and Special Deals</h3>
<p>Promotions (promociones) drive sales. Use clear, time-limited offers. In Spanish-speaking markets, phrases like <em>2x1</em> (two for one), <em>3 por 2</em> (three for two), and <em>envo gratis</em> (free delivery) work well.</p>

<p><strong>Common promotional phrases:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><em>Oferta especial: 2x1 en aceite de argn.</em> (Special offer: 2 for 1 on argan oil.)</li>
<li><em>Descuento del 20% solo esta semana.</em> (20% discount only this week.)</li>
<li><em>Compra 3 y llvate 1 gratis.</em> (Buy 3 and get 1 free.)</li>
<li><em>Envo gratis en pedidos superiores a 30 .</em> (Free delivery on orders over 30.)</li>
<li><em>Precio de lanzamiento.</em> (Launch price.)</li>
</ul>

<h3>6. Practice: Write a Social Media Ad for Your Business</h3>
<p>Use this checklist to write a social media ad in Spanish. Follow the AIDA model: Atencin (Attention), Inters (Interest), Deseo (Desire), Accin (Action).</p>

<ol>
<li><strong>Atencin:</strong> Start with a question or bold statement. <em>Buscas aceite de coco puro?</em> (Are you looking for pure coconut oil?)</li>
<li><strong>Inters:</strong> Describe your product and its origin. <em>Nuestro aceite viene de pequeos productores en Tanzania.</em> (Our oil comes from small producers in Tanzania.)</li>
<li><strong>Deseo:</strong> State the benefit and the offer. <em>Hidrata la piel y el cabello. Esta semana: 15% de descuento.</em> (Moisturises skin and hair. This week: 15% discount.)</li>
<li><strong>Accin:</strong> Give a clear call to action. <em>Pide ahora por WhatsApp: +255 XX XXX XXXX.</em> (Order now via WhatsApp: +255 XX XXX XXXX.)</li>
</ol>

<h3>Quick Reference Checklist</h3>
<ul>
<li> <strong>One product, one message:</strong> Do not advertise everything at once. Focus on one product per flyer or post.</li>
<li> <strong>Include a WhatsApp number:</strong> Most African and Latin American buyers prefer WhatsApp for orders.</li>
<li> <strong>Use simple Spanish:</strong> Avoid complex sentences. Use <em>usted</em> for formal markets and <em>t</em> for younger audiences.</li>
<li> <strong>Add a call to action:</strong> Every flyer, post, or ad must tell the customer what to do next (<em>Compre ahora</em>, <em>Escrbanos</em>, <em>Vistenos</em>).</li>
<li> <strong>Check regulations:</strong> Never make health claims without scientific proof. Follow local advertising laws.</li>
<li> <strong>Use local currency and measurements:</strong> State prices in euros, pesos, or local currency. Use metric units (kg, ml, cm).</li>
<li> <strong>Test with a native speaker:</strong> Ask a Spanish-speaking friend or use a free tool like DeepL to check your grammar before publishing.</li>
<li> <strong>Track results:</strong> Note which posts or flyers generate the most WhatsApp messages. Repeat what works.</li>
</ul>
