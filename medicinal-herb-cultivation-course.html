â  SAFETY WARNING â Chemical and Food Handling

Pesticides: Always wear gloves, a nose mask, and long sleeves when applying or handling agrochemicals. Never eat, drink, or smoke during application. Store pesticides in a locked, labelled cabinet away from food, feed, and children. Observe the pre-harvest interval (PHI) on the label â harvesting before the PHI leaves illegal residues that can cause buyer rejection and serious health harm.

Aflatoxin risk: Poorly dried or damp-stored maize and groundnuts can develop aflatoxins, which are carcinogenic and can cause liver damage. Dry grain to below 13% moisture before storage, use hermetic bags or clean, dry silos, and discard any mouldy or discoloured grain. Never blend mouldy grain into good grain for sale.

Food safety: Wash hands and equipment before handling produce. Keep raw and cooked products separate. If you suspect contamination, isolate the batch and contact your local public health or agriculture extension office immediately.

Quick Reference Checklist

  • Measure moisture before storage. Use a calibrated moisture meter; target below 13% for maize and below 9% for groundnuts.
  • Invest in hermetic storage. Hermetic bags or metal silos typically cut storage losses by 40â60% and pay back within one season.
  • Keep records. Track input costs, yields, and sales per plot â records are required for most loan and grant applications.
  • Know your buyer. Confirm quality standards (moisture, aflatoxin, foreign matter) before planting, not after harvest.
  • Use certified seed. Certified or improved seed gives more reliable yields and is often required for formal market contracts.
  • Apply agrochemicals safely. Follow label rates, wear protective equipment, and respect the pre-harvest interval.
  • Join a farmer organisation or cooperative. Aggregation improves bargaining power, access to inputs, and eligibility for group financing.
  • Plan for climate risk. Diversify crops, use drought-tolerant varieties, and consider index-based insurance where available.
