📱 Shared phone

Say It Again, But Simpler

If you do not understand the answer, the answer is not finished yet.

🎒 What you need

  • one shared phone
  • grown-up
  • paper
  • pencil

✋ Do this

  1. Ask Claude to explain something hard: why maize prices go up before harvest, or why load-shedding happens.
  2. When the answer is too big, type: 'Explain it again like I am seven. Five sentences only.'
  3. Then type: 'Now explain it in Swahili' — or Shona, Yoruba, Hausa, Amharic, whichever your family speaks at home.
  4. Read the home-language version to somebody who does not read English well. Ask them: did that make sense? Write down exactly what they say.
  5. Write the final five sentences on paper in your best handwriting. That page is a thing you can show anyone.
  6. Ask Claude for one drawing idea to go with your five sentences, then draw it yourself with the pencil.

💡 Why it works

Claude can shrink an idea, swap the language, or change who it is speaking to — but only if you tell it to. This is the skill that turns you into a bridge: information arrives in big English, and you hand it to your neighbour in the language they dream in. Bridges get paid.

🔥 Challenge

Explain your five sentences to a younger child with no paper at all, just your mouth.
