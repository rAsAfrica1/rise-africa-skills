Talking to Claude — Junior

Module 4 of 8: Say It Again, But Simpler

Shared phone

🧠 Brain Opener — 60 seconds

Before anything else

If you cannot explain it to a 7-year-old, you do not understand it yet. — Often attributed to Einstein. It is true. Test it today.

📚 Lessons

1. Simplicity is the proof of understanding

Anyone can make something complicated. Making it simple is the hard part. If your explanation is long, you do not understand it — you are reciting.

2. The 3-step drill

Round 1: explain like a professor (long words). Round 2: explain like a friend (short words, one example). Round 3: explain like a 6-year-old (one picture, one metaphor). The best answer is round 3.

3. Where it breaks

Simple can become wrong. "The sun is a big lamp" is simple and wrong. Simple must still be true. Simple and true — that is the target.

🎯 Cross-Skill Links

♟️
Chess

The best players explain a plan in one sentence: "I castle, I push the pawn, I attack on the kingside." Simple words, complex action. Every complex plan hides a simple one-sentence version.

🎹
Music & Piano

Learn a piece slowly, then faster, then with feeling. Slow reveals what fast hides. Simplicity in performance means every note has a purpose.

🀄
Chinese-Style Drill

简单 means simple. 精确 means precise. A good answer is both — simple and exact. Neither alone is enough.

🤖 Real-Time AI-Era Skill

Ask Claude: "Explain how the internet works, in 3 sentences, then in 1 sentence, then in exactly 5 words." Notice how much survives the cut.

🎮 Game

Do it now

The 5-word challenge. In exactly 5 words, explain this module. Winner is the one whose 5 words teach something new.
