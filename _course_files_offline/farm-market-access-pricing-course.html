Practical Reference

â  SAFETY WARNING â Pesticides, Aflatoxin and Produce Handling

This module covers produce intended for sale and human consumption. Two hazards carry legal and health consequences that can close your business overnight:

  • Pesticide residues: Never sell produce before the pre-harvest interval (PHI) stated on the label has elapsed. Always wear gloves and a face mask when spraying, never spray against the wind, and store agrochemicals locked away from food, feed and children. Residue rejection at a buyer's gate is a total loss.
  • Aflatoxin in maize and groundnuts: Damp grain stored above 13â14% moisture grows mould that produces aflatoxin, a carcinogen. Dry to safe moisture, use hermetic storage, and never blend visibly mouldy grain into a clean lot for sale. Rejected or adulterated lots can trigger regulatory bans and destroy buyer trust permanently.

If you suspect contamination, do not sell. Quarantine the lot and seek extension officer or laboratory advice before it enters any food chain.

Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Know your cost of production per unit. Calculate cost per kg, per crate or per bird before you quote any price. Selling below cost is not a strategy.
  2. Identify at least three buyers before harvest. Never plant without a shortlist of buyers â one market day is not a market strategy.
  3. Measure moisture and grade before selling. Graded, dry, clean produce earns a premium and avoids rejection.
  4. Fix the loss leak first. Invest in crates, hermetic bags or cooling before chasing a higher price â cutting 15% loss equals a 15% price rise.
  5. Price with a margin buffer. Add 15â20% to your cost floor to absorb transport, levies and price swings.
  6. Get terms in writing. Volume, price, delivery date and payment period in a signed offtake or purchase order.
  7. Aggregate with neighbours. Pooled volume unlocks institutional and export buyers that individual farmers cannot reach.
  8. Track every sale. Record quantity, buyer, price and payment date â data is your negotiating power next season.
