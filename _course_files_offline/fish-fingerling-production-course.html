â ï¸ Safety Warning â Fish Health, Water Quality & Chemicals

Fish fingerling production involves live animals, microbial pathogens, and chemical treatments. Observe the following without exception:

  • Never use chlorinated tap water for fry or broodstock without dechlorination (aeration for 24â48 hours or sodium thiosulphate at label rate). Chlorine kills fry within minutes.
  • Formalin, potassium permanganate, and hydrogen peroxide are hazardous. Wear gloves, eye protection, and a respirator when handling. Never mix chemicals. Store away from feed and out of reach of children.
  • Antibiotics are not a substitute for biosecurity. Misuse creates antimicrobial resistance and residues that can disqualify your fingerlings from formal markets. Use only under veterinary direction.
  • Zoonotic risk: Streptococcus agalactiae and Edwardsiella species can infect humans through open cuts. Cover wounds, wear gloves when handling sick or dead fish, and wash hands with soap after every tank session.
  • Do not sell moribund or diseased fingerlings. One contaminated batch can destroy your reputation and infect a buyer's entire farm. Quarantine new stock for 14 days.
  • Electrical safety: aeration pumps near water require RCD/earth-leakage protection. Never work on live equipment with wet hands.
