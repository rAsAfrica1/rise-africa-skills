⚠ SAFETY WARNING â PESTICIDE AND AFLATOXIN HANDLING

Agrochemicals used in maize, tomato, and bean production are hazardous. Always wear the full PPE specified on the label (gloves, boots, long sleeves, face shield), never eat, drink, or smoke during application, and observe the pre-harvest interval strictly â residues above Maximum Residue Limits will cause rejection at EU and premium African retail markets. Store pesticides in a locked, ventilated store away from food, feed, and children. For aflatoxin: never blend mouldy grain into clean lots; aflatoxin is a cumulative liver carcinogen and is not destroyed by cooking. Dry maize to below 13.5% moisture within 48 hours of harvest, use hermetic storage (PICS bags, GrainPro), and test suspect lots with a rapid kit before sale. Report suspected poisoning to your local public health office immediately.

Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Know your cost of production per unit â kg, litre, or head â before you negotiate any price. Update it every season with actual invoices, not estimates.
  2. Dry and grade before you sell. Moisture below 13.5% for maize and proper grading typically lifts farmgate price by 10â20% and cuts aflatoxin risk.
  3. Aggregate with neighbours when you cannot fill a truck. A 10-tonne lot attracts a different buyer than a 500 kg lot, and transport cost per kg falls sharply.
  4. Lock in at least one forward contract before planting for 30â50% of expected output. This de-risks input credit and smooths cash flow.

  7. Check certification requirements (GlobalG.A.P., organic, fair trade) before applying inputs â some products are prohibited and a single violation can cost the whole contract.
