â  SAFETY WARNING â Health, Food and Chemical Handling

Duck eggs and meat are high-risk foods for Salmonella and Campylobacter. Never sell cracked or soiled eggs. Wash hands with soap after every contact with birds, litter, or manure. Ducklings carry duck viral hepatitis and duck plague in many African regions â vaccinate and keep a vet contact on file.

If you use disinfectants, coccidiostats, or antibiotics: wear gloves and a mask, never mix chemicals in food containers, and observe the full withdrawal period before slaughter or egg collection. Misuse leaves residues that will fail buyer tests and can make your customers sick.

Cook duck meat to an internal temperature of 74Â°C and cook eggs until both yolk and white are firm. Do not sell raw duck eggs to pregnant women, infants, or the elderly without a clear cooking instruction.
