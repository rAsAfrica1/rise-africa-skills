1. Why Cabbage Is a Reliable Earner

Cabbage is the most forgiving commercial vegetable in Africa. Here is why it fits a smallholder perfectly.

Module 1 of 6 · Cabbage Farming for Profit

Why cabbage

Cabbage is one of the easiest commercial vegetables to grow and sell. It tolerates cool weather, resists most pests better than tomato, and stores for weeks without refrigeration. Every market in Africa sells cabbage.

MetricValue
Days to harvest75-100 from transplant
Yield per hectare40-70 tonnes
Price per kg (retail)$0.30-0.80
Water need30,000-40,000 L/ha/day
Cost of production per hectare$800-1,800
Revenue at 50 t/ha and $0.50/kg$25,000
Net margin55-70%

Why cabbage works for beginners

  • Low water need compared to tomato - 30,000 vs 55,000 L/ha/day
  • Fewer pests than tomato - mainly aphids, caterpillars, diamond-back moth
  • Reliable yield - forgiving of imperfect management
  • Stores well - 3-6 months in a cool room, 4-6 weeks at ambient
  • Local demand guaranteed - every market sells it

The three cabbage markets

MarketHead sizePriceVolume
Local market1.5-2.5 kg$0.30-0.60/kgHigh
Restaurants/hotels1.5-2.0 kg, tight heads$0.60-1.00/kgMedium, reliable
Supermarket1.2-1.8 kg, uniform$0.70-1.20/kgHigh, contracted

The three risks

  • Diamond-back moth - the number one cabbage pest. Scouts required.
  • Split heads - from uneven watering after dry spell.
  • Oversupply at harvest - 50 tonnes at once crashes price.
The cabbage rule: Plant cabbage in succession (every 3 weeks) rather than one big block. It keeps the price high and cash flowing.
Practical exercise 1: Visit 3 markets. Check the cabbage price on Monday vs Friday. Record the pattern.
