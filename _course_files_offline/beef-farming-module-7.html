7. Backgrounding and Finishing

Buy weaners, grow them on grass or grain, and sell finished cattle at premium prices.

Module 7 of 12 · Beef Farming for Profit

Three ways to grow cattle

SystemDurationWeight gainCost per kg gain
Grass backgrounding6-12 months0.5-0.8 kg/dayLowest
Semi-intensive (pasture + supplement)4-8 months0.8-1.2 kg/dayModerate
Feedlot finishing3-5 months1.2-1.6 kg/dayHighest

Backgrounding is the cheapest way to add weight - cattle grow on grass. Finishing is the fastest way to add weight - cattle grow on grain. The most profitable strategy often combines both: background on grass, finish on concentrate.

Backgrounding - the grass phase

Buy weaners (6-10 months old, 180-250 kg) and grow them on pasture until they weigh 350-400 kg. Duration: 6-12 months.

MetricTarget
Purchase weight180-250 kg
Sale weight350-400 kg
Average daily gain0.6-0.9 kg/day
Duration6-12 months
Cost per kg gain$0.80-1.50

Keys to successful backgrounding

  • Buy healthy weaners - from a known source, vaccinated and weaned for 2+ weeks.
  • Vaccinate and deworm immediately on arrival - stress weakens immunity.
  • Quarantine 21 days before joining the main herd.
  • Good pasture - rotational grazing, good grass, legumes.
  • Supplement - 1-2 kg concentrate per day improves gains.
  • Water and minerals always available.
  • Weigh monthly - track gains, identify poor performers for sale.

Finishing - the grain phase

Buy store cattle (350-400 kg) and feed a high-energy ration for 90-150 days to reach 450-550 kg with good fat cover.

MetricTarget
Purchase weight350-400 kg
Sale weight450-550 kg
Average daily gain1.2-1.6 kg/day
Duration90-150 days
Feed conversion5-7 kg feed per kg gain
Cost per kg gain$1.50-2.50

Starter ration: 12-14% protein. Grower: 11-12%. Finisher: 10-11%.

The adaptation period (critical)

Cattle must be adapted to a high-concentrate diet slowly. Rushing causes acidosis, bloat, and death.

DayConcentrateRoughage
Day 1-320%80%

Day 16-1980%20%
Day 20+85-90%10-15%
Watch for acidosis: Off-feed, staggering, rapid breathing, bloating. Treat immediately with sodium bicarbonate in water and reduce concentrate.

Feeding management in feedlot

  • Feed at the same time daily - 2 feeds per day is standard.
  • Feed to appetite - cattle should clean up within 2 hours but never go hungry.
  • Fresh water always available - 40-60 L per steer per day.
  • Adapt ration gradually - never jump concentrate levels.
  • Weigh feed - know exactly how much each pen receives.
  • Clean troughs daily - remove stale feed, especially in hot weather.
  • Provide shade - heat stress cuts growth and can kill.
  • Provide roughage - at least 10% of diet, for rumen health.

When to sell finished cattle

  • Target weight: 450-550 kg live for standard market.
  • Target fat: BCS 4-5, ribs no longer easily felt.
  • Target age: 18-24 months for premium market.
  • Do not over-finish - extra fat is wasted money and reduced price.
  • Market timing: festive seasons pay premium.

Combined (background + finish) captures the full margin: from $400 purchase to $1,800-2,200 sale = $400-800 net margin per head.

The finishing rule: Cattle add 1.2-1.6 kg per day on grain but cost $1.50-2.50 per kg of gain. You must sell at a price that covers that. Only finish when the market pays a premium for finish.
