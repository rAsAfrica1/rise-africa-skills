This reference block is built for operators, not students. Use it as a working desk tool: print the tables, pin the checklist to your wall, and run the numbers on your own business before you approach a bank, a sacco, or a mobile-money lender. Everything below is drawn from published, verifiable sources so you can quote them in a loan application or a board meeting.

â  SAFETY WARNING â MONEY, DEBT AND CHEMICALS

Never take a short-term digital loan at 30â100% annualised cost to fund a crop that takes four months to mature. The repayment date will arrive before your harvest. Roll-over of these loans is the single fastest way a small agribusiness loses its assets.

Never sign a loan document you have not read in full, and never sign a blank or partially blank form â including "group guarantee" forms where your name is added later.

If this module is used alongside crop-protection or input purchasing: agrochemicals are hazardous. Store pesticides away from food, feed and seed; never decant into drink bottles; wear gloves and a mask when handling; and observe the pre-harvest interval on the label. Money lost on a rejected crop is worse than money saved on a cheap unregistered product.

Quick Reference Checklist

  • Separate business and personal money today. Open a dedicated mobile-money wallet or bank account. One wallet, one purpose.
  • Record every sale and every expense the same day. A notebook is fine. Memory is not.
  • Calculate your gross margin per product line monthly. Know which line actually pays the rent.
  • Track days-to-collect and days-to-pay. If collection is slower than payment, you have a working-capital gap â size it in cash, not in percentages.
  • Build a 13-week cash-flow forecast. Update it every Monday. This is the document lenders trust most.
  • Price before you borrow. Compute the effective annual rate on any loan offer, including all fees, before signing.
  • Keep a loan file: statements, invoices, contracts, receipts, tax records â 12 months minimum.
  • Review monthly: cash position, top three expenses, overdue receivables, and next 30 days of obligations.
