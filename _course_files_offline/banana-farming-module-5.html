5. Pests and Diseases

Panama disease has no cure. This module shows how to prevent it and manage the other major threats.

Panama disease - the killer

Panama disease (Fusarium wilt) is caused by a soil fungus that blocks the plant's water channels. Symptoms:

  • Yellowing of leaf edges, starting with older leaves
  • Leaves wilt and collapse, sometimes splitting at the base of the midrib
  • Pseudostem splits lengthwise near the base
  • Cut the pseudostem - you see reddish-brown streaks inside
  • Plant dies within 1-3 months of first symptoms
Panama disease has NO cure. Once the soil is contaminated, it stays contaminated for decades. The only defence is prevention.

Prevention of Panama disease

  1. Plant only tissue-culture or certified disease-free material.
  2. Never bring suckers from an unknown field.
  3. Isolate the plot - do not share tools with other banana farms.
  4. Disinfect tools between plots with 10% bleach.
  5. Remove and destroy infected plants immediately - bag and burn, do not compost.
  6. Do not replant into the same spot - mark it and avoid for at least 5 years.
  7. Add lime to raise soil pH - reduces disease pressure.

Banana weevil

The banana weevil (Cosmopolites sordidus) is the biggest insect problem. Adult weevils lay eggs at the base of the plant. Larvae tunnel into the corm.

SignWhat to do
Small holes in the cormTrap adults - split pseudostem traps
Plant topples easilyRemove and destroy plant
Slow growth, yellowingCheck corm at base, treat if infested

Weevil control

  • Clean planting material: hot water treatment at 52-55 C for 20 min.
  • Pseudostem traps: split harvested pseudostems, lay cut-side down. Weevils hide inside. Check weekly, destroy.
  • Push traps: fresh cut pseudostem pieces set on the ground attract weevils - collect and destroy.
  • Soil drench around the base with recommended insecticide for severe cases.
  • Parasitic nematodes or Beauveria bassiana as biological control.

Black sigatoka

Fungal leaf disease. Symptoms:

  • Small yellow spots on leaves that grow into dark brown streaks
  • Leaves die early, reducing photosynthesis and yield
  • Bunches are smaller and fruit ripens prematurely
ControlMethod
Resistant varietiesFHIA hybrids, some Cavendish clones
Spacing2.5 x 2.5 m or wider - good airflow
De-leafingRemove the worst-affected leaves weekly and burn
FungicideMancozeb, chlorothalonil, or systemic products at 14-21 day intervals in wet season

Bagging bunches

Covering bunches with a perforated blue plastic bag from flowering until harvest is worth the effort:

  • Protects fruit from thrips, birds, and sunburn
  • Reduces scarring by 60-80%
  • Speeds maturity by 5-10 days
  • Improves fruit colour and uniformity
  • Raises market value 10-30%

Cost: $0.10-0.20 per bag, one bag per bunch. Use blue bags (not clear) to avoid sun scald.

The biosecurity rule: Never share tools or suckers with a farm that has Panama disease. Once it is on your land, it stays for decades.
Practical exercise 5: Set up 5 pseudostem weevil traps in your banana plot. Check weekly. Record the number of weevils caught.
