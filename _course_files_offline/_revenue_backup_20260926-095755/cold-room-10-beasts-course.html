Cold Room for 10 Beasts

A complete construction and business course for a chilled room holding ten cattle carcasses. Site, envelope, refrigeration, hygiene, backup power, commissioning and the business plan behind it.

12 modulesWritten methodKnowledge checks and capstones

  2. Site, Power and the Load-Shedding Question
  3. The Envelope — Insulated Panels, Vapour Barrier and Floor
  4. Refrigeration — Sizing, Selection, Installation
  5. The Room Inside — Rails, Doors, Drains, Safety
  6. Backup Power and the Blackout Discipline
  7. Hygiene, HACCP and the Health Inspector
  8. Costing the Build, the Run, and the Kill
  9. Commissioning, Loading and Temperature Discipline
  10. Repair, Service and the Long Run
  11. Scaling — Second Room, Freezer, or Farm Abattoir
  12. The Business Plan — 10 Beasts per Week

Who this is for

Rural butchers, abattoir operators, farm enterprises slaughtering for their own retail, and meat traders who currently lose stock to ambient temperatures. The course assumes no prior refrigeration or construction training. It assumes you are willing to make decisions by evidence and write them down.

This course teaches general principles of cold room design for a 10-carcass beef operation in tropical Africa. Refrigeration sizing, electrical installation, structural design and health licensing require a qualified local professional. Every figure is indicative and must be verified with local suppliers and the relevant authority before money is spent.
