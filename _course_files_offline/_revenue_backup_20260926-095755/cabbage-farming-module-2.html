Choosing by season

  • Hot season: early varieties, heat-tolerant hybrids
  • Cool dry season: medium and late varieties, red varieties
  • Rainy season: disease-resistant varieties, F1 hybrids
The variety rule: Buy hybrid seed for your main crop. The 20-40% yield increase pays back the seed cost many times over.
Practical exercise 2: Buy 2 varieties - one hybrid, one OP. Plant side by side. Compare head size and uniformity at harvest.
