5. Pests and Diseases

Diamond-back moth, aphids and black rot are the three big enemies. Here is the plan.

Module 5 of 6 · Cabbage Farming for Profit

PestDamageControl

AphidsCurled leaves, sticky honeydew, sooty mouldSoap spray, ladybirds, neem

Diamond-back moth - the number one threat

DBM is the most destructive cabbage pest in Africa. Signs:

  • Small irregular holes in leaves
  • Leaf mines (thin white tunnels)
  • Webbing on the underside of leaves
  • Small green caterpillars that wriggle when disturbed

DBM has developed resistance to many older chemicals. The best control is:

  1. Rotate crops - never cabbage after cabbage
  2. Use row covers on young plants
  3. Bt spray every 5-7 days at first sign
  4. Neem extract as anti-feedant
  5. Rotate chemical actives - never use the same one twice in a season

The four major diseases

DiseaseSignsPrevention
Black rotYellow V-shaped lesions from leaf edge, blackened veinsClean seed, rotation, copper spray
ClubrootStunted wilting plants, swollen distorted rootsLime to pH 7.0, 5-year rotation
Downy mildewYellow patches on top, white fuzz underneathDrip irrigation, spacing, copper spray
Alternaria leaf spotDark concentric ring spots on older leavesRotation, mulch, mancozeb

Preventive spray schedule

WeekProductTarget
Week 1Copper hydroxideBacterial black rot
Week 3BtCaterpillars, DBM

Week 7Mancozeb (if needed)Alternaria, downy mildew
Week 9+Only short-PHI productsStop 7-14 days before harvest
Respect the pre-harvest interval. Most cabbage chemicals have a 7-14 day PHI. Spraying closer to harvest puts residue on the food.

IPM order of operations

  1. Prevent - rotation, resistant varieties, drip irrigation, row covers
  2. Monitor - daily walk, count DBM and aphids
  3. Handpick - caterpillars, cutworms
  4. Biological - Bt, neem, soap
  5. Targeted chemical - correct product, correct rate, correct PHI
The rotation rule: Never plant cabbage or any brassica in the same bed two seasons in a row. Clubroot and black rot live in the soil for years.
Practical exercise 5: Scout your cabbage daily for 7 days. Record DBM, aphids, and any disease. Spray Bt only when DBM is present.
