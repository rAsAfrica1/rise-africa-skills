3. Land Preparation and Planting

Get the hole, the spacing, and the timing right and your banana plot will thrive for 15 years.

Module 3 of 6 · Banana Farming for Profit

Land preparation

  1. Clear the site of trees, stumps, and heavy brush.
  2. Deep plough or rip to 40-50 cm if there is any hardpan.
  3. Test soil pH - lime if below 5.5, add gypsum if heavy clay.
  4. Mark planting positions with stakes at 2.5 x 2.5 m.
  5. Dig planting holes 60 x 60 x 60 cm. Do this 4-6 weeks before planting.
  6. Mix topsoil with compost and let it settle.
  7. Plant windbreaks 4-6 months before the banana on exposed sides.

Planting

ItemDetail
Best timeStart of rainy season
Hole size60 x 60 x 60 cm
Compost per hole10-20 L well-rotted
DAP per hole100-150 g mixed with soil
Planting depthCorm 5-10 cm below soil surface
  1. Place the sucker or plantlet in the hole with the corm 5-10 cm below soil level.
  2. Fill with a soil-compost-DAP mix.
  3. Firm gently to remove air pockets.
  4. Water deeply (10-20 L per plant).
  5. Mulch 5-10 cm around the base, keeping 10 cm clear of the pseudostem.
  6. Shade plantlets for 3-5 days if hot weather.

Mulching

Mulch is essential for banana. It:

  • Conserves soil moisture (30-50% reduction in evaporation)
  • Suppresses weeds
  • Keeps soil temperature stable
  • Adds organic matter as it breaks down
  • Reduces nematode splash

Use dry grass, straw, chopped maize stalks, or banana leaves. 5-10 cm deep. Top up every 3-4 months.

Intercropping in year 1

Young banana has empty space for the first 6-12 months. Plant short, shade-tolerant crops between rows:

Good intercropsAvoid
Beans, peas, groundnutsMaize, sorghum
Sweet potato (early)Cassava (competes for nutrients)
Leafy greens, onionTomato (shared disease)
Herbs (coriander, mint)Other banana varieties

Stop intercropping by month 12 when the banana canopy closes.

Windbreaks

Banana pseudostems snap in strong wind. On exposed sites, plant a windbreak 4-6 months before the banana:

SpeciesHeightNotes
Casuarina10-15 mEvergreen, dense
Grevillea8-12 mFast, wind-tolerant
Gliricidia6-10 mAlso fodder, nitrogen-fixing
Bamboo (clumping)5-8 mDense, but must be contained

Windbreak rows should be 1.5-2x the height of the banana away from the plot.

The mulch rule: Banana mulch is the single cheapest yield increase. 5-10 cm of dry grass around each plant doubles water efficiency and suppresses weeds.
Practical exercise 3: Plant 20 tissue-culture banana plantlets this season. Record planting date, watering schedule, and survival at 1 month and 6 months.
