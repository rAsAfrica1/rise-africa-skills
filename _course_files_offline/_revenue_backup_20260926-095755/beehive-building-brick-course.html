Beehive Building — Brick

Fixed masonry hives in fired brick and stabilised block. For apiaries where metal and timber hives have been stolen, burned or eaten by termites.

12 modulesWritten methodKnowledge checks and capstones

The 12 modules

  2. Site, Orientation and Sun Exposure
  3. The Brick Hive — Walling and Internal Cavity
  4. The Block Hive — Adapting to Concrete Block
  5. Roofs, Rain Protection and Bee Access
  6. Comb Guides and Fixing Interior Structure
  7. Hive Stands, Base Plates and Ant Barriers
  8. Ventilation, Thermal Mass and Cooling
  9. Apiary Layout and Boundary Rules
  10. Harvest Access and Honey Removal
  11. Making Brick Hives for Sale
  12. Running a Fixed Hive Apiary

Who this is for

Beekeepers in areas of high hive theft, bush fire risk or severe termite pressure. Anyone building a permanent apiary. Basic masonry skill is assumed, or the willingness to hire it.

This course teaches general principles of masonry beehive construction. Hive cavity dimensions must match the bee species and the standards of your national beekeeping authority. Verify before committing labour and material.
