6. Slaughter, Processing and Selling

The last 48 hours decide whether your batch makes 300 or 600 USD.

Module 6 of 6 · Broiler Farming for Profit · quiz at the end

Lesson 6.1 - Before slaughter

  1. Withdraw feed 8-12 hours before slaughter. Keep water available.
  2. Schedule for early morning. Carcasses cool by midday.
  3. Set up your work area. Table, sharp knives, killing cone, scald pot, plucking area, chill bath with ice water.
  4. Have packaging ready. Trays, bags, labels, scale.
  5. Have your buyer confirmed. Never slaughter without a sale arranged.

Lesson 6.2 - Slaughter sequence

  1. Hang bird upside down in killing cone.
  2. Single cut to the throat. Allow full bleed (2-3 minutes).
  3. Scald at 60 C for 60-90 seconds.
  4. Pluck immediately.
  5. Remove head, feet (at the hock), and oil gland.
  6. Eviscerate carefully. Do not puncture the gut.
  7. Keep liver, heart, gizzard - many buyers want the giblet pack.
  8. Chill in ice water at 0-4 C for 30-60 minutes.
  9. Weigh, grade, package. Whole birds 1.4-2.0 kg are the sweet spot.
  10. Deliver cold. Never in the sun.
Never leave warm carcasses above 25 C for more than 2 hours. A chilled bird lasts 3 days. A warm bird lasts 6 hours.

Lesson 6.4 - Pricing

  • Cost per kg live = total feed cost / kg live. Target under 1.80.
  • Dressed weight = 70-75% of live.
  • Market price dressed: 3.00-4.50/kg in most African cities.
  • Festive season: +20-40%.

Lesson 6.6 - Batch record and scaling

After 3 batches calculate: mortality % (target under 5%), FCR (target under 2.2), cost per kg live (under 1.80), net profit per batch.

Scale up only when:

  • Three consecutive profitable batches
  • Mortality under 5%
  • FCR under 2.2
  • Buyer confirmed for the larger volume
  • Housing ready before chicks arrive
Practical exercise 6.1: Visit 5 potential buyers. Ask current price, weekly volume, delivery day. Bring a sample bird.
Practical exercise 6.2: Calculate cost per kg live from last batch. Never sell below cost plus 30% margin.
