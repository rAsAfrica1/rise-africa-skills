â  SAFETY WARNING: Chemicals, Hygiene and Client Health

This business uses chemicals, heat and sharp tools. Get this wrong and you can burn a client's scalp, cause permanent hair loss, spread infection, or face legal action.

  • Relaxers and bleach: Always patch-test 48 hours before first use. Never apply on broken, irritated or recently washed scalp. Follow manufacturer timing exactly â do not "leave it a bit longer."
  • Never mix products unless the label says to. Mixing relaxer and bleach can cause chemical burns and hair loss.
  • Sanitation: Disinfect combs, clippers and scissors between every client. Use fresh towels or wash at 60Â°C. Single-use gloves for chemical services.
  • Blood and skin: If you draw blood or see broken skin, stop, clean the area, disinfect tools, and use gloves. Do not share razors.
  • Ventilation: Work in a ventilated space. Chemical fumes cause headaches and breathing problems over time.
  • Client consent: Ask about allergies, scalp conditions, pregnancy and previous chemical treatments before you start. Record it.
  • If a burn or reaction happens: Stop immediately, rinse with cool water, do not apply oils or creams, and advise the client to seek medical help. Keep a record of the incident.

Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Price before you start. Quote the full price, including hair or product, before the first braid or cut. No surprises, no arguments.
  2. Take a deposit on long services (braids, weave, bridal) to protect your time from no-shows.
  3. Rebook at the chair. Before the client stands up, agree the next appointment date and send it to her phone.
  4. Keep a client card. Record hair type, formula, service date, price paid and any reaction. This is your real business asset.
  5. Disinfect tools between every client. This is non-negotiable and protects your licence and reputation.
  6. Track every dollar. Record cash in, supplies out and rent weekly. If you do not track it, you will not know your real profit.
  7. Check supplier prices monthly. Call 3 suppliers for braiding hair, relaxer and shampoo. Prices move with season and import costs.
  8. Review your price list every 6 months. If your costs rose and your prices did not, you are working for free.
