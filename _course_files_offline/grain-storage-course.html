Practical Reference

This reference section is built for operators who need to make storage decisions that protect margin. Grain storage is a financial business: every percentage point of post-harvest loss is money you already spent on seed, labour, and transport. The figures below are drawn from published African and international sources so you can benchmark your own operation against real numbers rather than guesswork.

â  SAFETY WARNING - AFLATOXIN AND CHEMICAL HANDLING

Aflatoxin is a serious health hazard. Grain with visible mould, discolouration, or a musty smell must never be blended with clean grain for sale or consumption. Aflatoxin is not destroyed by normal cooking. If you suspect contamination, isolate the batch and seek testing through your national bureau of standards or a certified laboratory.

When using storage pesticides (e.g. Actellic dust, phosphine fumigants): always wear gloves, a face mask, and eye protection; never enter a fumigated silo or sealed store for at least 72 hours; keep all chemicals locked away from children and food; and follow the label rate exactly. Over-dosing does not improve control and creates toxic residues. Store chemicals in original labelled containers only.

Quick Reference Checklist

  • Dry before you store: Test moisture with a calibrated meter. Maize at 13% or below; sorghum 12.5% or below; paddy rice 13.5% or below.
  • Clean the store first: Remove old grain, sweep, and apply a residual insecticide to walls and floor at least 7 days before filling.
  • Never mix old and new grain: Old grain carries insects and spores into your fresh harvest. Keep batches separate and labelled.
  • Use hermetic storage for seed and high-value grain: PICS or GrainPro bags pay back in one season if they save 10% of a 1-tonne maize crop.
  • Inspect every 2 weeks: Check for heat, moisture, insect webbing, and off-smells. Early detection saves the whole store.
  • Record everything: Log intake weight, moisture reading, date, and source. This data protects you in disputes and supports loan applications.
  • Never sell mouldy grain into the food chain: Divert to approved non-food uses only, and report suspected aflatoxin to authorities.
  • Plan your exit: Storage is a price-trade. Set a target sale month and a floor price before you fill the store.
