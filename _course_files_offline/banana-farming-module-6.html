6. Harvest, Post-Harvest and Marketing

Banana is not seasonal. Managing the harvest and market properly means weekly cash flow.

Module 6 of 6 · Banana Farming for Profit

When to harvest

Banana is harvested based on the "finger" shape and ripening stage. Do NOT wait for the fruit to turn yellow on the tree - harvest green and ripe.

UseHarvest stageDays after flowering
Export (distant market)3/4 full, very green90-110
Local market3/4 full, light green100-120
Restaurants and hotels4/4 full, still green110-130
Home useFull, still green120-140

How to tell if banana is ready

  • Finger shape: fingers are no longer angular - they are rounded and full
  • Angle: fingers point slightly upward or are horizontal
  • Colour: still green but not shiny
  • Days: count from flowering - usually 90-130 days

Harvesting technique

  1. Use a sharp curved knife or machete.
  2. Cut the bunch stalk leaving a 15-20 cm handle.
  3. Two people: one cuts, one catches the bunch on the shoulder or with a padded barrow.
  4. Never let the bunch fall to the ground - bruises cause rot.
  5. Cut the pseudostem back to ground level after harvest - the next sucker will replace it.
  6. Leave the cut pseudostem on the ground as mulch and weevil trap.

Post-harvest handling

StepAction
Immediately after harvestMove to shade. Keep bunches off the ground.
De-handingCut bunches into hands for market. Whole bunches for some markets.
GradingUniform fingers, no blemish, correct fill
Washing (optional)Damp cloth only - do not soak. Remove latex with damp cloth.
PackingSingle-layer cartons or shallow crates. Padded for transport.
TransportNever in the sun. Deliver within 24 hours.

Grading

GradeCriteriaPrice
Grade 1 (premium)Uniform, 3/4 full, no blemish, 15-20 cm fingers+30-50%
Grade 2 (standard)Uniform, minor blemish, 12-18 cmBaseline
Grade 3 (utility)Smaller fingers, minor scarring-20-30%

Ripening

Banana ripens after harvest. For premium markets, ripening is controlled:

  • Ambient: 5-10 days to full ripe, uneven
  • Controlled ripening rooms: 3-4 days, uniform, using ethylene gas
  • Smoking with banana leaves: traditional method, 2-3 days

For most smallholders, deliver unripe banana and let the buyer manage ripening.

The year-round rule: Banana gives weekly cash flow. Never let the whole plot flower at once - stagger by cutting suckers at different times.
