1. The Layer Business

A layer flock has a slow start but the best long-term cash flow of any smallholder livestock.

Minus labour, medication, bedding, depreciation: net 80-120/month, or 1,000-1,400/year from 100 layers.

The egg price rule: Below 0.13 per egg sale price, a layer flock is barely profitable. Confirm you can sell above 0.13 before buying chicks.

Lesson 1.3 - Where layers fail

  • Wrong feed drops lay rate from 90% to 50% in weeks.
  • Late vaccination - Newcastle at week 22 instead of 18.
  • Water shortages - 24 hours without water stops lay for 3 weeks.
  • Overcrowding - above 6 birds/m2 and lay rate falls.
  • No culling - a non-laying hen eats 18-25/year of feed for nothing.
  • No egg market - 90 eggs/day spoil in the sun.
Practical exercise 1.1: Visit 5 local markets and shops. Confirm you can sell above 0.13 per egg.
Practical exercise 1.2: Call 3 chick suppliers and 3 feed suppliers. Write the total cost of a 100-bird flock to first egg.
