Quick Reference Checklist

  • Cost the full chain, not just the field. Include aggregation, drying, storage, transport, and market fees before you quote a price or sign a contract.
  • Test your grain for moisture and aflatoxin before every sale. A cheap moisture meter pays for itself in one rejected load avoided.
  • Invest in hermetic storage. PICS or similar bags cut storage losses dramatically and let you sell into the lean season at higher prices.
  • Keep a simple enterprise budget per hectare. Track seed, fertiliser, labour, and transport separately so you know your true break-even price.
  • Diversify your market channels. Do not depend on a single buyer; combine local traders, processors, institutional buyers, and cooperatives.
  • Formalise your records. Buyers, banks, and insurers price risk on records. Even a notebook with dates, quantities, and prices builds credit history.
  • Check input quality and registration. Buy certified seed and registered agrochemicals only from licensed dealers, and keep receipts.
  • Plan cash flow across the season. Map when money goes out and when it comes in, and arrange working capital before you need it.
