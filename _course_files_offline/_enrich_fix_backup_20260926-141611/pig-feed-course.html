Course

Pig Feed Production

Feed is 70-75% of pig production cost. This course teaches you the ingredient options, the ration formulas, and the mixing, storage and feeding systems that turn cheap local ingredients into fast-growing pigs.

Home · All courses · Pig Feed Production

What this course covers

  • Nutrient requirements of pigs at each stage - starter, grower, finisher, sow
  • Which local ingredients can replace expensive commercial feed - and at what proportion
  • How to formulate balanced rations with the Pearson square method
  • How to mix, store and preserve pig feed on a small farm
  • How to feed for the best FCR (feed conversion ratio) at every stage
  • How to cut feed cost by 30-50% without reducing growth rate

Who this course is for

  • Pig farmers currently buying commercial feed who want to cut costs
  • Smallholders starting a piggery who need to budget feed correctly
  • Anyone with access to cheap local ingredients - maize, soya, cassava, brewery waste
  • Extension officers and trainers teaching pig production
  • Agripreneurs setting up a small feed-mixing business

What you will need

  • Access to at least 3 of the main ingredients - maize, soya, bran, fish meal, cassava
  • A scale for weighing ingredients (5-50 kg)
  • A clean, dry storage area with pallets for sacks
  • A hammer mill or grinder (or access to one nearby)
  • A notebook for ingredient prices and ration records
