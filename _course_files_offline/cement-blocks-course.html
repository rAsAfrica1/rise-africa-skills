Aflatoxin: Mouldy maize, groundnuts, and cassava can carry aflatoxin, a cancer-causing toxin that is invisible, tasteless, and heat-stable. It is not destroyed by cooking. Never blend mouldy grain into clean stock, never feed it to dairy animals (the toxin passes into milk), and never sell it for human consumption. Test with a rapid kit before any trade. In Kenya, KEBS limits total aflatoxin in maize for human consumption to 10 ppb; the EU limit for groundnuts destined for export is 4 ppb total aflatoxin. Exceeding these limits means rejected consignments and potential legal liability.

Agrochemicals: Always wear gloves, a nose mask, and long sleeves when applying or handling pesticides. Never decant pesticides into beverage bottles. Observe the pre-harvest interval (PHI) on the label â selling produce before the PHI is both illegal and dangerous. Store chemicals in a locked cabinet away from food, feed, and children. If you suspect poisoning, call your local poison control centre immediately and keep the product label for the medical team.

Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Measure your actual loss. Weigh or count a sample at harvest, after transport, and at sale. You cannot fix what you have not measured.
  2. Switch to crates for perishables. Replace sacks with ventilated plastic crates for tomatoes, mangoes, and peppers. The payback is usually under one season.
  3. Invest in hermetic storage for grain. PICS or GrainPro bags cut insect damage dramatically and remove the need for chemical fumigants in most cases.
  4. Buy a moisture meter. Store grain only below 13% moisture content (maize) or 9% (groundnuts) to prevent mould and aflatoxin.
  5. Test for aflatoxin before selling. Use rapid test kits at intake. Reject or divert contaminated lots â never blend.
  6. Plan transport for the cool hours. Move perishables early morning or evening; avoid midday heat and overloading.
  7. Train your team on handling. Most damage happens in the hands of workers, not in the truck. A one-day handling workshop pays for itself.
  8. Build a buyer relationship before harvest. Off-take contracts reduce the market-stage loss caused by gluts and price collapse.
