Dog Breeding for Profit

A complete 12-module course on running a small dog breeding kennel - from breed selection through whelping, health, training, and marketing.

12 modules · practical lessons · quizzes · free

What you will learn

  • How to choose the right breed for your market and climate
  • How to build or adapt kennels that keep dogs healthy and productive
  • How to feed breeding stock for condition, fertility and milk
  • How to manage breeding, pregnancy, whelping and puppy care
  • How to prevent and treat the six diseases that kill puppies
  • How to socialise, train and sell puppies at premium prices
  • How to keep records, pedigrees and contracts that protect you
  • How to build a reputation and scale a breeding business

Modules

Module 1Why Dog Breeding Pays Module 2Breeds and Selection Module 3Housing and Kennel Setup Module 4Feeding Dogs for Health and Reproduction Module 5Breeding and Whelping Module 6Health, Vaccines and Parasite Control Module 7Puppy Development and Socialisation Module 8Training and Behaviour Module 9Record Keeping and Pedigrees Module 10Selling Puppies and Building Reputation Module 11Advanced Breeding and Genetics Module 12Scaling, Financing and Long-Term Growth

Who this is for

  • Beginners starting a first breeding pair of dogs
  • Existing breeders wanting a more systematic operation
  • Kennels adding a new breed or improving production
  • Anyone considering a lower-capital livestock enterprise near a city
