5. Health and Biosecurity

One outbreak can wipe out a broiler batch in 4 days. Prevention is cheaper than treatment.

Module 5 of 6 · Broiler Farming for Profit · quiz at the end

Lesson 5.1 - Biosecurity

  1. One gate, one path, one pair of boots.
  2. Footbath at entrance. Fresh disinfectant or lime. Every visit.
  3. No visitors inside. Sell at the gate.
  4. All-in, all-out. Rest 10-14 days between batches.
  5. Clean thoroughly between batches. Remove litter, scrape, wash, disinfect, dry.
  6. Control wild birds and rodents.
  7. Never bring outside birds in.
  8. Record daily. Deaths, symptoms, feed and water intake.

Lesson 5.2 - Vaccination

AgeVaccineMethod
Day 1Marek's (at hatchery)Injection
Day 7NewcastleEye drop or water
Day 14Gumboro (IBD)Drinking water
Day 21Newcastle (booster)Drinking water

Rules:

  • Buy from licensed supplier. Keep cold.
  • Reconstitute in clean, chlorine-free water. Add 1 g/L skim milk powder.
  • Vaccinate in cool morning. Full dose in 1-2 hours.
  • Vaccinate whole batch same day.
  • Record batch number and date.

Lesson 5.4 - When to call a vet

  • More than 3 deaths per 100 in 24 hours.
  • Bloody diarrhoea in multiple birds.
  • Gasping or swollen heads spreading fast.
  • Sudden drop in feed and water intake.
The first-sign rule: Feed and water intake drop before any other symptom. A 10% drop from yesterday is an early warning. Find the cause that day.
Practical exercise 5.1: Set up a footbath at the entrance. Use it for one full batch. Track mortality before and after.
