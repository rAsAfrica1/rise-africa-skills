3. Planting and Early Tree Care

The first 2 years decide whether the tree becomes a 30-year asset or dies in year 3.

Module 3 of 6 · Avocado Farming for Profit

Land preparation

  1. Clear the site of stumps, brush, and weeds.
  2. Deep rip if there is a hardpan - 60-80 cm deep.
  3. Mark planting positions at 5 x 5 m or 6 x 6 m.
  4. Dig planting holes 60 x 60 x 60 cm - do this 4-6 weeks before planting.
  5. Mix topsoil with compost and let it settle.
  6. Test soil pH - lime if below 5.5, gypsum if above 7.5.
  7. Install windbreaks on the prevailing wind side - 2 rows of casuarina, grevillea, or moringa.

Planting

ItemDetail
Best timeStart of rainy season
Planting hole60 x 60 x 60 cm
Compost per hole20-30 L well-rotted
DAP per hole100-150 g
Graft union height15-25 cm above soil
  1. Remove the tree from the bag carefully - keep the root ball intact.
  2. Place the tree in the hole with the graft union facing away from the sun.
  3. Set the root ball level with the surrounding soil - do not bury deeper.
  4. Fill with soil-compost mix, firm gently.
  5. Water deeply (20-30 L per tree).
  6. Mulch 5-10 cm around the base, keeping mulch 10 cm away from the trunk.
  7. Stake the tree if it is tall or the site is windy.
Never bury the graft union. If the graft union goes below soil, the scion will root and the tree loses its disease resistance and dwarfing.

Watering young trees

Deep, less frequent watering makes stronger roots than shallow daily watering.

Split total annual fertiliser into 3-4 applications across the year.

Weeding and mulching

  • Keep a 1 m circle around each young tree free of weeds.
  • Mulch with 5-10 cm of dry grass, straw, or compost.
  • Keep mulch 10 cm away from the trunk - prevents rot.
  • Never hoe deep - avocado roots are shallow and easily damaged.

Intercropping for the first 3 years

Young avocado orchards have empty space between trees. Plant low-growing, shade-tolerant crops that do not compete:

Good intercropsAvoid
Beans, peas, groundnutsMaize, sorghum (tall, compete)
Cabbage, kale, spinachTomato (shared disease)
Onion, garlicSquash (heavy feeder)
Herbs (coriander, mint)Banana (heavy feeder, tall)

Intercropping pays the costs of establishing the orchard. Stop by year 3-4 when the canopy closes.

The graft union rule: The graft union must stay 15-25 cm above soil level. Burying it ruins the tree's disease resistance.
Practical exercise 3: Plant 10 avocado seedlings this season. Track survival at 1 month, 6 months, and 12 months. Target 90%+ survival.
