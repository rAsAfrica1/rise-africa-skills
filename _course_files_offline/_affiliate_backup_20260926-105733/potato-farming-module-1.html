1. Why Potatoes Pay

Potatoes yield more food per hectare than any other staple. Here's the business case.

Module 1 of 6 · Potato Farming for Profit

Why potatoes

Potato is the highest-yielding staple crop per hectare in the world. A well-managed hectare produces 20-40 tonnes of tubers in 90-120 days. No grain comes close.

MetricValue
Days to maturity90-120 depending on variety
Yield per hectare (good management)20-40 tonnes
Yield per hectare (poor management)8-15 tonnes
Price per kg (retail)$0.40-1.00
Total input cost$1,500-3,500/ha
Gross revenue at 25 t/ha and $0.55/kg$13,750
Net margin40-60%

Why potatoes work

  • Short cycle - 90-120 days from planting to harvest
  • High yield - 20-40 tonnes/ha
  • Universal demand - chips, crisps, boiled, mashed, in every market
  • Two crops per year in most of Africa
  • Processing market - chips and crisps factories pay cash for volume

The three markets

MarketSizePriceVolume
Fresh marketAny size, clean$0.40-0.70/kgHigh
Chips/crisps processorsLarge, uniform, specific varieties$0.30-0.50/kgVery high
Restaurants/hotelsLarge, uniform, washed$0.70-1.20/kgMedium, reliable

The three big risks

  • Late blight - the #1 potato disease. Can destroy a field in 7 days.
  • Seed potato cost - clean certified seed is 30-40% of total cost
  • Storage - potatoes sprout and rot without proper storage

What you need to start

  • Land with loose, well-drained soil, pH 5.0-6.0
  • Certified seed potatoes - never use table potatoes
  • DAP fertiliser and CAN for top dress
  • Fungicide program for late blight
  • Cool storage shed
  • Starting capital of $1,200-2,500 for 0.2 ha
The seed rule: Always buy certified seed potatoes. Table potatoes carry viruses that cut yield by 50%.
Practical exercise 1: Visit 3 markets. Ask the price of fresh potatoes and check who supplies them. Visit a chips factory if there is one nearby.
