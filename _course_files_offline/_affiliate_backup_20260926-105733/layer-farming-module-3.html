3. Raising Pullets to Point of Lay

The pullet you raise becomes the layer that pays your bills.

Module 3 of 6 · Layer Farming for Profit · quiz at the end

Lesson 3.1 - The first 24 hours

  1. Pre-heat the brooder 24 hours before. Floor 33-35 C.
  2. Water first, feed second. Chick loses 10% body water on day 1.
  3. Dip each beak in water. Teaches drinking.
  4. Feed on flat trays for the first 3 days.
  5. Count every chick in. Write the number.

Lesson 3.5 - Growth monitoring

AgeTarget weightFeed/day
4 weeks250-300 g25-30 g

16 weeks1,200-1,400 g75-90 g

Weigh 10 random birds every 2 weeks. Underweight pullets lay later and fewer eggs.

The 18-week rule: A pullet at 18 weeks should weigh 1.4-1.6 kg. Any pullet under 1.2 kg will lay late, small eggs, and fewer of them.
Practical exercise 3.1: Weigh 10 pullets at 12 weeks and 18 weeks. Record weights.
