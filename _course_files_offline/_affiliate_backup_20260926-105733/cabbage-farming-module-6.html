6. Harvest, Storage and Selling

Cabbage keeps for months. Storing properly lets you sell when the price is right.

Module 6 of 6 · Cabbage Farming for Profit

When to harvest

  • Head is firm and solid to squeeze
  • Reaches full size for its variety (1.5-3 kg)
  • Outer leaves still green and healthy
  • Before the head splits or the inner leaves start to bolt
  • 75-100 days after transplant, depending on variety

Harvest with a sharp knife. Cut at the base of the stem, leaving 2-3 wrapper leaves.

Harvest technique

  1. Cut with a sharp knife - do not twist or pull.
  2. Leave 2-3 outer wrapper leaves attached.
  3. Harvest in the cool of the day (early morning or late afternoon).
  4. Place gently in crates - never toss.
  5. Keep in shade from the moment of cutting.
  6. Do not wash cabbage destined for storage.

Price timing

Cabbage prices spike when supply drops (hot dry periods) and fall at peak harvest (everyone plants at the same time).

  • Glut: price falls 40-60% at main harvest
  • Lean period: price rises 40-100% 4-8 weeks after harvest
  • Store properly and sell into the lean period

The storage rule: Cabbage is one of the few vegetables that stores for months. Harvest into a cool shed and sell into the lean period for 2x the harvest price.
