4. Feeding Layers for Maximum Eggs

Feed is 70% of your layer cost. Getting every gram right is the difference between 80 and 180 USD profit per month.

Module 4 of 6 · Layer Farming for Profit · quiz at the end

Lesson 4.1 - What layer mash must contain

NutrientLevelPurpose
Crude protein16-18%Egg white and yolk
Energy (ME)2,700-2,800 kcal/kgMaintenance and lay
Calcium3.5-4.0%Eggshell
Phosphorus0.4-0.5%Bone, shell
Methionine0.35-0.40%Egg size
Lysine0.7-0.8%Protein synthesis

Calcium is the most commonly deficient nutrient in home-mixed layer feed. A hen laying 300 eggs per year removes 1.5 kg of calcium from her body.

Lesson 4.2 - Feeding schedule

StageFeed/hen/dayTiming
Point of lay (18-22 weeks)90-110 gSplit 2 feeds
Peak lay (22-45 weeks)115-125 g2 feeds
Mid lay (45-65 weeks)115-125 g2 feeds
Late lay (65-80 weeks)110-120 g2 feeds

Rules:

  • Feed at fixed times. Hens eat more efficiently on schedule.
  • Fill feeders to 2/3. Less spill.
  • Raise feeder lip as hens grow. Level with back.
  • Provide oyster shell free-choice in a separate feeder for strong shells.
  • Never skip a feed.

Home-mixing requires a 20 USD scale and correct ratios. If you can't weigh, don't home-mix.

Lesson 4.4 - The four numbers that define profit

  1. Lay rate: eggs / hens per day. Target 80-90% at peak, 75-85% at mid.
  2. Feed per egg: total daily feed / eggs. Target 1.4-1.6 kg per dozen.
  3. Cost per egg: daily feed cost / eggs. Target under 0.10.
  4. Feed cost ratio: feed cost / egg revenue. Target under 60%.

Worked example for 100 hens at 88% lay, 120 g feed/hen, 0.60/kg feed:

Lesson 4.5 - Reducing feed cost

  • Buy in bulk - 500 kg bags save 15-25% vs 50 kg.
  • Store properly - sealed, off ground, dry.
  • Nipple drinkers - less wet feed = 5-10% saving.
  • Fill feeders to 2/3.
  • Cull non-layers - a hen at 0% lay eats the same as one at 90%.
  • Home-mix a portion if you have cheap grain.
  • Add oyster shell separately.

Lesson 4.6 - Water

Layers drink 200-300 ml per day. Water restriction stops lay within 24 hours. Wash drinkers twice daily. Add electrolytes in heat above 30 C. Keep drinkers in shade.
