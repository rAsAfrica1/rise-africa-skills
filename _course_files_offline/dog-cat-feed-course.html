â  SAFETY WARNING â Agro-chemicals, food safety, and worker health

Agro-chemicals and food handling carry serious health, legal, and commercial risks. Non-compliance can destroy a brand, trigger export bans, and cause injury or death. Treat the following as non-negotiable:

  • Pesticide handling: Always use personal protective equipment (gloves, mask, boots, eye protection). Never eat, drink, or smoke during application. Store chemicals in locked, ventilated, labelled cabinets away from food and children.
  • Pre-harvest intervals (PHI): Respect the label PHI strictly. Selling produce before the PHI expires leaves illegal residues and can fail export testing.
  • Maximum residue limits (MRLs): Check the destination market's MRLs (EU, US, regional) before applying any product. MRLs differ by market and crop.
  • Aflatoxin risk: For maize, groundnuts, and other susceptible crops, manage moisture at harvest, dry to safe moisture (typically â¤13% for maize), and store on pallets in dry, ventilated spaces. Aflatoxin contamination is a major export barrier and a health hazard.
  • Food safety: Implement basic HACCP principles. Keep processing areas clean, separate raw and cooked, control water quality, and maintain cold chain for perishables.
  • Reporting: Report suspected poisoning immediately to local health authorities and your national plant protection organisation. Keep records of all chemical applications (product, rate, date, operator).

Quick Reference Checklist

Run this checklist before every season, loan application, or investor meeting. Each point should have a named owner and a documented evidence trail.

  1. Unit economics: Calculate gross margin per kilogram or litre at every stage you control. Know your break-even volume and price.
  2. Loss audit: Measure post-harvest loss at your specific bottleneck (harvest, transport, storage, processing). Target a 30â50% reduction within 12 months.
  3. Input plan: Confirm seed, fertilizer, and agro-chemical quantities, prices, and delivery dates. Verify product registration and MRL compliance for your target market.
  4. Offtake agreements: Secure at least one written offtake or supply contract with clear volumes, quality specs, and payment terms before planting or processing.
  5. Working capital: Map your cash conversion cycle. Identify the peak funding gap and arrange finance (bank, warehouse receipt, supplier credit, blended facility) before it hits.
  6. Compliance file: Maintain current records for food safety, chemical application, worker training, and traceability. Auditors and buyers will ask.
  7. Logistics plan: Confirm transport mode, cost per tonne-kilometre, cold chain availability, and border documentation for regional trade.
  8. Risk register: List your top five risks (weather, price, pest, policy, currency) with a mitigation action and a trigger point for each.
