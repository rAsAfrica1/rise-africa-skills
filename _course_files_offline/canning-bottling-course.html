â  SAFETY WARNING â BOTULISM RISK

Clostridium botulinum spores survive boiling water and thrive in sealed, low-acid, oxygen-free jars. A jar that looks perfectly sealed can still be lethal. Never water-bath process low-acid foods (vegetables, meats, plain beans, soups) â these require a pressure canner at 11 PSI minimum. Never taste a jar to "check if it's still good." Discard any jar with a bulging lid, off-odour, cloudy liquid, or spurting liquid on opening â boil the contents for 30 minutes before disposal and wash all surfaces with a 1:10 bleach solution. If you or a customer develops blurred vision, difficulty swallowing, or muscle weakness after eating home-canned food, go to a hospital immediately and say "suspected botulism."
