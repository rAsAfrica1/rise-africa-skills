â  SAFETY WARNING â READ BEFORE PRODUCING OR FEEDING

  • Raw soybean is toxic. It contains trypsin inhibitor and haemagglutinins that cause vomiting, diarrhoea and growth failure. Soy must be thoroughly roasted or boiled before milling. A properly roasted soybean is light brown, breaks cleanly, and has no raw bean smell.
  • Aflatoxin is a serious health risk. Never use mouldy, discoloured, insect-damaged or damp maize. Aflatoxin poisoning can be fatal and causes liver cancer with long-term exposure. Store grain dry (below 13% moisture) on pallets, off the floor.
  • Micronutrient premix is a concentrated chemical. Wear gloves and a dust mask when handling. Never exceed the manufacturer's stated dose â excess Vitamin A and iron can be toxic to young children. Store sealed, labelled, away from food and out of reach of children.
  • Do not feed this porridge to infants under 6 months unless a health worker directs otherwise. Breast milk or infant formula remains the only safe food for babies under 6 months.
  • If a child shows signs of severe acute malnutrition (visible severe wasting, oedema of both feet, inability to eat), refer immediately to a health facility. This porridge is a supplementary food, not a treatment for severe acute malnutrition.

Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Verify grain quality before purchase. Reject any maize or soy with mould, musty smell, discolouration or moisture above 13%. Test a sample from every new supplier.
  2. Roast soybean to full inactivation. Roast at 120â130Â°C for 20â30 minutes, stirring constantly, until light brown and nutty-smelling. Never skip this step.
  3. Weigh every ingredient. Use a digital scale accurate to 1 g. Eyeballing ratios destroys the protein balance and the nutrient profile.
  4. Mill to a fine, uniform flour. Target particle size below 0.5 mm so the porridge cooks smoothly and is digestible for young children.
  5. Add micronutrient premix after cooking â never during the rolling boil â to protect heat-sensitive vitamins. Mix thoroughly into the cooled blend.
  6. Cook to a rolling boil for 5â10 minutes. Stir continuously to prevent lumps and scorching. Serve warm, never reheated more than once.
  7. Package in food-grade, airtight containers. Label with production date, batch number, ingredients and a 3-month shelf-life. Store cool, dry, away from sunlight.
  8. Keep a batch record. Log date, ingredients, quantities, cooking time and any deviations. This record is your proof of quality to clinics, NGOs and buyers.
