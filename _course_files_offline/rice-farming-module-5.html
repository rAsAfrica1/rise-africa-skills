5. Fertiliser and Water Management

Rice responds strongly to nitrogen. Getting the timing right is worth 2 tonnes per hectare.

Module 5 of 12 · Rice Farming for Profit

Nutrient needs of rice

Phosphorus (P)40-60 kgRoots, early growth

Fertiliser schedule

StageProductRate/ha

Once per seasonZinc sulphate (if deficient)20-25 kg

Total nitrogen target: 100-150 kg N/ha for high yields.

Timing matters more than quantity

StageN applicationEffect
Basal30% of total NEstablishment, early tillering
Active tillering40% of total NNumber of tillers (grain-bearing)
Panicle initiation30% of total NPanicle size, grain number

Splitting nitrogen into 3 applications increases yield by 15-25% compared to a single basal dose.

Never apply all nitrogen at planting. It is lost to rain and leaching within 2 weeks. Split into 3 doses at the right stages.

Intermittent irrigation (SRI)

Instead of continuous flooding, keep soil moist and allow it to dry slightly between irrigations:

  • Water to 2-3 cm depth, then let it dry to mud before re-irrigating
  • Reduces water use by 30-50%
  • Increases root growth and yield
  • Requires good water control

Critical water stress periods

  • Tillering: Stress reduces tiller number
  • Flowering (panicle emergence): Stress causes panicle sterility - empty grains. This is the worst stress period.
  • Grain filling: Stress reduces grain weight and quality

Zinc deficiency - the flooded soil problem

Flooded soils commonly lock up zinc. Signs:

  • Brown or rust-coloured spots on leaves
  • Leaves turn rusty brown and die
  • Stunted plants, small panicles
  • Affects a patch or the whole field

Fix: Apply zinc sulphate at 20-25 kg/ha once per season. Or foliar spray (2 g/L) at tillering.

Water quality

  • Rice tolerates slightly saline water (EC up to 3 dS/m).
  • Above 3 dS/m reduces yield. Above 6 dS/m kills the crop.
  • Test your water if you use brackish or coastal sources.
  • Avoid water with high iron (orange-red colour) - it can cause zinc deficiency.

Drainage and harvest prep

  • Stop irrigation 2 weeks before harvest. Let the field dry.
  • Drain gradually to avoid sudden stress.
  • Ensure field is dry enough for harvest machinery or hand harvest.
  • Open the outlet fully during the last week.

Water saving tips

  1. Intermittent irrigation (SRI) - save 30-50%
  2. Laser levelling - save 10-20% by even water distribution
  3. Bund repair - stop leaks, save 5-10%
  4. Puddling - reduces deep percolation loss
  5. Mulch between rows where possible (upland rice)
  6. Use short-maturing varieties - less total water need
  7. Rainwater harvesting in ponds - supplementary irrigation
  8. Recycle drainage water where possible
The fertiliser rule: Split nitrogen into three doses: basal, tillering, panicle initiation. Same total amount, 15-25% higher yield. Never apply all at once.
Practical exercise 5: Mark three fertiliser application dates on your calendar: transplant, 3 weeks after, 6 weeks after. Apply 3 splits and compare yield with a single-dose control plot.
