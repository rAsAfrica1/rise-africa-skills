â  SAFETY WARNING â Pesticides, Aflatoxin and Food Handling

  • Never apply pesticides without full personal protective equipment (PPE): gloves, mask, goggles, long sleeves and boots. Re-entry intervals must be respected.
  • Never sell or consume grain or groundnut that shows mould, discolouration or a musty smell â aflatoxin is a Group 1 carcinogen and cannot be removed by cooking or boiling.
  • Store agrochemicals in a locked, ventilated, labelled store away from food, feed, water sources and children. Triple-rinse empty containers and puncture them before disposal.
  • Observe pre-harvest intervals (PHI) strictly. Residue above maximum residue limits (MRLs) causes automatic rejection at EU and Gulf ports.
  • Wash hands and equipment between handling raw manure, chemicals and harvested produce. Keep a first-aid kit and emergency contact numbers posted at the store.
  • Report suspected pesticide poisoning immediately to the nearest health facility and national plant protection authority.

Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Know your numbers: Calculate cost of production per hectare or per animal, break-even price and gross margin before the season starts.
  2. Secure market before planting: Have a written off-taker contract or confirmed buyer order with price, volume, quality grade and payment terms.
  3. Test your soil and inputs: Use accredited laboratories for soil, water, seed and agrochemical quality checks.
  4. Invest in post-harvest infrastructure: Drying beds, hermetic storage bags, cold rooms or crates â target at least 40% loss reduction.
  5. Comply with standards: Obtain required certification (GlobalG.A.P., organic, HACCP) and maintain batch traceability records.
  6. Manage chemical safety: Train all workers on PPE, PHI, MRLs and safe disposal; keep a chemical register.
  7. Protect cash flow: Maintain 3â6 months working capital, insure key assets and crops, and diversify buyers across at least two markets.
  8. Track and document: Keep records of yields, losses, sales, and compliance for loan applications, grants and investor due diligence.
