2. Land Preparation and Varieties

Get the seedbed and seed choice right, and half the yield is decided before you plant.

Module 2 of 6 · Maize Farming for Profit

Land preparation

  1. Clear the field of previous crop residue, weeds and stumps.
  2. Plough 20-25 cm deep. Break hardpan. Best done in the dry season.
  3. Harrow to break clods and level the surface.
  4. Apply lime if pH is below 5.5 - 2-4 tonnes/ha, 4 weeks before planting.
  5. Apply manure or compost - 5-10 tonnes/ha. Incorporate.
  6. Mark rows at 75 cm apart (or 90 cm for dry areas).

Seedbed quality matters

  • Fine tilth - no clods larger than a fist
  • No weeds - they compete from day 1
  • Good drainage - maize hates waterlogged roots
  • Correct pH - lime if acidic

Matching variety to rainfall

Seed quality

  • Buy certified seed from a licensed dealer.
  • Check the lot number and expiry date.
  • Do a germination test - 10 seeds in wet paper, count sprouts at 7 days.
  • Treat seed with a bird and soil pest repellent before planting.
The variety rule: Match variety to rainfall. A long-maturity hybrid in a short-rain area will produce nothing.
Practical exercise 2: Test 3 varieties on 0.1 ha each this season. Compare yield at harvest.
