Preservation Buildings — 10 Methods

Ten physical methods of preserving food, and the building each one needs. Drying, smoking, salting, brining, fermenting, sugaring, oil immersion, airtight storage, cold storage, and irradiation.

  6. Method 5 — Fermentation Chambers
  7. Method 6 — Sugar Preservation and Jam Kitchens
  8. Method 7 — Oil Immersion and Storage

  10. Method 9 — Cold Rooms and Cool Stores
  11. Method 10 — Irradiation and Industrial Methods
  12. Choosing a Method and Building Its House

Who this is for

Food processors, farmers who lose produce to spoilage, cooperative kitchens and anyone wanting to build a small food preservation business. No prior food science required.

This course teaches general building principles for food preservation. Food safety regulations differ by country and product. Confirm all requirements with your national food safety authority before processing any food for sale.
