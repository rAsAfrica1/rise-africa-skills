SAFETY WARNING - READ BEFORE HANDLING ANY MEDICINE OR CHEMICAL

This course involves live animals, vaccines, dewormers, and cleaning chemicals. Follow these rules without exception:

  • Never use human medicines on cats unless a licensed veterinarian prescribes them. Paracetamol and ibuprofen are lethal to cats.
  • Store all vaccines, dewormers, and chemicals in a locked cabinet away from children and food.
  • Wear gloves when handling sick kittens, birthing fluids, or faeces. Wash hands with soap for 20 seconds after contact.
  • Dispose of used needles in a sharps container. Never reuse needles.
  • If a kitten or queen dies suddenly, do not sell or move other kittens until a vet has ruled out infectious disease.
  • In case of human exposure to chemicals, rinse with clean water for 15 minutes and seek medical help immediately.

Quick Reference Checklist

Print this and tick it off every breeding cycle. It protects your cats, your reputation, and your profit.

  1. Queen health check before mating. Confirm weight, vaccination status, deworming, and dental health with a vet. A queen in poor condition will produce weak kittens.
  2. Record every mating date. Write the date, stud name, and queen name in your record book. Pregnancy lasts 63-65 days. Mark the expected queening date on your calendar.
  3. Prepare the queening box by day 55. Clean, warm, with soft bedding. Introduce the queen to it early so she accepts it.
  4. Weigh kittens at birth and every 2 days. A healthy kitten gains 10-15 grams per day. If a kitten fails to gain for 2 days, act immediately.
  5. Vaccinate kittens at 6, 9, and 12 weeks. Do not sell before the 12-week vaccine. This is non-negotiable for reputation and buyer trust.
  6. Deworm queen and kittens on schedule. Queen before mating, kittens at 2, 4, 6, and 8 weeks. Use the correct dose by weight.
  7. Socialise kittens daily from week 3. Handle each kitten for 5 minutes, introduce household sounds and gentle strangers. Socialised kittens sell for 20-40% more.
  8. Keep a buyer waiting list and take deposits. A 20-30% deposit secures the sale and reduces no-shows. Provide a written contract with health guarantees.
