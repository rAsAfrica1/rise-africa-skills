1. Why Onion Is the Store-Able Staple

Onion is the best vegetable for a farmer with land but limited market access. Here is why.

Module 1 of 6 · Onion Farming for Profit

Why onion

Onions are the best vegetable for a beginner with land but limited market access, because they store for 3-6 months. You harvest and sell as the price rises. No other vegetable gives you that flexibility.

MetricValue
Days to harvest120-150 from transplant
Yield per hectare25-40 tonnes
Storage life3-6 months (properly cured)
Water need30,000-40,000 L/ha/day
Cost of production per hectare$1,200-2,500
Revenue at $0.70/kg and 30 t/ha$21,000
Net margin50-65%

Why onion works

  • Stores 3-6 months - you sell when the price is right, not when everyone else is selling
  • Low water need relative to yield
  • Long shelf life - reduces waste, adds value
  • Universal demand - every household uses onions daily
  • Import substitution - many African countries import onions; local production is profitable

The three risks

  • Wrong day-length variety - equipping equator regions with long-day onions gives you zero bulbs
  • Thrips - tiny pest that ruins leaves and reduces bulb size
  • Poor curing - wet onions rot in storage, wasting months of work

What you need to start

  • Land with good drainage and pH 6.0-7.0
  • Certified onion seed (short-day or long-day, matched to your latitude)
  • Nursery trays or a nursery bed
  • Drip irrigation or sprinklers
  • A cool, dry storage shed
  • Starting capital of $600-1,500 for 0.2 ha
The onion rule: Grow the correct type for your latitude. Short-day for equatorial regions (Kenya, Uganda), long-day for southern Africa.
Practical exercise 1: Ask a local extension officer which onion variety works best in your area. Confirm the day-length requirement.
