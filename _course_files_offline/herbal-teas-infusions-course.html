â  SAFETY WARNING â AFLATOXIN AND AGROCHEMICAL HANDLING

Groundnuts, maize, and cassava products can carry aflatoxin, a mould toxin linked to liver cancer and stunting in children. It is invisible, tasteless, and heat-stable â cooking does not destroy it. Never sell or consume grain that is mouldy, discoloured, or has a musty smell. Dry all grain to safe moisture (maize â¤13%, groundnuts â¤10%) within 48 hours of harvest and store on pallets, never directly on the floor.

If you handle pesticides, fertilisers, or fumigants: wear gloves, a nose mask, and boots; never eat, drink, or smoke while handling; store chemicals locked away from food and children; and follow the pre-harvest interval on the label strictly. In case of skin contact, wash immediately with soap and plenty of water. In case of ingestion, seek medical help at once and take the product label with you.

Reference: WHO, Aflatoxins fact sheet; FAO/WHO Codex Alimentarius maximum levels for mycotoxins in food.

Quick Reference Checklist

  • Test moisture before storage. Buy or borrow a moisture meter. Maize above 13% and groundnuts above 10% will mould â and mould means aflatoxin and rejected loads.
  • Dry on a raised mat, not on bare ground. Ground drying re-introduces moisture and soil contamination within hours.
  • Store in hermetic or airtight containers. PICS bags, metal silos, or sealed drums. Never store grain in open woven sacks for more than a few weeks.
  • Grade before you sell. Separate clean, dry, uniform grain from broken and discoloured grain. Graded grain earns 10â25% more and opens formal buyer contracts.
  • Know your buyer before you plant. A verbal or written offtake agreement changes your financing options and your price risk.
  • Keep a simple cost log. Record every input, labour hour, transport trip, and sale. Enterprises without records cannot access credit or prove margin.
  • Never mix treated seed with food grain. Store treated seed separately, clearly labelled, away from the kitchen and from children.
  • Reinvest a fixed share of every sale. Ring-fence at least 20% of gross sales for the next season's inputs, storage, or equipment upgrade.
