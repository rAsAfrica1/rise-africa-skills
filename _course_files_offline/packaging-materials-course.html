â  SAFETY WARNING â AFLATOXIN AND CASSABA CYANIDE

Two hazards are non-negotiable in cassava and maize processing:

  • Aflatoxin: Maize and groundnuts with moisture above 13% can develop aflatoxin â a liver carcinogen. Never sell or feed visibly mouldy grain. Test with rapid kits (US$3â8 per test) before aggregation. Reject any lot with >10 ppb for food, >20 ppb for feed.
  • Cyanide in cassava: Bitter varieties contain cyanogenic glucosides. Inadequate fermentation or drying leaves dangerous levels. Always ferment grated cassava at least 24â72 hours and dry to <12% moisture. Never eat raw or under-processed bitter cassava.
  • Protective equipment: When handling lime, bleach, or pesticides during processing, wear gloves, goggles, and a mask. Train every worker before they start.

Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Know your moisture number. Buy a moisture meter (US$40â120). Target <12% for flour and gari, <13% for maize. Reject loads above threshold â no exceptions.
  2. Cost your chain before you buy equipment. Write down farm gate price, processing cost, logistics, and expected market price. If gross margin is below 20%, fix logistics or sourcing first.
  3. Lock in one off-taker before scaling. A signed supply agreement with a school, hotel, or food processor de-risks investment more than any machine.
  4. Train on aflatoxin and cyanide every season. Repeat training at harvest. Use visual charts in local languages; test every incoming lot.
  5. Track losses weekly. Weigh inputs and outputs. A 5% loss reduction on 100 tonnes is 5 tonnes â real money.
  6. Register your business and product. NAFDAC (Nigeria), FDA (Ghana), KEBS (Kenya), or your national bureau of standards. Certification opens formal markets and export.
  7. Build a simple record system. One notebook or free app (e.g., FarmLogs, Kobo Toolbox) for purchases, sales, and losses. Data is your negotiating power.
  8. Join or form a cooperative for Stage 3. Secondary processing needs capital and volume. Cluster with 5â20 peers to reach viable scale.
