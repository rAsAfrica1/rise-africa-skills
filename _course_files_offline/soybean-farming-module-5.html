Lesson 1

Knowing When to Harvest

In this lesson
  • Judge harvest timing by pod colour and seed moisture
  • Avoid pod shattering
  • Time harvest to the buyer's schedule

Signs of maturity

  • Leaves have dropped (or are dropping)
  • Pods are brown-yellow and dry
  • Seed rattles inside the pod
  • Seed is firm when bitten
  • Seed moisture is 13–15%

Harvest window

Harvest within 7–14 days of maturity. Earlier gives high moisture and green seed; later causes pod shatter (seed falls to the ground) and loss of 15–30%.

The shatter problem

Soybean pods shatter easily when dry. Harvesting in the early morning when pods are slightly damp reduces shatter. Do not harvest at midday in dry weather.

Lesson 2

Harvesting Without Splitting Seed

In this lesson
  • Harvest without cracking seed
  • Thresh at correct moisture
  • Handle carefully to preserve seed quality

Cutting and threshing

  • Cut plants at ground level with a sickle, or pull and cut roots
  • Bundle and dry in the field for 3–7 days
  • Thresh on a tarp or hard surface — never bare soil
  • Thresh at 13–14% moisture. Too dry and seed cracks; too wet and it moulds
  • Hand threshing with sticks is common but damages seed; use a light touch
  • Mechanical threshers must be adjusted for soybean — too fast cracks seed

Handling

  • Sort out stones, dirt, broken seed
  • Winnow to remove chaff
  • Store seed in dry bags
Lesson 3

Drying to Safe Moisture

In this lesson
  • Dry soybean to correct moisture
  • Test moisture without a meter
  • Prevent mould and quality loss

Target moisture

  • Storage: 12% or lower
  • Sale to feed mill: 12% or lower
  • Seed saving: 11% or lower

How to dry

  • Sun-dry on a tarp or concrete floor, 5 cm layer
  • Turn every hour
  • Cover at night to prevent dew re-wetting
  • 3–5 days of sun normally brings seed from 15% to 12%

Testing without a meter

  • Bite test: seed should crack cleanly, not squash
  • Salt test: in a sealed jar, 100 g dry salt and 100 g seed. Shake and wait 15 minutes. If salt sticks, moisture is too high.
  • Rattle test: shake a pod next to your ear. Dry seed rattles freely.
Lesson 4

Hermetic Storage

In this lesson
  • Store soybean without chemicals
  • Use hermetic bags correctly
  • Compare storage options

Hermetic bags (PICS, GrainPro)

  • Cost: $3–8 per 100 kg bag
  • Storage life: 12+ months without weevil damage
  • How: fill bag, expel air, seal at the fold line, keep in a cool dry place
  • Check monthly for punctures and reseal if needed

Other storage

  • Metal silos: $300–800 for 500 kg, excellent rodent resistance
  • Drum storage: old oil drums, cleaned and sealed
  • Traditional granary: 20–40% loss, use only if modern options are unavailable

Storage discipline

  • Dry to 12% moisture or lower
  • Clean seed of dirt and chaff
  • Store off ground, away from walls
  • Never mix old and new
  • Check monthly for weevils and mould
Lesson 5

Grading and Seed Saving

In this lesson
  • Grade for the market
  • Select seed for next season
  • Know when to buy fresh certified seed

Grading

GradeDescriptionMarket
Grade 1Uniform, no blemish, no splitFeed mills, seed processors
Grade 2Minor variation, few splitsLocal market, small mills
Grade 3Split, blemishedOil extraction
RejectMouldy, discolouredDo not sell

Seed saving

Soybean seed degrades fast. Only save seed from a very clean crop, and only for one season. Buy certified seed every year if possible. If you must save:

  • Select from the best plants, no disease symptoms
  • Dry to 11% moisture
  • Store in hermetic bags
  • Test germination before planting
  • Replace every season

Knowledge check — Module 5

1. What is the target soybean moisture for storage?

Correct: 12% or lower. Seed saving target is 11%.

2. What happens if soybean is harvested too late?

Correct: pod shatter. Soybean drops seed if left too long.

3. What is the best storage method?

Correct: hermetic bags. Chemical-free, 12+ months storage.

4. How often should you buy fresh certified soybean seed?

Correct: every season. Soybean seed degrades fast — 15–30% germination loss per year.

5. What happens at 14% moisture at threshing?

Correct: ideal range. Too dry cracks, too wet moulds.
