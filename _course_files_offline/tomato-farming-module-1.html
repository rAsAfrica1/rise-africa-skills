1. Why Tomato Is the King of Vegetables

Tomato earns more per hectare than any other vegetable in Africa - but only with the right system.

Module 1 of 6 · Tomato Farming for Profit

The tomato opportunity

Tomato is the highest-value vegetable for African smallholders. A well-managed hectare can produce 40-70 tonnes per year across 2-3 crops. At $0.80-2.00/kg retail, that is $32,000-140,000 gross revenue per hectare per year.

MetricValue
Days to first harvest75-90 from transplant
Yield per hectare (good management)40-70 tonnes/year

Net margin60-75%

The three big risks

  • Water stress at flowering - one dry day drops 20-40% of potential fruit.
  • Blight and bacterial wilt - can kill a crop in 5 days.
  • No buyer - 40 tonnes dumped on a saturated market crashes the price to $0.20/kg.
The tomato rule: Never plant more than 0.3 ha without a confirmed buyer.
