4. Feeding Goats

Goats eat browse, not just grass. Learn what to grow and what to buy to feed a flock cheaply.

Module 4 of 6 · Goat Farming for Profit

What goats eat

Goats are browsers, not grazers. They prefer shrubs, tree leaves and weeds over grass. This is why they thrive on land cattle cannot use.

Feed typeExamples% of diet
Browse (leaves, shrubs)Leucaena, moringa, acacia, gliricidia50-70%
Pasture (grass)Napier, star grass, Rhodes20-30%
Crop residuesMaize stalks, bean haulms, sweet potato vines10-20%
Concentrate supplementMaize, soy, bran, dairy meal0-30%

Plant a fodder hedge along your boundary. 200 m of Leucaena can feed 10 goats year-round.

Target 16-18% crude protein.

Water and minerals

  • Adult goats drink 4-8 L per day.
  • Lactating does drink 8-12 L.
  • Water must be clean and always available.
  • Loose mineral mix: always available free-choice.
  • Never feed mouldy hay or wet fodder - it causes bloat and listeriosis.
The browse rule: Plant 200 m of fodder hedge before you buy goats. Free feed beats bought feed every time.
Practical exercise 4: Plant 50 Leucaena or moringa seedlings this week along a fence line.
