This practical reference section is designed for agribusiness operators, extension officers, agro-dealers, and farm managers who need to move from theory to implementation. Every figure, table, and checklist below is drawn from verifiable sources so that you can cite them in loan applications, buyer contracts, donor reports, or internal standard operating procedures. The focus is on decisions that affect margin: input selection, application timing, handling safety, and record-keeping that stands up to audit.

â  SAFETY WARNING â Fertiliser, Manure, and Pesticide Handling

Fertilisers, raw manure, and crop protection products can cause serious injury, poisoning, or death if mishandled. Observe the following non-negotiables:

  • Personal protective equipment (PPE): Wear gloves, goggles, long sleeves, trousers, and closed boots whenever handling concentrates, granulated urea, or any pesticide. Never mix or apply pesticides with bare hands or in sandals.
  • Manure safety: Raw manure can carry E. coli, Salmonella, and parasites. Compost it for at least 15â21 days with turning before applying to vegetables or any crop eaten raw. Wash hands and produce thoroughly.
  • Storage: Keep fertilisers and pesticides locked away from children, livestock, food, feed, and water sources. Never store pesticides in beverage bottles or food containers.
  • Re-entry and pre-harvest intervals: Respect the label re-entry period (often 24â48 hours) and the pre-harvest interval. Do not harvest or allow workers into treated fields before these elapse.
  • First aid: If pesticide contacts skin or eyes, rinse with clean water for 15 minutes. If swallowed, do not induce vomiting unless the label says so. Call a poison centre or health facility immediately and take the label with you.
  • Environmental protection: Do not apply near wells, streams, or wetlands. Avoid application before heavy rain to prevent runoff and groundwater contamination.

Source: World Health Organization (WHO) and FAO, International Code of Conduct on Pesticide Management, 2023; FAO, Farmers' Guide to Safe Use of Pesticides.

Quick Reference Checklist

Print this checklist and keep it in the farm office or agro-dealer shop. Each point is an action you can verify today.

  1. Test before you invest: Take a soil sample (0â20 cm, at least 10â15 sub-samples per field) and send it to a certified lab or use a validated rapid test kit before buying fertiliser.
  2. Calculate replacement, not guesswork: Use Table 1 to estimate nutrient removal for your target yield, then compare with your soil test results.
  3. Check the value-cost ratio: Only adopt a fertiliser strategy if the expected value-cost ratio is above 2.0 under your own prices. Recalculate every season.
  4. Blend for your crop, not the shelf: Ask your agro-dealer for a blend matched to your soil test and crop, or ask about custom blending services. Avoid buying whatever is cheapest without a nutrient rationale.
  5. Integrate organic matter: Apply at least 2 t/ha of well-composted manure or equivalent organic matter annually to build soil carbon and improve fertiliser use efficiency.
  6. Train every worker on PPE and re-entry: Document the training. Keep a signed attendance sheet for audits and buyer compliance.
  7. Keep a field log: Record date, product, rate, method, weather, and operator for every input application. This protects you in disputes and helps you learn season to season.
  8. Review and adjust: At harvest, compare actual yield and cost against your plan. Adjust rates, blends, and timing next season using real data, not habit.
