â  SAFETY WARNING â Consumer Data, Payment Fraud & Misleading Claims

Digital marketing carries real legal and financial risk. Do not treat these as optional:

  • Never collect customer data without consent. Kenya's Data Protection Act (2019), Nigeria's NDPA (2023), and South Africa's POPIA all carry fines and enforcement. Ask permission before adding anyone to a WhatsApp broadcast list.
  • Do not make false health or income claims. "Cures," "guaranteed income," and "100% returns" are illegal in most African jurisdictions and destroy trust permanently.
  • Verify payment before delivery. Mobile money reversals and fake screenshots are common. Confirm the transaction in your own account, not from a customer's screenshot.
  • Protect your accounts. Enable two-factor authentication on every ad account and business page. Account hijacking is the single most common way small African businesses lose their audience overnight.
  • Never run ads for regulated products (medicines, supplements, financial services) without checking the platform's policy and your local regulator's rules first.

Quick Reference Checklist

  • Define one measurable goal per campaign (leads, sales, or sign-ups) â never "more awareness" alone.
  • Set your cost-per-lead ceiling before spending, using Table 1 as your starting benchmark.
  • Install tracking (Meta Pixel, Google Tag, or a simple UTM link) so you know which channel produced each sale.
  • Build a WhatsApp Business profile with catalogue, hours, and a saved reply for price enquiries.
  • Test 3 creatives minimum per ad set and kill anything below your benchmark after 3 days.
  • Follow up every lead within 24 hours â WhatsApp, call, or SMS. Speed of response drives conversion.
  • Reconcile spend vs. revenue weekly and record it in a simple sheet; never guess your return.
  • Back up your content and contacts monthly so a hacked account does not end your business.
