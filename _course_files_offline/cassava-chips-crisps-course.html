â  SAFETY WARNING â RAW CASSAVA IS TOXIC

Raw cassava contains cyanogenic glycosides (linamarin and lotaustralin) that release hydrogen cyanide when the root is crushed or eaten. Bitter varieties can contain harmful levels. Never sell or eat under-processed cassava chips. Always peel, wash, slice thinly, and dry thoroughly before frying â these steps reduce cyanide to safe levels. Symptoms of cyanide poisoning include dizziness, headache, confusion, vomiting and difficulty breathing; seek medical help immediately. Also: hot oil causes severe burns â never leave a frying pot unattended, never add wet chips to smoking oil, and keep a lid and dry cloth within reach. Discard used oil that is dark, foaming, or acrid. Follow your national food-safety authority standards (e.g. KEBS, SAZ, NAFDAC) before selling any packaged product.
