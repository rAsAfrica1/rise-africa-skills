11. Slaughter, Meat Handling and Value-Adding

Chevon sells at premium. Dressed and smoked meat fetch 2-3x the live price.

Module 11 of 12 · Goat Farming for Profit

Why slaughter and process

Selling live goats at market earns market rate. Dressed, graded, and processed meat earns 1.5-3x more. Restaurants and butcheries pay premium for consistent quality.

ProductMultiplier vs live
Live goat (market)1.0x
Dressed whole carcass1.4-1.7x
Portioned cuts (restaurant)1.6-2.0x
Smoked chevon2.0-2.8x
Dried (biltong/jerky)2.5-4.0x
Sausage, mince, processed1.8-2.5x

The slaughter process

  1. Withdraw feed 12 hours before. Keep water available.
  2. Rest the goat in a quiet pen 24 hours before - stress affects meat quality.
  3. Weigh live weight. Record.
  4. Restrain humanely. Killing box or casting pen.
  5. Single sharp cut to the throat. Sever carotid and jugular. Full bleed 2-3 minutes.
  6. Skin immediately - do not scald (goats are skinned, not plucked).
  7. Remove head, feet at hock, and internal organs.
  8. Wash carcass with clean cold water. Remove all blood.
  9. Chill to 0-4 C within 1 hour. Do not leave warm above 4 C for more than 2 hours.

Food safety - non-negotiable

  • Clean water only. Never use river or pond water for carcass washing.
  • Clean knives and table. Disinfect between goats.
  • Separate evisceration area. Do not mix with washing area.
  • Chill within 1 hour. Never leave warm carcasses in the sun.
  • Wrap or bag all meat for transport. Never in open crates.
  • Record batch number and date. Traceability for recall if needed.
  • Wash hands frequently. Use food-grade sanitiser.
Never sell meat from a sick goat. Animals that died of disease must be burned or buried, not sold. Disease can pass to humans.
The processing rule: A $50 goat sells for $50 live, or $80-100 processed and graded. The extra $30-50 is profit for 2 hours of work.
Practical exercise 11: Visit a butchery that sells chevon. Ask their price per kg for whole carcass vs individual cuts. Calculate the extra income if you processed your own goats.
