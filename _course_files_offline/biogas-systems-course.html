Use this material alongside the main module, not instead of it.

â  SAFETY WARNING â PESTICIDES, MYCOTOXINS, AND FOOD HANDLING

Agrochemicals and contaminated food are lethal hazards, not inconveniences. Apply the following without exception:

  • Pesticides: Always wear gloves, a long-sleeved shirt, trousers, boots, and eye protection. Never eat, drink, or smoke during application. Observe the pre-harvest interval on the label â selling produce before it expires is illegal and dangerous.
  • Mycotoxins: Aflatoxin-contaminated maize and groundnuts cause liver cancer and stunting. Never blend mouldy grain into clean grain for sale. Dry to below 13% moisture and test before aggregation.
  • Storage chemicals: Fumigants such as phosphine are highly toxic. Only trained, certified personnel should apply them. Never enter a fumigated store without a respirator and a minimum 24-hour aeration period.
  • Reporting: If a worker shows dizziness, vomiting, or blurred vision after handling agrochemicals, move them to fresh air immediately and call emergency services. Keep the product label for the medical team.

Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Know your unit economics. Calculate cost per kilogram or per litre for your top three products. If you cannot state your gross margin from memory, you cannot negotiate price.
  2. Lock your post-harvest plan before planting. Identify drying, storage, and transport capacity at least 60 days before harvest. Post-harvest loss is a planning failure, not bad luck.
  3. Test every input batch. Buy seed and fertiliser only from registered agro-dealers and keep receipts. Counterfeit inputs are the fastest route to a failed season.
  4. Build a buyer contract before scaling. Secure at least one written offtake agreement with defined volume, quality spec, price mechanism, and payment terms.
  5. Separate business and household money. Open a dedicated business account and pay yourself a fixed salary. Mixed finances destroy creditworthiness.
  6. Track moisture and quality at intake. Record moisture content, foreign matter, and grade for every lot. This data is your evidence in disputes and your collateral for finance.
  7. Insure what you cannot replace. Index-based crop insurance and asset cover are available in most African markets. Uninsured assets are a single-event business failure.
  8. Review finance costs quarterly. Compare your current borrowing cost against the instruments in Table 2. Refinancing a 30% loan to a 16% facility is often the highest-return action available.
