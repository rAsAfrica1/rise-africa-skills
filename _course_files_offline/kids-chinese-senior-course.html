Blocks, Not Characters

Complicated characters are simple characters stacked together, and once you see the stacking you never look at a character the same way again.

木 tree → 林 woods → 森 forest
2
🪨 No device needed

Buy or Sell — One Stroke Apart

Tones are not an accent, they are part of the word, and two words can differ only by their tone and mean opposite things.

买 mǎi = buy 卖 mài = sell
3
🪨 No device needed

How Much Is That, Really

Price vocabulary is the most immediately useful Chinese you can own, because the first Chinese speaker you meet may well be selling something.

多少钱？ = how + few + money = 'how much money?'

zh ch sh Against z c s

Six consonants split into two families, and getting them apart is the difference between being understood and being asked to repeat yourself.

zh ch sh 👅⬆️ | z c s 👅➡️

The Scholarship Words

There are real Chinese government scholarships open to students across the continent, and the vocabulary that surrounds them is small enough to learn in one sitting.

Typing Chinese With a Phone Keyboard

You type Chinese by typing Pinyin and choosing the character — which means your Pinyin and your tones are what unlock the writing.

Chinese characters on packaging can indicate food, medicine, pesticides, industrial solvents, or hazardous chemicals. Never assume a container, bottle or sack is safe because you recognise one character on it.

  • Do not taste, smell or open unlabelled containers to identify contents.
  • Characters for è¯ (medicine), æ¯ (poison), å (agriculture/pesticide) and å± (danger) must be treated as red-flag markers. If you see them on a container, stop and get a qualified translation before handling.
