â  SAFETY WARNING â AFLATOXIN AND AGROCHEMICAL HANDLING

Aflatoxin is a silent killer. It is invisible, odourless and tasteless. Grain that looks clean can still carry unsafe levels. Never eat, sell or feed mouldy, discoloured or musty-smelling grain or groundnuts. Do not attempt to "dilute" contaminated batches by mixing them with clean grain â this spreads the risk and can fail a buyer's lab test for the entire consignment.

Dry all groundnuts and maize to below 9% and 13% moisture respectively, and store off the floor on pallets in a dry, ventilated space. Long-term exposure to aflatoxin causes liver cancer and stunting in children.

For storage pesticides and fumigants: wear gloves, a nose mask and long sleeves; never eat, drink or smoke while handling; store chemicals locked away from food and children; and respect the pre-harvest interval and re-entry period on the label. If you suspect poisoning, contact your nearest health facility immediately and bring the product label with you.

Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Measure moisture before you store. Use a calibrated meter. Maize and rice to 13% or below; groundnut to 9% or below. Write the reading and date on the bag.
  2. Get grain off the ground. Use tarpaulins, raised racks or a clean concrete slab. Bare-ground drying invites soil, stones and mould spores.
  3. Grade before you sell. Remove broken, discoloured and foreign material. A clean lot can earn a 5â15% price premium and avoids rejection at the gate.
  4. Store hermetically where insects are the main threat. PICS or GrainPro bags for 100 kg lots; metal silos for tonne-scale storage. Seal properly â a poorly sealed bag performs like an ordinary sack.
  5. Stack on pallets, away from walls. Leave a 10 cm gap from walls and 30 cm from the floor to allow air movement and prevent moisture migration.
  6. Inspect every two weeks. Look for live insects, rodent droppings, caking, musty smell or hot spots. Act on the first sign â do not wait for the next season.
  7. Keep records that a lender will accept. Log harvest date, moisture readings, treatment dates, quantities in and out, and sale prices. Records convert a story into a credit file.
  8. Contract before you store. Know your buyer, grade specification and delivery window before investing in storage. Storage without an off-taker is speculation, not strategy.
