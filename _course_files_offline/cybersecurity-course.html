â  SAFETY WARNING â Passwords, Data, and Devices

Never store passwords, PINs, mobile-money credentials, or customer ID numbers in a WhatsApp message, a notebook kept on the counter, or an unprotected spreadsheet. If a device holding such data is lost or stolen, treat it as a data breach: change every password from a different device immediately, notify your bank or mobile-money provider, and report to your national data protection authority within the legal window (for example, 72 hours under Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 and Nigeria's NDPA 2023). Do not attempt to "clean" a malware-infected device that holds payment credentials â disconnect it from the network, stop using it, and get professional help. Deleting files does not erase them; only proper wiping or destruction does. If you handle health records, student records, or financial data, you carry a legal duty of care â a breach can cost you your licence, not just your money.

Quick Reference Checklist

  • Passwords: Every staff member uses a unique passphrase of 4+ random words; shared logins are banned. Store them in a password manager, not a browser.
  • Two-factor authentication: Turn on 2FA (authenticator app preferred over SMS) for email, banking, mobile money, and any cloud accounting tool.
  • Device hardening: Automatic updates ON, screen lock with PIN/biometric, full-disk encryption enabled, and no one works as administrator for daily tasks.
  • Phishing drill: Run one fake phishing test per quarter. Any staff member who clicks gets a 10-minute refresher, not a punishment.
  • Backups: Follow the 3-2-1 rule â 3 copies of data, 2 different media, 1 stored off-site or offline. Test a restore every month and log the date.
  • Payment verification: Any change of bank or mobile-money details from a supplier must be confirmed by a phone call to a known number before money moves.
  • Incident contact tree: Print a one-page list â bank fraud line, mobile network, IT helper, insurer, and data protection authority â and keep it offline.
  • Monthly review: 30 minutes at month-end to check who still has access, remove departed staff accounts, and confirm backups ran.
