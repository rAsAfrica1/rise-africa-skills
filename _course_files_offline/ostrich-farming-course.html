Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Confirm the market first. Call 3 abattoirs, 2 tanneries and 2 hotels. Get written prices before you buy a single chick.
  2. Check the law. Confirm national wildlife permits, movement permits and abattoir registration requirements for your country.
  3. Build the shelter before the birds arrive. Shade, windbreak, dry floor, brooder lamp ready.
  4. Secure 1.8 m fencing with no sharp edges. Ostriches injure easily and panic at night.
  5. Set up a feed contract with a local lucerne or feed supplier â don't rely on spot buying.
  6. Start a record book on day one. Feed in, weight out, mortality, treatments, sales. No records = no loan.
  7. Register with a vet and agree a vaccination and deworming calendar before the first bird lands.
  8. Plan the cold chain â slaughter slot, transport, refrigeration â before your first bird reaches slaughter weight.
