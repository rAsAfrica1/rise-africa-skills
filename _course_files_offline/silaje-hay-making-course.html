â  SAFETY WARNING: Agro-Chemical and Food Safety Compliance

Agro-chemicals and food handling carry legal, health, and market-access risks. Non-compliance can result in export bans, buyer delisting, and criminal liability.

  • Always read and follow the label. Using a pesticide off-label is illegal in most African jurisdictions and voids insurance.
  • Respect pre-harvest intervals (PHI). Selling produce before the PHI expires leaves residues that fail MRL tests and can harm consumers.
  • Wear PPE. Gloves, mask, goggles, and boots are mandatory when handling or spraying agro-chemicals.
  • Store separately. Never store agro-chemicals with food, feed, or seed. Locked, ventilated, labelled storage is required.
  • Keep records. Batch, date, product, rate, and operator records are required for traceability and buyer audits.
  • Aflatoxin control. For maize and groundnuts, dry to safe moisture (below 13%) and test before sale. Aflatoxin rejection is a total loss.
  • Dispose correctly. Triple-rinse empty containers and dispose per national regulations. Never reuse for food or water.

Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Validate your unit economics. Calculate cost per kg, break-even yield, and gross margin before planting or investing. Use your own farm records, not industry averages.
  2. Secure a buyer before you produce. A signed off-take agreement or confirmed purchase order de-risks production and strengthens your loan application.
  3. Quantify post-harvest loss. Weigh and record losses at harvest, drying, storage, and transport. A 10% loss reduction is often more profitable than a 10% yield increase.
  4. Comply with food safety standards. Know the maximum residue limits (MRLs) and aflatoxin thresholds for your target market, domestic or export.
  5. Build a 12-month cash-flow forecast. Agribusiness is seasonal; match working capital to planting, harvest, and payment cycles.
  6. Formalise aggregation. Cooperatives and contract farming reduce transaction costs and improve bargaining power with processors and buyers.
  7. Invest in storage and cold chain where justified. For perishables, cold chain access can raise realisable price by 20-50%.
  8. Track regulatory and trade changes. AfCFTA tariff schedules, national export bans, and MRL updates affect market access. Review quarterly.
