3. Land Preparation and Planting

Potatoes need loose, deep soil. Get the seedbed right and the tubers do the rest.

Module 3 of 6 · Potato Farming for Profit

Soil requirements

PropertyIdeal
TextureLoose loam or sandy loam
pH5.0-6.0 (slightly acidic)
DrainageExcellent - potatoes hate waterlogging
Depth30 cm of loose soil
Organic matter2-3%

Land preparation

  1. Clear field of previous crop residue.
  2. Plough 25-30 cm deep - deeper than for maize or cabbage.
  3. Harrow to break clods and level.
  4. Apply lime if pH is below 5.0 - 1-2 t/ha, 4-6 weeks before planting.
  5. Apply manure or compost - 15-20 t/ha. Incorporate well.
  6. Mark ridges at 75-90 cm apart.

Ridge vs flat planting

MethodAdvantageBest for
Ridges (raised rows)Better drainage, easier harvest, more tubersMost African conditions
Flat plantingFaster planting, cheaperVery dry areas with irrigation

For most smallholders, ridges are the better choice - better drainage in rain, easier harvest.

  1. Open a furrow 10-15 cm deep.
  2. Place seed tuber with sprouts facing up.
  3. Space 25-30 cm apart.
  4. Cover with 5-8 cm of soil.
  5. Form the ridge over the row.
  6. Leave the furrow between ridges for water drainage.

Fertiliser at planting

FertiliserRateWhen
DAP (18-46-0)300-400 kg/haAt planting, banded
Compound (e.g. 12-24-12)400-500 kg/haAt planting
Manure15-20 t/ha2-4 weeks before planting
Never let fertiliser touch the seed tuber. It burns the young sprouts. Place 5-8 cm below and to the side.

Earthing up

Earthing up = pulling soil up around the stems as the plants grow. This is critical for potatoes.

  • First earthing: when plants are 15-20 cm tall.
  • Second earthing: 2-3 weeks later, before flowering.
  • Benefits: prevents greening, protects tubers, improves drainage, controls weeds.
  • Do not earth up after flowering - damaged roots reduce yield.
The earthing rule: Earth up twice before flowering. It increases the number of tubers per plant.
Practical exercise 3: Plant 0.1 ha of potato this season. Record emergence date, first earthing date, and final yield.
