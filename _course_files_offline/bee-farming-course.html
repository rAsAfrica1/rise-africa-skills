â  SAFETY WARNING â FOOD SAFETY AND CHEMICALS

Honey is a food product. Careless handling can make you sick and destroy your brand.

  • Never harvest uncapped honey. Uncapped honey can exceed 20% moisture, ferment, and grow mould. Only extract frames that are at least two-thirds capped.
  • Never use veterinary or crop pesticides inside or near hives. Many insecticides (including pyrethroids and neonicotinoids) are highly toxic to bees and can contaminate honey. If a neighbour sprays, move hives or cover entrances.
  • Do not use unapproved chemicals to treat hive pests. Only use products registered for apiculture in your country. Illegal residues can cause honey to be rejected at export and may harm consumers.
  • Wear protective gear during every inspection. African honeybees are more defensive than European strains. A single suit is cheaper than a hospital visit.
  • Store honey in food-grade containers only. Never use pesticide drums, fuel cans, or non-food plastic. Honey absorbs odours and contaminants.
  • Keep children and visitors away from hives during inspections. Set your apiary away from footpaths and homesteads.
