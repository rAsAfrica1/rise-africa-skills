Red Safety Warning

â  SAFETY WARNING â HEALTH, FOOD AND CHEMICALS

If your niche is food, health, cosmetics, supplements, or cleaning products, you carry legal and ethical risk. In Zimbabwe, SI 265 of 2002 (Food and Food Standards Regulations) and the Medicines Control Authority of Zimbabwe (MCAZ) regulate food and health claims. Do not promote a food, supplement or chemical product without:

  • Written confirmation from the brand that the product is registered and compliant with local law.
  • A clear "advertisement" or "sponsored" disclosure on every paid post â required by most platforms and increasingly by consumer law.
  • No unproven medical claims ("cures", "prevents disease", "guaranteed weight loss"). These can trigger fines and destroy your brand.
  • Personal protective equipment (gloves, mask, eyewear) when demonstrating any chemical or cleaning product.
  • Never promote unregistered medicines or skin-lightening products containing hydroquinone or mercury â these are illegal in many African markets and harmful to your audience.

When in doubt, decline the deal. A single bad product can end your business and harm real people.
