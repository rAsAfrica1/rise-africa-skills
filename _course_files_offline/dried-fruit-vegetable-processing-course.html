â  SAFETY WARNING â AGROCHEMICALS AND FOOD SAFETY

Agro-chemicals used in crop and livestock production are hazardous. Always follow these non-negotiable rules:

  • Wear full personal protective equipment (PPE): gloves, mask, goggles, boots, and long clothing when handling pesticides or veterinary drugs.
  • Observe the pre-harvest interval (PHI) and withdrawal period strictly. Selling produce or animal products before the PHI expires leaves illegal residues and can cause poisoning.
  • Never store pesticides in food or drink containers. Store separately, locked, and away from children and animals.
  • Do not eat, drink, or smoke while applying chemicals. Wash hands and clothing thoroughly afterwards.
  • Dispose of empty containers safely â never reuse them for water or food, and never burn them in the open.
  • If poisoning is suspected, contact a health facility immediately and take the product label with you. In case of emergency, seek professional medical help.

Quick Reference Checklist

  • Cost your enterprise per unit (per bird, per hectare, per tonne) before setting prices â never price from guesswork.
  • Record every input and output daily in a simple ledger or mobile app; data is the foundation of credit access.
  • Separate business and household money using a dedicated account or mobile wallet to protect working capital.
  • Build a 3-month cash flow forecast that accounts for seasonal price swings and delayed payments from buyers.
  • Identify your top three production risks (e.g., disease, drought, price crash) and write one mitigation action for each.
  • Join or form a producer group to access bulk input discounts, shared transport, and stronger bargaining power.
  • Verify buyer contracts in writing â agree on price, quantity, quality standards, delivery date, and payment terms.
  • Review your gross margin monthly against the benchmarks in Table 1 and investigate any variance over 10%.
