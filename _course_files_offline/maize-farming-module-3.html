3. Planting and Spacing

Planting date and plant population decide more than any fertiliser.

Module 3 of 6 · Maize Farming for Profit

When to plant

  • Plant with the first reliable rains - when 30-40 mm have fallen and the soil is wet 15 cm deep.
  • Do not plant after the first shower if a dry spell is likely - seeds rot.
  • Late planting loses yield at 1-2% per day after the ideal date.

Spacing and population

SpacingPlants/haNotes
75 x 25 cm (1 per hill)53,000High yield, good rains
75 x 30 cm (1 per hill)44,000Standard medium
90 x 30 cm (1 per hill)37,000Dry areas
75 x 60 cm (2 per hill)44,000Traditional two-seed
75 x 90 cm (3 per hill)44,000Old system, wide rows

Best commercial range is 44,000-53,000 plants/ha. Never drop below 35,000.

Planting method

  1. Open the planting hole 5-7 cm deep with a hoe or dibber.
  2. Place 2 seeds per hole (1 if seed is expensive).
  3. Cover with soil, press gently.
  4. If using DAP, place a pinch 5 cm to the side, not touching the seed.
  5. Space evenly.
  6. Thin to 1 plant per hole at 2-3 weeks.

Fertiliser at planting

FertiliserRateWhen
DAP (18-46-0)100-150 kg/haAt planting, 5 cm from seed
Compound (e.g. 23-21-0+4S)150-200 kg/haAt planting
Manure5-10 t/ha1-2 weeks before planting
Never let fertiliser touch the seed. It burns the seedling. Place 5 cm to the side and 5 cm below seed depth.

Thinning

At 2-3 weeks after emergence, thin to 1 plant per hole (or 2 if you used wide row spacing).

  • Thin when plants are 15-20 cm tall.
  • Pull the weaker plants, leave the strongest.
  • Do not delay - late thinning gives poor yields.

First weeding

Weeds compete for water and nutrients. First weeding must happen at 2-3 weeks. Second at 5-6 weeks. After that, the maize canopy shades out most weeds.

The population rule: 44,000-53,000 plants per hectare. Wide spacing that "looks traditional" costs you half your yield.
Practical exercise 3: Mark out a 10 x 10 m plot. Plant at 75 x 30 cm. Count how many plants emerge. Compare to a wide-spaced control plot.

Quiz

  2. How deep should maize seed be planted?
  3. When is the first weeding due?
