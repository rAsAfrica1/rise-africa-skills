7. Harvesting and Threshing

When and how you harvest determines 20% of your rice quality. Here is how to get it right.

Module 7 of 12 · Rice Farming for Profit

Why harvest timing matters

Rice harvested at the right stage loses less than 5% of grain. Rice harvested too early or too late loses 15-30%:

  • Too early: many immature grains, low milling yield, high broken rice
  • Too late: shattering (grain falls off), bird damage, lodging (stems fall over), grain discolouration

The harvest window

IndicatorReady
Panicle colourYellow-brown for 80-90% of grains
Grain hardnessHard when bitten
Grain moisture20-25% at harvest
Straw colourUpper leaves starting to dry
Panicle droopHeads bending down from grain weight
Days from plantingRefer to your variety (90-160 days)

Best window: 25-35 days after flowering. Harvest too soon and you lose milling yield; too late and you lose grain to shattering and birds.

Hand harvesting steps

  1. Drain the field 2 weeks before harvest so soil is firm.
  2. Use a sharp sickle - a dull blade increases losses.
  3. Cut 15-20 cm above the ground, holding the panicle in one hand.
  4. Bundle into sheaves 20-25 cm thick.
  5. Leave sheaves to dry in the field for 3-7 days, upright or in small shocks.
  6. Move to threshing area when grain reaches 18-20% moisture.

Threshing best practice

  • Thresh on a clean, hard surface (tarpaulin, concrete) - not on bare soil.
  • Thresh when grain is 18-22% moisture - not too dry (shatters) or wet (moulds).
  • Keep livestock away - they contaminate the grain.
  • Clean the threshed grain immediately - separate straw and chaff.
  • Re-thresh straw for remaining grain - up to 5% is left in the straw.

Field losses - where they happen

StageTypical loss
Cutting2-4%
Bundling and stacking1-3%
Field drying1-5% (birds, rats, shattering)
Threshing1-3%
Winnowing1-2%

Total7-18%

Combines typically lose 2-4% - a big saving when you can access one.

Winnowing and cleaning

  • Winnowing separates chaff from grain using wind or a fan.
  • Clean grain is essential for good milling yield - foreign matter causes losses.
  • Simple winnowing basket: 100-300 kg/hour with practice.
  • Mechanical winnower: 500-1,500 kg/hour.
  • Clean to under 1% foreign matter for best mill prices.

The harvest rule: Harvest when 80-90% of grains are yellow-brown and hard. Do not wait for 100% - shattering and birds start the moment grain is fully ripe. Aim to finish harvest within 5-7 days of maturity.
Practical exercise 7: Walk through your rice field at 25 days after flowering. Test 10 panicles - how many grains are yellow-brown vs green? Plan harvest for when 80-90% is brown.
