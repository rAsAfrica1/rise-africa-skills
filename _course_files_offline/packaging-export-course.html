â  SAFETY WARNING â Agrochemical Handling and Food Safety

Agrochemicals used in crop production are hazardous to human health and the environment. Always follow these mandatory precautions:

  • Wear personal protective equipment (PPE): gloves, mask, goggles, long sleeves, and boots when mixing or applying any pesticide or herbicide.
  • Never eat, drink, or smoke while handling agrochemicals. Wash hands and exposed skin thoroughly with soap after use.
  • Observe the pre-harvest interval (PHI) stated on the label â failure to do so leaves toxic residues that can cause rejection at export markets and harm consumers.
  • Store chemicals in original labelled containers, locked away from children, food, and animal feed.
  • Dispose of empty containers according to national regulations â never reuse them for food or water.
  • If poisoning is suspected, contact a poison control centre or nearest health facility immediately and bring the product label.

Reference: WHO Recommended Classification of Pesticides by Hazard (2020); FAO International Code of Conduct on Pesticide Management.

Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Test your soil before buying fertiliser. A USD 20â40 soil test can save USD 100+ per hectare in unnecessary inputs.
  2. Calculate your break-even yield and price before the season starts. Know the numbers that define profit or loss.
  3. Secure a market before you plant. Verbal or written off-take agreements reduce price risk and guide crop choice.
  4. Invest in post-harvest handling. Proper drying, threshing, and hermetic storage can recover 20â30% of crop value.
  5. Keep records. Track every input cost, labour hour, and sale. Data is your negotiating tool.
  6. Use certified seed and approved inputs. Counterfeit inputs destroy yields and profits.
  7. Plan logistics early. Identify transport, aggregation points, and timing before harvest to avoid distress sales.
  8. Comply with safety and quality standards. Residue limits, aflatoxin thresholds, and traceability are non-negotiable for premium markets.
