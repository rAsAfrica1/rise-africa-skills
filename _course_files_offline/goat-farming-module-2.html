The genetics you start with decide your profit for the next ten years.

Choosing a breeding doe

  • Body condition: well-fleshed, not fat
  • Udder: two well-formed teats, no lumps
  • Age: 2-5 years (peaked production)
  • Teeth: check for broken or missing
  • Feet: no limping, no long hooves
  • Eyes: bright, no discharge
  • History: has she kidded successfully before?

Choosing a buck

  • Testicles: two, equal size, firm
  • Body: long, deep, well-muscled
  • Age: 1.5-4 years
  • Not related to your does

One buck serves 25-30 does. Change the buck every 2 years to avoid inbreeding.

Crossbreeding for profit

CrossKid weaning weightSurvival
Pure indigenous12-15 kg95%
50% Boer18-22 kg92%
75% Boer22-28 kg88%
The breed rule: Buy the best buck you can afford. He influences 50% of every kid.
Practical exercise 2: Visit 3 goat farms. Look at their does and bucks. Ask about kid survival.
