â ï¸ Safety Warning â Food, Heat, and Chemicals

Tomato processing combines three serious hazards: boiling liquids, caustic cleaning agents, and foodborne pathogens. Observe the following without exception:

  • Scalds: Never fill jars above the shoulder; hot paste at 85Â°C+ causes deep burns. Wear long sleeves, closed shoes, and use tongs â never bare hands.
  • Botulism risk: Low-acid sauces (pH above 4.6) that are not properly acidified or pressure-canned can grow Clostridium botulinum. Always verify pH with a calibrated meter or strips before sealing. Paste must be acidified to pH < 4.2.
  • Chemical handling: Caustic soda and chlorine sanitisers must be stored away from food, in labelled containers, and never mixed with acids. Rinse all surfaces with potable water after sanitising.
  • Mycotoxins: Reject any mouldy or fermented fruit. Aflatoxin and other mycotoxins survive heat processing and cannot be removed by boiling.
  • Water quality: Use only potable water for washing and processing. Contaminated water is the most common cause of failed batch testing in African small-scale units.
