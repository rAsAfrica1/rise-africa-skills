â  SAFETY WARNING â CHEMICAL & FOOD HANDLING

If your agribusiness involves pesticides, fumigants, aflatoxin control, or food processing:

  • Never store agrochemicals near food, feed, or seed. Cross-contamination can be fatal and will destroy buyer trust permanently.
  • Always use PPE (gloves, mask, goggles, boots) when applying or handling chemicals. Train every worker, every season.
  • Respect pre-harvest intervals (PHI) and maximum residue limits (MRLs). Export rejections for MRL violations are costly and often permanent.
  • Aflatoxin: test maize and groundnuts before sale. Levels above 10 ppb (total) or 4 ppb (EU standard for aflatoxin B1) will block export. Use certified drying and hermetic storage.
  • Food safety: follow HACCP principles. Document everything â buyers and regulators will ask.
  • Dispose of chemical containers through licensed waste handlers. Never reuse pesticide containers for water or food.

Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Know your unit economics. Calculate cost per kg (or litre) at every stage â farm gate, transport, processing, packaging. If you cannot state your break-even price, you are not ready to scale.
  2. Lock in off-taker contracts before planting or buying. Verbal agreements collapse at harvest. Get written volume, price mechanism, and payment terms.
  3. Measure and reduce post-harvest loss. Weigh in, weigh out. Track loss weekly. A 5% loss reduction on 100 tonnes is 5 tonnes of pure margin.
  4. Secure working capital early. Agri-finance takes 4-12 weeks to arrange. Start the process before you need the money, not when the truck is at the gate.
  5. Certify what the buyer requires. Do not pay for certifications nobody is asking for. Confirm buyer requirements (GlobalG.A.P., organic, HACCP, phytosanitary) in writing.
  6. Build a simple record system. Paper or digital â but consistent. Buyers, lenders, and regulators all want traceability. Start with batch numbers and dates.
  7. Train your team on safety and quality. One chemical incident or one contaminated batch can end a contract. Budget for annual training.
  8. Review your margin monthly. Input costs, transport, and exchange rates move. What was profitable in January may be loss-making by June.
