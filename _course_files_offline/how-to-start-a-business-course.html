â  SAFETY WARNING â Chemicals, Food and Health

If your business involves food handling, agro-chemicals, pesticides, veterinary products, or cosmetics, the following are non-negotiable:

  • Never store pesticides, fuel or chemicals near food, feed or drinking water. Store them in locked, labelled, ventilated areas.
  • Always wear gloves, eye protection and a mask when handling agro-chemicals. Wash hands and clothing immediately after use.
  • Follow the label and the manufacturer's pre-harvest interval (PHI) exactly â selling produce before the PHI expires can make food unsafe and illegal.
  • Food businesses must maintain clean water, hand-washing points, and cold-chain where required. Keep a daily hygiene log.
  • Register with your national food, drug or standards authority (e.g. KEBS in Kenya, NAFDAC in Nigeria, SAZ in Zimbabwe) before selling regulated products.
  • If a customer or worker reports illness linked to your product, stop sales immediately, retain samples, and report to the relevant authority.

Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Confirm demand first. Talk to at least 10 potential customers before spending money on stock or equipment.
  2. Run the Iron-Clad Price Check. Call 3 suppliers, compare prices by region and season, and record the quotes in your record book.
  3. Write your unit economics. Know your cost price, selling price, gross margin and break-even units per week.
  4. Register the basics. Business name, tax number, and a business bank or mobile-money account in the business name.
  5. Separate business and personal money. Never mix cash â it destroys your ability to see profit.
  6. Target your first sale by Day 60. A paying customer teaches you more than 10 more pages of planning.
  7. Keep a daily record. Sales, expenses, stock and customer names â every single day.
  8. Review monthly. Compare actual results to plan, adjust pricing or costs, and reinvest a fixed percentage of profit.
