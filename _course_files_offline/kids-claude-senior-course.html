â ï¸ Safety Warning â read before you handle any money, food or chemical

If your business touches food, cleaning chemicals, or cash handling, these rules are not optional.

  • Never mix cleaning chemicals. Bleach plus ammonia or acid produces toxic gas. Use one product, ventilate, and wear gloves. Store all chemicals above head height and away from food.
  • Food safety: wash hands with soap before handling any food sold to others; keep raw and cooked food separate; never sell food that has been out of the cold chain for more than two hours in heat above 30Â°C.
  • Cash hygiene: wash or sanitise hands after handling money and before eating or touching your face. Banknotes carry more bacteria than a toilet seat â this is documented, not a scare story.
  • Personal safety: never carry more cash than the day's float. Tell a trusted adult where you are working and when you will return. Do not enter a stranger's house alone.
  • Electrical safety: if you run a phone-charging station, use a surge protector and never daisy-chain extension leads. Overloaded sockets cause fires.
  • If in doubt, stop and ask an adult. No job is worth an injury. Report any burn, cut or chemical splash to an adult immediately and rinse with clean water for 15 minutes.

Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Twenty complaints collected â written down, with the person's name and street, before you choose any business idea.
  2. One-sentence offer written â "I help [this person] do [this thing] for [this price]." If you cannot say it in one breath, it is not ready.
  3. Cost and time measured â you know exactly what one job costs you in materials and minutes before you quote a price.
  4. Price set from margin, not nerves â margin per hour is at least equal to the local minimum informal wage; if not, change the job or the price.
  5. Every job recorded the same day â date, customer, job, money in, cost out, left over, lesson learned.
  6. Customer asked "what would you change?" after every single delivery, and the answer written down.
  7. AI assistance declared â if Claude helped draft or research, tell the customer "AI-assisted" honestly. Trust is the asset.
  8. Profit decision made on purpose â every week you decide what share is reinvested, what is saved, and what is spent. Nothing drifts.
