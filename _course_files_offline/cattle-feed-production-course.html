Quick Reference Checklist

  • Test moisture before buying: Use a cheap moisture meter or the salt-and-bottle method. Reject grain above 13% moisture â it will mould within weeks.
  • Weigh, never guess: Buy a 50 kg hanging scale (under $25). Ration errors of 5% can cut milk yield by 10% or more.
  • Grind to the right particle size: 2â4 mm for dairy, 3â5 mm for beef. Too fine causes acidosis; too coarse wastes feed.
  • Mix in the correct order: Add major ingredients first, then minerals and premix last. Mix for at least 8 minutes for a uniform batch.
  • Store in dry, raised, ventilated space: Use pallets, keep bags 10 cm off the floor and 30 cm from walls. Never store feed in the same room as pesticides or fuel.
  • Label every bag: Date, batch number, protein content, and intended animal (dairy vs beef). This builds trust and lets you trace problems.
  • Price for margin, not volume: Target at least 30% gross margin. If your cost per kg exceeds 70% of the local retail feed price, renegotiate ingredient supply before scaling.
  • Keep a simple record book: Daily ingredient purchases, batch outputs, and sales. This is the single fastest way to find where money leaks.
