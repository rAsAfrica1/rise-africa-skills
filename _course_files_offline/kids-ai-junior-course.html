⚠ SAFETY WARNING - READ BEFORE MODULE 4

  • Never photograph people's faces, ID documents, or private property for training data without written permission from a parent or guardian. In Zimbabwe, Kenya, Nigeria, and most African jurisdictions, personal data is protected (e.g. Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act 2021, Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019, Nigeria's NDPA 2023).
  • Do not upload photos of children to any app or cloud service. Train only on objects: stones, caps, leaves, bottles, tools.
  • Battery and charging safety: never charge a phone on a bare wire or near water. Use a certified charger or solar unit. Unattended charging is a fire risk in thatched or timber structures.
  • Small parts warning: the Module 7 salvage kit uses bottle caps, nails, and cardboard. Children under 7 must be supervised. Nails and blades are adult tools.
  • If a machine gives health, safety, or money advice, a qualified adult must verify it before anyone acts. This is the core lesson of Module 6.

Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Confirm the device plan. Modules 1-3 run with no device. Do not buy a phone for this course. Borrow one for Modules 4-6 only.
  2. Set a data budget. Cap mobile data at 500 MB for the whole course. Download the training app on Wi-Fi or at a hotspot if available.
  3. Collect 30 photos, not 3. Variety in light, angle, and background is what makes the machine work. Same object, many conditions.
  4. Label out loud. Have the child say "this is good" or "this is bad" for every photo. This is the training step in plain language.
  5. Always test on new examples. If the machine only works on the photos it saw, it has memorised, not learned. Module 6 covers this.
  6. Never trust a confident answer. Ask "how do you know?" every time. Teach the child to ask the same of the machine.
  7. Build the salvage kit before Module 7. Collect cardboard, bottle caps, string, and a clean tin at least one week ahead.
  8. Show the work to an adult. The capstone in Module 8 is only complete when someone outside the household has seen and questioned it.
