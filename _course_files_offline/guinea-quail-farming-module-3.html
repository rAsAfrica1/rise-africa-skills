3. Quail - Housing, Feeding, Breeding

Quail are the smallest and fastest-turnover poultry in Africa. 100 birds fit in 5 m2.

Module 3 of 6 · Guinea Fowl and Quail Farming for Profit

Why quail

Quail are the fastest-turnover poultry. Eggs at 6-8 weeks, meat at 6 weeks. 100 quail fit in a 5 m2 cage. Feed is 25 g per bird per day. Investment is $1-2 per chick.

Japanese quail is the standard commercial bird. Buy hatching eggs or day-old chicks from a licensed breeder.

Housing

SystemSpace per birdBest for
Cages (3-4 tier)0.05 m2Commercial, high density
Deep litter0.10 m2Small scale, lower investment
Floor pens0.15 m2Breeding, more natural behaviour

Cage height: 20-25 cm. Wire mesh 1 cm (quail are small and escape through anything larger).

Quail cage setup

  • Frame: wood or metal, 3-4 tiers, 30 cm per tier
  • Mesh floor: sloped 7-10 degrees for egg roll-out
  • Egg tray: at the front, catches eggs
  • Feeder: trough outside the cage, birds peck through mesh
  • Drinker: nipple or small trough
  • Manure tray: slides out for cleaning

Feeding

StageFeedProteinGrams/bird/day
Chicks 0-3 weeksQuail starter or chick mash + protein26-28%8-15 g

Layers 6+ weeksQuail layer mash20-22% + 2.5-3% Ca25-30 g
Meat birdsHigh-protein mash24%Free choice

Quail need high protein - much higher than chickens. Standard chicken layer mash is too low in protein for quail.

Brooding quail chicks

  1. Pre-heat brooder to 35-37 C 24 hours before arrival.
  2. Water with vitamins - dip beaks.
  3. Feed on flat trays or lids for first 3 days.
  4. Cover brooder sides with cardboard or mesh 20 cm high.
  5. Reduce heat 3 C per week.
  6. At 3 weeks, move to grower cages.
  7. At 6 weeks, move to layer cages.
Quail chicks are tiny - the size of a bumblebee. Use 1 cm mesh to prevent escape. Water in shallow dishes with marbles or stones to prevent drowning.

Quail are seasonal in the wild but with controlled lighting and temperature they lay year-round.

Egg handling

  • Collect eggs twice daily - shells are thin and crack easily.
  • Handle gently - even a light squeeze breaks them.
  • Store at 15-18 C, 75% humidity, up to 7 days.
  • For hatching: collect fresh, store pointed-end down.
  • Do not wash - the bloom protects against bacteria.

Sexing quail

Japanese quail are easy to sex at 3-4 weeks:

  • Males: rusty-red breast feathers, cloacal gland (small bump above vent)
  • Females: speckled brown/grey breast, no cloacal gland
  • Voice: males crow (small sharp call)
The quail rule: Quail need 22-24% protein layer feed. Standard chicken mash will not support 250+ eggs per year. Buy or mix quail-specific feed.
Practical exercise 3: Set up a quail brooder and raise 50 chicks. Record temperature, feed, water, and mortality for 3 weeks. Target 90%+ survival.
