6. Harvest, Post-Harvest and Marketing

A 40-year income asset depends on harvesting, handling and selling well every single year.

How to tell if citrus is ready

  • Colour: variety-specific - but colour is not always reliable (Valencia stays green even when fully sweet).
  • Size: full size for the variety.
  • Taste test: cut and taste a fruit from each tree. Sweetness and juice content are the best indicator.
  • Days from flowering: count - most varieties take 7-12 months from flowering to harvest.
  • Fruit firmness: mature fruit is firm but not rock-hard.

Harvesting technique

  1. Harvest in the cool of the day - early morning preferred.
  2. Use sharp, clean snips or secateurs.
  3. Cut the fruit with a short stem (2-3 mm) - do not pull.
  4. Place gently into padded crates or buckets. Never toss.
  5. Do not climb to the top - fruit at the top is often too ripe.
  6. Move to shade immediately after harvest.
  7. Never harvest wet fruit - it rots quickly.

The 40-year rule: Citrus is a 40-year asset. Every year you invest in good management is a year you build yield that will continue for decades. Do not treat year 2 like year 20.
