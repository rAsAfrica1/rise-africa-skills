â  SAFETY WARNING â CHEMICALS AND HEALTH

Pest control chemicals are poisons. They can kill you, your staff, your clients and their children if used wrongly. This is not a warning to skip over.

  • Never use a product that is not registered for the pest and the site you are treating. Unregistered or smuggled chemicals are a criminal and health risk.
  • Always wear the full PPE listed on the label â respirator, goggles, gloves, coverall, boots. No exceptions, even on "small" jobs.
  • Never eat, drink, smoke or rub your eyes while handling chemicals. Wash hands and face after every job.
  • Store chemicals in a locked, ventilated, labelled cabinet away from food, water and children. Keep the safety data sheet (SDS) for every product.
  • If someone shows symptoms of poisoning â vomiting, dizziness, blurred vision, difficulty breathing â call emergency services immediately and take the product label with you.
  • Follow the label dilution rate exactly. More chemical is not more effective â it is more dangerous and wastes money.

Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Confirm licensing first. Check your national pesticide regulator (e.g. Pest Control Products Board in Kenya, EPA Ghana, NAFDAC in Nigeria, Environmental Management Agency in Zimbabwe) before you trade. Unlicensed pest control is illegal and uninsurable.
  2. Quote only after inspection. Never price a job over the phone. Inspect, identify the pest, measure the area, then quote in writing.
  3. Buy registered products from a licensed agro-dealer. Ask for the registration number and the SDS. Refuse repackaged or unlabelled chemicals.
  4. Keep a treatment log. Record date, site, pest, product, batch number, dilution, quantity used, applicator name, and client signature. This protects you legally and wins commercial contracts.
  5. Train every worker on PPE and first aid. No untrained person applies chemicals in your business. Ever.
  6. Target commercial contracts. One restaurant on a monthly contract gives you predictable income. Build a portfolio of 10â15 commercial clients before chasing one-off house calls.
  7. Invoice and receipt everything. Use WhatsApp Business, a receipt book, or a simple bookkeeping app. Paper trails win tenders and bank loans.
  8. Reinvest 20â30% of profit into better equipment, more chemical stock and marketing. The business grows when the kit grows.
