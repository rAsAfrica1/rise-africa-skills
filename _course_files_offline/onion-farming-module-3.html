3. Nursery and Transplanting

Onion seedlings are delicate. This module shows the technique that gives uniform stands and full-size bulbs.

Module 3 of 6 · Onion Farming for Profit

Nursery setup

Raise onion seedlings in trays or a well-prepared nursery bed. Seedlings transplant at 6-8 weeks, when they are pencil-thick.

ItemPer hectare
128-cell trays300-400 trays (for 40,000 plants)
Growing media2,000 L
Onion seed3-4 kg
Shade netting50 m2

Growing media recipe

IngredientParts by volume
Sieved loamy topsoil3

Sowing

  1. Fill trays or prepare nursery bed with fine tilth.
  2. Water before sowing.
  3. Sow seed 6 mm deep, 1-2 seeds per cell.
  4. Cover with sieved media, very thin layer.
  5. Water with a fine mist.
  6. Cover with newspaper or shade cloth for 5-7 days.
  7. Remove cover at first sprout.
  8. Water once daily, morning.

Nursery care

WeekTask
Week 1Keep damp, in shade. Germination 10-14 days.
Week 2-3Water once daily. Remove any weak or spindly seedlings.
Week 4Reduce shade. Begin hardening off.
Week 5-6Full sun. Water every 2 days.
Week 6-8Ready to transplant when pencil-thick.

Hardening off

DayExposure
Day 1-250% shade, half day
Day 3-4Full sun, half day
Day 5-7Full sun all day

Transplanting

  1. Water seedlings 2 hours before.
  2. Trim the tops and roots to 2.5 cm (optional but improves uniformity).
  3. Transplant in the late afternoon or on a cloudy day.
  4. Set seedlings 2-3 cm deep, just above the root plate.
  5. Firm gently, water immediately.
  6. Shade for 3 days.
  7. Water every other day for week 1.

Spacing

Bulb size targetSpacingPlants/ha
Large (80+ mm)15 x 15 cm440,000
Medium (60-80 mm)10 x 15 cm660,000
Small / bunching8 x 15 cm830,000

Most commercial production targets medium bulbs at 10 x 15 cm.

Never transplant onion seedlings too deep. If the growing point is buried, the plant will not form a bulb.
The transplant rule: Transplants at 6-8 weeks when pencil-thick. Older or thicker transplants bolt instead of forming bulbs.
Practical exercise 3: Raise 500 onion seedlings. Track germination. Transplant at 6-8 weeks. Record survival at 2 weeks after transplant.
