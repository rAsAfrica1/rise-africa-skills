The premium positioning

Both species sell on scarcity and exclusivity. To hold premium prices:

  • Never sell to the same buyer at wholesale prices. Target restaurants and premium retail, not market bulk.
  • Present well. Clean, labelled, uniform packaging.
  • Consistent supply. A restaurant needs 20 guinea fowl every week - not 60 one week and 0 the next.
  • Educate buyers. Offer recipes, cooking tips, and point out health benefits.
  • Stay small on purpose. Doubling production kills your price. Grow buyers first, then volume.

The scarcity rule: Both birds sell best when local supply is tight. Never flood one market. Grow buyers before you grow the flock.
Practical exercise 6: Contact 5 restaurants and 3 health food shops. Offer samples. Ask what they would pay weekly for a consistent supply. Build your buyer list before scaling production.
