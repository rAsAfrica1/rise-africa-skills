Mushroom House — Brick

A fixed brick grow-house for oyster and button mushrooms, sized for a small commercial operation. Thermal mass, humidity control, air exchange and contamination control.

12 modulesWritten methodKnowledge checks and capstones

The 12 modules

  1. The Mushroom Business and What the House Must Do
  2. Siting, Orientation and Site Drainage
  3. Walling, Roof and Thermal Mass
  4. The Insulated Ceiling and Vapour Barrier
  5. Humidity Control, Misting and Water Supply
  6. Air Exchange, CO₂ and Fresh Air Without Contamination
  7. Shelving, Beds and Grow Bag Support
  8. Pasteurisation Room and Substrate Handling Area
  9. Contamination Control and Clean/Dirty Routes
  10. Lighting, Harvesting Access and Cold Holding
  11. Building Two Rooms — Grow and Rest Cycles
  12. Running the House and Pricing the Crop

Who this is for

Smallholders wanting to grow mushrooms commercially, food processors extending into fresh produce, and cooperatives setting up a shared grow-house. No prior mushroom training required.

This course teaches general principles of mushroom house construction. Mushroom species have specific temperature, humidity and CO₂ requirements that vary. Confirm the requirements for the species you intend to grow before building.
