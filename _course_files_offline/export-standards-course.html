Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Validate your market before you produce. Secure at least one signed letter of intent or off-take agreement with a buyer before planting or expanding processing capacity.
  2. Calculate your true cost per unit. Include labour, energy, transport, packaging, spoilage, and finance costs â not just raw material. Review monthly.
  3. Test your soil and water. A basic soil test (USD 20â50) prevents fertiliser waste and yield losses worth hundreds of dollars per hectare.
  4. Register your business and product. In Nigeria, NAFDAC registration opens retail and export markets. In Kenya, KEBS certification. Budget time and fees early.
  5. Invest in moisture meters and weighing scales. Accurate measurement protects you in both buying raw materials and selling finished goods.
  6. Build a 60-day cash buffer. Agribusiness is seasonal; working capital shortages kill profitable operations.
  7. Train staff on food safety and equipment maintenance. One contamination incident or breakdown can wipe out a season's profit.
  8. Track post-harvest losses weekly. If losses exceed 10%, investigate handling, storage, and transport immediately.
