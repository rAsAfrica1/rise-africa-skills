â  SAFETY WARNING â Agro-chemicals and Aflatoxin

Read this before you spray or store grain.

  • Always wear gloves, a long-sleeved shirt, trousers, boots, and a face mask when handling pesticides or treating seed. Never spray against the wind.
  • Store agro-chemicals in their original labelled containers, locked away from children, food, and animal feed. Never use pesticide containers to carry drinking water.
  • Observe the pre-harvest interval (PHI) printed on the label â spraying too close to harvest leaves residues that can cause your grain to be rejected.
  • Aflatoxin is a serious health hazard. Never eat or sell mouldy, discoloured, or musty-smelling grain. Dry maize to 13% moisture or below before storage.
  • If you feel dizzy, nauseous, or have blurred vision after spraying, stop immediately, wash with soap and water, and seek medical help. Take the chemical label with you.

Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Soil test before you plant. Know your pH, organic matter, and nutrient levels â it saves money on fertiliser you do not need.
  2. Use certified seed and treat it properly. Buy from registered agro-dealers and keep receipts for traceability.
  3. Plant on time with the first reliable rains. Late planting can cut yields by 30â50% in maize.
  4. Scout your fields weekly. Early detection of pests and diseases is cheaper than late spraying.
  5. Dry grain to 13% moisture before storage. Use a moisture meter; if you don't have one, salt-test or bite-test as a rough guide.
  6. Store in hermetic bags or clean, treated stores. Never mix new grain with old grain.
  7. Keep records of every input, cost, and sale. You cannot manage what you do not measure.
  8. Know your market before harvest. Check prices weekly via radio, SMS platforms, or local market bulletins and consider group selling.
