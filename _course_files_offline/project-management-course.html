â  SAFETY WARNING â Site, Chemical and Electrical Work

If your project involves construction, agro-chemicals, food handling, or electrical installation, the following are non-negotiable:

  • Never allow workers on site without personal protective equipment â helmets, boots, gloves, eye protection. This is a legal duty in most African jurisdictions and a direct liability on you as project manager.
  • Never store pesticides, solvents, or fuel near food, feed, or water sources. Keep a spill kit and a written disposal plan before the chemicals arrive on site.
  • Never allow unlicensed persons to work on live electrical installations. Isolate, lock off, and tag before any intervention.
  • Always keep a signed attendance and induction register. If an accident occurs and you cannot prove the worker was trained, the liability falls on you personally.
  • Always report and record every incident â even near-misses. Undocumented incidents become undocumented claims.

Reference: International Labour Organization, Safety and Health in Construction, Code of Practice, and WHO, International Health Regulations â Chemical Safety guidance. Check your national labour and environmental regulator for local statutory requirements before mobilising.

Quick Reference Checklist

  • Scope signed before spend. No written scope statement, no purchase order. Verbal agreements are not project controls.
  • Three quotes on every major line. Materials, transport, and equipment hire. Record the quotes in your project file â this is your audit trail.
  • Cash flow forecast in weeks, not months. African payment cycles are lumpy. Know which week you run dry before it happens.
  • Risk register updated weekly. Every risk gets an owner, a trigger, and a response. If nobody owns it, it is not managed.
  • Change control in writing. Any client request that alters scope, cost, or time gets a signed change note before work starts.
  • EVM snapshot every Friday. CPI and SPI calculated and recorded. Two consecutive bad weeks triggers a corrective action plan.
  • Safety induction logged. Every new worker signs the induction register before stepping on site.
  • Final account closed within 30 days. Handover certificate, retention release, and lessons-learned note filed together.
