3. Nursery and Transplanting

90% of tomato failures start in the first 14 days. Master the nursery and everything else is easy.

Module 3 of 6 · Tomato Farming for Profit

The nursery setup

Raise seedlings in 128-cell trays, not open beds. Trays give uniform plants, less root damage, and 90-95% survival.

Growing media recipe

IngredientPart by volume
Sieved loamy topsoil3
Well-rotted compost3
Coarse river sand1

Sowing

  1. Fill trays, firm gently, water before sowing.
  2. Sow 1-2 seeds per cell, 5 mm deep.
  3. Cover with a thin layer of sieved media.
  4. Water with a fine mist.
  5. Place in shade. Cover with newspaper for 3-5 days.
  6. Remove cover as soon as sprouts show.
  7. Water once daily, morning only.

Hardening off - the 7-day rule

DaySun exposure
Day 12 hours, morning sun
Day 24 hours
Day 36 hours
Day 48 hours
Day 5Full sun all day
Day 6Overnight outdoors
Day 7Ready to transplant
Never transplant unhardened seedlings. They look fine in the tray and collapse in the field within 48 hours.

Transplanting

  1. Water seedlings 2 hours before transplanting.
  2. Transplant in late afternoon or on a cloudy day.
  3. Water destination beds first.
  4. Lift seedlings by a leaf, never the stem.
  5. Set the root ball level with the surface.
  6. Firm gently, eliminate air pockets.
  7. Water immediately.
  8. Shade for 3 days.
  9. Water every other day for week 1.
Recommended spacing: 60 cm between plants, 100 cm between rows = 16,500 plants/ha.
Practical exercise 3: Raise 100 seedlings this week. Track germination daily. Target 95%+ at day 10.
