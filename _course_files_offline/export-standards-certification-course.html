â  SAFETY WARNING â Pesticide Residues and Food Safety

Export rejection for Maximum Residue Level (MRL) breaches is the single most common cause of lost shipments from Africa. Never apply a pesticide that is not registered for your specific crop in your destination market. Observe pre-harvest intervals (PHI) strictly â cutting a PHI short to meet a vessel deadline can destroy an entire consignment and trigger a RASFF alert that blocks your country's exports for months. Store all agrochemicals in a locked, ventilated, signed store away from food, feed and water sources. Train every spray operator on label reading, PPE use, and record-keeping. Keep a residue test result from an ISO 17025-accredited laboratory for high-risk crops (green beans, chili, sesame, herbs) before every shipment. If you cannot document it, do not ship it.
