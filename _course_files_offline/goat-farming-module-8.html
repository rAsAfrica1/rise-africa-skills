8. Advanced Health and Disease Management

Prevention, quarantine, and the routine that keeps the whole flock healthy.

Module 8 of 12 · Goat Farming for Profit

The FAMACHA system

FAMACHA uses eyelid colour to decide if a goat needs deworming. This prevents over-deworming (which creates resistant worms).

ScoreEyelid colourAction
1Red, healthyNo treatment
2Red-pinkNo treatment
3PinkMonitor closely
4Pink-whiteDeworm now
5WhiteDeworm now, urgent - may also need iron

Check 10-20 goats per month. Only treat scores 4 and 5. This slows resistance.

Quarantine protocol

  1. Separate pen 50-100 m from main flock.
  2. Separate boots and tools. Never share.
  3. Feed and water new goats last in the daily routine.
  4. Watch for 21 days. Any sign of disease - treat or cull.
  5. Deworm and vaccinate before introduction.
  6. Introduce gradually - 1-2 goats at a time to the main flock.
Never skip quarantine. One infected goat can wipe out a 50-goat flock in 3 weeks with PPR.

Hoof care

  • Check hooves every 3-4 months.
  • Trim overgrown hooves with sharp hoof shears.
  • Cut back to the sole level - do not cut into the quick.
  • Footbath with 10% zinc sulphate after trimming if any sign of rot.
  • Provide dry, rocky areas for natural hoof wear.

Body condition scoring

ScoreDescriptionAction
1Very thin - bones visibleFeed more, check for worms/disease
2ThinIncrease feed
3Ideal - ribs felt but not seenMaintain
4FatReduce feed if not pregnant
5ObeseReduce feed, check for pregnancy toxaemia risk

Check body condition monthly. Score 3 is target for does, 3-4 for bucks in breeding season.

Daily health routine

  1. Morning: Walk the flock. Check for limping, coughing, dull eyes, off-feed animals.
  2. Midday: Check water. Count animals - a missing goat means a problem.
  3. Evening: Observe feeding behaviour. Sick goats eat last or not at all.
  4. Weekly: FAMACHA check 10 goats at random.
  5. Monthly: Body condition score all adults.
  6. Every 3 months: Hoof trimming, routine deworm.
  7. Annually: PPR, CCPP vaccines.
The prevention rule: Vaccines and biosecurity are cheaper than treatment. A $2 vaccine prevents a $100 loss. Do the routine.
Practical exercise 8: Set up a health calendar for your flock. Mark vaccination dates, deworm dates, hoof trimming, FAMACHA checks. Post it where you see it daily.
