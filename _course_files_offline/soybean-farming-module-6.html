Lesson 1

The Soybean Price Calendar

In this lesson
  • Map soybean price seasonality
  • Use storage to sell into the peak
  • Understand the global commodity link

Soybean prices track the global soybean meal market plus a local premium/discount. In most African markets:

  • Harvest months: lowest prices, $0.35–0.50/kg
  • 3–6 months after harvest: rising, $0.50–0.65/kg
  • Late dry season: peak, $0.65–0.90/kg
  • Feed industry shortage: can spike above $1.00/kg

Using storage

Hermetic storage lets you hold soybean 6–12 months. If you harvest at $0.40/kg and sell in the off-season at $0.70/kg, storage adds 75% to your revenue on the stored portion. Cost of hermetic bags is repaid many times over.

Lesson 2

The Five Buyer Types

In this lesson
  • Name and price the five soybean buyers
  • Match quality to buyer
  • Build buyer loyalty

1. Local market vendors

Small lots, cash, $0.35–0.55/kg. Baseline price.

2. Feed mills

5–50 tonne lots, $0.45–0.70/kg. Require low moisture, clean grain, sometimes protein test. Payment on delivery or 30 days.

3. Food processors

Soya chunks, soya flour, soya milk manufacturers. Buy in 5–20 tonne lots, $0.60–1.00/kg. Require food-grade quality, testing, sometimes organic certification.

4. Oil millers

Buy for oil extraction, $0.40–0.65/kg. Accept any clean grain with specific oil content (18–20%).

5. Seed producers and aggregators

Certified seed producers buy certified parent seed at 2–3x food price. Aggregators buy for regional export or supply contracts.

Lesson 3

Value Addition — Soya Chunks, Flour, Oil

In this lesson
  • Understand the soy value-addition process
  • Calculate the margin multiplier
  • Choose your value-add step

Soya chunks / textured vegetable protein

Process: clean soybean → dehull → defat (or use full-fat) → extrude at high temperature and pressure → dry → pack. Yield: 1 kg soybean → 0.7–1.0 kg chunks. Price: $2.00–4.00/kg. Requires extruder ($1,500–15,000).

Soya flour

Process: clean → roast → grind. Yield: 1 kg soybean → 0.9 kg flour. Price: $1.50–3.00/kg. Requires a mill ($300–2,000).

Soya milk

Process: soak → grind → boil → filter → flavour → bottle. Yield: 1 kg soybean → 5–8 L milk. Price: $1.50–3.00/L. Requires milk processing equipment and cold chain.

Soybean oil

Process: press or solvent extract. Yield: 1 kg soybean → 0.18 kg oil + 0.80 kg cake. Oil price: $1.20–2.50/kg. Requires press ($2,000–15,000).

Lesson 5

The One-Page Business Plan

In this lesson
  • Complete the one-page plan
  • Set year 1 and year 2 targets
  • Decide scale triggers

Before next season, fill in this one page:

  1. Business type: raw grain, food processing, or seed?
  2. Capital tier: how much will you invest?
  3. Land and water: hectares, water source, drainage
  4. Variety and why
  5. Target yield: 2 t/ha in year 1
  6. Buyers: three named contacts
  7. Revenue target: price × yield
  8. Total cost: every line
  9. Net margin: revenue − cost
  10. Value step: what product to add in 12 months
  11. Scaling trigger: condition before expanding

Knowledge check — Module 6

1. What drives soybean demand globally?

Correct: feed industry. Soybean meal is 20–30% of poultry feed.

2. What is the margin multiplier for soya chunks?

Correct: 3–6x. Requires an extruder ($1,500–15,000).

3. How many litres of soya milk from 1 kg soybean?

Correct: 5–8 L. Needs cold chain for sale.

4. How much oil from 1 kg soybean?

Correct: 0.18 kg oil plus 0.80 kg cake. The cake sells as feed.

5. When should you scale the soybean business?

Correct: after two profitable cycles. Buyer contract first, then scale.
