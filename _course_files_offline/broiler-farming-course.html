Course

Broiler Farming for Profit

Broilers are the fastest-turnover livestock enterprise for a smallholder. A batch of 200 birds finishes in 6-7 weeks, uses a small structure, and pays back its capital inside one cycle.

Home · All courses · Broiler Farming for Profit

What this course covers

  • How to calculate the real cost of a 200-bird broiler batch before you start
  • How to build or adapt housing that keeps broilers healthy and gaining weight
  • How to brood day-old chicks with 95%+ survival through the first 2 weeks
  • How to feed for the best FCR at the lowest cost
  • How to vaccinate and keep disease out of a broiler house
  • How to slaughter, dress, chill and sell for premium market prices

Modules

  • Module 11. The Broiler Business
  • Module 22. Housing and Equipment
  • Module 33. Brooding Day-Old Chicks
  • Module 44. Feeding for Weight and FCR
  • Module 55. Health and Biosecurity
  • Module 66. Slaughter, Processing and Selling

Who this course is for

  • Smallholders starting a first broiler batch on 100-500 birds
  • Farmers who already keep chickens but lose money on every batch
  • Youth groups and women's groups setting up a collective broiler project
  • Anyone wanting a 7-week cash cycle instead of a seasonal harvest
  • Agripreneurs supplying restaurants, hotels or supermarkets

What you will need

  • A small structure or the ability to build one (3m x 4m holds 200 broilers)
  • A reliable day-old chick supplier and a feed supplier
  • Daily time - 20 minutes for 200 birds, 45 minutes for 500
  • A notebook for daily feed, water and mortality records
  • Starting capital of 250-600 USD for a 200-bird batch
