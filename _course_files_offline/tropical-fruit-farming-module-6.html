Finding buyers

  1. Contact your national horticulture board for export packhouse contacts.
  2. Visit urban supermarkets and juice bars - ask for the produce buyer.
  3. Talk to fruit juice processors - they often buy in bulk.
  4. Join a farmer group or cooperative for volume and export access.
  5. Consider Fair Trade or GlobalG.A.P. for premium prices.

Selling strategy

SituationBest action
Local glutDry, juice, or jam the excess - do not sell at any price
Steady supermarket demandSupply consistently graded, packed fruit weekly
Export contact availableMeet grade, packaging, and volume requirements before committing
No buyer yetStart with local markets and juice bars, build relationships

The value-add rule: Fresh tropical fruit loses 30-50% to spoilage. Every kg that goes into drying, juicing, or pulp keeps 100% of its value in a shelf-stable product.
