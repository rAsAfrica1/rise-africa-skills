6. Eggs: Collection, Grading and Selling

Eggs are the perfect product: fresh daily, easy to grade, always in demand. The margin is made in handling and marketing.

Module 6 of 6 · Layer Farming for Profit · quiz at the end

Lesson 6.1 - Collection

  1. Collect 2-3 times per day. Morning, midday, evening.
  2. Use clean, dry hands or a soft basket. Wet hands spread bacteria.
  3. Do not wash eggs destined for market. Washing removes the bloom that protects the egg.
  4. If you must wash, use warm water (warmer than the egg) with food-safe sanitiser. Dry immediately.
  5. Sort damaged eggs immediately. One cracked egg can spoil the tray.
  6. Record daily total. Track lay rate by week.

Lesson 6.4 - Storage

TemperatureStorage life
Ambient (25-30 C)7-10 days
Cool room (15 C)3-4 weeks
Refrigerator (4 C)6-8 weeks

Store eggs away from strong smells, meat, fish, direct sun, and temperature swings.

Lesson 6.5 - Buyers

Approaching a new buyer:

  1. Go in person, morning, before service.
  2. Bring a tray of your best eggs as sample.
  3. Ask their current price, weekly volume, delivery day.
  4. Offer a trial: one week's supply. Then agree a standing order.
  5. Deliver on time. Every time.
The consistency rule: A cafe buying 30 eggs per day, every day, for a year is worth more than a market day where you sell 300 eggs once.

Lesson 6.6 - Records and scaling

RecordWhat to write
EggsDaily count, grade split, cracked/soiled
Feedkg per day, cost per kg
MortalityDeaths, cause
SalesBuyer, eggs, price, total
ExpensesFeed, medication, labour, transport
VaccinationDate, product, batch, dose

Monthly, calculate: lay rate (target 80%+), cost per egg (under 0.10), feed conversion (under 1.8 kg/dozen), net profit.

Scale only when:

  • Three consecutive profitable months
  • Lay rate above 80%
  • Mortality under 1% per month
  • Buyer confirmed for extra output
  • Housing ready before new chicks arrive

Lesson 6.7 - Selling spent hens

At 72-80 weeks, lay rate drops below 60%. Sell the flock as spent hens:

  • Price 40-50% of a live broiler - usually 3-5 USD per hen.
  • Sell locally as soup or stew hens.
  • Best to sell in one batch, not piecemeal - clears the house for the next flock.
  • Empty, clean, rest the house 10-14 days.
  • Start the next batch of chicks before the old flock leaves - 18-week lead time.
Practical exercise 6.1: Visit 5 potential egg buyers. Ask current price, weekly volume, delivery day. Bring a sample tray.
Practical exercise 6.2: Calculate cost per egg from last month's records. Write it on the wall. Never sell below cost.
