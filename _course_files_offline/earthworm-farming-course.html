â  HEALTH & FOOD SAFETY WARNING

Vermicompost and worm tea can carry E. coli, Salmonella and other pathogens if feedstock includes fresh manure, meat, dairy or pet waste. Never apply raw worm tea directly to leafy greens or root crops you will eat raw. Compost must reach a curing stage before use on food crops. Wear gloves when handling feedstock and wash hands after every session. Do not feed worms meat, dairy, oily or cooked food â it attracts pests, flies and rodents. Keep children and animals away from beds. If you feel unwell after handling waste, seek medical advice. Sell only cured, screened compost to food growers, and label worm tea clearly with dilution instructions.

Quick Reference Checklist

  2. Site the beds in shade: 60â70% moisture, 15â25Â°C, away from direct sun and flooding. Raise beds 10 cm off the ground.
  3. Balance the feedstock: aim for a C:N ratio near 25:1. Mix dry carbon (paper, stover) with wet nitrogen (kitchen waste, manure).
  4. Never overfeed: add feed only when the previous layer is nearly consumed. Overfeeding causes heat, smell and worm death.
