4. Feeding, Watering and Sucker Management

Banana is a heavy feeder. The right sucker management doubles your harvest.

Module 4 of 6 · Banana Farming for Profit

The banana plant cycle

Understanding the banana growth cycle is the key to year-round harvest:

StageDurationWhat happens
Establishment3-4 monthsRoots and leaves develop
Vegetative growth4-6 monthsPseudostem grows tall, leaves expand
Flowering2-3 monthsFlower emerges from the top
Fruit fill3-4 monthsBunch grows and fills
HarvestOne eventCut the bunch, plant dies back
Sucker takes over-One sucker becomes the next "mother"

Each plant produces one bunch, then dies back. A sucker takes its place. Managing suckers is the single most important management task.

Sucker management

Each banana plant produces 5-15 suckers. You cannot keep them all - they compete for water and nutrients.

SystemSuckers per plantNotes
Single stem1 (the follower)Largest bunches, easiest management
Double stem2Balanced yield and continuity
Multi-stem3-4Higher total yield but smaller bunches

For most smallholders: keep 1-2 suckers per plant - one "follower" and one "young" sucker for continuity.

Selecting the right sucker

  • Choose a sword sucker growing from the base, close to the ground.
  • Position: on the opposite side from the previous bunch - so the plant rotates around the spot.
  • Remove all other suckers by cutting them at ground level or twisting them out.
  • Check monthly - new suckers appear constantly.
The sucker rule: One mother + one follower + one young sucker = perfect rotation. More than that and every plant starves.

Split fertiliser into 3-4 applications per year. Never apply all at once.

Watering

StageWater needFrequency
Establishment (month 1-3)20-30 L/week2-3 times per week
Vegetative (month 4-6)50-80 L/weekEvery 2-3 days
Flowering (month 7-9)80-120 L/weekEvery 2 days
Fruit fill (month 10-12)100-150 L/weekEvery 2 days

Drip irrigation delivers water and fertiliser efficiently, and reduces disease by keeping foliage dry.

Mulching

Top up mulch every 3-4 months. Banana responds dramatically to mulch - it is the single cheapest yield increase. Use dry grass, banana leaves, chopped pseudostem, or compost.

De-suckering tools

Use a sharp knife or machete. Cut at ground level or use a "banana spear" to cut below the soil. Do not leave stumps - they rot and harbour weevils.

Practical exercise 4: Walk your banana plot and count suckers per plant. Remove excess suckers to leave 1-2 per mat. Record the number removed and the sizes of retained suckers.
