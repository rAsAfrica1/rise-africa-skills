Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Map your referral chain before you open. Know the name, phone number, and travel time to the nearest Tier 3 and Tier 4 facility. Write it on a wall chart in the consultation room.
  2. Stock only the national essential medicines list. Extra SKUs tie up cash and expire. Track batch numbers and expiry dates monthly.
  3. Verify your cold chain weekly. Log fridge temperatures twice daily. A failed vaccine fridge destroys both stock and community trust.
  4. Train every staff member in danger-sign recognition. Maternal danger signs, severe dehydration, and fast breathing in children must trigger immediate referral, not observation.
  5. Price for break-even, not for charity. Calculate your daily patient-visit target and review it quarterly against actual attendance.
  6. Keep a referral transport fund. Even USD 200 set aside prevents the commonest cause of delayed referral â no fuel money at 2 a.m.
  7. Report notifiable diseases within 24 hours. Build a one-page form and a phone contact for the district surveillance officer.
  8. Audit your records monthly. Count consultations, referrals, deliveries, and stock-outs. Data is what unlocks government and donor financing.
