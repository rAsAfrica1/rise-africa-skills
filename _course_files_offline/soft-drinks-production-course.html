â  SAFETY WARNING â Chemical and Food Safety Hazards

Post-harvest chemical use and mould contamination can cause serious illness or death. Observe the following without exception:

  • Aflatoxin: Never sell or consume maize, groundnuts, or other grains with visible mould, discolouration, or a musty smell. Aflatoxin is a carcinogen and is not destroyed by normal cooking. Dry grain to below 13% moisture and store hermetically.
  • Fumigation: Phosphine and other fumigants are highly toxic. Only trained, licensed operators may fumigate. Never enter a fumigated store or container without proper respiratory protection and gas monitoring. Keep children and animals away.
  • Pesticide residues: Respect pre-harvest intervals and maximum residue limits (MRLs). Buyers in the EU and formal African retail chains reject consignments that exceed MRLs. Keep spray records.
  • Personal protective equipment: Wear gloves, masks, and eye protection when handling any post-harvest chemical. Wash hands and clothing after use. Never eat, drink, or smoke during application.
  • Cold chain and food hygiene: Keep perishables out of the temperature danger zone (5â60Â°C). Clean crates and cold rooms between cycles to prevent cross-contamination and fungal growth.

Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Measure before you invest. Weigh a sample consignment at harvest and again at sale. Calculate your actual loss percentage per stage. Do not guess.
  2. Target the biggest loss stage first. Use Table 1 to identify whether your problem is handling, storage, or transport â then act there.
  3. Dry grain to safe moisture. Maize and beans below 13% moisture; test with a moisture meter before storage. This prevents aflatoxin and insect damage.
  4. Use the right packaging. Ventilated plastic crates for perishables; hermetic bags or silos for grains. Never stack perishables more than three crates high in transit.
  5. Keep records. Log harvest date, weight, storage conditions, transport time, and sale weight. Records are your evidence for buyer contracts and credit applications.
  6. Train your team on hygiene and chemical safety. Every worker handling produce or chemicals must know the MRL rules, PPE requirements, and aflatoxin red flags.
  7. Secure a buyer before you scale. Confirm price, volume, quality grade, and payment terms in writing before expanding production or buying equipment.
  8. Review payback every cycle. Track the value recovered against the cost of each intervention. Drop or adjust anything that does not pay back within five cycles.
