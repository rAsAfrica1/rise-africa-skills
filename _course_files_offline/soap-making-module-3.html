3. Safety with Caustic Soda

Caustic soda will blind you, scar you, or kill you if you treat it carelessly. It is the single most dangerous thing in your kitchen. Learn the safety rules once and follow them every single time.

Module 3 of 12 · Soap Making · practical exercises · quiz at the end

Lesson 3.1 - Why lye is dangerous

Sodium hydroxide is a strong base. It reacts violently with water, skin oils, and eyeball tissue.

  • On skin: feels slippery, then soapy, then burns deep. Damage continues for hours after contact unless rinsed off.
  • In the eye: can cause permanent blindness within minutes.
  • If swallowed: causes fatal internal burns.
  • Mixed with water: the water reaches 90°C in seconds - hot enough to melt plastic.

This is not a scare tactic. Every year, small-scale soap makers end up in hospital because they skipped one safety step.

Lesson 3.2 - The non-negotiable PPE

ItemCostWhy
Chemical splash goggles (sealed)$4-8Lye in the eye is an emergency
Rubber or nitrile gloves$2-5Long cuff to protect forearms
Long-sleeve shirt and trousers-No shorts, no sandals
Closed shoes or boots-No bare feet
N95 or better mask$3-6Lye fumes irritate lungs

Put all of these on before you open the lye container. Every single time. No exceptions.

Lesson 3.3 - Mixing lye safely

  1. Always add lye to water - never water to lye. Adding water to lye causes an instant boil-over.
  2. Use cold water - ideally with ice cubes in it - to absorb the heat.
  3. Mix in a well-ventilated space - outdoors or near an open window. Fumes are corrosive.
  4. Use a heat-proof container - stainless steel or heavy HDPE plastic. Never aluminium (it reacts), never glass (it can shatter).
  5. Stir slowly with a stainless steel or silicone spoon. The mixture will get hot and cloudy, then turn clear.
  6. Wait for it to cool to 40-45°C before adding to your oils.
  7. Never lean over the container while it is reacting.
Water-to-lye rule: "Snow on the mountain, not mountain on the snow." Lye flakes go ON the water. Never pour water onto lye.

Lesson 3.4 - First aid

ExposureImmediate action
Skin contactRinse with cool running water 15-20 min. Do not use vinegar or any acid - it makes it worse.
Eye splashFlush eyes with water 15-20 min. Go to hospital immediately.
Inhaled fumesMove to fresh air. Sit upright. If breathing is hard, go to hospital.
SwallowedDo not induce vomiting. Drink water. Hospital immediately.
The safety rule: A $5 pair of goggles is cheaper than a hospital visit. Wear the PPE every single time, even for "just one small batch".
Practical exercise 3.1: Buy and wear full PPE for a practice mix. Dissolve 50 g of lye in 100 g of water. Feel how hot it gets. Get comfortable with the process before making soap.
Practical exercise 3.2: Write your emergency plan on paper: nearest hospital, poison control number, first-aid steps. Tape it to the wall of your work area.
