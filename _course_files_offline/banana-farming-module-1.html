1. Why Banana Is a Cash Machine

Banana is the highest-yielding food crop in Africa. Plant once, harvest for 10+ years.

Module 1 of 6 · Banana Farming for Profit

The banana opportunity

Banana is one of the highest-yielding food crops in the world. A well-managed hectare produces 30-60 tonnes of fruit per year, every year, for 10-20 years from a single planting. No annual crop comes close.

Why banana works

  • Year-round harvest - daily or weekly cash flow, not seasonal
  • Perennial - plant once, harvest for 10-20 years
  • Multiple products - fruit, leaves (fodder), pseudostem (animal feed), suckers (sale)
  • Local demand always strong - banana is a staple in many regions
  • Reliable yield - less weather-sensitive than annual crops
  • Intercrop friendly - plant with coffee, cocoa, or vegetables for first 2 years

The three big challenges

  • Panama disease (TR4) - the killer. Once in the soil, there is no cure.
  • Banana weevil - tunnels in the corm and destroys the plant
  • Black sigatoka - leaf disease that reduces yield 30-50%

What you need to start

  • Land with deep, well-drained soil, pH 5.5-7.0
  • Certified tissue-culture plantlets or clean suckers (not from a diseased field)
  • Reliable irrigation or 1,200+ mm rainfall
  • Windbreak on exposed sites
  • Starting capital of $800-2,500 for 0.5 ha
The sucker rule: Buy tissue-culture plantlets or certified clean suckers. Suckers from a neighbouring farm can carry Panama disease and weevils that will destroy your plot within 5 years.
Practical exercise 1: Visit 3 markets. Check the current banana price per kg. Check if there is a chips or banana flour factory nearby that buys in bulk.
