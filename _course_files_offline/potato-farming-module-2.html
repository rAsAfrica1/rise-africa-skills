What is certified seed?

Certified seed potatoes are grown under strict conditions to be disease-free. They cost 3-5x more than table potatoes but yield 2-3x more.

Never use table potatoes as seed. They carry viruses that cut yield by 50% or more and infect your soil for years.

Seed size and cutting

  • Whole small tubers (30-45 mm) - best, no cutting needed
  • Cut large tubers - cut into 2-3 pieces, each with 2-3 eyes
  • Cut seed: allow 2-3 days to heal (form a cork layer) before planting
  • Never plant freshly cut seed - it rots in the soil
  • Seed rate: 2-2.5 tonnes/ha for whole small seed

Chitting (pre-sprouting)

Chitting = sprouting seed potatoes before planting. It speeds emergence by 7-14 days and increases yield 10-15%.

  1. Spread seed tubers in a single layer in a cool, bright room.
  2. Keep 2-3 weeks before planting.
  3. Sprouts should be 1-2 cm, green, and sturdy.
  4. Handle carefully - broken sprouts set back the crop.
The seed rule: Certified seed pays back 2-3x the extra cost in yield. Never cut this corner.
