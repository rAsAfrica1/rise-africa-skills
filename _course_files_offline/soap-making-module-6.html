6. Choosing Oils and Butters

Every oil brings something different to soap - hardness, lather, mildness, colour, shelf life. Once you know what each one does, you can design recipes instead of copying them.

Lesson 6.3 - Recipe design principles

  1. Balance hard vs soft oils. Aim for 40-55% hard oils (coconut, palm, shea, tallow) and 45-60% soft oils (olive, sunflower, avocado).
  2. Keep coconut oil at 20-30%. Above 30% it strips skin moisture.
  3. Use castor at 5-8%. More than 10% makes the bar sticky.
  4. Superfat 5% for body soap, 8% for sensitive skin, 3% for laundry soap.
  5. Water:lye ratio 2:1 is standard. Higher for soft oils, lower for hard bars.
  6. Insulate the mould for the first 24 hours - especially in cool weather - to prevent soda ash.

For Recipe C, use 8% superfat - the extra free oils make it mild enough for children.

The recipe rule: Start by copying one of the three recipes above. Make it three times. Then change one oil at a time and note the difference. Do not change everything at once.
