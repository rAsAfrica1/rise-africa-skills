â  SAFETY WARNING â CLIENT DATA, PAYMENTS, AND ELECTRICAL RISK

  • Never store client passwords, card details, or personal data in plain text. Use a password manager and environment variables. A leaked client database can end your business and expose you to legal liability under data protection laws now active in Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa, and Zimbabwe.
  • Never take live card payments on a site without HTTPS. An unencrypted checkout page can expose customer card numbers to interception on public Wi-Fi â common in many African cities.
  • Protect yourself from electrical surges. Frequent load-shedding and grid instability can destroy a laptop mid-project. Use a surge-protected extension and a UPS or inverter. Back up to cloud storage daily â a dead hard drive is a dead business.
  • Do not deploy code you do not understand. Copy-pasted scripts from unknown sources can contain malware or hidden crypto-mining code that will get your client blacklisted by Google.
  • Keep a written contract. Verbal agreements on scope and payment are the single biggest cause of lost income for African freelancers. A one-page signed agreement protects both sides.

Quick Reference Checklist

  • Scope in writing first. One page listing pages, features, deadline, price, and payment schedule. Both parties sign before you open a code editor.
  • Collect 50% deposit before starting. No deposit, no start. This filters out time-wasters and funds your data and hosting costs.
  • Build mobile-first. Over 80% of African web traffic comes from phones. Test on a real budget Android device, not just your desktop browser.
  • Compress every image before upload. Large images kill load speed on slow connections and cost your client visitors. Target under 200KB per image.
  • Install HTTPS on every site, no exceptions. Free via Let's Encrypt. Google flags non-HTTPS sites as "not secure."
  • Back up daily to cloud storage. Automate it. Never rely on a single laptop or external drive.
  • Hand over only after final payment clears. Deliver source files, hosting credentials, and a short training video at the same moment the balance lands.
  • Sell a maintenance retainer at handover. Monthly updates, backups, and fixes. This is where sustainable income lives.
