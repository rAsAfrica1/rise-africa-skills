Talking to Claude — Junior

Module 5 of 8: Claude Can Be Wrong

Shared phone

🧠 Brain Opener — 60 seconds

Before anything else

Claude sounds like it knows everything. It does not. It predicts the next word — the way your phone finishes your sentences. Sometimes it predicts wrong. Your job: notice.

📚 Lessons

1. Confidence is not correctness

Claude speaks in the same calm voice whether it is right or wrong. You cannot tell from tone. You tell by checking. Checking is a skill — it is the skill.

2. The 3-part drill

Round 1: pick one fact, one number, one name. Round 2: ask Claude for them. Round 3: verify each one against a non-AI source — a book, a parent, a teacher, a different website.

3. Where it breaks

You will never check everything. So check what matters most: prices, dates, distances, names, medical or legal claims. "What colour is the sky" does not need a check.

🎯 Cross-Skill Links

♟️
Chess

Both players make mistakes. The winner is usually the one who notices first. In chess, "blunder-check" is a habit. In AI, "fact-check" is the same habit.

🎹
Music & Piano

You will play a wrong note. The skill is not to stop — the skill is to notice and keep going. The listener barely notices if you do not wince.

🀄
Chinese-Style Drill

信 means trust. 疑 means doubt. Balance both. Trust the tool, doubt the output, verify what matters.

🤖 Real-Time AI-Era Skill

Ask Claude a question where you already know the answer (your birthday, your school's name, the capital of your country). Find the mistake — if there is one. Do this daily for a week.

🎮 Game

Do it now

Two claims on the board. One is true, one is false. Vote. Then check. Points for voting right; double points for voting wrong and admitting it.
