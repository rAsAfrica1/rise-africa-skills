Cattle Farming

Raise cattle: beef breeds or dairy cows. Capital-intensive but high-value. Beef ROI: 18-24 months. Dairy: monthly income (milk sales). Requires land (2-5 hectares), veterinary care, and market access.

MODULE 1  Cattle Farming Business Planning5 videos

What you will be able to do:

  • Choose cattle type: beef breeds, dairy breeds, dual-purpose
  • Understand market: meat prices, milk demand, breeding stock value
  • Calculate profitability: growth rates, feed cost, sellout weight
  • Assess startup capital: land, breeding animals, housing, equipment
  • Plan for 18-24 month beef cycle or long-term dairy operation
