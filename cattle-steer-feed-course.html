â  SAFETY WARNING â FOOD, HEALTH AND CHEMICALS

Aflatoxin is a lethal, invisible risk in maize, groundnuts and other staples. Never sell or process grain that is mouldy, discoloured or smells musty. Dry all grain to safe moisture (maize 13%, groundnuts 8â9%) before storage. Never mix clean and suspect grain. Store grain off the floor on pallets, away from walls, and keep it dry. When using pesticides, fumigants or any agrochemical: read the label, wear gloves and a mask, never eat or smoke during handling, store chemicals locked away from food and children, and observe the full pre-harvest interval. If you suspect aflatoxin contamination, quarantine the batch and contact your national bureau of standards or agriculture extension officer immediately. Selling contaminated food is a criminal offence in most African jurisdictions and can kill your customers â and your business.

Quick Reference Checklist

  1. Measure every kilogram in and out. Keep a simple logbook of quantity received, quantity processed, quantity sold. If you cannot measure loss, you cannot reduce it.
  2. Dry before you store. Test moisture with a meter before bagging. Maize 13%, beans 12%, groundnuts 8â9%. Wet grain is dead money â and a health hazard.
  3. Grade and sort. Remove broken, discoloured and foreign matter before sale or processing. Graded produce earns a premium and builds buyer trust.
  4. Use hermetic storage. Hermetic bags or silos cut insect loss dramatically without chemicals. They pay for themselves in one season.
  5. Know your cost per kilogram. Add raw material, fuel, labour, packaging, transport and a fair share of fixed costs. Price below that and you are subsidising your customers.
  6. Secure a buyer before you process. A signed order or contract beats a hopeful trip to the market. Contract volumes stabilise cash flow.
  7. Brand and label properly. A clean, labelled, food-grade pack with weight, date and contact details commands more than an anonymous sack.
  8. Protect your health and your customers'. Practise hygiene, control moisture and temperature, and never compromise on food safety for a quick sale.
