5. The Business Seed Record

This is not a certificate, and we never call it one. rise AFRICA skills is not a school. We do not run academic terms, we do not examine you, and we issue no diploma, degree or accredited qualification of any kind. What you receive is a Business Seed Record: a confirmation that you worked through and completed a particular enterprise pointer exercise.

What it actually says

  • That you completed the exercise. It names the seed you worked through and the date you finished it.
  • A unique number and a verification URL, so anyone you show it to can check it is genuine and came from us.
  • Nothing more. It does not state a grade, a competency level, or a professional standard.

What it is not

  • It is not an academic or professional qualification.
  • It is not accredited by any education authority, in any country.
  • It will not satisfy a licensing body, a regulator, a visa requirement or a formal entry requirement. If you need something that will, you need an accredited institution — and we will tell you so rather than sell you this.
  • It does not certify that you can perform the work competently. Only practice and real customers do that.

Practical notes

  • It is issued in the name you give us, so please spell it correctly — corrections after issue take time.
  • Some learners find it useful when approaching partners, suppliers or lenders, as evidence that they have done the groundwork. That is the honest extent of its value.
  • We may withdraw a Record obtained dishonestly, for example where someone else completed the exercises.
  • Do not present a Business Seed Record as an accredited qualification. Doing so breaches these terms, and may amount to misrepresentation in your own country.
