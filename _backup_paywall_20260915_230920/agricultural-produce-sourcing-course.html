Agricultural Produce Sourcing Business

Buy from farmers, sell to markets, restaurants and processors — profit on the spread.

Between the farm gate and the consumer's plate sits a long chain of middlemen — and the smart ones make serious money. This course teaches you how to source produce directly from farmers, grade and sort it, handle logistics, negotiate with buyers, and build a sourcing business that serves markets, restaurants, hotels, processors and export channels.
