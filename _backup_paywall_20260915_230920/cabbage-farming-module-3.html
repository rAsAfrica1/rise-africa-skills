3. Nursery and Transplanting

Cabbage transplants at 4-5 weeks. Get the nursery right and you get uniform heads.

Module 3 of 6 · Cabbage Farming for Profit

Nursery setup

Raise cabbage seedlings in 128-cell trays or a well-prepared nursery bed. Trays give uniform plants and 95%+ survival.

ItemPer 10,000 seedlings
128-cell trays80 trays
Growing media500 L
Cabbage seed400-500 g

Growing media recipe

IngredientParts by volume
Sieved loamy topsoil3

Sowing

  1. Fill trays, firm gently, water before sowing.
  2. Sow 1-2 seeds per cell, 6 mm deep.
  3. Cover with sieved media.
  4. Water gently with a fine mist.
  5. Keep in shade. Cover with newspaper for 3-5 days.
  6. Remove cover as soon as sprouts appear.
  7. Water once daily, morning only.

Hardening off

DayExposure
Day 12 hours outdoors, morning only
Day 24 hours
Day 36 hours
Day 4Full day outdoors
Day 5Ready to transplant
Never transplant unhardened seedlings. They collapse within 48 hours in the field.

Transplanting

  1. Water seedlings 2 hours before transplanting.
  2. Transplant at 4-5 weeks, when seedlings have 4-5 true leaves.
  3. Transplant late afternoon or on a cloudy day.
  4. Water destination beds first.
  5. Lift by a leaf, never the stem.
  6. Set the root ball level with the surface.
  7. Firm gently, water immediately.
  8. Shade for 3 days.
  9. Water every other day for week 1.

Spacing

Head size targetSpacingPlants/ha
Large heads (2-3 kg)60 x 60 cm28,000
Medium heads (1.5-2 kg)45 x 60 cm37,000
Small heads (1-1.5 kg)40 x 50 cm50,000
The spacing rule: 45 x 60 cm gives medium heads that every market accepts. Wide spacing makes huge heads that are hard to sell.
