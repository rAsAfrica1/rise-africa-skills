1. Why Avocado Is a Long-Term Goldmine

Avocado is the highest-value fruit tree in Africa. Plant once, earn for 30 years.

Module 1 of 6 · Avocado Farming for Profit

The avocado opportunity

Avocado is one of the most profitable fruit crops in Africa. A mature tree produces 200-500 fruits per year and lives 30-40 years. Global demand for Hass avocado has exploded - Europe and China pay premium prices for African-grown fruit.

Why avocado works

  • Long-term asset - plant once, harvest for decades
  • Year-round income once mature - fruit drops over 3-5 months
  • Export crop - hard currency earnings in USD or EUR
  • High value per kg - 4-10x more than maize
  • Intercrop for first 3 years - grow vegetables or beans between young trees
  • Low water need once established (compared to vegetables)

The three markets

The three big challenges

  • Long wait - 3-4 years before first fruit
  • Water quality - avocados are very sensitive to salty water
  • Root rot - Phytophthora kills trees in poorly drained soil

What you need to start

  • Land with well-drained soil, pH 5.5-6.5
  • Grafted Hass or Fuerte seedlings (not seedlings from seed)
  • Drip irrigation or reliable rainfall (800+ mm/year)
  • Windbreak if site is exposed
  • Starting capital of $500-1,500 for 100 trees
The grafting rule: Always buy grafted trees from a certified nursery. Trees grown from seed take 7-10 years and produce variable quality fruit.
Practical exercise 1: Visit 3 markets. Ask the current avocado price per fruit and who the local buyers are. Check if any exporter has a packhouse nearby.
