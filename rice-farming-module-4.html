4. Planting and Transplanting

Rice can be transplanted or direct-seeded. Each has advantages. Here is how to do both right.

Module 4 of 12 · Rice Farming for Profit

The two planting methods

MethodDescriptionBest for
TransplantingRaise seedlings in nursery 21-30 days, then transplant to flooded fieldBetter weed control, higher yield, water-limited areas
Direct seeding (wet)Sow pre-germinated seed into puddled fieldLabour-saving, larger areas
Direct seeding (dry)Sow dry seed into prepared dry soil, then floodUpland and rain-fed lowland
SRI transplantingYoung seedlings (8-12 days), single plant, wide spacingHigher yield with lower inputs

Nursery setup for transplanting

  1. Site: Well-drained, near water source, sunny, fertile.
  2. Bed size: 1 m wide, any length. 10-15 cm high.
  3. Seed rate: 40-60 kg/ha of paddy. 1 kg sows ~20 m2 of nursery.
  4. Preparation: Fine tilth, mix well-rotted compost.
  5. Sowing: Even broadcast or row-sow, cover with a thin layer of soil.
  6. Water: Keep moist but not flooded for the first 3 days. Then 1-2 cm water.
  7. Care: 21-30 days until 4-5 leaves. For SRI: 8-12 days.

Transplanting steps

  1. Prepare the main field: Puddle, level, and flood with 2-3 cm water.
  2. Lift seedlings carefully: Loosen the soil, lift by the roots. Do not tear.
  3. Trim roots and tops (optional): Trim to 3-4 cm for uniformity.
  4. Space correctly: See spacing table below.
  5. Depth: 2-3 cm deep. Shallow is better than deep.
  6. 1-3 seedlings per hill for standard; 1 per hill for SRI.
  7. Water: 2-3 cm shallow water immediately after transplanting.
  8. Replace: Fill gaps after 5-7 days with spare seedlings.

Direct seeding

Wet direct seeding

  1. Pre-germinate seed: soak 24 hours, drain, keep damp 24-36 hours until sprouts show.
  2. Puddle and level the field.
  3. Drain water to leave a muddy surface (no standing water).
  4. Broadcast or drill sprouted seed evenly.
  5. Seed rate: 60-80 kg/ha (higher than transplanting).
  6. Re-flood lightly after 3-5 days (2-3 cm).

Dry direct seeding (upland)

  1. Prepare dry seedbed with fine tilth.
  2. Sow dry seed 2-3 cm deep, in rows 20-25 cm apart.
  3. Seed rate: 80-100 kg/ha.
  4. Cover with soil, firm gently.
  5. Wait for rain. Weed aggressively in first 4 weeks.

Seed treatment

Treat seed before sowing to prevent disease:

  • Salt water float: Soak seed in 10% salt solution. Discard floating seed (unviable), keep sinking seed. Rinse thoroughly.
  • Hot water treatment: 52-55 C water for 15 minutes. Kills seed-borne diseases. Cool immediately.
  • Fungicide seed dressing: Thiram or similar, per label rate.
  • Bio-fertiliser: Soak seed in compost tea or effective microorganism solution for 30 minutes.

The transplanting rule: Transplant 21-day-old seedlings (or younger for SRI). Older seedlings (30+ days) have less tillering potential and lower yield. Younger transplants respond faster and yield more.
Practical exercise 4: Raise a nursery of 100 m2 for 0.1 ha of transplanting. Track seedling height daily from day 10. Transplant at 21 days exactly. Compare to a plot transplanted at 30 days.
