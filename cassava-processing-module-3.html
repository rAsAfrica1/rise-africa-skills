🛠️ Implementation Steps

  1. Source Cassava: Obtain 100kg from farmers or harvest
  2. Peel & Wash: Remove skin and rinse thoroughly
  3. Grate/Process: Use grater or processor to shred
  4. Ferment: Allow 3-7 days fermentation as needed
  5. Dry: Sun-dry for 2-3 days or use mechanical dryer
  6. Package: Store in sealed containers with labels
