Course contents

🐷 Pig Feed Formulation

Formulating feed for piglets, growers, finishers and breeding stock — from first principles to a profitable operation.

What this course covers

12 modules · 72 written lessons · about 50,896 words · 360 quiz questions · 12 capstone projects.

Every lesson has learning objectives, worked numbers, a "do this today" task and a knowledge check. Videos are free material made by other people; the written lessons are the course.

Module 16 lessons

Swine Nutrition Fundamentals

The foundations every pig feeding decision rests on. Covers how the pig's single-stomach digestive system actually works, how energy is measured and paid for, how lysine and the other amino acids drive lean growth, which vitamins and minerals matter and why availability differs from the number on the bag, and how to run a drinking system that does not quietly cap your feed intake.

  1. Digestive System of Pigs
  2. Energy Requirements for Pigs
  3. Protein and Amino Acid Requirements
  4. Vitamin Requirements
  5. Mineral Requirements and Availability
  6. Water and Drinking Systems

  1. Colostrum and Early Development

Finishing Pig Nutrition (25-120 kg)

Where most of the feed is eaten and most of the money is made or lost.
