Pig Health and Disease Management

Learning Objectives

  • Identify common pig diseases prevalent in African smallholder farms and their clinical signs.
  • Implement biosecurity measures to prevent disease introduction and spread.
  • Administer basic first aid and treatments for minor pig ailments.
  • Develop a vaccination schedule suitable for your farm's location and disease risks.
  • Recognize when to call a veterinarian for professional diagnosis and treatment.
  • Maintain accurate health records for tracking disease outbreaks and treatment outcomes.

Key Concepts

Common Pig Diseases in Africa

African swine fever (ASF) is a highly contagious viral disease with no vaccine, causing high mortality. Classical swine fever (CSF) and African swine fever are often confused; CSF has vaccines. Other common diseases include swine erysipelas, pasteurellosis, and parasitic infections like mange and worms. Early detection is crucial; isolate sick pigs immediately.

Biosecurity on the Farm

Biosecurity prevents disease entry. Limit visitors, especially those who have contact with other pigs. Use footbaths with disinfectant at entry points. Quarantine new pigs for at least 30 days. Control rodents and wild birds. Do not feed kitchen waste unless properly cooked to prevent ASF.

Vaccination and Deworming

Vaccinate against common diseases like CSF, erysipelas, and foot-and-mouth disease where available. Deworm pigs every 3-4 months using ivermectin or albendazole. Consult local veterinary authorities for region-specific recommendations. Keep a vaccination calendar and record all treatments.

Recognizing Sick Pigs

Signs of illness include loss of appetite, lethargy, coughing, diarrhea, vomiting, skin lesions, and abnormal breathing. Take temperature; normal is 38.5-39.5°C. Isolate sick pigs immediately. Observe group behavior daily to catch problems early.

Basic Treatment and First Aid

For wounds, clean with antiseptic and apply antibiotic ointment. For diarrhea, provide electrolytes and clean water. Use antibiotics only as prescribed; misuse leads to resistance. For minor injuries, keep pig in a clean, quiet area. Know your vet's contact for emergencies.

Record Keeping for Health

Keep records of vaccinations, deworming, treatments, and disease incidents. Note dates, products used, and dosages. Records help identify patterns and improve management. Use a simple notebook or farm management app. Share records with your vet for better advice.

Knowledge Check

Q1. Which disease has no vaccine and causes high mortality in pigs?
Classical swine fever
African swine fever
Swine erysipelas
Pasteurellosis
Show answer
Correct: B — African swine fever is a viral disease with no effective vaccine, leading to high death rates. Classical swine fever has vaccines, while the others are bacterial and treatable with antibiotics.
Q2. What is the recommended quarantine period for new pigs?
7 days
14 days
30 days
60 days
Show answer
Correct: C — A 30-day quarantine allows time for any incubating diseases to show symptoms before introducing new pigs to the herd, reducing disease spread.
Q3. What is a key sign of illness in pigs?
Active playing
Loss of appetite
Increased feeding
Shiny coat
Show answer
Correct: B — Loss of appetite is often the first sign of illness. Healthy pigs are eager to eat; refusing food indicates a problem requiring immediate attention.
Q4. What is the ideal age to wean piglets for a small-scale farm in Africa?
A. 1-2 weeks
B. 4-6 weeks
C. 8-10 weeks
D. 12-14 weeks
Show answer
Correct: B — Weaning at 4-6 weeks balances piglet health and sow recovery. Earlier weaning increases stress and mortality.
Q5. Which feed ingredient is a good source of energy for pigs in tropical Africa?
A. Cassava peel
B. Fish meal
C. Soybean meal
D. Bone meal
Show answer
Correct: A — Cassava peel is rich in carbohydrates and widely available. Protein sources like fish meal are for growth, not energy.
Q6. What is the recommended floor space for a growing pig in a pen?
A. 0.5 square meters
B. 1.0 square meters
C. 2.0 square meters
D. 3.0 square meters
Show answer
Correct: B — Growing pigs need about 1.0 square meter each for proper growth and to reduce stress. Less space leads to fighting and disease.
Q7. Which of these is a common sign of heat (estrus) in a sow?
A. Standing still when pressed on the back
B. Refusing food
C. Excessive drinking
D. Coughing
Show answer
Correct: A — The standing reflex is a key indicator of estrus. Other signs include swollen vulva and mucus discharge.
Q8. What is the gestation period of a pig?
A. 3 months, 3 weeks, 3 days
B. 4 months
C. 5 months
D. 6 months
Show answer
Correct: A — Pig gestation averages 114 days, often remembered as 3 months, 3 weeks, and 3 days. This helps in planning farrowing.
Q9. Which nutrient is most critical for pregnant sows in Africa?
A. Protein
B. Carbohydrates
C. Fats
D. Vitamins
Show answer
Correct: A — Protein supports fetal development and milk production. Adequate protein prevents low birth weight piglets.
Q10. What is a common cause of piglet diarrhea in smallholder farms?
A. Overfeeding
B. Dirty pens
C. Loud noise
D. Too much sunlight
Show answer
Correct: B — Poor hygiene leads to bacterial and viral infections causing diarrhea. Clean pens reduce disease risk.
Q11. Which practice helps prevent internal parasites in pigs?
A. Regular deworming
B. Feeding only maize
C. Keeping pigs in dark pens
D. Mixing age groups
Show answer
Correct: A — Deworming every 3-4 months controls worms. Proper sanitation also helps.
Q12. What is the best way to cool pigs during hot weather in Africa?
A. Provide shade and water for wallowing
B. Keep them in closed pens
C. Increase feed
D. Reduce water
Show answer
Correct: A — Pigs lack sweat glands, so shade and wallowing help them cool down. Heat stress reduces growth and fertility.
Q13. Which of these is a good source of protein for pigs in Africa?
A. Maize bran
B. Soybean meal
C. Cassava
D. Rice husk
Show answer
Correct: B — Soybean meal is high in quality protein. Maize and cassava are mainly energy sources.
Q14. What is the purpose of iron injection in piglets?
A. Prevent anemia
B. Prevent diarrhea
C. Promote growth
D. Prevent worms
Show answer
Correct: A — Piglets are born with low iron stores and need injection at 3 days old. Anemia leads to weakness and death.
Q15. Which breeding system involves mating a sow with different boars over time?
A. Inbreeding
B. Line breeding
C. Crossbreeding
D. Rotational breeding

Correct: C — Crossbreeding introduces new genes and improves vigor. It helps avoid inbreeding depression.

D. Loud grunting

Q17. Which feed additive can help prevent diarrhea in piglets?

B. Probiotics

D. 32-35°C
