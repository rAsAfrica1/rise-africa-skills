QUAIL & GUINEA FOWL PROFIT - MODULE 51

Value-Added Egg Products

51 Value-added egg products

Fresh eggs earn one price. Processed eggs earn two to five times that. This is where real profit lives.

1. Pickled quail eggs - boil, peel, brine in vinegar with spices. Sells in jars of 12-24. In West Africa a jar retails 2,500-4,000 FCFA. Shelf life 6 months.

2. Salted eggs - pack in salt-mud or brine for 3-4 weeks. Popular in Asian and coastal markets. Sells at 1.5-2x fresh egg price.

3. Century eggs (pidan) - alkaline cure for 4-6 weeks. Premium product for Asian restaurants. Very high margin.

4. Egg powder - dehydrate and mill. Long shelf life, no cold chain. Sells to bakeries, food processors, and export buyers.

5. Boiled and peeled eggs - sell to street vendors, hotels, and supermarkets. Ready-to-eat premium. Delivered daily.

6. Quail balut (kwek-kwek) - 14-16 day incubated eggs, boiled and sold as street food. Sells 3-4x fresh egg price. Highest-margin egg product in the Philippines; spreading across Africa.

Packaging matters: clean label, clear expiry date, business name, contact. Ugly packaging loses 30% of value.

Rule: never sell 100% of eggs fresh. Process at least 20-30% into value-added products. That alone can double your profit per bird.
