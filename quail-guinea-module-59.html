Agri-Tourism and Farm Experiences

59 Agri-tourism and farm experiences

People pay to visit a farm. This is one of the fastest-growing income streams in African agriculture.

What visitors pay for:

  • Farm tours - 60-90 minutes, 2,000-5,000 FCFA per person
  • Family visits - children feed chicks, collect eggs. 5,000-15,000 FCFA per family.
  • School trips - educational programmes. 500-1,500 FCFA per student, 30-100 students per visit.
  • Photo sessions - Instagram-worthy farm backdrops.
  • Farm-to-table meals - 5,000-15,000 FCFA per person.
  • Birthday parties - for children who love animals.
  • Team building - corporate groups. Higher price point.

What you need: clean, safe, welcoming farm. Shaded seating. Clean toilets. Hand wash station. Small shop selling your products. Guide. Booking system.

Setting prices: consider what a cinema ticket costs locally. Add 20-30% for unique farm experience. Group discounts for 10+. Charge extra for food and take-home products.

Realistic weekly example:

  • 4 family visits on Saturday = 40,000 FCFA
  • 1 school group per week (50 students x 1,000) = 50,000 FCFA
  • Sunday farm-to-table lunch for 8 = 80,000 FCFA
  • Weekly total: about 170,000 FCFA ($270)
Agri-tourism adds income without more birds. Your existing farm becomes the product. Saturday alone can match a week of egg sales.
