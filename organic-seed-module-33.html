🎁 Reveal a strong answer

A launch is not a party. It is the day you deliver your first product to your first paying customer. The first seven days are about setup: registration, records, tools, one demonstration plot. The first 90 days are about delivering consistently and asking for referrals.

📖 Farai Launches

Farai had completed 32 modules of the course. He knew more about organic seed than anyone in his village. He had not sold a single seed.

His teacher said one thing: "Learning is done. Launching is next."

Farai made a list of seven days.

Day 1: He registered as a seed producer with the agricultural office. Free. Took 3 hours.

Day 2: He set up WhatsApp Business with his name, address, and a small catalogue of three planned products. Free. Took 2 hours.

Day 3: He built a simple spreadsheet with four sheets. He put his first entry in the customer log - his neighbour Mrs Nyathi, who had agreed to be a test customer. Free. Took 1 hour.

Day 4: He planted a 100 square metre demonstration plot of his strongest crop - kale. Cost: 40 dollars for seed and compost. Took 4 hours.

Day 5: He interviewed three potential buyers. He asked the five questions from module 21. Took 6 hours of walking.

Day 6: He planted 20 Tephrosia trees for future seed. Cost: 10 dollars. Took 2 hours.

Day 7: He wrote his 3-month plan. One page. What he would produce, for whom, at what price, by when. Took 3 hours.

By the end of the first week, Farai had a legal business, digital tools, a customer, a demonstration plot, three buyer conversations, a long-term investment, and a written plan.

The first sale came in week 12. Two kilograms of kale seed at 30 dollars per kilogram. Sixty dollars.

It was not much. But it was the first sale of a real business.

Two years later, Farai was earning 8,000 dollars per season. He taught this course to 12 other farmers in his district. His demonstration plot had become a training centre.

And every season, he planted 20 more trees.

He had not waited to be ready. He had launched.

💡 The Big Idea

Launch in seven days, grow in ninety

A launch is a series of small actions taken in a specific order. Registration, digital setup, first customer, demonstration plot, buyer conversations, long-term investment, written plan. Seven days. Then ninety days of consistent delivery. That is the whole launch.

📚 Three Deep Lessons

1

The seven-day launch

Day 1 - Legal registration. Register as a seed producer. Free or low cost. Takes 3 to 4 hours.

Day 2 - Digital setup. WhatsApp Business, spreadsheet, mobile money. Free. Takes 2 to 3 hours.

Day 3 - Record system. Paper or digital. Start with the first entry today. Takes 1 hour.

Day 4 - Demonstration plot. Plant a small visible plot with your best seed. Cost: 20 to 50 dollars. Takes half a day.

Day 5 - Buyer interviews. Five buyers minimum. Ask the five questions. Takes one full day.

Day 6 - Long-term planting. Trees, perennials, or another slow crop. 10 to 50 dollars. Takes half a day.

Day 7 - Written plan. One page. What, who, when, how much. Takes 3 hours.

2

The first 90 days

Weeks 1 to 4. Plant your main crop. Water. Weed. Record everything. Post weekly updates on WhatsApp.

Weeks 5 to 8. Continue crop care. Rogue the demonstration plot. Take photos weekly. Talk to one new buyer per week.

Weeks 9 to 12. Harvest, process, test, package. Prepare for sale. Contact the buyers you interviewed. Deliver.

The 90 days are not complex. They are consistent. Every week you do the same three things: work the crop, update the records, contact a buyer. That is the whole method.

3

What comes after the first sale

Ask for a referral. After every delivery, one question: "Do you know anyone else who needs this?" Most of your next sales come from this question.

Write a case study. One page: what you produced, for whom, at what price, what the buyer said. Every buyer loves a case study about another buyer.

Plan the next season. Before you have finished selling this season, plan the next one. What will you double? What will you add? What will you drop?

Teach one other farmer. The best way to consolidate learning is to teach. Show one other farmer how to do what you just learned. It cements your knowledge and builds your network.

🎮 Game - Seven-Day Sprint

3 to 12 players - One week

Every player runs the seven-day launch in the same week. At the end of the week, each shares what they actually did. Points: legal registration (2), digital setup (2), first record (1), demonstration plot (3), 5 buyer interviews (3), long-term planting (2), written plan (2). Highest score wins. What matters is not the score but that each player actually did it.
