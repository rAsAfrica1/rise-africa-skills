Phase 1 Daily Workflow (2-3 hours/day)

  1. Morning (30 min): Contact 2-3 farmers to take orders for that day
  2. Measuring (20 min): Measure out ingredients for 20-30 kg batch
  3. Mixing (30 min): Hand-mix in buckets thoroughly for 10+ minutes
  4. Bagging (20 min): Portion into 20 kg bags for delivery
  5. Delivery (40 min): Deliver to 2-3 farmers (establish trust)
  6. Collection (10 min): Collect payment, discuss feedback

Daily Income Potential: 20-30 kg × $0.40 profit/kg = $8-12/day
Weekly: $56-84 | Monthly: $240-336 (grows to $400+ as customers increase)
