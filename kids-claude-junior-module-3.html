Talking to Claude — Junior

Module 3 of 8: Your Grown-Up Opens It, You Do the Thinking

Shared phone

🧠 Brain Opener — 60 seconds

Before anything else

Why does the pilot land the plane and not the passenger? Because the pilot trained. You are the pilot. The grown-up just opens the door of the cockpit.

📚 Lessons

1. Ownership vs use

The phone belongs to the adult. The ideas belong to you. Claude's terms of service require an adult account. So the adult opens it, and you type the questions. Not the other way around. If the adult is asking Claude for you, you are no longer the pilot.

2. The 3-part drill

Round 1: name the task ("I want to find three gift ideas for my mother"). Round 2: name the tool ("I will use Claude on my aunt's phone"). Round 3: name the rule ("My aunt opens the app, I type the question, I read the answer out loud").

3. Where it breaks

The break happens when you ask the adult to "just ask Claude for me". That is like asking the pilot to fly while you sit in the passenger seat. You learn nothing. You will need to ask again next time.

🎯 Cross-Skill Links

♟️
Chess

The rules belong to the board. You are allowed to move. The board will not move for you. You move, you lose or win, you learn. Same with the phone.

🎹
Music & Piano

The instrument sounds only when you press the key. The teacher cannot press it for you, no matter how kind she is. Skill is a physical act — you do it, you own it.

🀄
Chinese-Style Drill

借 means borrow. 用 means use. You borrow the phone, you use the AI. Two characters, one rule: borrow ≠ own, use ≠ let someone else use for you.

🤖 Real-Time AI-Era Skill

Write three questions you want to ask Claude. Hand them to your grown-up. Have them open the app. Now YOU type the questions. Read the answers aloud. Nobody else types.

🎮 Game

Do it now

Split the room. Half are "grown-ups" who hold the phone. Half are "thinkers" who say the questions. Grown-ups may not speak. Thinkers may not touch the phone. Type via a third person if needed.

✅ Module 3 Quiz

1. Who should type the question into Claude?

2. Why does Claude require an adult account?

3. If the adult asks Claude for the child, what is lost?

4. 借 (borrow) and 用 (use) teach…

5. In chess, "you move, you lose or win" means…
