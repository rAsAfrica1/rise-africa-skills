Manure as a Profit Center

53 Manure as a profit center

Most farmers throw away their best free resource. Quail and guinea fowl manure is one of the richest organic fertilizers available.

Nutritional value: quail manure contains roughly 4-5% nitrogen, 2-3% phosphorus, 2-3% potassium - richer than chicken manure. High demand from vegetable gardeners, flower growers, tea/coffee plantations, nurseries, and home gardeners.

Three ways to sell it:

  1. Raw manure - dry, bag, sell in 20kg sacks at 500-1,500 FCFA each.
  2. Composted manure - mix with crop waste, turn weekly for 6-8 weeks. Compost sells at 2x raw price. Pathogen-free, no smell, premium product.
  3. Liquid fertilizer - soak manure in water 1:5 for 2-3 weeks. Bottle in 1L and 5L containers. Sells at 800-2,000 FCFA per litre. Highest margin.

Additional income from manure:

  • Biogas - 100+ birds produce enough gas for household cooking. Saves $30-80/month.
  • Worm farming - feed manure to earthworms, sell worm castings and worms.
  • Black soldier fly larvae - grow on manure, harvest as protein feed. Cuts feed cost 20-30%.

Simple setup: collect daily in covered bin, dry 3-7 days, bag in 20kg sacks, label "Pure Quail Manure - Organic Fertilizer", sell at gate or to landscapers.

100 birds produce about 3-5 kg of manure per day. At 500 FCFA/kg that is 1,500-2,500 FCFA daily - up to $1,200/year from waste.
