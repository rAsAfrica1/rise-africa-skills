How Much Is That, Really

Price vocabulary is the most immediately useful Chinese you can own, because the first Chinese speaker you meet may well be selling something.

🎒 What you need

  • cardboard cards
  • a marker
  • a friend to play the trader

✋ Do this

  1. Write each of the five words on its own card, character on the front, Pinyin and meaning on the back.
  2. Learn 多少钱 duōshao qián as one unit, not three words. Say it twenty times until it is a single sound.
  3. Set up a market game. Your friend lays out five real objects and plays the trader. You point and ask 多少钱?
  4. They answer with a number in Chinese. You reply either 太贵了 — tài guì le — too expensive — or 好，我买 — hǎo, wǒ mǎi — good, I'll buy.
  5. Swap roles and run it again. Make the prices ridiculous so you have to use 太贵了 often.
  6. Write out the whole exchange on cardboard as a script and hang it up.

💡 Why it works

Role-play with real objects forces you to produce language under a small amount of pressure, which is what makes it available later. And the phrase 多少钱 is high frequency — the words you will use most are the ones worth over-practising.

🔥 Challenge

Run the market game with a sibling who does not speak Chinese and teach them their lines as you go.
