Play a round, learn a habit

Every game here drills something a real business owner does weekly — pricing, margins, break-even, spotting a bad deal. Two minutes each. No score is saved, no account needed.

Why games? Because the arithmetic that decides whether your business survives — margin, break-even, cost per unit — is the arithmetic most people avoid. Do it as a game twenty times and it stops being frightening.

50 things you can make at home and sell

Small-capital businesses that start in a kitchen, a yard or a rented corner. Costs shown are rough starting ranges — call three suppliers in your own town before you trust any number here.

50 food & drink products from what grows near you

Spices, sauces, natural drinks and health foods built on ingredients you can actually buy at a local market — baobab, moringa, hibiscus, groundnut, tamarind, ginger, chilli.

Read before you bottle anything. Low-acid foods (meat, fish, most vegetables, garlic and herbs in oil) can grow Clostridium botulinum in a sealed jar. That is a fatal risk, not a spoilage risk. Low-acid products need a pressure canner or must be kept refrigerated and sold fast. High-acid products — vinegar pickles, most fruit jams, hibiscus drinks — are safe in a boiling water bath. If you are unsure which yours is, treat it as low-acid.

50 farming businesses, from a backyard to a block

Sorted roughly by how much land and money you need to begin. Cycle length matters as much as profit — a fast cycle lets you learn and recover from mistakes; a slow one ties up your money for years.

Before you choose: match the enterprise to your water, not your ambition. The single most common reason a small farm fails is picking something thirsty in a place with an unreliable borehole.

What buyers are asking for

Patterns worth knowing before you choose a product. These are directions of travel, not guarantees — verify demand in your own market before you spend money.

How to test a trend for free. Make ten units. Stand at a market or a taxi rank on the busiest day. If you sell all ten before noon and three people ask for more, you have something. If you carry six home, the trend is real but your version, price or place is wrong. Change one of those three and repeat next week.
