1. Who we are

rise AFRICA skills ("we", "us") is a practical-skills training platform operating at www.riseafricaskills.com. We are the data controller for the personal information described below.

Contact: all@riseafricaskills.com · WhatsApp +263 77 300 1353

Quiz answers and course progress are stored in your own browser using local storage. That information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

3. Why we collect it

  • To give you access to courses you enrol in — we need to know who you are to show you the right content.
  • To issue certificates — a certificate carries your name.
  • To contact you about your enrolment — confirmations, access details, and replies to questions you ask us.
  • To answer support requests — by email or WhatsApp.
  • To understand which courses are useful — how many people start and finish a course, so we can improve or retire it.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We do not use it to serve you advertising, and we do not pass it to advertisers.

4. Where your data is stored

Your data leaves your country. We use Supabase, a database service whose servers are located outside Africa (primarily in the United States and Europe). When you create an account or enrol, your name, email, phone number and enrolment record are transmitted to and stored on those servers.

We use Supabase because it lets a small team run a secure platform affordably. If you are not comfortable with your information being stored outside your country, please do not create an account — you can still contact us on WhatsApp and we will help you directly.

Our course pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which serves the pages themselves. GitHub receives standard web-server information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page.

6. Children under 18

Our rAs Kids courses are designed for children aged 7 to 16. Children's data is treated with extra care, and the law in most countries we operate in requires a parent or guardian to be involved.
  • A parent, guardian or teacher must create and hold the account. We ask that children do not register an account in their own name.
  • The kids' course pages themselves collect nothing. Progress and badges are stored in the browser on the child's own device and never reach us.
  • Where an AI assistant is used in a course, the account must belong to an adult, and the terms of that AI provider apply.
  • If you believe a child has given us personal information without a parent or guardian's involvement, email all@riseafricaskills.com and we will delete it promptly.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account details — for as long as your account is open, then deleted within 90 days of you asking us to close it.
  • Enrolment and certificate records — kept while you may need to prove a certificate is genuine. If you want them erased sooner, ask us, but be aware we will then no longer be able to verify your certificate.
  • Support messages — up to 24 months.
  • Bulk enquiry details — up to 24 months, or sooner on request.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights under laws such as Zimbabwe's Data Protection Act, Kenya's Data Protection Act, Nigeria's Data Protection Act, South Africa's POPIA, and the GDPR. Wherever you are, we will honour all of the following:

  • See what we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete it.
  • Get a copy in a portable format.
  • Object to a particular use of your information.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Complain to the data protection authority in your country.

To use any of these, email all@riseafricaskills.com with the subject "Data request". We aim to respond within 30 days and we will not charge you for it.

9. How we protect it

  • The site is served over HTTPS, so traffic between your device and us is encrypted.
  • Passwords are stored using one-way encryption. Nobody at rise AFRICA skills can read your password.
  • Access to the database is limited to the people who need it to run the platform.
  • Payment card details never touch our systems.

No system is perfectly secure. If we ever discover a breach that puts your information at meaningful risk, we will tell affected users and the relevant authority as required by law.

10. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

We use your browser's local storage to remember practical things: which courses you have opened, your quiz progress, your theme choice, and your language preference. This stays on your device. Clearing your browser data will erase it, including your saved course progress.

If you sign in, an authentication token is stored so you are not asked to log in on every page.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. For significant changes affecting how we use your data, we will notify account holders by email.
