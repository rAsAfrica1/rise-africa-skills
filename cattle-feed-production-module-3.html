Phase 2 Daily Workflow (2 hours/day)

  1. Morning (20 min): Take phone orders from 5-10 farmers
  2. Weighing (20 min): Measure ingredients accurately using scales (80 kg batch)
  3. Grinding (30 min): Grind coarse materials using manual grinder
  4. Mixing (30 min): Hand-mix ground ingredients thoroughly
  5. Bagging (15 min): Portion into 20 kg bags
  6. Delivery (25 min): Deliver to 3-5 farmers

Daily Production: 80 kg × $0.40 profit = $32/day
Weekly: $224 | Monthly: $960

Recommended Suppliers for Phase 2 Equipment

  • Manual Grinders: Local agricultural equipment shops, online (Amazon/AliExpress)
  • Platform Scales: Veterinary supply stores, agricultural markets
  • Feed Bags: Bulk agricultural suppliers or local packaging companies
  • Mixing Troughs: Farm supply stores or fabricate from plastic sheets
