📋 Module 6 Concepts

Practical Communication Skills

Module 6 focuses on real-world application: ordering food, asking directions, shopping, making friends, understanding native speakers. Grammar becomes invisible - focus shifts to fluency and confidence. Mistakes are expected and necessary for progress. Immerse in Russian media (shows, podcasts, music). Join conversation groups. Practice with native speakers regularly.

🚀 Real-World Project

Part 1: Conversation Partner

Find Russian language exchange partner (Tandem, HelloTalk, Speaky). Schedule weekly 30-minute conversations. Start in Russian, switch to English for clarification as needed. Build ongoing relationship.

Part 2: Media Immersion

Watch 3 Russian TV shows/movies. Start with subtitles, progress to minimal subtitles. Listen for pronunciation, stress patterns, natural speech rhythm. Note new vocabulary.

Part 3: Real-World Scenarios

Practice ordering at restaurant, asking directions, hotel check-in, job interview. Role-play with language partner. Record yourself. Review and improve pronunciation.

Part 4: Cultural Exploration

Read about Russian traditions, holidays, food, social customs. Attend Russian cultural events if available. Join Russian community online. Deepen cultural understanding for better language retention.

Part 5: Listening Mastery

Find 10 Russian podcasts for learners. Listen daily (10-15 min). Gradually move to native-level podcasts. Pause, repeat, understand without translation when possible.

Part 6: Reading Progress

Start with simple Russian books (fairy tales, children's stories). Progress to news articles, blogs, literature. Maintain vocabulary notebook of new words in context.

Part 7: Speaking Goals

Set specific speaking targets: 2-minute monologue about yourself, 5-minute conversation on topic, presenting idea in Russian. Record, self-evaluate, improve.

Part 8: 90-Day Commitment

By end of Module 6, commit to 90-day intensive practice: daily immersion (1 hour minimum), 3 conversation sessions/week, weekly media consumption. Track progress. Set next milestone.
