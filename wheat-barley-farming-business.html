The Business Seed — Wheat & Barley Farming

Africa imports billion of wheat every year. This is the business blueprint for competing with those imports.

What is here

1. The Business Idea 2. Three Capital Tiers 3. Value-Addition Ladder 4. Buyers You Will Approach 5. One-Page Plan

1. The Business Idea

You are training to run one of these businesses around wheat and barley:

  • Wheat milling supply — grow, dry, sell to flour mills against imports
  • Malting barley production — contract with breweries and maltsters
  • Feed grain production — grow feed wheat and barley for feed mills
  • Certified seed multiplication — 2–3x food price

2. Three Capital Tiers

Tier 1 — Smallholder (0.5–1 ha)

– ,500

Irrigated or high-altitude rain-fed plot. Certified seed, DAP, one or two nitrogen splits. Sell to a local flour mill or aggregator. Learn the crop.

Tier 2 — Commercial (2–5 ha)

,000 – ,000

Contract with a flour mill or brewery. Full rust and septoria programme. Storage for off-season sales. Hire a combine at harvest.

Tier 3 — Enterprise (5+ ha or seed)

,000+

Own equipment, storage silos, seed production. Contract farming with neighbouring smallholders. Vertical into flour milling or malting on a small scale.

Rule: wheat pays on volume and quality. Get the contract first, then plant to the contract.

3. Value-Addition Ladder

ProductMultiplier
Raw wheat at harvest1x baseline
Cleaned, dried, stored wheat1.2–1.4x
Milling-grade wheat (protein spec)1.3–1.6x
Malting barley (contract)1.5–2.2x
Certified seed2–3x
Small-scale flour milling2–4x

The value ladder for wheat is shorter than for vegetables, but the volumes are much larger. Storage and quality specification are the two highest-return steps.

4. Buyers You Will Approach

  • Flour mills — main wheat buyer, 10–100 t lots, 30–60 day terms
  • Breweries and maltsters — main malting barley buyer, contract-based
  • Feed mills — feed wheat and barley, cash or 30 days
  • Seed distributors — certified seed, 2–3x food price
  • Government purchase programmes — guaranteed prices in some countries
Rule: name three buyers before you plant. Wheat is easy to sell but the price gap between feed and milling is 20–40% — the difference between profit and loss.

5. Your One-Page Plan

  1. Business type: milling wheat, malting barley, feed, or seed?
  2. Capital tier.
  3. Land and water (irrigation is critical).
  4. Variety and rust resistance.
  5. Target yield (4 t/ha year 1).
  6. Three named buyers.
  7. Revenue target.
  8. Total cost.
  9. Net margin.
  10. Value step in 12 months (contract or seed).
  11. Scaling trigger.
