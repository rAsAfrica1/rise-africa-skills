Live Bird Sales and Breeding Stock

55 Live bird sales and breeding stock

The highest-margin sale is not the meat bird - it is the breeding pair, the day-old chick, and the proven layer.

1. Day-old chicks

  • Quail chicks: 150-400 FCFA each
  • Guinea fowl keets: 500-1,500 FCFA each
  • You need incubators and brooders but no feed cost
  • Margin: 60-80% per chick

2. Point-of-lay pullets (16-20 weeks old) - premium price: 4,000-8,000 FCFA per bird. Buyer knows they will get eggs within 2 weeks.

3. Breeding pairs / trios - 1 male + 3 females = a trio. Sells for 2-3x the meat price. Guaranteed production ready.

4. Proven layers - older hens (1-2 years) sold as "currently laying" - 3,000-5,000 FCFA. Documentation of laying record commands premium.

5. Hatching eggs - fertile eggs for other farmers to incubate. 2-3x the price of eating eggs. Sold in batches of 30 or 100.

Marketing breeding stock: keep detailed records (hatch date, parent stock, vaccinations, laying %), take photos of healthy birds, post on farming WhatsApp groups and Facebook, attend agricultural shows, offer a "starter pack" with care guide and 30 days support.

One breeding pair can produce 200+ chicks per year. At 500 FCFA each that is 100,000 FCFA from one pair. Far more profitable than selling that pair for meat.
