1. Fish Nutrition Basics

Fish are more efficient than land animals at converting feed to body weight. But only if the feed is correctly formulated and shaped for the fish to eat.

Module 1 of 6 · Fish Feed Production · quiz at the end

Lesson 1.1 - Why fish feed is different

Fish are cold-blooded and live in water. Two things make their feed different from pig or poultry feed:

  • Floating or sinking? Tilapia feed at the surface. Catfish feed at the bottom. The pellet must match the fish.
  • Water stability. Pellets must hold together for 5-10 minutes underwater without dissolving.
  • Protein is expensive. Fish need 25-45% protein depending on stage - much higher than land animals.

Catfish need more protein than tilapia because they are carnivorous in nature. Tilapia are omnivores and can eat some plant matter.

Lesson 1.3 - Why feed is 60-70% of cost

A tilapia from fingerling to 500 g market weight eats 800-900 g of feed at FCR 1.6-1.8.

The difference is 7x profit per fish. Home mixing is the difference between a hobby and a business.

The feed rule: In fish farming, the money is made or lost in the feed bag. Every $0.10 saved per kg of feed is $0.14-0.18 profit per fish.

Lesson 1.4 - FCR in fish

FCR = kg of feed ÷ kg of fish growth. Fish FCR is much better than land animals:

SpeciesFCRVerdict
Tilapia1.5-1.8Excellent
Tilapia1.8-2.2Good
Tilapia2.2-2.5Acceptable
Catfish1.0-1.3Excellent
Catfish1.3-1.6Good
Catfish1.6-2.0Acceptable
Practical exercise 1.1: Weigh your current commercial feed sack. Calculate the cost per kg. Multiply by 1.4 (kg feed per fish). That is your feed cost per fish.
Practical exercise 1.2: Ask 5 fish farmers in your area what commercial pellets cost. Compare to your local ingredient costs.
