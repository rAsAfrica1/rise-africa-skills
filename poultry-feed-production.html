What You'll Learn

Poultry feed production is one of the most profitable animal feed businesses. This course covers broiler starter (23% protein), broiler finisher (19% protein), and layer feed (16% protein) formulations. Learn to supply 1,000+ bird operations and earn $8,000-$12,000 per 6-week broiler cycle.

Master the unique nutritional requirements of poultry, rapid scaling strategies, and the high-margin premium poultry market. Production cost remains $0.085/kg but retail pricing is 10-20% higher than cattle feed due to specialized formulations.
