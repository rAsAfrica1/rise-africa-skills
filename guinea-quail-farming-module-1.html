1. Two Premium Birds, Two Businesses

Guinea fowl and quail are the highest-margin poultry in Africa. Here is why.

Module 1 of 6 · Guinea Fowl and Quail Farming for Profit

Why these two birds

Guinea fowl and quail are the two most underfarmed poultry species in Africa. Both sell for premium prices because supply is limited and demand is rising in cities. Both are hardier than chickens and need less space.

Why guinea fowl

  • Hardy - tolerates heat, disease, and poor feed better than chickens
  • Premium meat - sells 2-3x chicken price at festive season
  • Low input - free-ranges well, eats insects and weeds
  • Watchdog - alarms at intruders, protects the compound
  • Pest control - eats ticks, beetles, and grasshoppers

Why quail

  • Fastest turnover - eggs at 6-8 weeks, meat at 6 weeks
  • Tiny space - 100 quail fit in a 5 m2 cage
  • Health market - quail eggs sell as a health food
  • Cheap to feed - 25 g per bird per day
  • Year-round eggs - 250-300 eggs per hen per year
  • High-value niche - restaurants pay premium

The niche rule: Guinea fowl and quail sell on scarcity. If your market already has 10 suppliers, prices drop. Do a market check before starting - talk to restaurants and shops first.
Practical exercise 1: Visit 5 restaurants and 3 health food shops. Ask if they buy guinea fowl or quail, and at what price. Write the answers.
