🛠️ Implementation Steps

  1. Define: Clarify scope and objectives
  2. Plan: Create schedule and budget
  3. Organize: Assign roles and resources
  4. Execute: Kickoff and manage tasks
  5. Monitor: Track progress and adjust
  6. Close: Deliver and document lessons
