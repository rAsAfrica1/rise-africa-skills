🎬 6 Weeks of Viral Russian

WEEK 1: "The Hook" — Greetings That Pop

  1. Привет (Priveyt) = Hi — Said like "PREE-vyet" (literally "I greet you"). Used with friends, casual. The TikTok way to start.
  2. Здравствуйте (Zdrastvuytye) = Hello (formal) — Said like "ZDRAS-tvoo-tye". Use with strangers, officials, elderly. The professional opener.
  3. Спасибо (Spaseeba) = Thank you — Your new best friend word. Works EVERYWHERE. Literally "save" you. Magical word.
  4. Пожалуйста (Pozhaluysta) = Please — Sounds like "paz-HAHL-stuh". Always say this before asking. Russian politeness rule #1.
  5. До встречи (Do vstrechi) = See you later — Literally "until meeting." How Russians end conversations. Smooth exit line.
🤯 Fun Fact: Russians LOVE formal greetings. Say Привет to a stranger and they think you're being disrespectful. Context = everything.
💪 Challenge: Practice saying all 5 words 10 times today. Record yourself. Does it sound natural? (It won't. That's okay. Day 1 struggles are real.)

WEEK 2: "The Story" — Survival Phrases That Save Your Life

  1. Я не понимаю (Ya ne ponimayu) = I don't understand — Your lifeline when confused. Say this with confidence, not shame.
  2. Повторите, пожалуйста (Povtorite, pozhaluysta) = Repeat, please — Because you WILL not understand the first time. This is normal.
  3. Где туалет? (Gde tualyet?) = Where is the bathroom? — Universal need. Say this and you're golden. Survival skill #1.
  4. Сколько это стоит? (Skolko eto stoit?) = How much does this cost? — Travel phrase that prevents financial disasters.
  5. Я говорю только по-русски мало (Ya govoriu tolko po-russki malo) = I speak only a little Russian — Set expectations. Gives you wiggle room.
  6. Помогите! (Pomogite!) = Help! — Emergency word. Yell it. People will respond.
😂 Fun Fact: Russians appreciate honesty. Saying "I don't understand" gets you REAL help, not slow English. They respect the effort.
💪 Challenge: Use 3 of these phrases in real life this week (or roleplay convincingly). Take screenshots of the interaction and share the vibe.

WEEK 3: "The Plot Twist" — Conversations That Impress

  1. Как дела? (Kak dela?) = How are you? — Small talk starter. Answer "Хорошо" (Khorosho = good) OR "Не очень" (Ne ochen' = not great).
  2. Меня зовут... (Menya zovut...) = My name is... — Self-introduction. "Меня зовут Максим" = My name is Maxim.
  3. Откуда ты? (Otkuda ty?) = Where are you from? — Start real conversations. Я из Нигерии (Ya iz Nigerii) = I'm from Nigeria.
  4. Что ты делаешь? (Chto ty delaesh?) = What do you do? — Ask about jobs, hobbies, life. Я работаю в... = I work in...
  5. Мне нравится... (Mne nravitsa...) = I like... — Share interests. Мне нравится музыка (I like music). Мне нравится еда (I like food).
  6. Мне не нравится... (Mne ne nravitsa...) = I don't like... — Set boundaries. Мне не нравятся холодные дни (I don't like cold days).
🎭 Fun Fact: Russians ask DEEP questions right away. "Что ты делаешь?" isn't just about your job — they want to know your LIFE. Prepare for real talk.
💪 Challenge: Have a 30-second conversation with yourself (or a friend playing Russian). Greet → ask name → ask where from → share something you like. Record it. It'll be awkward. Perfect.

WEEK 4: "The Drama" — Numbers, Money & Time (Survival Tactics)

  1. Numbers 1-10: Один (1), два (2), три (3), четыре (4), пять (5), шесть (6), семь (7), восемь (8), девять (9), десять (10). Quick drill: count to 10 out loud 50 times today.
  2. Numbers 11-20: одиннадцать, двенадцать, тринадцать, четырнадцать, пятнадцать, шестнадцать, семнадцать, восемнадцать, девятнадцать, двадцать. Harder than it looks. Embrace the struggle.
  3. Money Talk: Рубль (Rubl) = ruble. "Это дешево" (Eto deshevo) = It's cheap. "Это дорого" (Eto dorogo) = It's expensive. Say these at markets to negotiate.
  4. Time Basics: Сейчас (Seychas) = now. Завтра (Zavtra) = tomorrow. Вчера (Vchera) = yesterday. Раньше (Ranshe) = earlier. Позже (Pozhe) = later.
  5. Hours & Minutes: Который час? (Kotoryy chas?) = What time is it? Два часа (Dva chasa) = 2 o'clock. Половина третьего (Polovina tretyego) = 2:30.
💰 Fun Fact: Russian numbers don't follow English logic. "Один" (1) changes based on gender. 1 book = "один книга." 1 girl = "одна девочка." Don't worry, Russians mess this up too sometimes.
💪 Challenge: Count to 100 in Russian (sounds impossible, but breaks it into 10s). Time yourself. Try to do it in under 60 seconds by end of week.

WEEK 5: "The Climax" — Russian Culture & Slang (Get Real)

  1. Ладно (Yadna) = Okay/Alright — Casual agreement. How young Russians say "yes." Super relaxed energy.
  2. Круто (Kruto) = Cool/Awesome — Literally "steep." Use when impressed. Это круто = That's cool.
  3. Не белый медведь (Ne belyy medved) = It's not a white bear — Idiom meaning "it's not that hard." Russians express struggle poetically.
  4. Бояться (Boyatsa) = To be afraid — Я боюсь пауков (I'm afraid of spiders). Russians bond over fears.
  5. Чай (Chai) = Tea — Russians drink TONS of tea. Tea culture is REAL here. Offer tea, get friendship. Known fact.
🐻 Fun Fact: Russians love proverbs. "Душа нараспашку" (Soul wide open) = being honest. Russians are deep. Way deeper than small talk.
💪 Challenge: Watch a 2-minute Russian TikTok/YouTube video (with captions). Write down 5 words you hear. Look them up. You're learning in the wild now.

WEEK 6: "The Finale" — Your First Real Russian Flex

  1. Build a 1-minute introduction about yourself: Привет, меня зовут [Your Name]. Я из [Your Country]. Я говорю по-русски мало, но мне нравится учиться. (Hi, my name is X. I'm from Y. I speak Russian a little, but I like learning.)
  2. Master small talk combos: Greet → ask name → ask where from → ask what they do → share your interests → say goodbye. Practice this 10 times.
  3. Emergency phrases locked: Я не понимаю + Повторите = Your escape route. Use them guilt-free.
  4. Number power: Can count to 50, understand prices, tell time. You can BUY THINGS in Russian. Level up.
  5. Cultural awareness: Know that Russians value directness, honesty, and good tea. Show respect. They'll show it back.
  6. The real flex: Text a Russian friend (or find one online) and have a 5-minute conversation in Russian. Screenshot it. Share the W.
🎯 Fun Fact: Russians LOVE when foreigners try their language. You'll get encouragement and correction. They're not rude—they want you to speak correctly. It's respect.
💪 FINAL CHALLENGE: Film yourself introducing yourself in Russian (full 1 minute, no script visible). Post it on social media with #LearnRussianWithRAS. The world needs to see your effort. You just became a polyglot wannabe. That's a win.
