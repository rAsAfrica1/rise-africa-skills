Course contents

🐠 Fish Feed Formulation

Feeding fish from fry to market size — tilapia and catfish nutrition, pellet quality, water quality and pond economics.

What this course covers

12 modules · 72 written lessons · about 45,931 words · 360 quiz questions · 12 capstone projects.

Every lesson has learning objectives, worked numbers, a "do this today" task and a knowledge check. Videos are free material made by other people; the written lessons are the course.
