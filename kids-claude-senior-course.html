For a parent or teacher

The Claude account is held and opened by you, the parent, teacher or older sibling — Claude's consumer terms are written for adults, so an adult stays alongside while the young person does the thinking and the trading. Modules one and two require no device, the free tier has usage limits that change so check them before each session, and module eight involves handling real money, which is a good moment to agree together on how earnings are split and stored.
