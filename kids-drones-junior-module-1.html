Kids Drones — Junior

Module 1 of 8: What Flies (and What Just Falls)

No device needed

🧠 Brain Opener — 60 seconds

Before anything else

Drop a stone. Drop a piece of paper. Which one flies? Neither — but the paper falls slowly, and that difference is the whole secret of flight. Write down in one sentence: what makes the paper fall slower than the stone?

📚 Lessons

1. Four forces, one balance

Every flying thing — bird, kite, drone, plane — is a fight between four forces: lift (up), weight (down), thrust (forward), drag (backwards). If lift equals weight, it hovers. If thrust beats drag, it moves forward. If weight wins, it falls. That is it. Everything else is detail.

2. The 3-part drill

Round 1: hold a sheet of paper flat and drop it — it flutters slowly. Round 2: crumple the same sheet into a ball and drop it — it falls fast. Round 3: try to invent a shape that falls even slower than the flat sheet. This is the entire history of flight, in one exercise.

3. Where the idea breaks

A drone does not fly like a bird or a plane. A plane needs forward speed to make lift — stop the engine and it falls. A helicopter or drone makes its own wind with spinning blades, so it can hover in one spot. Hovering is the drone's superpower. Planes cannot do it. Rockets cannot do it. Only rotorcraft can.

🎯 Cross-Skill Links

♟️
Chess

Chess has four forces too: material (pieces), space (squares), time (moves), structure (pawns). A player who is strong in three and weak in one can still lose. Drones are the same — good lift and thrust with bad drag will still crash.

🎹
Music & Piano

A held note is lift. A quick note is thrust. A note that dies immediately is weight winning. Listen to any piece — the composer is balancing forces without knowing the words.

🀄
Chinese-Style Drill

飞 (fēi) = fly. 落 (luò) = fall. Every flying machine spends its life between these two characters.

🤖 Real-Time AI-Era Skill

Ask Claude: "Why can a bumblebee fly when maths says it shouldn't?" Read the answer carefully. Notice that the old bumblebee story is a myth — maths never said bees can't fly. This is your first lesson in verifying AI.

🎮 Game

Do it now

Paper flight tournament. Each person folds one thing — a paper plane, a helicopter, a wad, a fan — and drops it from the same height. Time each fall. Longest wins. Then draw the winner's shape and name the force that kept it up.
