Course contents

🐔 Broiler and Layer Feed Formulation

Feeding broilers, layers and breeders — phase feeding, formulation, housing and the economics of each bird.

What this course covers

12 modules · 72 written lessons · about 49,977 words · 360 quiz questions · 12 capstone projects.

Every lesson has learning objectives, worked numbers, a "do this today" task and a knowledge check. Videos are free material made by other people; the written lessons are the course.

Module 16 lessons

Poultry Nutrition Fundamentals

The building blocks every poultry feeding decision rests on - energy, protein and amino acids, vitamins, minerals, water and the ingredients you can actually buy in an African market. Every figure here is traceable to NRC 1994, Aviagen Ross 2022, Hy-Line or Lohmann published standards, and every calculation is worked step by step in dollars so you can repeat it with your own prices.

  1. Energy Metabolism in Poultry
  2. Protein and Amino Acid Requirements
  3. Vitamin Requirements and Functions
  4. Mineral Requirements and Balance
  5. Water Requirements and Quality
  6. Feed Ingredient Characteristics

Module 26 lessons

Broiler Chicken Nutrition

Feeding the modern meat chicken from day-old to sale, phase by phase, using Aviagen Ross 2022 specifications and NRC 1994 standards. Covers what genetics have done to growth rate, exactly what each of the three phases must deliver, which additives are worth their cost in an African house, and how to run the flock so the feed you bought becomes the weight you sell.

  1. Broiler Genetics and Growth Rate
  2. Starter Feeds (0-10 days)
  3. Grower Feeds (10-24 days)
  4. Finisher Feeds (24-42 days)
  5. Feed Additives for Broilers
  6. Flock Management and Feeding Strategy

Module 36 lessons

Layer Chicken Nutrition

Feeding the laying hen across her whole life - from the day-old pullet whose frame you are building, through the pre-lay switch, peak production, and the long declining tail after 60 weeks. Built on Hy-Line Brown and Lohmann Brown-Classic published standards and NRC 1994 daily requirements, with the calcium, particle size and body weight arithmetic worked out in full.

  1. Pre-Lay Pullet Nutrition (0-16 weeks)
  2. Early Lay Nutrition (16-30 weeks)
  3. Peak Layer Nutrition (30-60 weeks)
  4. Late Lay Nutrition (60+ weeks)
  5. Eggshell Quality and Calcium Metabolism
  6. Flock Uniformity and Performance Monitoring

Module 46 lessons

Breeding Flock Nutrition

Feeding the birds that produce your chicks. Breeders are the only poultry class where over-feeding is as damaging as under-feeding, where the male and the female need different diets, and where the consequences of a nutrient shortfall show up in someone else's brooder house a week later. Covers male and female feeding, the fertility nutrients, what passes from hen to chick, body condition control, and breeder-specific health.

  1. Breeder Male Nutrition
  2. Breeder Female Nutrition
  3. Vitamin and Mineral for Fertility
  4. Nutritional Impacts on Chick Quality
  5. Managing Body Condition in Breeders
  6. Flock Health and Disease Prevention
