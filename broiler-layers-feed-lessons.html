Course contents

🐔 Broiler and Layer Feed Formulation

Feeding broilers, layers and breeders — phase feeding, formulation, housing and the economics of each bird.

What this course covers

4 modules · 24 written lessons · about 16,199 words · 120 quiz questions · 4 capstone projects.

Every lesson has learning objectives, worked numbers, a "do this today" task and a knowledge check. Videos are free material made by other people; the written lessons are the course.

Module 96 lessons

Poultry Health and Disease Prevention Through Nutrition

How feed decisions prevent the diseases that kill African poultry flocks - gut health, coccidiosis, necrotic enteritis, leg problems, heat stress and water quality - using published nutrient standards instead of guesswork and antibiotics.

  1. Feed as the First Line of Defence: Gut Barrier and Immune Nutrition
  2. Coccidiosis Control: Coccidiostats, Immunity and Withdrawal Rules
  3. Necrotic Enteritis: Feeding to Keep Clostridium Under Control
  4. Antioxidant Nutrition and Heat Stress in Hot Climates
  5. Preventing Leg Problems: Calcium, Phosphorus and Vitamin D3
  6. Water Quality: The Cheapest Nutrient and the Most Neglected

Module 106 lessons

Feed Quality, Storage, Mycotoxins and Biosecurity

Protecting the biggest cost on your farm. How to buy grain you can trust, recognise and manage mycotoxin risk in African maize and groundnut, store feed so it does not spoil, control anti-nutritional ingredients, keep honest batch records, and run biosecurity that a smallholder can actually afford.

  1. Buying Grain You Can Trust: Practical Intake Checks Without a Laboratory
  2. Mycotoxins in African Maize and Groundnut: The Limits That Matter
  3. Building a Dry, Rat-Proof Feed Store on a Small Budget
  4. Anti-Nutritional Factors: Cottonseed, Rapeseed, Phytate and Safe Inclusion Limits
  5. Mixing, Milling and Batch Records That Prevent Costly Mistakes
  6. Farm Biosecurity a Smallholder Can Actually Afford

Module 116 lessons

Specialty and Alternative Poultry Production

Free-range, organic, indigenous breeds, slow-growing meat birds and enriched eggs. When these premium routes genuinely pay in an African market, how to feed the birds correctly, and how to price and sell a product that costs more to produce.

  1. Deciding Whether Free-Range Pays on Your Farm
  2. Feeding Indigenous and Dual-Purpose Chickens Properly
  3. Organic and Antibiotic-Free Production: Rules, Feed and Realities
  4. Enriched Eggs: Omega-3, Selenium and Deep Yolk Colour
  5. Slow-Growing Meat Birds and Cockerels for Local Taste Markets
  6. Pricing and Selling Specialty Poultry Products

Poultry Business Development and Scaling

Turning a poultry operation into a business. Costing production honestly, planning cash across a cycle, keeping records that change decisions, deciding when to mill your own feed, financing growth without dangerous debt, and scaling in steps that do not break what already works.

  1. Knowing Your True Cost of Production Per Bird and Per Egg
  2. Cash Flow Planning Across a Production Cycle
  3. Records That Actually Change Decisions
  4. Deciding When to Mill and Mix Your Own Feed
  5. Financing Growth: Savings Groups, Loans and Contract Arrangements
