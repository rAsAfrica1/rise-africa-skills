Seedling Nursery for Farmers

Grow seedlings, sell to farmers — a business that reproduces itself every season.

Every farmer needs seedlings at the start of the season — and few raise their own. A well-run seedling nursery can sell hundreds of thousands of seedlings per season to vegetable, fruit, and tree farmers. This course teaches propagation, nursery setup, quality control, logistics, and the sales channels that keep nurseries profitable.
