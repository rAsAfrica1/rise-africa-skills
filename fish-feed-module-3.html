3. Ration Formulation

Formulation is a calculation, not a guess. With the Pearson square and the formulas in this module, you can build any fish feed in 10 minutes.

Module 3 of 6 · Fish Feed Production · quiz at the end

Lesson 3.1 - The Pearson square

Example: Make a 30% protein tilapia grow-out feed using maize (9% CP) and fish meal (60% CP).

  1. Write 30 in the centre.
  2. Write 9 and 60 in the left corners.
  3. Subtract diagonally: 60-30 = 30, and 30-9 = 21.
  4. Result: 30 parts maize and 21 parts fish meal, i.e. 58.8% maize + 41.2% fish meal.

In practice, this ration is too expensive and lacks minerals. We use the square to balance the protein, then add the rest of the ingredients and re-check.

Lesson 3.6 - Binder and oil

Pellets must hold together for 5-10 minutes underwater. Add:

  • Cassava starch - 3-5% of total. Cheap and works well.
  • Vegetable oil - 2-5%. Also adds energy and reduces dust.
  • Wheat gluten or gelatin - commercial binders, more expensive.
The formulation rule: Weigh every ingredient to the gram. A fish can't pick and choose what it eats - whatever you mix is what it eats. Accuracy is everything.
