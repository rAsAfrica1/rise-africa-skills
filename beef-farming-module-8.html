Pricing strategy

  1. Know your break-even - cost per kg of beef produced.
  2. Never sell below break-even - even in a glut.
  3. Price the grade, not the animal - premium cattle earn premium prices.
  4. Consistency beats one-off premiums - regular buyers are worth more.
  5. Factor payment delay - 30-day invoiced sales cost you interest.
The market rule: One confirmed buyer before finishing cattle is worth more than 10 buyers you hope to find at market time. Sign simple agreements - weight range, grade, price range, delivery day, payment terms.
Practical exercise 8: Visit 5 potential buyers - 2 butcheries, 2 restaurants, 1 auction. Ask their current prices, weekly volumes, and delivery preferences. Write everything down.
