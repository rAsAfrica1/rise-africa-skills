Course contents

🐄 Cattle and Steer Feed Formulation

Feeding cattle from calf to finished steer, and smallholder dairy — rumen function, forage, rations and economics.

What this course covers

12 modules · 72 written lessons · about 56,954 words · 360 quiz questions · 12 capstone projects.

Every lesson has learning objectives, worked numbers, a "do this today" task and a knowledge check. Videos are free material made by other people; the written lessons are the course.
