🛠️ Implementation Steps

  1. Budget: Track income and expenses
  2. Emergency Fund: Save 3-6 months expenses
  3. Debt: Pay down high-interest debt
  4. Invest: Start with stocks or funds
  5. Retire: Contribute to retirement accounts
  6. Review: Reassess quarterly
